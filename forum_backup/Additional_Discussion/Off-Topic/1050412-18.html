                                                            02-24-2017, 03:33 PM
                                                            Originally posted by hm8453a View Post

                                                            How did that go, by the way?
                                                            So just shy of one year to the day, I shot new White Wolf an email in the aftermath of one of their first interview that really rubbed me the wrong way.

                                                            Hello,
                                                            Reading your interview for the new direction you're taking the World of Darkness in, there seems to be an undertone of flipping the bird to political correctness. Maybe I'm reading too much into things, but I'm terrified of losing out on what feels like an essential part of the WoD history; from the original games use of 'she' as the default pronoun to the CofD's inclusiveness towards LGBTQA individuals, I've always viewed the property as profoundly progressive. Can you put any of my fears of a backlash against this to rest?

                                                            Best regards,

                                                            Lex, a longtime fan of both the World of Darkness and Chronicles of Darkness
                                                            And I received two replies, first from Tobias:

                                                            Hi Lex,

                                                            Great that you point that out, we need to review how we expressed ourselves. Both me and Martin are with you to 100% that being both progressive and testing the barriers is something that is absolute key for World of Darkness.

                                                            Definitely please let us know if we don't do our job on that front.

                                                            Best Regards
                                                            /Tobias
                                                            And then our very special friend Martin:

                                                            Mosdef not flipping the bird. Quite the opposite. We are including the most flammable real world themes to explore them and address them both artistically and with a strong political analysis. White Wilf could never be a right-wing. It's progressive to the core. Lifelong activist meself. To do progressive analysis sexual violence, oppression, poverty etc. need to feature prominently and played. To me excusing them smacks of Victorian / American moralism and conservatism. WoD is a wehicle for exploring the forbidden. Yes, and working on some interesting designs on gender, sexuality and "ethnicity" you have never seen in computer games.
                                                            WoD is art. It explores. It allows us to take a first person perspective on complex social issues and learn its workings and the temptation it brings. With this analysis we stand more ready, as individuals and as a collective, to kill the vampire in us and oppose it in society.

                                                            Blood and souls




                                                            Remi. she/her. game designer.
