                            While I am extremely sympathetic towards... all of that... having worked on too many failed educational "fixes" at this point in my life... a big fix to US culture would be to sell the ends of means testing as a way to ensure individual freedom. Right now means testing is the source of the vast majority of bureaucratic red tape, but far too many Americans are sold on the idea that we need to means test everything to make sure the "wrong" people don't get stuff... no matter how many people that really need it don't get it because the system is busted, and how much we waste enforcing these rules that don't actually produce the results we want.

                            Means testing is one of those things that kind of sounds good on an intuitive level (only poor people need food stamps, so lets make sure people asking for food stamps are poor), but in reality they always makes the system worse in aggregate. It is not hard to frame this as an attack on individualism and personal freedoms... because it is. It reduces people to numbers and strips them of context, so they can be spat of the system without the help they need to "save money" (even if it costs more to reject them than it would have to help them), to the point where there are people that can't get married, or buy a new car, or get a new job, because the system would screw them if they did! The government is threatening to hike your taxes for starting your own business to get out of poverty? That's an insult to American ruggedness!

                            But that's not how it gets sold to the public at all. If it was, we could actually fix a lot of problems by getting rid of the bureaucracies that stand in the way by getting rid of the rules they exist to enforce.
