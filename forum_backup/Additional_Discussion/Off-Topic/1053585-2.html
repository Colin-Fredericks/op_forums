                                                        There's a lot of, 'I don't agree with these people, so they should shut up.' With some 'they should die or be arrested' interspersed in these threads, not just this one.

                                                        And I get it.

                                                        I don't like people complaining. Pointing out problems I don't necessarily have to deal with, and couldn't do anything about anyways.

                                                        But at the other end of things, if people don't stand up, these things go on and on and on. 'Why didn't you say something sooner?' 'That's a once in a blue moon thing.' And we've seen crimes that have gone unreported for years and years because people didn't want to make a fuss. Institutions of callous indifference that go on and on because people don't want to kick over a rotting log and see the bugs underneath. The state I'm currently living in had a Eugenics Board that forcibly sterilized people well into the 70s. And while it was outwardly for the 'public good,' to keep people from becoming burdens of the state, the truth is it started targeting non-whites in disproportionate numbers, considering their population.

                                                        And yes, not every white person was going out and doing these things. And plenty of doctors didn't want to do it or get involved, but it still happened.

                                                        Even more recently, the same state, after laws and time had passed that allowed more and more people to vote until POC were voting near to the level that whites were, the state legislature flat out requested data on how people voted, by race, then 'enacted legislation that restricted voting and registration in five different ways, all of which disproportionately affected African Americans.'

                                                        These are things people do in our modern times. Sure, some of it is simply borne from an, 'us versus them' mentality, but we're not animals, and biases have been shown to be things people can get over. But it's not something that's ever going to go away if it's never acknowledged in the first place.
