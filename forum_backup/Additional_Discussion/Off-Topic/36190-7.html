                                                            Now I've had time to think about it I can completely see why CCP cancelled it. The MMO market is both saturated and shrinking. WoW's bleeding subscribers and most other high profile games are either shutting down or going F2P (which seems to be the final step before shutting down). The dismal launch of Elder Scrolls Online was probably the impetus to can the project, after all a WoD MMO would be a niche game in a difficult market when CCP need to shore up their main (only) product to prevent it going the way of everything else in the MMO space.

                                                            Hopefully the project can be resurrected once the MMO sector has stabilised at a lower level and in the meantime the assets already produced could be licensed to a thord party developer to create a single player Vampire game that CCP can publish to get some return on their outlay.
