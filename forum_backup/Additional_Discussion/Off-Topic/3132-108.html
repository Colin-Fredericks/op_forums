                    My parents are in the midst of a messy divorce and to say I am depressed by it is an understatement. On the positive side, this is happening when I am 22 and not a child, and my younger siblings have reached adulthood as well, so it could be worse, but I am still torn up about it.

                    You see, my parents have hated each other for years and years now (though both of them loved and cared for me and my brothers, and still do) and upon realizing that his marriage was essentially dead, my father began seeing another woman and had been planning a divorce for quite some time, and then in a verbal fight, my dad tells my mom about this woman and everything gets kicked into overdrive with the venom, bitterness, and hatred that had been brewing for at least a decade, maybe more. Now my dad is out of the house, living in Wise County, VA, while I am still living with my mother, brothers, and uncle here in Roanoke (a good four hour drive away from Wise), and while I still keep in touch with my dad as best I can, and I still love him (I am a little mad at him for not waiting until the divorce was finalized before he began dating again), it hurts me very much not having him in the house.

                    I know it seems pathetic, a twenty-two year old man missing his father and wanting him back in the household, but since I was a small child, my dad was one of my closest friends and still is. We have a lot in common, and if it weren't for him, I wouldn't be on this forum because he was the one who introduced me to RPG's when I was thirteen (my first RPG was D&D 3.5, I got into WoD when I was 16). Worst part is, I partially blame myself for this. While intellectually I know I had nothing to do with the divorce, and both my parents assured me of that fact, I still emotionally feel guilty because of my rebellious behavior during my adolescent years as well as my various mental health issues. I love both my parents equally and refuse to take sides, but it still hurts me. And this divorce really threw a curveball in my life, as I was in the process of moving out of my parents house, but now I must stay a little longer to help out my overburdened mother until things stabilize. I am glad to help my mom, she works hard at both a full-time job and her classes (she is a LPN working for the state, but is in the process of acquiring her RN status. For those not in the know, RN stands for Registered Nurse while LPN stands for Licensed Practical Nurse), but this did come at a bad time for all of us in our family. But I must remain vigilant and hopefully things will stabilize for everyone involved in this crisis.

                    I apologize in advance for whining about this and laying this burden on you guys, I just feel really bad about all this and need to vent. Maybe this should go in the mental health thread, but I do hate the fact that my parents are divorcing and my dad now lives four hours away (especially since I don't have a driver's license or a car yet).
                    Last edited by Camilla; 11-13-2015, 03:24 PM.
