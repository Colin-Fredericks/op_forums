                        Those are both tough questions.

                        For Devils, back in 3e it was Levistus hands down, with Bel and Fierna as runnerups. The Hag Countess grew on me later on. Then Pathfinder (specifically, Wes) came along and made all of the Archdevils I thought were bland into these super interesting fiends, then tossed in some extra cool ones just for fun. Suffice to say, when I run Planescape these days there are more than nine Archdevils, but some of them play bureaucratic roles like Hell Senechal (Barbartos), Hell's General (Moloch), or Hell's Traitor (Geryon or Levistus). I've written a bit about this here, although I've been meaning to say more.

                        Demons is kinda worse, because I'm Abyss person (although really I'm a Yugoloth person), but Dagon, Baphomet, Obox-ob, and Pale Night stand out for me. I love all of them though (as villains).

                        What about evil demigods? Kyuss gets my vote. The Kyuss worms are just the freakiest things ever and I once had a grand campaign arc where it was Kyuss and who cause the Great Modron March instead of Orcus (although he was being manipulated by the Baernoloths).
