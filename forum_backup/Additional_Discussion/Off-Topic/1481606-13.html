                                    So, I've seen Furiosa and it was good. A fun action-movie. I don't think it really made a good case for why we needed this prequel and it isn't nearly as good as Fury Road but on its own terms it's good fun. The action is still good, although the movie clearly relied on more CGI this time around. One thing I really didn't expect was for how long you'd see kid-Furiosa in this movie and it's only at the midway-point where it transitions to Anya Taylor-Joy. I think her performance is good and once she makes that final transformation to where she looks like in Fury Road, she actually manages to do a decent Charlize Theron impression. Of course, it also helps that she doesn't have to say a whole lot. Chris Hemsworth as Dementus is also good, I think. He's doing the "funny Thor"-thing but with more psychopath-vibes. Furiosa has this relationship with Tom Burke who plays this driver who had Furiosa's position as main-driver before her. And that part's a bit weird. They're close but it's very understated. In fact, it's so understated, it's hard to tell whether they're in love or just good friends or it's a mentor-student-connection... And it doesn't get a lot of screen-time either, so that makes it even harder to pinpoint what exactly was going on there.

                                    But the standout for me was the costume-design and the character-work in the movie. You of course already have this striking imagery with Imorten Joe and the War Boys. But Dementus has this band of misfits and there are like five or six very distinct henchmen who just ooze personality (both in looks and performance). The movie absolutely nails that part. Like, the doctor in the group calls himself "the Organic Mechanic". Just a lot of creative ideas on display there.
