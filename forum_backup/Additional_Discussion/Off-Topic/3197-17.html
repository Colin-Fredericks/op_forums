                        ArcaneArts, I finally saw EG2, and pretty much agree with every single thing you wrote about it...except the conclusion. I dunno, while I agree with your analysis of the storyline problems, in the end, they just weren't bad enough to throw me out of the narrative. I have no problem with saying that I enjoyed this one more than the first, in fact!

                        The music was certainly better, thanks largely to the Dazzlings. I also noticed that there were fewer impromptu musical numbers, most of the singing was actual in-setting performances (it always kinda bothered me that the first story in the series lampshaded how shocked the Mane Six were that Pinkie would suddenly break out into song...only for them all to start doing it throughout the rest of the series!). And I think I LOL more at the comedy than at any other story, esp Snips and Snails' rap and Celestia's deadpan reaction ("Please do not drop the microphones!").

                        I'm actually sad that I didn't get to see it theatrically, as it would have been amusing to hear the audience's reaction to a lot of the shout outs and references...half of the audience, anyway, although seeing the non-Brony kids and their parents' reaction to jokes they were getting (like Maud's cameo and Octavia suddenly speaking) would also have been interesting.

                        In fact, I was really surprised how, aside from the opening credits pantomime and a couple of quick lines here and there, the movie explained very little about what had happened before and who was who, to a degree I'm usually used to from anime film adaptations! You didn't need to have seen the series to get the first movie, but if you were walking into this one by itself, I suspect you'd be pretty lost...

                        And yeah, I am sold on reformed Sunset now, esp seeing her having to deal with the consequences of her actions. Interestingly, the Dazzlings aren't even given as much as she was in the prior movie. What happened to them? I half expected to see them handcuffed in the back of a police van or something, but...nothing! Certainly no indication that they've been "reformed", so apparently they're still evil, just powerless? Hmm...

                        As a Trixie fan, it was nice to see her get much more screen time, effectively becoming the secondary villain! And apparently her act was good enough to make it to the finals on its own; didn't really like her song, but it's annoyingly catchy! Anywho, hopefully this will inspire a return for Pony Trixie in the new season, or the upcoming Pony-Only movie they just announced...
