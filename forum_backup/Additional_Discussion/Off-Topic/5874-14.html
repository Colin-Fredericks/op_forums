                    Game Show Network (GSN) is experimenting with an interesting "haunted house"-themed game show. Hellevator debuted last week, immersing the contestants in the story of a murderous undertaker. And it looks very promising, using a lot of traditional haunted house tricks to spice up each challenge. The second one, for example, was getting exactly twelve pounds of "human organs" on a scale. And as the contestant start removing organs from a pair of "cadavers", they suddenly woke up and started screaming and begging for help. Classic gimmick, new venue.

                    Not sure how well it will fare as we move farther away from Halloween. But for now, it looks good.

                            Originally posted by etherial View Post
                            I'm thinking of canceling my recordings of the new Muppet Show. Uncle Deadly is the only consistently good character, and every single episode has at least one plotline that is "Kermit is a terrible person and you should despise him".
                            I've been reading lots of back-and-forth about this series, largely along the lines of "This sucks, the Muppets that I watched growing up didn't have adult humor in it!" and "Oh, it *absolutely* did, you just weren't old enough to notice it back then!" Similarly, while many don't like the unpleasant qualities of Kermit, others point out that he *always* had those qualities, although they may be admittedly more pronounced now. OTOH, Kermit in the movies was more of an all-around Nice Guy, and that's probably had more impact on the collective memory of the character.

                            So, in a way, the new show is just getting Back To Basics.

                            For a similar approach to another children's icon, look at Bugs Bunny's new show "Wabbit.", which gives all the folks who complained about The Looney Toons Show what they wanted. Personally, I thought TLTS was *fantastic*, and can barely watch the new one. It's not *bad*, it's just...the Looney Toons have Been There and Done That, and this isn't really an improvement. In fact, TLTS specifically avoided that type of show because it had already been done and they knew they couldn't top the original LT shorts, and they wanted to go in a new direction with these characters!

                            I really hope the people whose complaints brought about Wabbit. watch it and realize, "Oooh, yeah...Now I get why they didn't just do this in the first place...yeah, bring back the other show."
