                    Originally posted by AzraelFirestorm View Post
                    Wednesday, January 13th, just after midnight


                    I hesitate with snaking fingers typing this. Hitting all the wrong keys. Don't know why yet. I should know.

                    Life is seemingly fruitless thus far. I tell myself it's unfair, but that I'll make something good of myself. Is it that easy to lie to yourself? How often do we do that, if so? We're purpose built to take the easy route when possible, people. Insects would probably think we were downright flattering. If they could think.

                    Are We Thinking, though? I wonder. I see people, sometimes especially myself, being confused and distracted by all the shiny new trash they sell like hotcakes. Give them your time, money and health, they'll give you water tainted with human waste and a paycheck that still qualifies you for welfare. If you're lucky.

                    People so famous people scream their names, their pop songs and their bastardized blues masquerading as new Country music. I don't recognize them. They appear from nowhere, but they matter to people. I've listened to them, hoping to hear a new angle, a new idea...or just some heart. I'm disappointed

                    Writing enough to not feel like a waste. Delving into the Vicious World. I always am anyway. Telling stories. That's good. People like stories. I don't have a choice. I can't understand the why without story.

                    So many invisible lines that everyone else seems to navigate just fine. Am I somehow blind? Keep grasping at straws with my petty feelings. I need to give it up. People like me don't have happy endings.

                    We rant.
                    We fall quiet.
                    We fight.
                    We are silenced.
                    We find freedom.
                    We die either free or silenced.
                    The crazy ones never get taken seriously.
                    Imagine if we were.

                    The blade, resting now
                    Bloody hands held it before
                    Cold, dishonored hands

                    There's no purpose for people who want to start some shit anymore. Picking fights with authority and railing against the heavens is shouting into the void....

                    ..but I'm going to keep shouting. I believe in the triumphant weird! I believe in LOVE! I believe in motherfucking HAPPY ENDINGS!
                    fuck YOU, VOID!!
                    Having an absurdism moment, are we? Enjoy it for a while. It can be fun.

                    - If you must be ridiculous, I must ridicule you.
                    - Those that can give up essential liberties in exchange for temporary safety deserve neither liberty nor safety. -- Benjamin Franklin

                                            Things You don't Realize When You're Delusional And Psychotic


                                            I'd wager we've all had bad days (PB is covering the bets. It's cool.). Everyone does. Even Taylor Swift. That's not me wishing ill on a pop music star, either. It's just an example. Also, just had to air out some schadenfreude fantasy, before I build a Hate Shrine. You build Hate Shrines, right? Of course you do.
                                            Those bad days we have are..bad, obviously. They bruise your feelings, break your heart and spill Mountain Dew on your PS4.

                                            What does a bad day look like, though, when reality has left the building?
                                            Resident comedian and actual crazy person, Me, has answers.

                                            1. You Don't Know At All

                                            I used the word psychotic. If you 'member your thinky-words correctly, this first point is obvious. To you. That's not so for the person laboring under (why under? Does this phrase need to be retired? I've never been whipped on the job.) a delusional psychosis. The world just goes bananas, and psychotic person just lives that shit. Like they were dragged to a Grateful Dead concert because they fell asleep in a friend's Hyundai and everyone said "fuck it" in unison before giving them the brown acid.

                                            2. You Do Weird Shit And It Seems Perfectly Reasonable

                                            As an extension of the first point, this might also come as no surprise. The surprise is: you can also do normal shit in weird motherfucking ways, too! Yay, variety! I've personally had my share of "hide all the sharp objects. He thinks we're dangerous ninjas" moments, where I've done such exciting things as setting my arm on fire because Fire Punching, speaking in code around...everyone, and challenging the neighborhood kids to skateboard fights, bat'leth style.

                                            They did WHAT to our foreheads??

                                            What you won't usually see is the mild but undoubtedly skewed normal person things: eating in the bathroom because the rest of the house is "watching", taking random noises personally in the same way you take a sideways shit-talking text message (oh, no you didn't call ME a diva, vibration of the universe!) and calling your friends...to make sure they're really your friends...and also just real. There are more examples, but what am I, a professional exampler? Use your imagination.

                                            Moving on..

                                            3. Nobody Understands (You think)

                                            Feeling misunderstood is common. As common as zits, awkward sex and fumbling awkward sex when you have ALL the zits. We've all been there. Nobody "gets" you. It's alienating. It's painful. It usually doesn't involve yelling at a cat for being judgmental. That's a psychotic break for ya!


                                            I've personally had a weird goddamn year. Don't blame the cat. He's actually a cool dude. I've been screwed with in ways I'd never experienced, made loads of friends (and made some into bitter enemies!) and lost too many damn people in general. It's normal for a person in those circumstances to feel bitter, and hurt by the world. They might make poor decisions or lose a certain joy for some things. It happens.

                                            I, on the other hand, decided to murder myself slowly for artistic integrity. If you'll remember, artistic integrity was what we used to validate creative people before the internet pooped Twitter onto the actual television news.

                                            Really glad I purposely had my teeth chipped for a film I barely smile in.

                                            No offense, but using anesthetics is a bitch move.

                                            Walking in the footsteps of Hemingway, John Belushi, and some other guys (memory's a little fuzzy), I started drinking. A lot. I was and am lapsing into unusual and sometimes delusional thoughts, but I was getting stuff Done. I skipped past my usual three pages of comedy a week to three a day. It was good stuff. Trust me, I'm totally funny and stuff. Suuure, I was drinking myself to sleep, drinking to "feel creative", drinking myself out to lunch, but I was living the dream! Nobody understood my genius! I told myself that while avoiding my family this past thanksgiving. Why? Totally thought someone was going to ask me to murder someone for them. .
                                            I mean, I'm obviously a supercharged killing machine, but I couldn't be bothered with that uncreative murder stuff! I had writing to do.
                                            By Christmas, I was just always drunk. It made the weird feel less weird. I felt warm inside. The warmth was mostly hard liquor, but my point stands; I was "fine". I even thought the whole thing had a romance to it. Leaving Las Vegas is a romantic comedy, right? Right.

                                            I was "misunderstood" and just "doing my thing". Writing comedy, thinking people were following me, crying on the floor and drinking mouthwash. You know, the everyday grind.

                                            4. You Feel So, So Bad When It Fades

                                            I won't go on about the crazy amount of diarrhea and weird smells you start to produce when you're binge drinking and doing enough drugs to make the booze wonder if you're cheating on it. Your imagination probably did already.
                                            Stop it. That's gross. But that's not the bad feeling I mean. ..which makes me wonder why I mentioned diarrhea..

                                            Anyway, you feel bad emotionally when whatever it is snaps back into place. You might feel sad. You might feel ashamed. You might think you're a genuine asshole...possibly because you were being an asshole. You might feel like someone just shut off your "I'm a good person" valve, and that it's your fault no matter the circumstances, like your friend should feel after spilling Mountain Dew on your PS4. The transition is fucking brutal, and it's all you, baby. Hey, you remember that time you wish you could forget? Haha, you don't have amnesia, asshole! You remember your sins like a masochist in a monastery where you beat yourself like that albino guy in The Da Vinci code. They're YOURS.
                                            Being nice to yourself can be hard at this point, because you've been The Crazy One. And not the cool way where you did a handstand on your skateboard and chugged a Mountain Dew, Sean. Your mind was fucking with YOU, and by proxy, everyone else around you. Mental illness can be like your sluttiest of exes, getting all intimate with your closest friends while still actively messing with your head.

                                            The snapback IS brutal, but it's a comforting brutal in its way. It's like a punch to the face that hurts like hell, but you've been blinded by some strange alien acid spit and you REALLY needed to know that you still had a face.
                                            For me, that was about two hours ago (premature documentation?). It was a process, stuffing my noodles back into my brain's ramen bowl. A surprisingly fast one, if you don't know the rainbow of psychoses possible with my conditions. It started with my friends being worried and getting on my ass. With love. Lots of love on my ass.
                                            They made me think, and kindly offered non psychotic logic to aid me. I'm still going through the rough shit, but hey, at least I didn't set myself on fire again!

                                            Yet.
