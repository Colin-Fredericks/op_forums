                                I have recently become aware of the existence of a game called Vampire Syndicate, which is a tactical RPG that combines elements of Vampire: the Masquerade with X-Com, and is set in a Cyberpunk world. As the game is still in early access, it's very bare bones right now; but from what's been talked about so far, it shows a ton of promise.

                                The basic premise to the game so far, is that you start off as a mortal working the streets of Moonfall, who hires themselves as a mercenary to one of four possible lineages of vampires. As you continue to do missions for them and build your reputation, you steadily climb up the ranks, eventually being granted the privilege of becoming one of them. It's also implied that in future updates, we may be able to seize control of the lineage as its new leader, and force our rivals out of the city.

                                What makes the vampires stand out from each other, is that they aren't just divergent bloodlines. Each of the different lineages exist as their own totally separate species, who's powers and physiology are unique to their particular lineage. Even the manner in which they reproduce is different.


                                The lineage which I'm currently most interested in are the Cthonians, who have a Lovecraftian theme to them. They claim that their origins are linked back to some kind of eldritch being, whom they worship like a god, and are trying to find a way to bring about their return to our world.


                                Another lineage are the Sunseekers, who's progenitor was cursed with vampirism after slaying an Egyptian snake goddess and being splattered in its blood. She originally created her lineage as a knightly order to protect mortals from supernatural threats, but they have since fallen from this purpose and joined the very monsters they swore to oppose.


                                There is also the Dragonblooded, who are obsessed with the study of magic and previously held the position of strongest lineage, before their empire collapsed. Since then, they have adopted an isolationist approach towards the outside world.

                                As well as the Exiled House, who boast the greatest martial prowess among their order of honorable warriors. Rumors have accused them of being allegedly responsible for a great act of betrayal, that lead to the downfall of the previous vampire nation, forcing the surviving lineages to retreat into the shadows.
