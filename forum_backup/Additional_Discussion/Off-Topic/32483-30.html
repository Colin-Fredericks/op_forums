                                            You don't want to be god. It is an implausible concept which cannot exist in a confirmed universe.

                                            There is more than pain here. That's where the Buddhists take the wrong turn. Life is what you choose to make it. If you think it sucks, change your mind until it doesn't. It's only as hard as you make it, and believe me, I know it's hard. I fight it every day.

                                            Someone without fear is someone with nothing left to lose. Fear is nothing to be afraid of - fear is a superpower. Fear can make you faster and cleverer and stronger… Fear doesn’t have to make you cruel or cowardly. Fear can make you kind. So listen. If you listen to nothing else, listen to this: you’re always gonna be afraid, even if you learn to hide it. Fear is like a companion. A constant companion, always there. But that’s okay. Because fear can bring us together. Fear can bring you home. Fear makes companions of us all. (Thank you, Doctor)

                                            Evil is perspective which can be changed. Things are usually never that black & white. Our stories often are written as such because they are what we wish the world could be instead of what it is.

                                            Suicide is not freedom. That's an illusion. No life is not freedom, it is the total opposite. It is permanent transfixion within an inanimate state. That's like saying a rock is free. Life is the only freedom as it is the only state in which you have options.

                                            Insanity is also not a freedom and another illusion. That is allowing one part of your body to decide everything else that's going on, whether real or not. That's like letting your liver decide if you can see a tree in front of you. The Cartesian duality is insanity. It separates the whole person into discrete parts, ignoring the whole person.

                                            It's not insanity to acknowledge that the universe makes no sense. It is, in fact, perfect clarity. The universe only has natural forces interacting with each other. Any other sense, order or patterns are man-made. Human brains are hard-wired to make patterns where there are none. We crave it, we need it, we believe that we cannot live without it and, to a degree, we are correct. We mad little humans could not go on without getting over the hump of "universal forces do not a patterned universe make". You can check out of life if you can't deal with it, but as I've already said, I don't recommend it. You can MAKE your own patterns, decide what is important to you and live that way. Or you can simply acknowledge the absurdity of the universe, have a good laugh about it, and move on with the business of living.
