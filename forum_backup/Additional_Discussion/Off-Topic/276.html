    Introductions and Greetings

    11-01-2013, 01:47 PM
    Since we're on a new version of the forums, I thought it might be a nice thing to have a thread where people can introduce themselves and talk a little bit about their history with gaming in general and White Wolf/Onyx Path games in particular. Since I started this thread, I guess I'll go first. =)

    My name is Jeremy, and I've been gaming since I was 13--about 20 years now. I started with GURPS and moved on to D&D as my third or fourth game. My first exposure to the World of Darkness was in high school, but I didn't really get into White Wolf stuff until college, where I helped set up and manage a Vampire LARP that lasted about two years. I have a love/hate relationship with the Storyteller system; I love the settings, but I've never been able to run a tabletop game that lasted more than five sessions. XD

    My particular love is Exalted. Though I'm not that fond of the 2E system, I have a nearly complete collection of EX2 and I was a backer for EX3. I've been running an Exalted game for the last two years using Mutants & Masterminds. We have an Obsidian Path wiki if anyone wants to see what we've done with the game. I'm really looking forward to the new edition; maybe I'll actually get to run Exalted using the base system this time around.

    I was the user hidajiremi on the old forums; blackwingedheaven is what I use on most other forums, though, so I'm switching to it here.

    I've written for a few role-playing games, and I'm technically still a freelancer even though I haven't done much work lately. Graduate school don'tchaknow. In a few months, I'll have a master's degree in library and information science, and I'm already a card-carrying member of the ALA. I also write fiction, and I'm currently working on my first novel, Seven Bridges, in addition to writing a scandalous amount of Skyrim fanfiction.

    Hi, everyone! Let's make the new forums awesome! ^_^

      My name is also Jeremy!

      I've been playing White Wolf/Onyx Path titles for long enough that I can count their presence in over half the years I've lived. Everything from World of Darkness to Trinity Continuum to that awesome few years when Ravenloft was in Arthaus hands.

      My particular loves are, in no particular order, both flavors of Werewolf, Exalted, Trinity, Demon: the Fallen, and now Demon: the Descent. Changeling: the Dreaming too, but that's a secret.

      Other than that, I'm a filthy quote-unquote story gamer. Indie titles the likes of Monsterhearts and Smallville and Dresden have influenced how I approach the storyteller system.

      When I'm not playing make-believe, I'm a designer for an alternative energy company, and I doodle like whoa. Got a bit of graphic design under by belt. I love cats. And sriracha. Horror movies! I'd kill for a good backrub.
