                I hate Demon: The Fallen.

                In my opinion, it is a rather problematic game that seems forcibly shoehorned into the greater World of Darkness as a whole, and is very religiously insensitive on so many levels. I don't think WW was trying to be offensive to nearly every major religion (and even some minor ones as well) when they made Demon, but the whole game comes across as awful to me. The Earthbound book was the absolute worst offender in this regard, by stating that all non-Abrahamic religions were evil creations of the Earthbound and that the Abrahamic faiths were literally invented by Satan. I know it's just a game and that White Wolf wasn't trying to insult people's beliefs, but someone clearly did not think that one through.

                Overall, I find Demon to be a bad Paradise Lost/Menmoch The Devil fanfic game, and the fact that a lot of people consider it to be the "canon" origin story to the entire World of Darkness also kind of irks me. Which is why I Rule Zero Demon out of my World of Darkness.

                Others may like Demon: The Fallen, and that is fine with me. I won't bash anyone for liking a game, but I don't like Demon at all. I'm rather sensitive when it comes to discussions of religion, so maybe I am being over-sensitive, but I don't want Demon anywhere near my World of Darkness games.
