                                                Originally posted by solomon birch View Post
                                                What do you think about Twilight Sparkle becoming an alicorn?
                                                Dude, couldn't you have gone for something simple, like, I dunno, what is the meaning of life?

                                                *sigh* Okay, here we go.

                                                There are two ways to look at this: how she became an alicorn and her actually being an alicorn. I'm going to tackle the second first.

                                                My initial reaction to this is simply "They jumped the gun", which is hard to substantiate. See, I'm okay with Twilight becoming an alicorn because of what this ends up saying about the themes and attitude of the show-the idea that the virtues of friendship are what exalt the individual. Twilight becomes equal to the gods of her world not through cleverness or knowledge, but through the principles of knowing friendship and being friendly. I can wax on philosophical about this for a long old time about the bridge between the ideal and the real the show is promoting, but that in and of itself is a good idea.

                                                The show going on is also a really good idea, as it also sets new stakes and creates a new frame for the struggle of maintaining those virtues in "more real" or "more adult" situations-how do you practice kindness to everyone when you're the President of the United States and everyone has a specific form of kindness expressed, ignoring EVERYTHING ELSE. It grounds out the series.

                                                Too much of that is a problem though. Really, this sort of move should be saved for the last season, to be the game where you can break all the rules and try things out. On top of that, it keeps the narrative weight consistent, it hits as it should. Hence the feeling that it jumped the gun....sort of.

                                                The problem is that I have no idea how much of the show is coming after this. Kids shows like this actually are expected to have a life span of five seasons or so, and it could be that FiM is wrapping up now, or it could be doing a Season Five. If it ends this season, I'm saddened to see it go, but I'm glad it ends on this note. If not, I'm okay with it going on, but I think it betrays more bad comprehension about story telling.

                                                    So her actually being an Alicorn is an okay idea. Her not being one had potential, but quite frankly I'm okay with this. If nothing else, it makes the world more 'secular' for everyone, and that pisses people off less as a rule. I just hope they handle it okay.

                                                    So, the first side. What did I think of how she became an alicorn?

                                                    God fucking damnit, I hated it and I don't know why people like it so fucking much.

                                                    Magical Mystery Cure is BAD. It's SOOOOO BAD. IT'S RIDICULOUSLY BAD. It breaks the world, it's that bad. But for brevity's sake, we'll try to keep it to actual alicornication.

                                                    MMC is the episode where Twilight passes through her metaphorical Gate of Revolution(as it is traditionally used, not how Utena approached the idea), the culmination of all her lessons and experiences manifested as the final trial for her state of existence as it stands now. This is where she simultaneously defies and proves the world and opens up a whole new realm of opportunity. And to be fair, they set the stakes higher than they ever have in FiM-the lasting sense of satisfaction with self in her friends. Due to Twilight's errors, her friends faced a life where they would never really wholly be happy with themselves because they couldn't be themselves. That is heavy shit-your friends are basically denied themselves and the right to truly be happy and it's all your fault Harry Dresden.

                                                    Now, were this just a thing, magic as an answer with a healthy heaping of friendship slapped on is fine. But as the Gate of Revolution? As a Almighty Magnum Opus? Nope. Nope. Nope.

                                                    I suppose part of the problem is that it's hard to translate the metaphor(the spell) into the real world-is there an comparison to our lives, even as a general set of ideas, that fits the image? I dunno. But what should have happened(on the list of everything that should have happened) should have been the culmination of her understanding of friendship, the self-inflicted baptism of Dagda and Kali-that her friendship was enough, that her willingness to sacrifice for others could, help, could heal, could fill the gap left open. It shouldn't have been "Oh fuck,some magic and getting the original person to do shit to snap back memory will do the trick". This is the trial forged the lesson, the key to self-transformation that became the words of the spell that fucking changed her destiny-not only that excellence and sense of self can be realized through friends, but that friendship is, in some ways, above and beyond personal excellence(because a lot of people will never be excellent).

                                                    ​Add in everything else and how Twilight became an alicorn is abysmal. Songs were kind of nice though.
