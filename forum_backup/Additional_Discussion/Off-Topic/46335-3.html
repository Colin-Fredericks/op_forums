                                    So the ramen place was really good. The whole thing was apparently a bit hectic--they hadn't expected anywhere near the crowd that they got, so about 6:30 they stopped taking reservations, since they figured they were out of things to prepare--especially the titular noodles.

                                    My wife and I got the same dish, a bowl of ramen with corn, garlic oil, green onions, a soft-boiled egg, and pork--it should have had a single slice, but supplies being what they were we got some tender, coarsely chopped meat instead--with miso mixed into the broth. It was wonderful, and my wife informed me as we drove off that it put what I did to shame. I had no problem conceding this argument. I normally don't like my eggs anything but scrambled and dry, but here it worked. We're definitely coming back.

                                    TIL Snickerdoodles go really well with coffee.
