    More shows like "Firefly"?

    09-11-2016, 12:06 PM
    I was recently rewatching Firefly because, why not? Now I find myself looking for something with a similar look and feel, and hopefully, at least half as entertaining!

    But there seems to be a surprising lack in the "small band of misfit heroes traveling space in their smallish starship, trying to make ends meet while dealing with personal issues and overcoming dangerous obstacles" genre, or maybe I just don't know where to look, so, any suggestions? About the only other one I can think of is Cowboy Bebop (yes, anime is acceptable, too!)...

    Apropos of little, some friends of mine where debating about a "scale of importance/effectiveness" in sci-fi shows, that ranges from casts of characters with little to no importance to the universe in the grand scheme of things (however cool and entertaining they might be anyway), such as the Serenity and Bebop crews, to characters who are THE movers and shakers of events on a galactic (or larger) scale, such as Star Wars and Babylon 5. Starfleet crews on the various Star Trek series fit somewhere in between, being highly trained, valuable personnel, who are nonetheless just doing their jobs, which *might* make a larger difference depending on the plot (as in DS9)...

      I think "The 4400" is leagues better than "Firefly". As I understand it, part of the reason "Firefly" didn't continue was because Summer Glau left the show to be on "The 4400". She starts in season 2. And I just, all around, find it much more compelling. Probably because I find Whedon's writing sloppy and campy in most cases. But the characters are compelling, there's this incredible child character who's one of my favorite characters in anything. It's so good. Check it out. It's on Netflix, and season 1 is only six episodes.

      Also, "Black Mirror" is an amazing sci-fi show. "Terra Nova" is another, same writers as "The 4400". All of these are on Netflix. "Black Mirror" is only seven episodes total (Netflix is releasing six more in October), and "Terra Nova" is one season.

      Disclaimer: these are not all western sci-fi shows, but they're all sci-fi, and great sci-fi. That's the best I can do, but I highly recommend all of them.
