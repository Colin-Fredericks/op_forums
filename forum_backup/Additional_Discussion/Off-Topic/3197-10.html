                            S4E19 - For Whom the Sweetie Belle Toils

                            And we are back to form! I quite liked this episode. For one, I can totally relate to Sweetie Belle, as I've felt that kind of rage as well; that unreasonable, illogical (and mostly in my case, though not in Sweetie Belle's, undirected) anger when some detail just doesn't work out like you plan. I've been there, and it's aggravating.

                            Also, LUNA! The influence from A Christmas Carol was excellent. I always like when she shows up, but it's best kept rare. We've seen her twice as some sort of helping dream spirit, walking the line between the real and the ethereal, and while I lament that she's not made more of a character, I also don't think she'd be good as a main focus of an episode. That's also, I recon, why Celestia is kept mostly off camera; retaining the mystery and grandeur befitting millennia-old entities requires not, y'know, showing to much of them. But I do think the Royal Canterlot Voice could stand making a return.

                            Yeah, really good. Absolutely a fan.

                                                            Pony? Pony.

                                                            S4E20 - Leap of Faith

                                                            Oh shit son, this one rocked! Applejack episodes have been up-and-down recently, but I absolutely loved the sheer amount of stuff they put into this one. In talking about the placebo effect (but not using that word, of course), it touches on issues of scientific morality and the price of honesty. And what makes that so effective is that we get to see Applejack, the Element of Truth, deal with that dilemma while it affects those she cares about the most. The production really brought home how she felt at times.

                                                            And I'm so glad to see Flim and Flam again, this time as actual snake oil salesmen. They also played quite well the subversive part, seeming to have a point at the start but then showing what the conclusion of that line of reasoning becomes. Awesome to see them come back!

                                                            One thing though, that made me kind of flinch. I realise that it's a cartoon and screw physics, but seriously, catching Granny Smith in a lasso like that? I had to pause for a minute. NO give in that rope would have made her not lose her hind legs.

                                                            But yeah, super strong episode. I can't believe I keep saying this. Season 4 is shaping up to be the best one yet.

                                                            And from the posts here, I think the next epsiode will be interesting as well, given that I'm currently studying to become a teacher.
