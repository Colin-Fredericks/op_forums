        I like to read and write fanfiction. I even have a crossover that uses Garou. The world is more like it is in Buffy, then the pre-apocalyptic world of Werewolf: tA. Though Buffy has it fair share of small to world ending apocalypses.

        ​If you want to check it out, it's on AO3, under the username TwistedImage and it is ch. 4 of a one-shot collection named Throw a Penny in the Wishing Well. I only have the beginning up and it was the very first draft of the story, so a good bit has been changed since then. Mostly to do with the Umbra and interaction with Elder Garou.

        ​I actually have some questions that maybe someone more familiar with the game could answer. I've tried to read as much as I can online but I didn't buy the books, since I was only doing research for the crossover. Which has spiraled out of control.

        ​1-Do Garou look different in the Penumbra/Umbra? I read somewhere that older souls (ones that have been reborn many times) that have done great or terrible deeds can have a reflection of that in the Umbra. Though I can't find where I read that and it may have been a person's own design, instead of canon to the games.

        ​2-What would the Sunnydale Hellmouth be? So far, I've just kept it as a portal into a hell dimension but the hell mouth monsters that come out of it will be 'wyrm' creatures, especially that multi-headed one that happens during mid-season 3 of Buffy. I'm trying to mix the lore a little from each.

        ​3-Some ideas on how to work banes, formori, and even BSDs, along with demons and Sunnydale vampires would be great.

        ​I might have a few more but those are the top three.
