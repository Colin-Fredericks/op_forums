            I forgot to introduce myself. I'm a 24 year Italian old boy. I've always had the impression women weren't THAT interesting to me, though I did prefer girl's company because they were less of an asshole, compared to male schoolmates, at least up to junior high school. Plus, horrible dreams about the cross, being crucified and Jesus going after me to punish me for my sins, tormented me up until about when I turned 21 yo.

            My family is loving and while religious, it's quite tolerant and permissive. It's not like I asked much anyways. I've also always been both introverted and attention whore at the same time. I feel bad during parties when people do not pay attention to me, any kind of attention, positive or negative.

            I realized my sexual orientation while fantasizing about a whipping session between the protagonist from Gothic 1 (the sub) and a female NPC of the videogame (the mistress). I understood I was keeping attention only to the male character. It was about the same period when I realized I was an atheist.
            So, after being bullied for being weak during the three years of junior high school, I spent my first years of high school desperate for my future. I was a closeted gay atheist. I didn't fear my family THAT much.

            What I did fear was the possibility I'd die alone. I viewed my existence as meaningless. Humans are born to procreate, but I'm not willing to go with a woman, so what's my purpose?, I thought. Plus, being introverted and having no contact with gay people was discouraging, and led me to think I would never find anyone who loves me.
            I was continually thinking about death. My reasoning was that if I had no purpose, there was no use in doing anything. Plus, while an atheist, I still feared religion, and the dreams where still there.

            About 2007 I had my first short relationship with a dickish bisexual older boy. About 2008 I came out to my parents, because I was having really bad marks at school, and my mind was focused on a school crush I had. And because I wanted them to care about me instead of attacking me. And because I'm an attention whore.
            My parents didn't react particularily bad, so that was useless. I also came out to my school crush, in vain. And that ended my darkest period (which compared to other people might actually seem not that dark).
            About 2009 I started to connect with gay people more thanks to my best friend coming out to me and introducing me to his friends. About 2011 I got rid of religion, my scary dreams.

            From then on, I live my life telling myself that "life's meaning is what you make out of it". I stopped whining and started living. Now I'm dating a man which is about 40 yo, and I was questioning myself if dating someone who's that much older is good for me. But...I don't really care that much anymore. Life is short, and I should live it how I want it.

            As long as it works, I'll be fine.

                                Hi everyone, just introducing myself. I'm pansexual and gender-fluid with generally more feminine leanings, but unfortunately have to present entirely male most of the time. I'm an actor professionally, and as a business where appearance is everything, I can get turned down for an acting job for reasons that would absolutely never fly in any other business. So even though I would ideally like to take estrogen and present generally more feminine most of the time, it's just not a realistic option for me. In part because I make a reasonably attractive man, and despite my partner's assurances I don't feel at all confident about my appearance when I present how I would like to, but also because I'm already established as a male actor and transitioning would dramatically change the kind of roles I would be considered for. I also have a really deep voice, which would be a big obstacle to getting female roles if I did transition. And on top of all that, I've basically spent my entire life learning to pretend to be someone I'm not and be damn convincing at it, so I have an incredibly easy time living in the closet in my professional life. It's a little disheartening to not be able to be myself at work, but at the same time, being someone else is pretty much my job description, and it's something I came to accept a long time ago. In my personal life though, I am pretty open about my gender. I don't think my family really "gets it", even though I've come out to them. They're very open-minded and accepting, and were totally fine with my identity and sexuality, but since I generally dress and act in a typically male manner, I'm not sure they entirely understand. Luckily my partner is incredibly supportive, and makes me happy to be who I am, which helps me not get beaten down by the necessity to constantly hide my identity. It also helps to have places like this I can come to and not be judged by my appearance. So thanks to everyone here for that.

                                Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.
