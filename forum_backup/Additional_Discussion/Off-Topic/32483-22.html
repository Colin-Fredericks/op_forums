            Tonight has been a fucking Sadness Extravaganza. I talk to a VERY good friend and make myself sad by being honest about myself with them and not realizing the gravity of what I was saying and what it said about my life. We smoked our first cigarettes together. We shot my first pellet gun together. I even took him on vacation with me and my family to Mexico, and he met my paternal grandfather, my aunts and uncles, my cousins (most of my dad's side of the family is still in Mexico) and now deceased grandmother, none of whom I've been able to visit since.

            He was family.

            I had to break it to him that I didn't get better. I only kept getting getting worse, and that now I'm disabled. I had to tell the guy I considered my brother, a guy I was planning to join the military with, and we were SERIOUS about it to the point that I tried getting into military school (He got in. I...just....), that I can't even function. I feel so helpless and pathetic and I'm too afraid to even look at his reply.

            I feel like a failure at life. I hope it's not all my fault.

                                I am very depressed and have been very depressed for the past week or two, I can't focus on things I like and I am constantly haunted by my childhood and my teenage years, both mistakes I have made and mistakes that were beyond my control.

                                All I can think about is bad things from my past, and I tried to distract myself by coming up with an idea for a World of Darkness game that was anime-influenced and not traditional Gothic-Punk personal horror (as I like anime and generally prefer the more action-oriented "Vampions" style of gameplay, though personal horror is good for me in small doses and I can respect the tastes of others so long as they respect mine). Unfortunately, I proposed this on another forum and got bashed. Rather than do the mature thing and leave it alone, I got pissed and kept arguing back and forth for a few days, even lapsing into old habits from my days on the White Wolf forums (attacking the Goth subculture and the like).

                                Combine this with my recent depression and constant haunting memories of my childhood and teenage years, and I in a fit of anger came up with a grimdark revenge-filled Werewolf chronicle (one that I wasn't even going to actually run) where the players would destroy my childhood past and erase all that bad mojo. It was supposed to be an exercise of catharsis but ended up making me even more angry and sad. So naturally, we argue back and forth on this board for a while and I bring up my Appalachian upbringing and bad memories related to it (Even though there were good memories too, I was just so focused on the negative that I left that behind and grew more upset) and it degenerates to more bickering and insults on both ends.

                                After one shot of vodka and a bottle of Mountain Dew later (first time I have consumed alcohol in weeks, and don't worry, I drink responsibly and in small amounts), I am now realizing what I have done. I don't feel angry anymore, just really depressed and I kind of hate myself for letting myself relapse into old emotional habits, I feel really depressed, sad, and guilty over what I have done and I don't know what to do.

                                I know most of you guys here on Onyx Path don't like me, but I wanted to say that I am sorry for all this chaos, whether it be on the old White Wolf Forums, on RPG.net's forums, or on /tg/. I am sorry for my ranting and raving on how I was wronged by goths in an old LARP group from years ago and how that festered into this mindset I had for a long time, one I thought I had gotten out of, but relapsed into recently.

                                No matter what your tastes are, whether you be a goth or a metalhead, a punk or an anime otaku, or whatever, I am fine with what you like and whatever subculture you choose to identify with (if any), or however you play your games, WoD or otherwise. I just feel so bad about my past and haunted by all the things that I have done and I feel so fucking guilty.
