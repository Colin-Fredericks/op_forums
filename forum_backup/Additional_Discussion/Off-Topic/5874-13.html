                Newish to me shows:
                Steven Universe: A fantastic animated series with short but deep episodes and a slowly growing mythology. Highly recommended, though the main character is a tad annoying in the first couple episodes.
                Another Period: A guilty pleasure. Everything I liked about Downton Abbey without any pretense of seriousness.
                Sex, Drugs, and Rock & Roll: Denis Leary as a trashed out rock star. Need I say more?
                Mr. Robot: A cerebral take on the cyberpunk genre. Not sure if the main character is supposed to be autistic, brain damaged, or suffering from deep trauma. Possibly all three.
                Sense8: Transhumanism from JMS and the Wachowskis. Need I say more?
