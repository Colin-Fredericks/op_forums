            One thing about themes:

            Themes are inherent to any creative process because, whether we try to put them in or not, we think certain ways about certain things and even internally reward or punish things, and so whenever we deal with subject matter, our individual takes will manifest in a variety of ways, even if it's simply giving a topic more time and others no time at all. Our unconscious interests bleed out into our every presentation, even down to how we walk.

            So you may not think you have themes, but there's is content to read, and that content carries ideas, and thus themes. The big difference of focusing on a theme is that you hold more control over the presentation that simply letting your biases show.

            And boy howdy, there's little more embarrassing than have your nasty little asides slip out in unexpected ways when you're so sure they'd never show.

            ANYWAYS, moral of the story is you can either control your meanings or have your meanings confine you.

At absolutely no point am I trying to push forward some kind of political agenda, or make some kind of artistic statement about our world.
