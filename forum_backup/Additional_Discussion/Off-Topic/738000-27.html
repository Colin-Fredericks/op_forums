    Originally posted by Uxas View Post
    Just the opposite: It will divide even more the community. There are three ways of doing that:
    a) Cancelling one of the brands
    b) Mixing both games in a single world
    c) Creating a new world (with or without some elements of the other two)

    In the first case, they'll alienate playera of the cancelled world. It remembers me a Stalin meme that said: "I'll make everybody happy by killing anybody who's not happy."

    The other two options will create a third fanbase community: cWoD fans, nWoD fans and uWoD fans (unified WoD).

    I don't say that they are going to do any of that. Just that the idea of a unified worlds, if they want to unite fan communities is the worst one.

    You could theoretically have a Unitied Multiverse in the same manner as the Dungeons and Dragons settings all being part of the same overarching multiverse, but that's quite difficult to do in C/N WoD's case because of the different game mechanics making crossover on the mechanical level hard to do without translation books. In theory, this one could be solved, but it'd require axing one side of the equation's dice mechanics, and some of us have our preferences.

    Secondly, in order to do the multiverse thing at all; both worlds would be completely seperate for the most part, except for in unusual, interesting crossovers.

    Say, an adventure path that requires Mages from the Free Council meeting members of the Traditions through magic mirrors and the sorts of political drama that results from that. Or Cain finds himself stranded in NWoD, and because God in NWoD is not the same one in OWoD, he's mortal.



    The Multiverse thing would atleast be more acceptable to the overall community rather than a single unified world. In theory, it's already been that way... just one shard out of many after all.

        I'm hoping the new owners stay the course as is.

        Classic WoD is for the large number of folks that go in for the overarching, thicker than pea soup metaplot. As a gamemaster/storyteller it was always one of the first things I jettisoned. Keep some of its trappings sure. But Caine literally being the ancestor of all Vampires? Nope.

        I remember not being convinced about New WoD when it first came out (partially because I'm not a fan of buying the same game over and over again). But once I finally settled in and read through the game lines I was very pleased with the more open world sandbox that they provided. I was free to use one of the multiple myths they provided or make up one of my own. Very liberating because I like to tell my own stories.

        Even the release of 2nd edition has been relatively well timed as (IMO) good games only need new editions once every 10~20 years.

        Also, in the 25 years I've been collecting, reading, running, and playing role-playing games, I have never seen a game so well written, thorough, and completely in-tune with its themes as Werewolf: the Forsaken 2nd edition. This is the best of the best. Since Requiem 2nd ed's quality is not very far behind and The Descent is really pretty good (though it's trying something new and so didn't have the same advantage of years of figuring out what works and what doesn't). So I really hope the new owners take these matters into account.

        I won't be saddened though, if the new owners exercise some firmer editorial oversight. Looking at Beast, the developers and writers could really have used some push back on certain things that eat up valuable word count without contributing anything to either Beast as a setting or Beast as a game (I'm looking at you copy-pasted ephemeral being rules). Even though I have my Kickstarter Backers copy, I wouldn't mind at all if OPP delayed Beast's release another quarter to replace this copy-pasted section with a description of ephemeral beings that are more setting appropriate (like the other things prowling the Primordial Dream).

        I might also observe that a game intended to facilitate cross-overs should have been given a larger page-count (something on par with M20) but at this point the game is more or less what it is. (Expect a crap-ton of hacks over the Winter months.)
