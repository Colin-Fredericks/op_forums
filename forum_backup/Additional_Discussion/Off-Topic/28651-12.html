            I have a strange recurring nightmare. Just before waking I hear,

            "You are dead."

            It's actually kind of terrifying. It's a disembodied voice, but one that doesn't feel like my own. There is no other context for the voice. It's like a voice from the other side has just informed me of my sudden demise.

            Funny thing is, I consider myself an atheist, so I don't expect there will be a voice when it happens. But even still, even after having this dream a hundred times, I still believe the voice, and I still sit straight up in bed with my heart going a mile a minute, and it still takes me a moment to figure out that no, I am not dead, this is not some bizarre afterlife, I just had that weird dream again.

                                            So the dream I had on Friday night was a weird one.

                                            In my dream I was working as the manager of a department store or supermarket and my employees were really weird. I forget the specifics but the weirdest was an old lady who’d just walk up to people and ask if they’d considered suicide like it was a lifestyle choice or something, I think that she had pamphlets about it.
                                            Anyway people were excited because for some reason David Tennant was there and we were excited about that until he tried a free sample of food and died.

                                            At which point someone from regional management became involved and they were incredibly callous about the whole ordeal - someone was in a panic saying "oh my god we killed Doctor Who" and he calmly corrected her that it wasn’t the current Doctor Who. As well as saying that they couldn’t resign over this because that would be admitting full responsibility for Tennant’s death and that if anyone did they’d be charged with his murder. So at that point we turned on each other, particularly the creepy old lady who we thought might have “convinced him to let go”.

                                            Once the infighting was over, I start talking to someone who is obviously the most normal person there , she’s as horrified by all these goings on as I am and generally a reminder that I’m not going insane. So when we finally get off work my brother shows up to give us both lift home (for some reason he had a mullet) in this tiny little electric car.
                                            He drops her off; he drops me off and just as I think I’ve reached the pinnacle of crazy I come home to be greeted by ten robots who tell me that the woman I was with was how their mom sees herself in her dreams
                                            I guess I was supposed to be their mother which makes the whole thing very meta.

                                            success is the ability to go from one failure to the next without any loss of enthusiasm
