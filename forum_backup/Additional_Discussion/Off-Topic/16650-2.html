                                                        Oh I don't know about 'British humor'. Certainly there are references sprinkled here and there that people in other parts of the world won't get but they aren't all that common or even all that noticeable. Besides the humor is just one small part of what makes Doctor Who awesome.

                                                        Eccleston was the Ninth Doctor, the first of Doctors following the restart of the series.

                                                        Best advice I can give you is to pick a Doctor (or several) and start watching their episodes. Each incarnation of the Doctor, each actor who has played the Doctor has brought something to the role to make their version memorable. Of course by the same token not everyone likes all the incarnations of the Doctor. Personally I love them all though if I had to list my top five, in no particular order, they would be the First Doctor, the Third, the Fourth, the Seventh and the Eleventh.

                                                        Not to long ago a couple of my friends came to me asking about what episodes of the old series are worth watching so I've been slowly putting together a list of episodes for them. At the moment I only have the first three Doctors as the Fourth was the longest running Doctor so there's a lot of episodes to sift through.

                                                        Though you could look for The Doctor Revisited, a series created as part of the lead up to the recent 50th Anniversary. Each episode starts off talking about that particular incarnation of the Doctor, the companions he traveled with, the famous foes he fought and some of the memorable moments which is then followed up by one of the better episodes of that Doctor. Failing that, find and watch the episode shown with that Doctor in the series. I know for a fact you can find the episodes of the old series (Doctors 1-7 and movie of 8) on the website DailyMotion.
