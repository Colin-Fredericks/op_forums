                                        Welp, I win the award for coming in at the wrong time to complain about trivial things.

                                        So, first off, let's have an honest perspective thing. On the one hand, yes, some problems are objectively larger than others. Yes, it's important to understand where one's issues are in relation to others.

                                        But let's also be honest: if we went by that, none of us could complain until we achieved paradise, and that's probably a lie.

                                        Here's the flipside to the objective relation thing: these problems are yours. Yes, other people have issues that might be bigger than yours, but that doesn't matter because your problems hurt you. It pays to have empathy and humility towards others, but it also pays to recognize when something is heavy, or hard, or hurtful to you. It might be trivial, but your happiness depends on you dealing it. Your burdens matter because, to you, they are as large as other issues, and far more immediate. That must be respected as well, if we are to be happy not only as individuals, but as a community.

                                        We are all one family, and we must learn to treat all problems with the care and understanding it deserves. That includes our own.
