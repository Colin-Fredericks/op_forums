                                    Originally posted by atamajakki View Post

                                    I’m genuinely happy to hear you’ve come around on it.

                                    Yeah, the pool of people interested in First Edition has pretty much gone dry and since V5 isn't looking good to me at all, I went "Fuck it!" and decided to give 2E a chance. I even posted a thread in the Requiem sub-forum asking questions about it if you want to check it out.

                                    Turns out a lot of my mechanical grievances were simply me overreacting to change back in 2014-2015 and given my preferred playstyle, I am inclined to like 2E more, which is why I am buying it when I get paid next month.

                                    As for the setting elements I don't like (Strix, God-Machine, Clan Akhud, no Belial's Brood or Fog of Ages), those can easily be hand-waved out since there's no actual metaplot and everything is explicitly stated to be optional.

                                    I'm actually planning a Chronicles of Darkness 2E play-by-post chronicle that can support a wide variety of themes and stories (it's a sandbox game) and I may need help from other forum users integrating the other splats I'm less familiar with, like Mages or Werewolves. (I'm mostly familiar with Vampire, Changeling, and Hunter)

                                    I still don't want to include the Strix, the God-Machine/Demon: The Descent, or Beast: The Primordial but that's just due to personal preference. Any of the other CofD gamelines I am open to.

                                        There's a comic convention in Milan at the beginning of March. Not the biggest one around here, but still big enough to be important and manage to have cool stuff and important guests. Last year I got Kieron Gillen to sign my copy of The Wicked + The Divine, we chatted a bit (even about his work for Scion!) and I found some books and comics I was looking for.

                                        Point being, I do like it a lot. Possibly even more than our major one, Lucca, which I found to be...too much. I was making plans to get there and trying to save enough money, when the people from my local comic store called me asking if I was available to help them at their stand there. They noticed stores here and there keep calling me for the holiday season or for the sales, so I do have some hard-earned experience (if you survive several Christmas at Gamestop, you're the equivalent of a war veteran). While I have college duties, I don't have a job right now and thus accepted.

                                        Which means I'm getting there, trip, entrance and other expenses covered by them and also get paid for it. It won't be much and I'm aware it's not the same as going just for fun, but hey, still a good thing I'd say
