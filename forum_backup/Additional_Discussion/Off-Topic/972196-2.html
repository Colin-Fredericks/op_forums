                                        Different pipeline, though they do originate in the same place. Both pipelines effectively "start" in Waha, Texas (in fact there's a pipeline going from Houston to Waha). Then there's two splits, with one pipeline going to San Elizaro (and from there into Mexico) - this is Comanche Trail Pipeline and the one we're protesting here in El Paso.

                                        The other split goes south through Big Bend National Park and Balmorhea State Park - that pipeline is the Transpecos Pipeline.







                                        Here's another picture of Mexico to get a similar idea - 3 is the Comanche Trail Pipeline, 4 is the Transpecos Pipeline.
