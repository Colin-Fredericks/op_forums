                                                            I am reposting this from my Facebook:

                                                            The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug is very good. Excluding a few technical issues (that I won't go into to avoid spoilers), the movie was exciting and entertaining. If you enjoyed the first one, you should enjoy this one as well and probably a little bit more.

                                                            That said, as a life long fan of the source material, I do not like a lot of the changes Peter Jackson made to this portion of the story. While the first movie featured some small changes to Tolkien's tale, I felt Jackson was still working within the realm of what I would consider Tolkien's work. This film features major changes to the narrative, as well as the creation of completely new characters and story lines. At this point, Peter Jackson's The Hobbit is only loosely based on the narrative penned by Tolkien. While the movie buff in me enjoyed the movie, the literature buff in me did not.

                                                            Anyone who knows me, knows I am an advocate for, "The movie is not the book, the book is not the movie." That said, I am still disappointed because The Hobbit had everything it needed to be a great movie. Jackson's influence does not make the story any better, just different.

                                                            All that aside, Smaug is... absolutely amazing. Jackson did an absolutely brilliant job in capturing everything that is great and terrifying about the dragon. Jackson's Smaug is the new high bar when it comes to this mythical creature in film.
