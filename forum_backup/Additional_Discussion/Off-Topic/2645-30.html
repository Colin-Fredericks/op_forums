                                                        Before I went to see the movie, I wrote about how I hoped there was a lot of truly new concepts, as opposed to rehashed stuff. Well, there ARE a lot of recycled ideas: The Republic is back (apparently not called the New Republic, as in the novels), the First Order is basically the Empire (or Imperial Remnant, again from the books), the Resistance is the Rebellion, etc. There are new aliens and droids, but none (save for BB8) that are named or have any impact on the story, they're all background flavor. The ships are streamlined, but otherwise still X-Wings, Star Destroyers and TIE Fighters...Also, no new locations or cities that didn't look like anything we'd seen before.

                                                        Honestly, the "shock-baton" that lets ordinary people fight lightsabers was the only truly *new* thing in the film.

                                                        So, yeah, that's a mark against it, but it still does *very* well with what it has, even if it isn't blazingly original. Hopefully the next movies will take us some places and show us some things that we haven't seen before!
