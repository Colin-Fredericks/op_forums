                I thought apart of the curse was you could never walk in the day light anymore.
                For those that aren't aware, I feel it is worth pointing out that the vampires in The Vampire Diaries burst into flames and burn away to ash in sunlight - but witches of that universe are capable of enchanting a bit of jewelry, usually a ring, that allows the one vampire it was made for walk unharmed in sunlight while wearing it.

                So that particular universe actually has, in my opinion, a "best of both worlds" approach because it lets the vampires that need it for narrative reasons (such as successfully pulling off pretending not to be a vampire) walk around in daylight, but also provides the opportunity for a vampire to be vulnerable by being "trapped" somewhere unable to escape until nightfall.
