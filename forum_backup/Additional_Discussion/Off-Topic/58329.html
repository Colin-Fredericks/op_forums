                      I have not had an epidural. My sister has had three, all for the same back problem. She said it's normal to have to take multiple shots, because it's difficult to hit the right place on the first try.

                      She said almost everyone feels better the first day, then significantly worse for the second and third because the epidural fluid is competing with spinal fluid for space and there's a lot of pressure. If you aren't feeling better than you originally were on day four, then the epidural probably hit the wrong place and you need to go back for another. She added that even if an epidural works, physical therapy will be a key component of actually healing your back.

                      She warned that for the first few days, you will want to be laying down as much as possible, because spinal fluid will leak out and that's a problem no one needs. She said that sometimes relief can last for a while, for a few weeks, but then fade away, which means the epidural wasn't in the right place and you'll need another shot.

                      That's all the testimony I got due to a screaming (happily screaming) baby. I hope it helps.
