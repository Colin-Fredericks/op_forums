                        Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                        Anyone know a way to git gud at this game that isn't boring and I can do while doing chores and stuff? As those who say my post in the You Know What I Hate Thread know, I suck at this game despite playing for years and it's less effort to just get good enough to win ocassionally without a mana-screwed opponent than to sell off all my cards and find a new way to spend my weekends.
                        I’m pretty decent at the game (I’m no pro or anything, but I can hold my own at FNM.) I could probably give you some pointers, but it would help to know more specifically what you struggle with.

                        One of the most common problems people who struggle with the game have is that they overvalue certain things (e.g. protecting their life total at all costs instead of treating it as a resource) and undervalue others (e.g. not understanding the importance of tempo and/or card advantage), which causes them to make poor deckbuilding decisions and evaluate board states poorly.

                        The best way to improve, in my experience, is to play with more skilled opponents (or watch them play, if you don’t have the ability to play against them yourself) and pay close attention to the way they play, especially when they make decisions that seem strange to you. Look at top-tier deck lists, proxy them, and play with them to figure out what makes them tick.
