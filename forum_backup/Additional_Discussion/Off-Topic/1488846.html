    Inheritance Untouched

    08-02-2022, 01:26 PM
    In his obituary, my American paternal grandfather was credited with some rank in the Freemasons. His involvement in the order had mostly concluded by the time we started taking trips down to see my grandparents, when my sister and I were very young. He had retired early. Over the years, we lost contact following my parents' divorce. I was informed of a fund in my name that my grandfather had created. I left it untouched. Dividends are automatically reinvested until such time that I claim it.

    Recently, I came across an interview with Barret Brown on the Konkrete Podcast. This link is to a point in the interview just before mention of the Bhopal Chemical Disaster in India. As it was told to me, there was a point when a lawsuit was being ruled on. I don't claim this to be precisely accurate, only what I was told. The story went that my grandfather, a stock trader, attended a meeting with his fraternal brothers. He left that meeting believing that he knew how the judge in the Bhopal case was going to rule - against the families of dead and injured workers. He sold his car. He sold every stick of furniture in the house and took out a second mortgage. He bought preferred shares in Dow Chemical whose share value was negatively affected in the short term by the pending lawsuit. He retired comfortably.

    Should I access the funds?

    Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.
