                  NicoB. I don't know if he's the funniest or the best LetsPlayer on YouTube. He most likely is not. I also cringe at a minority of the examples of his (likely pretty mainstream) choices of joke content, word choice and views of the world. And god knows there's the bias that he was my introduction to LetsPlays sometime in the impressionable period of my late teens and still my favorite.

                  But I love him and the community he has. He's a funny, creative, and kind guy who's good at setting boundaries. And he and his mods have expertly pruned together a YouTube comment section that is genuinely not a shitty hellscape, the utter miracle workers.

                  He genuinely loves what he's doing and does lots of improv, focusing on using the impressive amateur voice-acting range he has built up over the past decade and his impressive character consistency between ad-lib jokes. How many other let's players have made the funny voices they use into such recognizable archetypes in their fanbase that they got their own jankily-animated, but superbly-written dramedy spinoff series?

                  A god is just a monster you kneel to. - ArcaneArts, Quoting "Fall of Gods"
