                                                I really loved it. Not as good as Guardians of the Galaxy or The Winter Soldier, but I do feel it was better than The Avengers. Fortunately for me, every time a Marvel movie comes out, I have different groups of friends who each invite me out to see it. On opening weekends, I get a lot of repeat viewings in, so I'll be going for a third one today.

                                                Also, I do think it's worth noting that Marvel announced that the Blu-ray will include the extended cut of the film, which is around three hours long. It's pretty much the Director's Cut, the full vision of what Joss Whedon had in mind for the movie before it was chopped down to increase the number of theater screenings. When all is said and done, much like the LOTR films, I think Age of Ultron will be judged more fairly by its Extended Edition than by its theatrical one.
