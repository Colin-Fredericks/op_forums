                I'm not really a frequent poster so i feel kinda weird posting here but

                I've always known that I have very serious ADD, and it's been something I've dealt with my whole life. College has been an uphill battle for that reason, but one that for the most part has felt worth fighting. However, since summer of freshman year of college, I've known that I've inherited my mom's clinical depression. It's an issue that comes and goes but it's been a pretty steady drain on my health and grads over my last few years of school. In the past I've always been able to overcome my learning and focus issues because I've always had determination and enthusiasm, but depression pretty much sucks all the drive out of a person. Combined with ADD it's a pretty deadly combo.

                I honestly thought I was on the up-and-up this year. First quarter I powered through all of my classes just fine, and I'm living with dear and supportive friends who have really made life feel good again.

                This quarter, though, things haven't just relapsed, they've dipped down to a point I've never before encountered, as of just last night. The quarter went from normal and manageable to a nightmare in no time flat, and now as of just these two weeks I'm dealing with possibly failing out of my beloved major due to past mistakes when I was a freshman idiot, a missing wallet, a job that no longer supports me, looming deadlines, and minor sad social life events (a three year long campaign is just falling apart all of a sudden and i'm disproportionately sad) and honestly while I know in the logical part of me that all those things have hope of being resolved they've pretty much overloaded my system. I feel constantly dazed, I have trouble remembering things, i don't have the energy or number of fucks needed to feed or care for myself properly. I haven't finished a book for my own pleasure or spent any quality time outside in months if not almost a year, let alone do anything else i used to love doing other than pointlessly surfing the net to distract myself. I'm pretty sure I'm starving myself by forgetfulness and neglect but between appetite suppressant meds and depression it's hard to tell. All I know is today i took a shower to try and wake myself up and start my day moving and halfway through i just lost any will or desire to continue doing ANYTHING, let alone keep scrubbing. I pretty much just got out and lay on the floor until i was dry. I'm pretty sure i'm caught in a vicious cycle of depression, hunger, and physical fatigue but the scary thing is i don't think i have the energy needed to break out of it anymore. Or the hope.

                Idk I'm sorry, I don't really know where I'm going with this. I don't want to hurt myself or anything i just AM hurting myself by neglect and i don't know how to stop

                I want to live fully again and I want to think clearly again. The hardest part is remembering how bright and vibrant things felt just a few years ago.

                                                Well, my attempts to join the ranks of the medicated has turned out in a not wholly unexpected way.

                                                My psychiatrist insisted, despite my attempts to explain that it's a greater issue, upon starting me off on an anti-depressant. Now, at least she was smart enough to acknowledge that she was only 80% certain that was my problem and warned me what to watch out for if it was a greater issue. After three doses over three days, nearly those exact reactions she told me to be wary of occurred. I went from being kind of down to slightly relaxed to back where I was to a seething mountain of rage ready to unleash it upon the world in a glorious frenzy of destruction, blood and death.

                                                See, I wasn't quite able to explain to her properly that while I am prone to mild manias in which I have trouble sleeping and my mind races, it's not those states that I get the most nor are they the true danger of my existence. My "mania" expresses itself as RAGE, thus my choice of constant maniacal activity seems restricted to a small area of interest which usually involves WANTING TO HIT EVERYTHING AROUND ME!

                                                After leaving a few messages with her nurses, I was finally able to get the doctor's response, which was to wait for my next appointment, which is 3 weeks from now, and that I will be receiving no further medications until then.

                                                Since I work in behavioral health at my current job, I did not argue or fight or scream. I quietly, but very reluctantly, accepted that answer. I know what the problems are trying to get to see a psychiatrist these days are - too many people in need and not enough people willing to take the time, money and effort required to become a psychiatrist. It's insane amount of education and training required and they'll probably be facing a mid-life crisis not long after they finally get to begin their practice.

                                                So, now I wait and I do my best to maintain without. Which I used to be quite good at and now I can't seem to get a handle on things. Thus is the life of a man who's brain is working against him.
