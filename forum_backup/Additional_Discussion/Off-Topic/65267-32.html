                                                Mark Millar decided he could write for movies so all his TPB-aimed books basically test the waters for possible film projects.

                                                I was pleasantly surprised to find that Image's Beauty has been continued. It was part of their Pilot Season thing where readers get to vote on a bunch of #1 issues to see which one gets continued. I don't follow Image that closely but I've always been interested in the pilot season winners, because they never seem to get their #2 issues, or if they do, it's years afterward. Beauty won the contest in 2011, for instance. I've never bothered to look into the whys and wherefores, I just find it interesting.

                                                A lot of the Pilot Season comics seem pretty good. Genius depicted a sort of tactical genius growing up in a bad neighborhood in LA and with her people sort of declaring war on the police. It won in 2008 and got it's second issue in the past year when the protests rose up in Baltimore. Beauty was about a sexually transmitted disease that, surprise, makes you gorgeous. The story picks up a while afterwards when sufferers start dying and the police investigating the matter find out there might be a conspiracy behind the disease, just in time for the male detective to find his wife has been cheating on him by waking up and seeing himself turned good-looking in the mirror. Twilight Guardian, I think, was probably the most noteworthy one, with a troubled girl who patrols the streets of her suburb at night as a would-be crime-stopper. That one got the rest of its series a while ago, I think.
