                          Honestly, the Malkavian confusion is exactly why I think they should be a first play through clan, rather than a subsequent. The entire gimmick of the Malkavian is NOT knowing the difference between what is real and imaginary. If you play through the game first as a 'sane' vampire, it diminishes the Malkavian experience, because you can then recognize when your character is hallucinating.

                          The devs have said they want to make the Malkavians more nuanced this time around, and present a more realistic take on depicting mental illness, not as something wacky or comical. And given the brief clip we see of the player battling the Malkavian girl and seeing her create a bunch of clones, I'm hoping that this means we'll get some kind of F.E.A.R type of experience, where our Malkavian suffers full blown hallucinations at various parts of the game.
