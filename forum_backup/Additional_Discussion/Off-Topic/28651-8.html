I kept walking approching the small creekish place near my apartments, except in this dream it was a river maybe 10 feet wide and was quite shallow looking, with a steep embankment and deep black water.

                                        Up untill this point i could see just fine the light of hte streetlights had aided in that but as i found myself almost unwillingly walking towards the edge of the embankment away from the safety of the lights it grew dark ,i could hardly see. I forwhatever reason felt compelled to look up, across to the other shore, standing in the shadows but still clearly seen,I see a figure cloaked in black shirtless in this cold night, clawed feet digging into the salt, with white skin, his dark feathered black wings folded against his back. I know him..I Know him well. My guide. my friend...I havent seen him in my dreams in so long. I wanted to go to him, i wanted, no needed to talk to him needed his guidance once again.                                         I hit the stark black water excepting to end up on my ass in the water. Instead for a moment my head slipped under the water. i flailed to break the surface so I could breath, desperatly i tried to find my footing but no mater what i did my feet would not touch the ground the water was so deep. I felt like I couldnt move, I couldnt get to the shore again even though it was close on either side. It took all I had to stay above.
