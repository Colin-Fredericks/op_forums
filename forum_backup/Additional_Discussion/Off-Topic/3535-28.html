                                                            Yep, still in love with Bloodborne.

                                                            The game mechanics per se do not differ much from the Souls Series that From has previously brought, except that this time, you have to be much more aggressive since there are no shields to hide behind. (Actually, there is one, but it is so fragile).
                                                            The weapons all have at least two modes of play, usually one- and two-handed styles, and when using the one handed mode of a weapon, you always carry a handgun on the offhand. It doesn't do much damage, but stops even some bosses in their tracks, making it possible for you to land a stronger attack on them.
                                                            The part, however, that has really piked my interest, is, as usual for a Souls Series game, the Lore.
                                                            You are a Hunter going about a city infested with plagued, flesh-eating zombie-like citizens. What is the plague? We do not know, but it is called the Blight. Your job is to slaughter them and all you find in your way through the night of the Hunt, so that the sane and pure - words here used freely and not necessarily accurately - may be safe.
                                                            There is one point of mechanics called Insight, and you get a point or three of Insight every time every time you see and defeat a new horror (find and/or kill a boss), and every time you travel to another world, be it to help another Hunter or to slaughter them (online playing). The catch is, the higher your insight, the closer you get to seeing the true form of your enemies, since your mind can better understand their Eldritch beings. The closer you come to their true forms, the more powerful and horrendous they become!
                                                            This could easily be ignored if one of the markets in the game did not use Insight points as a currency. So you gather more and more of them in order to buy more powerful weapons, and go crazier in the process.
                                                            The idea is sweeeeeeet.
