I keep...the dreams are about me, where I'm all the people of a horrible, crime-ridden city, or maybe I should say they're all me. I don't know. I am also cards in a deck, and each card being played forces another one to be played. The cards are degradations the people-selves of the crime-ridden city have to inflict on themselves in order to survive, compromising themselves by acting as spies and accomplices just in order to stay safe. When one of the me-people of the city tries to break the cycle by getting free of the influence of the card-crimes, they are killed, buried under an avalanche of playing cards and left for the families to cripple themselves financially paying for a funeral that leaves them in a vulnerable position to the crimes in the deck of cards.

                            I have died so many times tonight. I've been someone who just wanted to help up an old woman who fell down on the street, and a virginal young activist who wanted to make life better for people whose last thought as he bled out was that his heart was breaking he never even had a chance to fall in love before he died, and dozens of others who passed so fast while I was busy being everyone in a city (and a deck of cards) that I can't remember them. It took me about an hour and a half to claw my way awake, and the clawing was literal as I kept thrashing around in bed to force myself to get up. Every time I tried to sit up, I'd fall back over on my side, so I looked like I was doing some weird bastardization of the Curly Shuffle. Kept talking to myself trying to wake myself up, and though I remember the things I said, I don't know what they meant. "Vitruvius, not Vesuvius, who's Vitruvius?" I announced to the room at one point. At another, I was trying to sing the words to the Pink Panther's theme song, which I'm pretty sure doesn't have words, but I was kind of desperate for noises to wake me up.

                            This is all because of whatever's going on in my stomach; my brain is fried by fever. Cold is crawling over my skin like fingers and my feet are so tender that walking hurts, but none of that seems important next to all the goddamned cards being played in my bed. I really wish I could throw up and have done with it.

                            Ugh, fuck it, I'm exhausted and more or less lucid. I'm taking my chances and going to bed again.

                                                        I was at a school. The elevator has three buttons, despite the school only having two floors. I was with a girl, I don't remember who. We pushed the extra button and the elevator started dropping faster and faster. Eventually it stopped and the doors opened. Standing, facing away from us, were 3 (?) men dressed in Victorian wear, top hats and such. The elevator had taken us into the past. The room was like an arcade, but they were focused on a thing that looked like an oven. They were putting a man into it. We (the girl and I) tried to escape, but they captured her. They forced her to watch as they pulled the skeleton of the man from the oven.

                                                        I rescued her and we were able to successfully board the elevator and start going up. However, one of the men was on an elevator in the same shaft above us. He went to another door, higher than the school, which was a couple of days into the future. I followed him. The school was burning and he was killing the last witnesses. He noticed me, and followed me back to the present. He didn't capture me, but I had to leave my shoes behind. He wore them as he started the flames and framed me.

                                                        Later, seemingly disconnected. I was at a friend's house. He knew about the situation and he was resigned to let it happen. He had a staircase that went down 18 floors into that past room in his house. Eago, yes from Aladdin, came up the stairs. Somehow I thought we could prevent the event from happening. We grabbed Eago and I was going to do whatever it was I thought would help. But I decided to discuss it with my friend. We left Eago in someone else's care and went to discuss it. I told him my plan and he said, "We barely got you out of there. There's no guarantee you won't get trapped again." I reluctantly assented and we went to go kill Eago. But we couldn't find him. He'd put everyone to sleep and escaped. We began searching. At this point I woke.
