                    Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                    On a totally different tack. I'm really tired of reboots of good films and tv series from my childhood and getting ruined to reflect today's reality and I wish Hollywood would look at different properties to make from Manga, lesser known horror, suspense and fantasy writers for something different.
                    While the quality of reboots tends to be suspect... most of them aren't "ruining" anything by trying to appeal to a new audience the original didn't; esp. anything primarily aimed at children anyway. You can't make a kids show from the 80s popular with kids born in the 2010s without changing things. It needs to be relatable to today's reality or the intended audience isn't going to give a shit about the show's reality.

                    And I think people vastly underestimate how many things they grew up with were actually reboots/reimaginings/etc. of existing stories. Like go look at Shakespeare's plays, and compare how many of them are "original" stories, and how many are just retellings of older stuff. This is not some new, or even Hollywood, phenomenon.

                    Also, yeah, the Netflix "original Anime" thing has been blegh enough. The world has a whole corporate industry pumping out TV shows and movies based on manga... it's just in Japan and for some reason we get very little of it released in cinemas outside of Japan. Even though the Demon Slayer movie was the highest grossing movie (period, all movies) of 2020, it's still an inane struggle to see Japanese created movies based on Japanese IPs get wide releases outside of anime specific streaming services. We don't need Hollywood to adapt manga, we need the cinema industry to pick up Japanese movies internationally.
