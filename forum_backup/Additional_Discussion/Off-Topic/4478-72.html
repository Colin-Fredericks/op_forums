                            The sheer breadth of Exalted's setting is one of the reason I love it, honestly. It's just so packed with cool stuff, it's like 5 games for the price of one.

                            I tried to emulate that with my own setting, The Divine Plane; whether or not that's working is your guess. The setting is not a hack but an original one, though the Exalted influences are clear: of my splats, they basically go:

                            -Not-solars, except they intentionally align themselves with the setting's equivalent to yozis (who they're also fighting...it's complicated) for power
                            -Not-abyssals (they're twisted mockeries of the above splat) but guided by this setting's equivalent of Yozis
                            -Some screwed-up mix of DB's (weaker than everyone else but there's a shitload of them) and Sidereals (best martial artists in the setting, tasked with making sure the world doesn't fall apart) except they're more like Divine Communist Kung-Fu Police than Sids. They don't deal in fate, they just beat the hell out of you when they think you're out of line. Also, they're crazy and want to kill every other splat and live in a world made entirely from gemstone
                            -These...uh, sorta-infernals (often viscerally disgusting, hell-bent on causing chaos) but they're all lead by this one chick who's closer to a Deathlord (she got executed by the above people and then her soul tore a hole in reality due to rage). Also they're obligate cannibals, undead, and are all damned to become horrifying eldritch abominations
                            -Not-alchemicals, except anyone can build them in their backyard (still expensive though) in a week or so. They can't become cities though and they don't suffer from stuff like Clarity

                            The last few don't map to any Exalted splat.

                            Looking back on it, I kinda made it look like an Exalted rip-off, but I'm omitting a ton of important details since that would just be boring
