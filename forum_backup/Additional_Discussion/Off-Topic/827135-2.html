                            Those aren't add-ons, they're a more thorough statement of your identity. Sometimes you don't need to be thorough, sometimes you do.

                            I'm an American of European descent. More specifically I'm half German, and mostly half Ukrainian (the Ukrainian side of the family's last name seems to be a modification of a French name rather than any Ukrainian name we can figure out, so there's a bit of French in there even if it was very dilute before leaving Europe). The German and Ukrainian aspects of my ancestries are not "add-ons" to the more general idea of being of European ancestry, there's just more specific.

                            Sometimes "male" is all that counts - just like noting my European-ness is all that matters - such as using the bathroom labelled for males. If there's a gendered bathroom for males, it doesn't matter if your cis or trans, hetero, bi, or gay. You're male, the bathroom is for males.

                            Other times the rest of your identity matters in a fashion that is either implied or explicit.
