                    Much like the MG, I'm not a big politician, at least not when it comes to party politics. But I will assert his statement that this is bullshit.

                    In Sweden, we have a multi-party system. Any party with more than 4% of the people's votes gets into the government with a number of proportionally allotted mandates. Traditionally, there are two big ones who usually get the lion's share of the votes, namely Socialdemokraterna (the social democrats, left wing) and Moderaterna (the "moderates", right wing). This happened this time too; in this election, Socialdemokraterna won over the sitting right-wing government, getting 31.2% of the votes, and they will construct a government with the other left wing parties in the coming weeks. Moderaterna was the second biggest party with 23.2%.

                    What is the issue here? It's who got in third. Sverigedemokraterna (the Sweden democrats, extreme right wing), a nationalist, racist party that only got into the government for the first time in 2010 with 5.7%, today achieved 12.9% of the people's votes. Nobody in the government wants to cooperate with them, but the fact is that the Swedish people have given them sufficient power to be able to turn the outcome of votes on their own. 12.9% of the governmental power cannot go unused for 4 years. And it sits in the hands of people who are racists in all but name (and who are not even very good at hiding it), as well as sexists, homophobes and fascists.

                    This is, indeed, bullshit.

                    Personally, I'm also rather annoyed that Feministiskt Initiativ (the feminist initiative, left wing), an up and coming feminist party that has managed to turn the political discussion in Sweden these last months, only got 3.1% and thus didn't get in, but that's just me.
