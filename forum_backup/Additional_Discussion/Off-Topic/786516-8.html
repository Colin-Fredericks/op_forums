            Changing Subjects some: I have been rping for going on 20 years next year (and it all started with VtM revised) and I must ask: How do you handled transgendered characters (not players) at the table? I ask as this is a topic near and dear to my heart (I am a gay man who fights vehemently for the BTQAI part of the LGBT community against group ignorance that the mainstream LG community can and does propagate). I graduated from playing chainsaw Gangrel and beatstick Brujah with a transgendered Daughter of Cacophony who was desperately afraid the other DoCs would find out about her physical sex versus her true gender back in 2000 because a friend of mine came out as trans and I wanted to try to understand through a kind of immersion play. I asked my friend at the time all kinds of questions while she was exploring how she felt as a woman instead of the man she had been raised as. My character lasted a good year and change before my ST moved and I came to realize, through rp and my friend's struggles, how hateful the world could be to non-binary people.

            In my most current game I am running a character is being played as a thinblooded Tzimisce Koldun (house rules: Lose Animalism, Auspex, or Vicissitude for Koldunic Sorcery) who has no talent for Vicissitude but is hearing all kinds of rumors about how her clan can shape flesh and mold bodies to fit their desires and is going haywire in game trying to figure out what is "wrong" with her blood. The character is very well developed, I like the struggles of how she should have a supernatural "get out of jail free" card but does not, and the learning of that she's looked down upon by the Camarilla for her clan and lack of blood potency and not because she is transgender.

            As a gay male nerd, VtM always helped me with my self-esteem. I was odd (half-Asian male who liked men and gaming and rap and on and on) but in the WoD the LGBT community was not overshadowed, was not pushed aside, it was embraced. I remember the LGBT only packs of the Sabbat and the one in Montreal; Celeste from Nights of Prophecy (you stole my idea WW!), Troile and Moloch from Clanbook Baali (okay, let's ignore the opening fiction yeah? Yeah.)! I feel that the LGBT community and the World of Darkness are well tied together and have always been buddies while many other rpgs gloss over the ideas (D&D) or are openly hostile to them (FATAL). But, to be fair, no one in their right mind plays FATAL.

            I think I may have rambled some: Anyway, not counting people who blatantly hamfist characters (My guy is a super flamboyant drag queen lolololol!) have you had any players who wanted to explore transgender and/or transsexual issues? Have you enjoyed such characters if you did? If any didn't seem to 'get' the concept did you encourage them to learn or shift their focus to another character type?


            Debating between ghouls and hunters PbP
