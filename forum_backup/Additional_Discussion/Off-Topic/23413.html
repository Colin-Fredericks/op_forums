    Dealing with parents, even at my age...

    12-04-2013, 08:57 PM
    Today, Mom had to go to the thyroid doctor for her yearly checkup (she has a couple of nodes that bear watching). The doctor went ahead and arrange the bloodwork and the ultrasound so we can get everything done in one day. Since we had a couple of hours before the ultrasound, we decided to go find some craft stores.

    No luck.

    Instead, we found a Super Walmart with a good craft section. Picked up canvases, acrylics, pastel chalks and glitter glue so the kids can design their stockings this weekend (depending on the weather). We were leaving the parking lot and she goes south. The imaging center is north.

    When I travel, I use landmarks so I won't get lost. I told her she was going in the wrong direction but she did not listen until we went out of the city and she realize that I was right. She straiten herself out and I got her in the right direction, finally arriving at the center with time to spare.

    I feel I want to mention to the doctor that she is getting a little "strange". Maybe it is old age, maybe the start of dementia. I have started the routine of making sure she takes her pills, checking her bank account when she is online, controlling the phone for scammers. She is suppose to be my guardian but she is my payee for my disability. Our roles are becoming reversed. My bipolar is pretty leveled out (I still have racing thoughts and sometimes hear voices). I inventory our foodstuffs and make a list, praying we get everything in one trip (I use my SNAP for bread and milk). I get the discounted meats so we can have stews in the slowcooker (I make a mean beef tips).

    I plan to talk to my brother about this. I cannot afford to be taking care of someone who is possibly losing her mind who is suppose to be taking care of me. Next month, I have my Runescape bill due and I play it as part of my therapy when I am not crocheting.

    Bipolar vampire...can't decide to feast for fun or for rage...
