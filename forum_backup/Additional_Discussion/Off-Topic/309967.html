    The thread of the Left

    11-12-2014, 04:16 AM
    Are there any members of the OP fora that adhere to some ideology of the Left? Let us get to know one another by introducing ourselves and discussing some burning political issues (in comradely civility ).

    I am currently a student doing an MA in Politics (4 years at a Scottish university) with a focus on Marxism and the incompatibility between neoliberalism and democracy (not just in the liberal sense of representation but also the Classical Greek ideal), as well as wealth accumulation and its effects on society and the economy. Initially I set out to study Management and International Management at a prestigious and expensive US institution, but dropped out after attending a seminar presented by a member of upper-management for Vodafone Greece. We were told that it is our duty to become ruthless profit-makers and, on the issue of climate change and pollution, advised us to "blow up the planet if that will turn us a profit and we can get away with it".

    Politically, I consider myself Marxist (not necessarily a Leninist, although I agree with some of his views, and definitely not a Stalinist), influenced by Aristotelian and direct democratic ideals. Here's my political compass map from this website if you fancy doing it (mostly for laughs).
