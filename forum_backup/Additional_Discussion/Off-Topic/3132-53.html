    I hate the word "white-knighting". Never has there been a more rage-inducing verb. And the noun form "white knight" goes with that too.

    It means nothing, it can't be replied to because it means nothing, and still people use it like it actually meant something! I've seen the same term used to describe someone agreeing with a woman (obviously, he's just white knighting to get into her pants), defending a black person (obviously, he's just trying to relieve his white guilt) and once, in fact, someone who attacked a black person with racism. I don't even know about that last one. Maybe they thought it meant, like, a member of the KKK?

    The word is empty, except of hate. Or actually, it's even worse than that; it also carries the strain of disbelief. Calling someone a white knight (at least in certain places, because lord knows this word can't keep hold of a meaning to save its life) seems to imply that they don't believe that someone would genuinely hold these opinions. I get ascribed some ulterior motive, which is the reason I put up this charade.

    I loathe that. I abhor the idea that they won't even fight the things they disagree with, but try to make it out like I don't actually disagree. I'm just putting up a front for an calculated reward at the end. They tell me what my opinions are, because they know it better than I!

    In the misquoted words of Randall Munroe, FUCK. THAT. SHIT.
