                                            We're... really not that great at acting smart as a collective; because we're not actually wired to run as multiple hundreds of millions of humans as on society.

                                            Also, the idea that you need to be not-stupid to become apex predators flies in the face of all the other apex predators not being as intelligent as us. The reason why we rose to dominance so quickly is a combination of two things: tool-use based adaptability and highly opportunistic expansionism. Most apex predators come from a long natural history of predators and happened to have the right combination of evolution, timing, and location to take over as a or the top predator in an ecosystem. We forged a rather unique and fast paced track by jumping from opportunistic omnivore foragers to high end predators in an incredibly short amount of time by evolutionary standards.

                                            The problem with how we did that, is that it's always put us on the road to unsustainable growth instead of equilibrium. But for most of our natural history, nature has kept us in check with disease and disaster. But we're overcoming those even faster than ever.

                                            The general point being, the whole portal situation hits all of our worst buttons. It's something new, thus we react with fear, territoriality, and aggression that we've' never shaken off from our pre-hominid ways, combined with our extreme curiosity, covetousness, and expansionist tendencies towards what such an event could mean. Combined with governmental systems that are very prone to misbehavior as they try to harness our negative qualities into positive ones, and we're primed to act destructively in response to such an event.

                                            We'd like to think we're more logical than that, but we're not. We'd love to think our intelligence is designed to win out in such as situation, but it isn't. Apex predators need to be able to avoid each other or things get ugly. We suck at avoiding conflict, because we engage in conflicts far too large for our brains to comprehend who is and isn't the strongest side and settle things quickly. We get in other predators' spaces, and generally speaking, we devastate them; often to our own detriment because we're not thinking about the disruption to the habitat that causes.

                                            So yes, as a whole, humanity tends to not act intelligently, and this scenario is designed to press all of our collective-stupid buttons.
