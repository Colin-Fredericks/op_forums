                                                        That's still the same issue, yes. It's a combination of extremely reliable and casual magic and the need for every non-magical ability to be repeatable and always-available. The wizard's ability to blind someone is balanced by only being available a limited number of times per day - the fighter's ability to do so can't be. But I think by now we know that this doesn't even out the way it's meant to.

                                                        I also feel like we can compare it to Exalted here - since Solar Charms cover a lot of the same ground that legendary skill feats are meant to. A Solar doctor quickly curing a wasting disease is miraculous, because it doesn't have to compete with someone just wriggling their fingers at it and making it go away. Even if they can only do it a limited number of times per day.
