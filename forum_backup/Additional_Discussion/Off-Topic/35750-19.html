                                            Hi everyone! I recently had a request to make up a special version of the V5 sheet with a Stain tracker. The requestor didn't think the change be worth updating every sheet for, but I thought making a special version with the stain tracker would be nice.

                                            I know that Stains are supposed to be recorded by adding a slash into the Humanity checkboxes, but since I can't make dual state checkboxes in Acrobat (easily) my compromise is that the sheet has another row of boxes under Humanity for you to record the Stain, which you do by typing in a slash into the box.

                                            I hope you like how they turned out!

                                            You can download them in V5 section of my site here: https://mrgone.rocksolidshells.com/wod.html#v5

                                            But here are some direct links:
