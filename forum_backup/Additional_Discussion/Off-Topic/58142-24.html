It seems like non-bi people have a lot of trouble trusting bi people's intentions, always suspicious they might secretly be playing for the other team.

                Despite being openly pansexual, almost everyone I know tends to think of me as straight (or lesbian, if they know I'm trans) because I'm AMAB and my partner is AFAB. Even when they came out as bigender, they got so many people saying "does [charlaquin] know? How does he feel about it? Are you two still going to stay together? Won't that be hard for him?" Now, the last thing I want to do in a conversation about bi erasure is contribute to bi erasure myself, so I feel it's important to point out that pan and bi are not the same thing, and I don't claim to have faced the same struggles. I just wanted to provide an example of a personal experience with similar attitudes of "you can only have one favorite" in hopes of helping kickstart the conversation.

                The second part is a huge thing, based on my observations. If you're in a monogamous relationship with someone, you obviously only are into people of that gender... it's like other preferences that also are there are Shcrodinger's Cat and disappear as soon as they aren't seen.
