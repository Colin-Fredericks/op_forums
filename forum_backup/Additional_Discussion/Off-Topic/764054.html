              I'm not at all into the idea of Onyx Path (or anyone else) taking on D&D.

              All of the things I like about 5th edition are things that would invariably be diminished or obliterated by someone else taking over production - including but not limited to being able to pick up releases at my FLGS in hard copy, not missing out on cool stuff because it's locked up in a kickstarter backer reward tier that not only costs more than I want to pay for the nifty this-or-that but also includes stuff that I don't actually want, and that what I like for D&D is not the same as what I like for other games and thus the designers for other games are not the designers I like for D&D.

              Plus, the whole reason why this is being brought up - the slow release schedule - is a feature, not a bug. Any faster on the schedule means a drop in playtesting time which means a drop in the quality of the game-stuff, and that's a no-go for me.

              Not so noble anymore.
