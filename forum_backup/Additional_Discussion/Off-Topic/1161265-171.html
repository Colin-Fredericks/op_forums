    Other thoughts on the latest UA:


    Creation Bard - I've always dug the mythical conceit of the world having been sung into existence. As if art were not merely a passtime, but a primordial force. Primeval magic from the dawn of time, when words were unreliable tools, but dance and song and the ringing of primitive instruments could convey the emotional content of a magician's Will. The Bard has always had that kind of undercurrent, but it's nice to see a subclass that really digs into it.

    I find it amusing that the Dancing Item a high level Creation Bard can animate uses the same stat block (albeit with character-determined stats), regardless of whether it's a bed frame or a pocket watch. So long as it's Large or smaller, it tanks and deals damage equally well. Sure, without a Large (or Medium, for Small sized PCs) item animated in this way, you don't get the option of riding it like a mount (or at least a mechanical bull). But it does mean the Bard can animate something small enough to fit in her pocket. For personal reasons, I enjoy the image of a spinning battle top (or Beyblade, if you will) that wobbles on the ground, before spinning at a foe and tearing them up something fierce.

    The UA doesn't necessarily allow for it, but I also like the idea of the Creation Bard animating a spare musical instrument, and having it play on its own. I just really think Bards could use options for having some kind of magical accompaniment, and have been wanting something like that for a while.


    Clockwork Soul Sorcerer - You know what else I've been yearning for? A Sorcerer subclass revolving around a Sorcerer who gets power from a melding between man, magic, and machine. That's why while I feel the mechanics (pun intended) of this subclass are pretty solid, I don't know how much I care for the flavor being centered squarely and solely on the plane of Mechanus.

    I've been wanting a kind of Sorcerer you could drop in a cyberpunk or SF style DnD game. Or in any of the many worlds with steampunk/clockpunk/magipunk are on display (Eberron, Ravnica, Kaladesh, Dominaria, Endon, etc.). Dudes with cyber augmentation, clockwork hearts, magical silver hands, or blood rich with nanomachines, son. The exact flavor of man-machine-magic integration is less important than providing a framework upon which any such Sorcerer can wield magic because of internalized artifice. And this assumes the Sorcerer isn't just a machine outright, like in the case of the Warforged, or some kind of futuristic robot or android.

    It's not that I don't like "got the magic from the plane of pure logic". It's that I'm disappointed this is the ONLY option the article presents as a possibility.

    Magestuck: The Ascension

                                                WotC released another set of subclasses for UA:
                                                • Armorer (Artificer): You get to play Iron Man. That's it.
                                                • Circle of Stars (Druid): A druidic version of the Diviner Wizard, what with the ability to predict the future and manipulate fate in small ways. Except you can also wildshape into an Enchantment Creature from the latest MtG Theros set. I like the bit about the building of megaliths and other large structures to help chart the stars.
                                                • Fey Wanderer (Ranger): Where the worlds of Men and Fairies meet, chaos reigns. So it's nice to have a Ranger who can serve as an intermediary between them. Also, I like how this Ranger gets a flavorful trait added to them because of their contact with the Fae, like horns, butterflies that swarm around them, or hair that changes color with the seasons.
