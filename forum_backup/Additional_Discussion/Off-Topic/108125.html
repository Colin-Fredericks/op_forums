                      Originally posted by Morty View Post
                      Yeah, the Malkavian outfits are just... bonkers. But at least Malkavians really are mentally disturbed, and their weird outfits aren't Masquerade breaches.                       You should try it, definitely. Nosferatu get excellent starting bonuses, and IMO Obfuscate is one of the most useful Disciplines in the game. The game acts like you'll have to strain yourself to avoid detection, but as long as you don't get right up in anyone's face, you won't trigger a Masquerade breach. And if that wasn't enough, Nosferatu get more special dialogue than usual, and get to bypass some social checks. It's awesome.
