                        I will agree and disagree. It wasn't that they didn't have the long events and huge(depending on what you consider huge) crossovers, but that generally they still completed a smaller story while they occurred. You at least had some feeling of completion.


                        Edit:

                        Originally posted by Cielle View Post
                        I saw this video last night where Max Landis talks about Superman in general and what makes him a worthwhile hero:


                        I felt like sharing it because it really expresses why I like Superman as a hero in a way that I hadn't been able to articulate. More broadly, it kinda covers why I'm sick of antiheroes and ambiguous/unhappy endings.
                        I agree with some of what he said. I think he was spot on about how Superman didn't have to be angsty to arrive at his need to be a hero and the fact that "he will always make the right choice". I even agree that Superman shouldn't be allowing the city to be destroyed. Not that an unavoidable situation couldn't happen, but that really in MoS he was never allowed to try and stop it. The few times where the story allowed him to make a decision it was so he could make the wrong one. Then it would be all about getting to the next plot point. Its the difference in a story that tries to feel organic and one where it feels like it was written. It kind of the difference between Superman I and the Man of Steel.

                        I disagree when he gets to the point of Superman killing and the killing of Zod is where I don't agree. To me it is the same situation as the rest of the movie. It didn't feel to me that he had no choice but to kill Zod, but that the writers were not going to give him any choice but to kill Zod. Nothing else he could do would be good enough yet he could still break his neck. He couldn't put his hand over his eyes, move in front of the beams, he couldn't try to fly them both out of there, and a hole host of other things. But he could break his neck. That is the thing about Superman, he is always looking for the alternative.

                        This is why I still say that Superman vs the Elite is the best Superman story I've watched in years(I hadn't read the original comic run). He thought outside of the situation rather than give over his values. And that is really the most important aspect of the character. Not his powers but his vales. He comes from a set of values that might be more perfect than realistic, but ones that used to be believed in. I don't think they are gone just that media has decided that they are. That only this is the movie or story we want, but that realistically they are not giving us any other story.

                        It is kind of like why so many stories that tried to follow Harry Potter or *shudders* the Twilight Saga for the most part did poorly. They decided that people like the formula and that isn't the case. If you could simply follow with the exact same thing or some formula then every story would be a hit. We pay money for the best of what we are offered, but that doesn't mean we wouldn't pay for something else.

                        At the end of the video I agree with what he said about how many are stuck on the super and missing the HERO part.
