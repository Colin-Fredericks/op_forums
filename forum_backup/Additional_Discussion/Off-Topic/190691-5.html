    Originally posted by Papa Bear View Post
    Have you ever started to do something you've done 1,000 times before, something you're quite confident about in your abilities to perform, something that's very vital to your person or character or well being.........................and you couldn't do it? You did everything the way you were supposed to and nothing happened.

    Halloween night, that happened to me. I've never hid the fact that my mystical/spiritual/psychological beliefs are anything but "not mainstream". I talk to the street light to make then turn green. I threaten my computer to scare the gremlins inside. Last Halloween I had a visit from my great-grandmother and my grandfather and had a conversation with my totem (a bear of course, not a polar bear though). Earlier this year, I lost my grandmother, so I was hoping to get a visit from her.

    And what I got was nothing.

    I was out there completely alone.

    Nothing and no one came to visit.

    I don't even know what to do about it.

    I don't know how much of this is from the little asshole who lives in my head, but I keep thinking that I just need to walk away from all of it. The problem there is that these things, these beliefs, these practices are what has kept me away from the psychiatrist and the heavy medications they want to put me on.

    I don't want to go on their drugs.     I can't talk to the spirits anymore. I am at an impasse.




    So, how's your day?
    Yes, I'm quoting myself, fuck off.

    See that part I highlighted above?

    Guess where I'm going next Tuesday?

    Yup, to get drugs.

    I'm trying hard to not think of this as a defeat because I know I've crossed the line where everything else I've done for years can help anymore. I'm conceding a single battle to the little asshole in my head, but if I'm still here, then the war is not over.
