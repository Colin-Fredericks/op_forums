            After a lifetime of working shitty entry-level positions for barely above minimum wage; after being unemployed for three months following a night at the hospital due to panic attacks (which forced me to resign from my job that was killing me), I'm finally going to get a chance at a real job that I deserve. I applied back in December to work for the Federal Government ... it was a strange job listing because there was no specific position to apply for. A while later I received an email saying I had been screened into a position that is several ranks above entry-level with a salary that is three times what I was making at my most highest-paying job so far. There was testing involved, an oral board review, security checks ... it went on and one. I hadn't heard from them for about three weeks and assumed that I had been rejected, so I started to apply for other jobs. I was getting frustrated. Yesterday I applied for ten jobs and even though that's typical for my job-application activity, I had barely heard anything back from anyone. Couldn't even get a job washing dishes in a hotel restaurant.

            Then, today, I received a phone call. The Gov wants to hire me! It's a temporary position, only for three months, but with the money I'll make I can survive for six to nine months of unemployment afterward. Also, having a Federal position on my resume will look amazing. It will also make it a lot easier to get more work with the Federal Government in the future because, once you're in, you're much more appealing - you're already in the system so it means much less work needs to happen.

            I'll be finished some time in May/June which means I'll probably have the summer off. I am thrilled!
