                                A bit late to this but here's my two cents. If I'm retreading over previously talked about material than I apologize.

                                I've recently run a W20 game about high school students so I have a bit of experience with this kind of game. When I was doing it I tried focus more on the isolation and the unknown, the local Garou were preoccupied elsewhere, leaving them adrift to figure out their true nature, the real world around them and everything else on their own. With your idea it's much the same expect in all likelihood they are among the first of the DB so they won't have someone eventually come and help them make sense of it all. Nor would they have people who know about the dangers of the WoD so things would be a lot harder for them. Some of the ideas and material from Hunter: the Reckoning would do well here for that.

                                Of course I wouldn't involve other supernaturals and the like right off the bat, allowing the PCs to start to get a feel for things and think that they understand the world only to get the surprise that there are other things out there, especially if you haven't told them about the CWoD aspects.

                                Though I have to admit I'm not sure how things would translate between worlds. Even the DB are kind of overpowered compared to most average WoD supernaturals. Unless you're going to reduce or increase the power levels of one.

                                One thing I would recommend taking a look at is Worm, a web serial set in an alternate Earth where in the 80's people spontaneously started gaining super powers. Fast-forward to today and parahumans are everywhere with some as Super Heroes but more as Super Villains, giant unkillable monsters periodically stomping through the place*, hidden conspiracies scheming, characters with hax powers, etc. Oh and the source of the parahuman powers sounds a lot like the Exalted Shards.

                                *And when I say stomping through the place I would liken it to back when the Primordials would periodically stomp around Creation. Seriously by the time the story starts the monsters had already destroyed the islands of Japan and Newfoundland during their rampages.

                                Anyway the story revolves around Taylor Hebert, a fifteen year old girl who is... well the definition of an outcast, tormented regularly by a trio of girls until one day they pull a especially traumatic prank** on her and that event causes her to trigger, gaining super powers. And while I haven't yet finished the story it reminds me some of the ideas and themes in WoD/CWoD lines and Exalted. It would actually make for a great alternate setting for said games. With your game it would make for an excellent possible future for the world with DB taking the place of the parahumans, depending of course on how the PCs act.

                                **I use prank in the loosest terms here cause what they actually do is really horrible.

                                Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.
