        I'm all for peaceful protests, sit in's...ect..ect.... but torching an auto parts store and clearing out cash registers at a target does what exactly?
        Perhaps put some time into the psychology of violence during protests?

        Because violent acts during protests get worse extremely predictably when... police escalate. If the police show up in riot gear, with armor vehicles, and use tear gas and rubber bullets on the peaceful protesters? We know it makes the protesters feel more desperate, and decreases the protesters desire to stay non-violent.

        When the police de-escalate, protesters generally exercise control over each other more effectively and you see less looting and so on. Not none, because there are non-protesters there that are willing to take advantage of the protest and know that the media and people on the Internet aren't going to care about that.

        Violence also increases with... repetition! The more peaceful protests fail to lead to change, the more violent protests crop up.

        None of this is actually terrible difficult.

        If you don't want stores looted in a riot...complain about of the reasons people are rioting in the first place instead of complaining about rioting. It might actually do something. Complaining about the poor physical stores just furthers the idea that people subjected to police brutality are less important that a building, and need to be that much louder to be heard.
