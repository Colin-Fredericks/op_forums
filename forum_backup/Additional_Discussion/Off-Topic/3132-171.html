        Originally posted by Kammerer View Post
        Oh come on, they are hilarious. And Chick's dead now, so we'll never get any new ones ever again.

        True. As awful as most of Chick's works were, Dark Dungeons is still good for a laugh every now and then due to the sheer inaccuracy and campy writing.

        I grew up in rural Appalachia back in the 1990's and early 2000's, and I attended a fairly conservative and borderline fundamentalist church as a kid. So I saw a lot of Chick Tracts back in my day.

        My parents, who are both Christians, never cared for Chick. Even my fundamentalist grandparents generally considered Jack Chick to be a crackpot, though my grandfather did like the Chick Tract titled The Sissy, which is admittedly one of his less offensive tracts, seeing as it doesn't contain any paranoid conspiracies or hate-mongering. Granted, The Sissy (the tract about the trucker who finds Jesus) was sort of an exception rather than the rule, and my grandfather still considers Jack Chick to be completely insane.

        Oddly enough, Chick Tracts did lead me to begin questioning Christianity as a kid, leading to me becoming a bitter extreme atheist as a teenager, and later on, a more mellowed out polytheistic pagan. Ironic, isn't it?
