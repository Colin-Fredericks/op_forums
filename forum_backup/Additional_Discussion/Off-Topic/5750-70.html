                                                            Rogue One was fine to me. Other than that though, I felt that the film never really raised above just good through most of it and I think that's because the characters and plot are just satisfactory at best. The main characters are all archetypes; the reluctant rebel who realizes she can’t run away from the galaxy’s problems, the commander who has to do morally questionable things, the wise and spiritual monk, the cynical solider that learns that there is still hope, the comedy relief robot. All the actors do their job and none of these characters can be called bad or cringe-inducing, but beyond those archetypes they are paper-thin without much in the way of interesting interpersonal relationships or conflicts. Even the main female lead Jyn is ultimately just fine, with her relationship with her father, which I imagine is supposed to be the emotional heart of the film, being far too predictable. You probably already know everything that is going to happen with her character arc.

                                                            In regard to the plot, well, just think about the film you expected to see after watching the trailers and think about basic war film plots that you’ve seen hundred times before. Yeah, whatever you were imagining is exactly what you’ve get in Rogue One. The hope speeches, the last-minute saves, the heroic sacrifices, and tear-jerking moments all come when you’d expect them too. Even the supposed darker elements and atmosphere is average Hollywood fare. If you are a veteran of Walking Dead or Game of Thrones you’re going to be very unimpressed by the “darker” elements of this film. That all being said, the last third gives has an action set piece that is just mind-blowing. This is the best space battles we've have ever seen in a Star Wars film. Period. Seriously, the last action third is worth the price of admission alone. Episode 8 is going to have to go all out to beat it. Oh, there’s also two CG lookalikes in this film. I’ve heard people complain about them but I thought they looked fine.

                                                            So, yeah, to sum it all up this is a good Star Wars film with great action and visual aesthetics but lackluster plot and characters. Go see it for the holidays but don’t expect to see genius filmmaking.
