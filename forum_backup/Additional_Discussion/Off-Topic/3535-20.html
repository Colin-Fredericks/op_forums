                After I read the whole book in english first, the names in portuguese didn't stick. So whenever I am trying to explain Changeling in portuguese I have a hard time, haha.

                Saudade is unique because it encompasses. Since there is not a definition exactly for it. Lunatyk has asked me about something like this before, it was fun trying to explain.

                I even heard that there is no equivalent in other languages because of the way we Brazilians/Portuguese/Latins in general miss people. While Germans, for example, miss seeing someone, or americans may miss speaking to someone, we miss the person as a whole and in the flesh. We miss hugging and kissing and the proximity. That's my theory why Saudade is unique and hard to explain: we miss different.

                                            It's a characteristic of all latins, perhaps the French are the exception. In Portugal, Spain, Italy, Brasil, Argentina, etc. people are quite warm, talkative, touchy and kissy. Some are more than others, but when compared to non-latins we are much more than them. Latins are usually very social, which our neighbours to the north find strange and sometimes shocking (my brother kissed a finnish girl two times in the face - that's how everyone says hello around here - and she almost had a heart attack, I myself was almost slapped by an american girl for the same reason when I was in Miami as a 17yo boy who didn't know american ways).

                                            In Portugal you will find the best hosts in the World. No one can receive better and make you feel more welcome than us The Brasialians have a slightly different characteristic, they are so warm, talkative and friendly you can be BFF after 5 minutes and not even know how the hell you got in there (that's also the reason they are masters in marketing and publicity, no one communicates better).
