          We usually say some variation of "In Brazil, everything ends in pizza". Currently it is almost always used to denote political scandals, abuse of power from authorities, and white collar crimes in general ending up with no punishment or incredibly lenient sentences, due to ingrained corruption in the justice system, politics, media, business, et cetera.

          It actually originated from soccer, way back in the 60s. The directors of a team had a meeting that went on for over 14 hours, voices were raised, and people almost came to blows, but the more diplomatic of the present managed to calm the mood and they settled things. Starving, the group then went to a pizza place and got themselves 18 pizzas. Next day a newspaper has "Crisis in [soccer team] ends in pizza".

          The expression popularized from there and over the years got the current connotation. There is still a little subtext about meetings settling things, but now between political figures and the like making deals behind the scene to subvert the justice system.
