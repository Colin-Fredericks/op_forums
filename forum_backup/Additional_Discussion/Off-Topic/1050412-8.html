        Well, "White Wolf", we had a p.good relationship since 2004, but this is where I get off the train. Since 2004, I've actually paid you the money for books I couldn't afford to pay for in my youth, so I stole them. I felt like I owed it to you, especially because in my eyes, I wanted to "earn" that copy of Charnel Houses of Europe that I five-finger discounted from Walden Books way back when. So much of my youth was informed by White Wolf games opening my eyes to real world issues. Over time, the content and takes on those issues appealed to me less and less, and, as the games slowly slumped towards completion... some better than others (Apocalypse: best ending book. Fight me.), my foot was already out the door, then nWoD happened.

        nWoD was like the designers learned a ton of lessons about the mistakes/flaws/ugly points of their games. I genuinely LOVE the re-launch of the line. It was exactly what I wanted, and needed as a gamer at the time (not so enthused about 2E tho, which is making this easier) White Wolf and I since that time have been excellent partners. I give them money, they make quality stuff, almost die off as a company, repeat. I would've been happy with this, but, nothing gold can stay. The culture has moved past me, clearly.

        Hiring Zak S, and based on comments from Martin seen previously, I feel like these people don't realize it's not 1999 anymore. Edgy doesn't cut it, and more to the point, I'm fairly convinced the median age of a white wolf player is not teenagers. It's 20-40 somethings, who frankly don't give a shit about this "edgy" stuff. Even if they promise Charnel Houses of Europe, we will probably get Rage Across New York, and lots of it. The internet exists in a widespread manner now, people can fact check things, leading to a lack of satisfying spaces to fill with supernatural conspiracies. The reversal of perspective on the Technocracy in the mage fandom should be proof enough of that! Ripped from the headlines isn't gonna cut it when people are writing Sandy Hook Fanfiction days after it happened. That was in poor taste, I know. Think of how the books are going to look.

        So, White Wolf, this is what I'm gonna say to you. You want to act like it's the 90's again, where you can be edgy and you are too small for a PR disaster hiring to effect you? Fine. I'm gonna act like the 90's again and get most of my books off the internet. Maybe not on BBS anymore, but you get what I mean.

        It was, genuinely, fun while it lasted.

        Incentive is not permission or justification.

                                            The old White Wolf tended to approach mature topics that other RPG companies didn't want to touch, which was cool. That was, to be clear, the whole reason I got into White Wolf in the first place. Yet at the same time, they dealt with subjects in a fairly disrespectful or irreverent ways. For every book like Charnel Houses of Europe, which approached the subject matter with a respectful eye, you had two more books like Gypsies. When White Wolf really wanted to tackle a subject and treat it with respect, they could pull it off, but all too often it felt like they didn't care quite enough to do that.

                                            It's a bit worrisome to me that current White Wolf feels like going back and embracing all of that material, without taking the time to separate what did work (the stuff that handled this sort of material respectfully) from what didn't work (the edgy bullshit). Hopefully that's not the case, but haven't seen much to instill any faith in new White Wolf in me.
