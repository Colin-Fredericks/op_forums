                                Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                                I remember back in school when you had to do a report or essay is that you had to show proper references to provide support for your side and so on. Now it seems it's optional and I wonder if it's even a thing in school anymore?
                                This is complicated. These sorts of assignments are absolutely still a thing in most modern education systems. However most modern education systems are still struggling to adapt to a world were students have access to near unlimited information, and educators have no time to check sourcing quality. The explosion of misinformation (both purposeful disinformation, and just the incidentals of things like chat-program generated content growing increasingly hard to separate from human sources) has only compounded the difficulty in training students on propped sourcing habits. Justifiably there's a lot stronger of a push for at least primary and secondary education to focus more on teaching students to assess source quality and then push traditional sourcing and research projects into late secondary/early tertiary education. Or in less academic terms, people that aren't going to be writing papers for a living are better served having more time as children learning how to spot crap sources, and those headed for higher levels of academia are going to get more time focusing on formal citation skills.
