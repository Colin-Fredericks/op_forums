    A few thoughts on what has been said already:

    - If New World of Darkness gets cancelled, for whatever reason, homebrew and fanon still exists. I don't know about you guys, but in my opinion, if things get dropped and the community needs to flesh out our beloved world on our own? We shall. If things like Genius: the Transgression and the recent Hunter fanbook are any indication, we are more than capable of writing our own stories should the license owners not.

    - Regarding the recent debacle with the WoD MMO, I would be cautious but optimistic. On the one hand, there is a lot of video game potential for our worlds, and WoD could get a bigger spotlight. On the other hand, I will admit, I will be upset if Paradox gives the axe to some of our game lines or writers (Acrozatarim is one of the first names that comes to mind; he's one of the most active writers I can think of, and he's been great in participating and talking to fans and players). I understand the financial concerns the gaming industry has to look at, but "cost-saving measures" can sometimes create great divisions in a community.

    - We need more info before we can start getting worried. If anyone is concerned about the future of their game, do as someone earlier mentioned and send a POLITE e-mail to the new license owners with your questions, concerns, comments, etc. Any smart ruling or governing body of any kind should listen to their people, and if people make a big enough effort, it's more pressure not to ignore the people.

    - I'm really hoping Paradox doesn't use this as an excuse to bring more attention to themselves and use the WoD as window-dressing. From what I've read, they aren't the people that do that kind of thing, but some of the things quoted (like the whole "unite the community under one banner" talk) have me worried.

    I apologize if this is rambling or incoherent. I have very mixed feelings on this.
