It was a meditation bell sounding thing.

    I got up to go to my class, and Elder Ballard came up to me as I was walking toward my class (and commented on the ringtone to which I kind of mumbled a reply) and said something about how women are treated inappropriately. Then he grabbed my phone and tried to take it away, implying that I obviously couldn't afford it (because I was a woman) and therefore must have stolen it. Then an old friend came up and I tried to get him to tell Elder Ballard that it was my phone and he started to say maybe Elder Ballard was right because I was always concerned about money. Then I woke up.

            I dreamed I had traveled to Loxosceles's house for reasons the dream wasn't clear on. I don't think he knew I was coming, either. I knocked and no one answered, so I just let myself in and climbed the stairs to his room, where he was asleep. I stood over him, staring down at him for a few minutes, and then--again, for reasons the dream didn't offer--stole a long, plastic tube he was holding in his left hand. It was a tube used for blowing soap bubbles, though why he went to sleep with it in hand was beyond me.

            When I went to the stairs to leave, two armrails had popped up in the middle of the stairway, narrowing the available walking area until I had to turn sideways, suck in my gut, and inch down the stairs to get to the door. It wasn't a quiet process; I was shaking the armrails as I went down, and I think that noise is what woke Loxosceles up a few steps from the bottom. I hurried as fast as I could to the door and let myself out, telling myself he would think all the noise I was making was part of a dream he was having.

            Out on the street, I was walking back to my car, strolling really, when I heard Lox's roar of rage at the discovery that his home had been invaded and his three-foot soap bubble tube had been stolen. I was only a few steps from the car, but that roar panicked me: I thought he might get downstairs and see me driving off, and I couldn't let him see my identity, so I ran down the road a couple of hundred feet to the house two doors down and turned let to duck behind its far side. It was painfully slow (though I suspect that was the slowness of me being old and fat rather than the slowness of trying to outrun a pursuer in a dream), and by the time I found my hiding place I was exhausted, using my arms to drag myself forward. I leaned against the house's wall for support and dared a look around the corner to see what was happening. Loxosceles and his roommate, an immense wall of a human being whom I had not known was there, were out in the street by his house and my car, hunting for the thief and coming in my direction. Lox had an iron rod in his hand for punitive beating.

            I was too weak and shaky to run any more, so I walked shakily along the side of the wall away from them, trying to circle around the house in the hopes I'd be able to sneak back to my car when they passed by. It was then I realized that I couldn't see them any more, and they might have cut through the yard between his house and the house I was hiding in and they might see me when I turned the corner so I should stay where I was but if they didn't turn then they'd see me when they passed this house on the street and I was paralyzed by fear and couldn't move or think or make a plan and just waited there for him to discover my betrayal--why had I even done it?--and to beat me and then I woke up.

            My heart was pounding in my chest hard enough to scare me, though that might have been residual fear from the nightmare. It was painless, but I'd convinced myself I was only a few steps away from a heart attack, and tried desperately to calm myself down as I lay there with sweat dripping from my hair into my pillow. A glance at the clock told me I'd been asleep for an hour.

            Now I'm awake again, for the third time today, if you're counting. I feel like it's going to be a bad day.
