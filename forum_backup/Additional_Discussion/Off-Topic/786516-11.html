        I'd be very interested in hearing you (or anyone else) explain their views of both masculinity and femininity. How do you define "a man" and how do you define "a woman"
        In my opinion, neither is really a thing that exists independent of a culture that defines them. Masculinity is no more or less than the traits, preferences, and behaviors that a culture attributes to men, and femininity the same for women. "Man" and "Woman" are socially constructed identifiers that give context to the concepts of masculinity and femininity. Each individual has their own unique combination of traits, preferences, and behaviors, some of them considered masculine or feminine by the culture they live in. Often, people find that the gender that the majority of their traits, preferences, and behaviors are culturally associated with is the one they prefer to be identified by. Other times, it's less cut and dried, and they would prefer to be identified a different way.

            I would view Masculine or Feminine as cultural traits, but where the definition of man or woman has some different details they are more or less universal.

            Not speaking for anyone but myself here: I do not merely identify as female. I am female. It is intrinsic and inseparable from who I am. I identify as a Goth. It affects how I dress, how I make up, and impacts my interests. I identify as a Gamer. I identify as a Kinkster and a Bohemian. But I could turn my back on those things. I cannot turn my back on being female. God knows, I tried, It left me damaged. My femaleness is intrinsic. I chose to be a Goth, because I like a lot of things about it. I chose to be gamer because I enjoy it. I did not choose my being female.

            In that vein, I don't merely identify as a lesbian, I am one. In a culture or world isolated from western culture, I would still be attracted to women, just as I would be one, regardless of the body I am in.

                    Hey,I'm trans too, I'll see if I can help.

                    It takes as long as you make it but for the sake of your own sanity, understand that things like hormones take time. A lot of time. In cisgender women puberty takes a decade. You'll see results you might be satisfied with before that though. I've been on estrogen and spiro for a bit more than a year and I'm just starting to see boobs. Some ladies get big ones really quickly and some stay flat for a while.
