                Also, the tastes of the times change...comics back then were written in a much more melodramatic, florid voice, unlike the attempts at "realistic" dialog today.

                But this impact is what I was getting at above: If Dark Phoenix had ended with Jean getting depowered and retiring (which I'm sure would have been undone in good time anyway), then that story would not have made anywhere near the impact it did or been remembered so fondly. So, sometimes, Executive Meddling can actually make things better, believe it or not!

                Similarly, Byrne got really pissed off at Claremont for changing his Days of Future Past storyline, but that change led to the character of Rachel, a personal favorite of mine, becoming a regular!
