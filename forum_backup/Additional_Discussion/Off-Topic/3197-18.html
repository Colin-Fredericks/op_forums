                        Well, since I inadvertently actualized ponies for myself again, I saw this video posted on EQD. It discusses whether FiM got worse or better after Faust left, and while I'm sure it's been talked about before, it made me kind of curious to see where the opinions lie. Do you think the show got better or worse, or did it stay roughly the same quality level?

                        My opinion:
                        S1 was the only season that Faust was fully in charge of, but she was still a consulting producer in S2. That makes S3 the first season without Faust's involvement at all, and frankly... it wasn't all that. There's no reason to think that this was all due to Faust's non-involvement, but neither did it inspire confidence. Luckily, S4 was my favourite so far, and I feel like one of the best things it did was to conclusively prove that we do not need Faust to produce quality FiM.

                        However, it's not fair to compare a first season to a fourth season and say "good luck they fired her". The production team is just that: a team, and as a team they now have more practice and a better understanding of the show than they had at the start. Faust might well have made a splendid S4 if she had still been on. It would have been a different season, I'm sure, but that's not to say it wouldn't have worked in the context she would have built up for it beforehand.

                        So, do I think Faust leaving had a direct impact on the quality level of the show? No. Do I think it has gotten better since? Yes, and I also think it has not peaked yet. In fact, depending on what they chose to do with the premise set up at the end of S4, it might well be that Faust's intentions of world building and adventure will play a bigger part in S5, which in a way makes her legacy live on through another lens.
