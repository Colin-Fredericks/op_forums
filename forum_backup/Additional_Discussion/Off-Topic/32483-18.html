                                                Originally posted by Scelesta View Post
                                                I am SO CONFUSED. Why is it the more depressed I get the easier it is for me to role-play or draw.
                                                I've mentioned this one before, but have you ever been so drunk (or known someone who was) that they couldn't remember what they did? It's kind of like that.

                                                Your brain gets used to making certain connections when the chemicals/hormones are in a certain configuration. The memories of the drunken debacle are in there behind the wall of "need more alcohol" because that was the dominant influence on the brain at the time. In your case, you always feel like you're more creative because you're gotten some good material out of your head when depressed. The mental pathways are accustomed to heading for the creative places when running short of serotonin. It's not that you CAN'T be creative without being depressed, it's just the easier, established pathway. It's the Dark Side. (LUKE: Is the dark side stronger? YODA: No, no, no. Quicker, easier, more seductive.
