I love this because the woman on the phone was not actually calling for me; she was calling for my sister's ex-mother-in-law, who has been giving out my number as hers to avoid phone calls from her creditors and stuff. There are apparently legal actions being taken against her, and I am mean enough to not even feel schadenfreude at this but simply pleasure at her getting her due. This is a woman who, when her son was dying of cancer and we were throwing benefits for him to pay his medical bills, offered to work the concession stand and whom I saw stealing money from the till; this is a woman who asked to move into her son's unfinished basement (it didn't have sheet rock over the studs of the walls or flooring over the concrete or anything), promising to pay the costs of making it livable and to pay rent, but doing neither and then refusing to speak to or visit her son during the last four or five months of his life when he told her he could not afford to support her financially any more and needed her to live up to her promises; this is a woman who asked to borrow her daughter's lawnmower and then sold it to buy narcotics for her boyfriend, leaving the daughter to buy it back.

                                    (She also embezzled I don't even know how much money from the local community college during one of her first jobs and let her husband pay it all back, but I don't care much about that in light of the other shit she's done.)

                                    So I got to participate in the legal system by calling the process server back and giving them her real phone number and an approximate location; no one I know knows where she lives because she won't give out that information for reasons that in light of this story I'm sure you've figured out. It was a pleasure. I kind of hope to do it again.
