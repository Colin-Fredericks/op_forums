                        Congratulations, AZ! It is great to have family that will try their best to understand you, and supports you wether they fully understand or not.

                        I love this community, so I'm going to take a risk here and express something pretty personal. I wouldn't say that I identify as Otherkin, but I do empathize strongly with the feeling of being something else in human skin. It's not so much as you describe, AZ, feeling like your body is supposed to be different than it is. More that I feel no connection to my body at all. It's like my body is where I am, but not what I am, the same way I'm not my house just because I live inside it.

                        I've read about people experiencing dissociation, and the way I've seen it described sounds almost exactly like the way I feel, except that from what I've read it's usually a temporary feeling, and a response to abuse or trauma. But I've had this feeling all my life, and I had a very happy childhood, I've never been abused, and I don't think the trauma I have experienced in my life has been especially severe. Besides, I very much enjoy this feeling, whereas it sounds like most people who dissociate find it very distressing.

                        As an aside, I think this feeling of disconnect from my physical body might be part of the reason I've never felt dysmorphia related to my gender identity. My body doesn't feel like mine in the first place, so how could it be "wrong"? It's just the vessel I need in order to exist in and interact with the world around me, why would I care what sexual organs it has?
