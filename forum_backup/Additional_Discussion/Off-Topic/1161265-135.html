                        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                        How many spells can a wizard make a day? Like, say, if the wizard's level 1.

                        As I understand it with the current systems I've been presented with, it depends on both your level, as well as how high your casting attribute is. The maximum level of spells you can use is dictated by your casting attribute 10 + whatever the spell's level is. So you need a casting attribute of 11 to cast 1st level spells, 12 to cast 2nd level, est.

                        For wizards, the casting attribute is Intelligence. For sorcerers, paladins and warlocks, it's Charisma. And for rangers, druids and clerics, it's Wisdom.

                        At least when it comes to older versions, you also gained bonus spell slots by having a very high casting attribute.


                        ------------------

                        Funny story: I recently found out about a newbie player on Pathfinder who apparently had never played the game before, and thought that Sorcerers used Intelligence. He made one with 10 Charisma, and then wondered why he could only use cantrips at level 5!
