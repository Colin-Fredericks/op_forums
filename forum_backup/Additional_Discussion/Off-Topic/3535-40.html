            Originally posted by etherial View Post
            I hope that things work out for you. I do recommend taking a vacation from each other so you can reorient your relationship properly.

            I agree. Take a break from your ex. Figure out who you are without being in a relationship. It will suck because you won't want to be alone, but it is necessary to heal.

            You will also need to reorient a lot of your other relationships too. You were part of a couple. Now people will have to deal with you singly.

            It's not all bad. Leave your clothes on the floor. Cook the food your ex hated the smell of. Blare the music your ex hated. Go on a trip by yourself (later, when you are feeling better. Right now you'll just be wishing your ex was there to share all of your experiences). Dig deep into your hobbies. There is freedom in being single. Enjoy it.

            I write things.
