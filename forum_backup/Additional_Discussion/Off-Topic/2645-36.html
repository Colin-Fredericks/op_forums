        Moar theory.

        ​
        So in the prequel trilogy when Palpatine is telling Anakin about Darth Plagueis he mentions a few things. Plagueis is such a master of the Dark Side that he can both overcome death physically (as opposed to spiritually like the Jedi later do) and he can direct midichlorians to create new life. That leads us to the inferred conclusion that Anakin's immaculate conception was really engineered by Plagueis. He then lets Sidious "kill" him and take his place so he can slip into the background with no one being aware he is still around, not even his former apprentice. Anakin falls, as he was expected to, since he was supposed to bring balance and there were so many Jedi his fall was a foregone conclusion. Now that seems like a short term plan for someone called Plagueis the Wise. Anakin meets a girl, has kids who are both strong in the Force, and one is eventually trained to the point he supposedly surpasses his father (arguably).

        Now we have Luke, with a 20 year gap between the time of RotJ and TFA teaching and training a new group of kids, but we don't know about any relationships that Luke may have had or if he had any kids. There are enough theories out there that Rey is his daughter that are plausible that I'm going with that assumption, and also assuming part of the reason Luke failed was because he broke the Jedi rule of not falling in love, he followed in his fathers footsteps and fell in love, having a family no one was supposed to know about. It would help explain him hiding Rey on a remote desert planet like he was raised on, as well as explaining his self imposed exile. It's really a hypothesis if you want to be technical about it since I rely on a large untested assumption.

        And who has been manipulating the Skywalkers from the beginning, Plagueis that's who. Snoke is most likely Plagueis (and since the First Order has been modeled on the Nazis who's to say Snoke isn't engaging in a eugenics project of his own?) He certainly looks like someone whose head was struck by a lightsaber. He also seems to be showing patience for Kylo Ren even though Kylo seemingly failed him multiple times in the movie, the hallmark of someone with a long term plan. The clues seem to be there to support this theory, but who has been countering Plagueis moves, keeping the Skywalkers on the Light path in opposition to Plagueis' design? Could Luke have actually been introduced to the mother of his child by an agent of the Light Side, in order for her to give birth to a female Light Side counter to Plagueis' male project? I'd keep an eye on Maz Kanata over the next two movies. Over a thousand years old, gains possession of Anakin/Luke's blue saber just so Rey can find it, guides Rey through her Force vision, female counterpart to Snoke's male, yeah she's a major player IMO.
        Could be..... I like it.

                    What exactly does she have to do for it to make sense that she has this knowledge? Poe literally just informs us that he has it and we accept it because he's a resistance pilot. Rey's background is kept intentionally vague, so all we have to work from is her actions throughout the course of the film. She demonstrates her technical and piloting skills significantly better than Poe ever does, and yet we question if she should be able to? What ever happened to "show, don't tell"?

                    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                    The guy who did it for a living and presumably had years of experience with it, versus the person who had spent most of her life scavenging and cleaning scrap metal for food?
                    We don't actually know either of those things are facts. Poe apparently is a pilot for the resistance, but beyond that we don't know his profession, or anything about his background besides "I can fly anything". How did he acquire this unlimited knowledge of ships and how to operate them? That's never explained. Likewise, we don't know that Rey spent most of her life scavenging and cleaning scrap metal for food. We know that's what she was doing at the point where we were brought into this extremely en media res story, and we know that she was left on Jaku as a fairly young girl. Everything before being left there and everything between being left there and the first time we see her on the film is still unknown. She clearly demonstrates skill with technology, linguistics, and the force. The natural conclusion to draw from this, in my opinion, is that there's more to her backstory that we don't know yet. At the minimum we can safely assume that she does a lot of tinkering with complex machinery in her free time.

                    If you don't like the comparison to Poe, how about a more direct analogue. Anakin also lived on a shithole desert planet, working as slave for a pawn shop owner. He "can fix anything" and built a pod racer (which we are told is "the fastest ever"), and a droid with knowledge of over 6 million languages. Out of scraps. When he was nine. Oh, and he had more latent force potential than Yoda. I don't remember anyone calling him a Gary Stu. And already we've seen more justification for Rey's talents than we ever did for Anakin's, nevermind the fact that there are heavy implications that there are big secrets about her past that haven't been revealed yet.
