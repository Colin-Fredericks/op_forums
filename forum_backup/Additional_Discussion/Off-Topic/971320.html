    An Open Letter to Onyx Path Forums and the White Wolf Fandom

    09-17-2016, 11:03 AM
    Okay, this is technically about WoD and CofD, but since I'm discussing both and more importantly, the fans and community surrounding these games, I figured I'd post it here in the general board. I have some things I need to get off my chest and that need to be addressed.

    I'm not going to sugar-coat it, I'm widely disliked here and I know it. And I think I know why. It's because of two things. The first is that I have an unconventional approach to WoD and CofD games (I like the settings but not their themes or the metaplot in the case of oWoD) and more importantly, that I am very adamant about said unconventional approach (I genuinely prefer "Dark Superheroes" over "Personal Horror" and am not afraid to admit it), often to the point of obnoxiousness. And for that, I am truly sorry. Part of it came from the way I was treated back on the old White Wolf forums (some of you who know my old username will definitely remember) and part of it comes from bad LARP experiences in real life. Sort of a developed knee-jerk reaction to things.

    Often, people tell me that I should play "Some Other Game", that WoD and CofD are not for me and that I'm "playing the game wrong". One user even has the "Some Other Game" argument in her signature, no doubt partly inspired by me and my obnoxious and horrible behavior. Usually, I would get infuriated at the "Some Other Game" argument, saying that I didn't want "Some Other Game" but a modified WoD or CofD instead. But now I'm beginning to think they may be right. Maybe I should just give up on White Wolf and play "Some Other Game".

    I know it's just a game and that I shouldn't be worrying this much about it. But given the fact that this game and my interactions with it and its fans has given me nothing but trouble and misery for the past few years, then maybe I should move on. Maybe I should just stick to BESM and the like and leave WoD for the serious gamers. It's obvious that I'm in the minority when it comes to my playstyle and how I like the WoD and CofD to be.

    In short, I am sorry for my awful behavior on these forums and I really want to like the WoD and CofD games, but maybe I should give up on them, at least for a little while. Should I continue to play and promote my modified WoD games or should I quit and play something else? What should I do?

    Any input would be appreciated and I'd like this thread to keep civil.

                          As others have noted, there is a very wide substantive and psychological gulf between threads concerning discussions about how someone prefers to alter the setting or include house rules in their personal games and other threads incessantly complaining about the setting and themes and demanding obviously radical changes to suit personal tastes, despite the fact that the clear majority of everyone else (the actual intended audience), more or less, likes things just the way they are.

                          The former is part and parcel of expected forum discussion, the latter ranges form immature whining to trollish, disruptive behavior.

                          As to why others might suggest you explore other games, it's not really that surprising. The very foundation of the CofD is it's designed and intended as a modern horror setting. If you want "dark superhero," science fiction, steampunk, transhuman, fantasy, or any other of a myriad of other RPG genres, there are ample offerings. Demanding that the CofD essentially alter its basic premises to suit you will not lead to productive discussions and implicitly devalues (and depending on style, often very strongly insults) those who like the genre and setting as presented and intended as well as the creative vision, professionalism and material of the developers and writers on the various game lines.

                          Simply, its quite easy to amicably discuss the changes you like or would make in the setting (or seek help to make such changes) without doing little more than offering shrill and repeated criticisms of the setting and those who enjoy it. If you or anyone else cannot thread that proverbial needle, pushback is to be expected, and will likely not be kind.
