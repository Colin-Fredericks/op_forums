            As I am on SSI (bipolar and numerous other disorders), this is what I recommend.

            1. Get in touch of a doctor who can treat you NOW. Sign a medical release form and give him/her a list of your former doctors so they can get a hold of your records.
            2. Find a lawyer that specializes in disability cases. Social Security has an 85% rejection rate on the first application. Mom and Dad were exceptions, getting their SSI at first go around. I had to go through 2 appeals and that took me over 2 years before I was finally accepted. The lawyer will work for a fee, usually a percentage of what you will receive which SS will pay and expenses. I only had to pay over $200 for final expenses.
            3. See the doctor whenever you get sick so it can be recorded. This is how Medicare realized I had fibromyalgia as well and paid for several treatments (chiropractor, nerve burning, possible back brace). I already got a leg brace for when the left leg acts up.
            4. DON'T GIVE UP! You got friends here and if you need to vent, go for it.
