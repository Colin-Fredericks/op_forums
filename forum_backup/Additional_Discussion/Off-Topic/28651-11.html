                    So, I had some interesting dreams last night. I may have to ask Papa Bear to do some dream interpretation here.

                    1. I was in an awesome minis shop that displayed all sorts of fantastic Gunpla style model kits and Warhammer type miniatures, eliciting a "kid in a candy/toy store" feeling. There was some amazingly cool stuff in there that I'm not sure could have existed in real life, but whenever I tried to touch something to get a closer look, I would inevitably break something fragile and have to hurriedly fix it. I had some mixed emotions by the end of that one.

                    2. This one was more in depth: It was sometime during the Cold War, and the Mainland U.S. was being invaded by the Soviet Union. I wasn't sure exactly when during the Cold War this was taking place, as we seemed to have some modern technology (like flatscreens), but the military tech and gear had a very definite post-WWII-Vietnam-war vibe. It seemed that the majority of the fighting was taking place on rivers and canals that criss-crossed America, and the primary weapons for the fight were hovertanks that floated across the waterways. They didn't appear to be very practical though; the tanks were a bit bigger than regular tanks, and the cupolas were not armored: the crew members were exposed, but that didn't seem to worry them.

                    Naturally, the dream didn't cover any of the actual fighting, because that would just be too interesting (sigh). Instead, it followed two principal characters, both fictional: the first was the President of the United States, who was trying to win the conflict or resolve the situation peacefully while navigating the perilous waters of politics and espionage. The second was the XO of one of the hovertanks, who was trying to usurp command by exploiting the fact that the existing CO was erroneously declared KIA during one of the more chaotic battles.

                    One thing that struck me after I had woken up was that, as far as I can remember, there were no female characters in any of the dreams I had. They may or may not have been in the background, but neither I nor the dream focus characters interacted with any for the duration. This seems rather odd and a bit distressing to me; the only reason I can think of for there being no women in the stories was because the dreams did not have a romantic or sexual element, a thought that just raises further questions.

                    So yeah, odd. Anybody have any ideas?
