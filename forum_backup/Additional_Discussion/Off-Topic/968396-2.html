                            Y'know, going into this thread, I didn't think I had anything to do with Furryism. But thinking back on it...

                            I've been into animal characters since I was a kid, coming up with OCs for things like Watership Down and crossing over The Rescuers with NIMH...and don't even get me started on Redwall! Later on I got into Fred Perry's Gold Digger, and I'm no stranger to his more risque Furry stories. When I got into WW, I admit that I probably made more Pooka characters than any other Changeling type, but I never really saw Werewolf as being "Furry"...but now that I think about it, however, the guys I played with in college basically threw out most of the games' canon setting so that they could play lighthearted "League of Super-Fera" stories...

                            But I was never really into the overall community. I've never even heard the term "fursona" before this thread, for example! The closest I come to that is that I used to be really into Astrology, but being very introverted, I don't match my Leo sign at all (stupid Scorpio Ascendant...!), so I used to symbolize that as me being a Black Panther, not unlike the Marvel Mangaverse version (art obviously not by me):

                            ]

                            ...Shit, maybe I am a Furry.

                            Does being a Pony fan count? There seems to be three different variants to Pony fan-art/fiction, basic Ponies, Humanized Ponies, and Anthros, which are somewhere in between. I would definitely count the Anthro-fans as Furries, but not sure about the rest...Anyway, I've really drifted away from the above over the years, aside from being a Pony fan (tho not a transgender one, despite my avatar!)

                            I hope this thread doesn't awaken something within me! (Community reference, for those who haven't seen the show!)
