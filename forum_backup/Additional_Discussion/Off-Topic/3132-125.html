                        04-13-2016, 01:12 PM
                        I am on my way. A tropical paradise vacation with my father and mother. They have friends along.....but.....I can't remember who they are. Family maybe? It's not important. We are walking towards our bungalow, the sun is shining, cars and beach goers have flooded the well maintained sun-baked sidewalks as we make our way through the resort area, magnetic key cards in hand. We enjoy the ocean for the day with tons of beach goers. The waves are perfect. I love this vacation.

                        Now it's night. I cannot sleep. So I step outside to enjoy the ocean free of people. The cooled evening sand on my bare feet feels wonderful. I make my way towards the end of the hill as it descends towards the ocean below. I see the surf and the sea is roiling! I....I don't remember the hill down being so steep/why is it so high up?! "Am I/Are You even allowed on the beach this late?" The question hangs in my head, a command prompt waiting.....No ocean for me tonight. No matter. I turn my head to the right at the top of the cobble stoned edge and see a long sauna. No it's a lap pool as I blink away the heat and bubbles. I dive in and begin long clean strokes to the far end. The sun, even distorted above the water line is welcoming. I surface half way there and stand up. I am disappointed. People are there, with their small children, Disney character floatees attached diligently to their arms. They don't want to brave the stormy ocean either.

                        I am making my way back towards the bungalow. I/You have had enough ocean now. I am walking along the cobbled path, partially cut grasses along each side extending through the layout closed off units that rise two stories on either side of me. Wait.....grass? The units are homogeneous and the design repeats itself as I wander through each towards my own and an inviting bed. There is a pool, there is a pool. Left to right, right to left. This repeats and repeats and repeats. The corner of my eye as I pass close to the blue tiled edges of a pool. I keep walking through the complex. This is taking too long. The corner of my eye as I pass close to the marble blue veined tiled edges of a pool. A man floats in the water. His eyes are closed as his face hovers in the water turned up to the grey sky. He is dead. Wait....no.....I keep going.

                        I am now in the bungalow.Where IS everyone else?! I can't sleep in the pitch blackness. There is a storm, but the lashing wetness isn't a comforting white noise. It's rush is repeating violence against the walls and roof. I stumble through through the room into the common area. I am afraid. The main door is a thick orange piece, sturdy and securely latched. The storm cannot get to me through that despite the angry pelting unstoppable noise outside. SLAM! The door opens. I reach out and use both hands to bang it back home hard enough to hear the familiar click of the latch. Nature does not care apparently. It slams open again. Motivated by the weather. I shove it back again. Rinse and repeat. I am afraid. Paranoid thoughts enter my brain. Is someone pulling at it, clawing it open against me? My imagination is the enemy. It taunts me with the image of a gnarled and pocked blue skinned arm ending with spindled fingers grasped firmly onto the door-bar.

                        The door holds and I turn towards the kitchen. I am ok. My parents and their friends are smiling and talking rolling luggage pulled along. I see this through the kitchen door window. It is sunny. I think I'll have a swim after all. They never open that kitchen door. Everything is green, everything is transparent. I am confused. For a moment I am not me. I am simply vision outside myself. A raised enclosed metal case. Coffin shaped but far bulkier. A lens shift. A click and the perspective changes. A dingy beat up warehouse the only light hanging above casting dusty dim white light on the coffin shaped box below it. A lens shift, another, and another until I lose count. It is dark outside, thick clouds above spin visibly. I am reminded of an industrial mixer constantly flipping whitened dough over and over. Everything surrounding this place id devastated. It has been forgotten for a long time. No one walks through cracked streets. No lights, no movement except the wind. There is nothing here anymore.

                        I am awake inside. My eyes open but I cannot see through the opaque glass immediately in my vision. What am inside of?! It beings to slide open. I can see a hand of long spindled fingers, the skin is pocked and cratered and very very thick. It wraps neatly around my waist and pulls me up out of the coffin-boxed thing.....

                        ^And that is why I HATE when I dream. That's the damn dream I just woke from half an hour ago.
