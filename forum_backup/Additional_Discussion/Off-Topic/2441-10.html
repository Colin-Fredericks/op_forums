                    Originally posted by cerealkiller View Post
                    what kind of show are you looking for?
                    I'm actually not actively looking for anything, but since I'm running out of episodes for a few of the things I have been watching, I wouldn't mind recommendations within the following guidelines:
                    • Combat: I'm in the mood for fighting animes. It doesn't have to be overly violent or necessarily the major focus. I also have a distinct preference for melee and hand to hand fighting or combined melee and ranged fighting over a major focus on firearms or just throwing magic at opponents or things like that.
                    • Non-Ecchi: little to no fanservice is a preference. Alternatively, equal-opportunity fanservice also works for me. Fanservice that is well justified within a good narrative is also okay (like, a sexy scene because one character is trying to seduce another is probably OK. All the female characters coincidentally having their clothes sexily destroyed in battle is probably not, though I do excuse a lot if it is played for comedy)
                    • Non-Tragedy: I don't mind a serious plot, though I'm slightly more in the mood for something lighthearted lately. Definitively nothing too dramatic and absolutely no tragedies, though.
                    • Short: something with less than 30 episodes is preferable, but it is not that big of a deal up to 40-50. Only something really good to get me to watch 60+ episodes and truly phenomenal for 100+
                    • Animation/Artwork: I'm an art guy, so it is hard for me to overlook poor animation and artwork even if the story is good
                    • Not exceedingly shonen: I don't mind shonen anime, but I prefer ones that don't rely too heavily on the tropes of the genre.

                    Stuff I have on my list to watch or re-watch include stuff like: both seasons of Ghost in the Shell: Stand Alone Complex plus the Solid State Society movie, Heartcatch Precure (and probably other Precure after I finish it, since I really liked it), Big O, possibly Durarara! (I have only seen 3 episodes, so I'm not sure if it is my cup of tea), Birdy the Mighty: Decode.

                    And among the Mangas/Manhuas/Webtoons: The Gamer, Tower of God, Onepunch-Man, The Breaker: New Waves (already read The Breaker), Noblesse, Ability, History's Strongest Disciple Kenichi (I like the premise, but it is so shonen it hurts and the fanservice has grown to ridiculous levels over time, so I'm currently giving it a break), Vagabond, Teppuu.

                                    So the next episodeof Sailor Moon Crystal came out, and now I have a few gripes. It's been a while since I read the manga, so this isn't really a whole on comparison.

                                    1)What's with the dialogue? So much of it seems really weird, particularly for Ami, having her say things that seem really out of place for her character(describing Luna as an angel who came out of the sky when she clearly jumped out of a tree? what?). Some of it is stupid, and some of it is out of character(even if the afore mentioned scene wasn't stupid, Ami doesn't describe things like that).
                                    2)So Mamoru being in a tux in the first episode was cute, even cheeky, and somewhat justified given the event. But for him to be running around in that tux all the damn time? That's not funny, that's lazy guys.
                                    3)I can't remember if the manga did this or not, but I feel like we got a clear understanding who the enemy is by now. But as is, I don't think our heroes even know who they're fighting or why, beyond quick little snippets that don't stick.
                                    4)I've often described the Sailor Moon manga as being surprisingly WoD-esque. This episode was a great example....that somehow lost that in translation.

                                    Still, despite this, and some other weird choices(why is that ridge over Sailor Moon's ass in the transformation sequence, seriously?), TOday was still a fun episode that introduces Best Sailor Ami Mizuno(giving her glasses definitely made her cuter, which I guess speaks to my fetishes), and despite my desire to see them mix the original anime episode and the manga chapter together, it was still a thrill to see that variation of the story come to life. And now that we have Best Scout, things can only get better.
