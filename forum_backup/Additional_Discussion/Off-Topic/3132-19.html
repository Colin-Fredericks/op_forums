                    I have been invited to join a PbP Exalted game on these forums. Normally I wouldn't go for it, partly because I have never done PbP and mostly because fuck Exalted's second edition, but Omicron is in, and I have wanted to play a game with him for a long time. I do not intend to miss this opportunity to play with him.

                    I've had several false starts with character ideas that stalled out or were fundamentally flawed. I might be having another such false start now with a concept that I think is pretty solid but is unfortunately too archetypical: Exalted characters, if you don't know this, are granted a caste that defines their abilities, and the character I've designed fits the caste I've chosen so well I can't think of anything to do with all my character creation points to expand upon him as a person. He fits his caste too well.

                    That's not what I hate. It's a minor problem, and I'll think of a solution (I think I'm onto one now, having the character's past life inform all the stuff I can't figure out anything to do with, ending up with something like the Corinthian 2.0 from Sandman). What I hate is how hard it is for me to flesh this character out because I can't think of interests or character traits apart from his job. That seems to be the character's entire personality, and I am increasingly worried about what that says about me. After all, a character is always going to be just me viewed from a different angle, and as I look at this imaginary person who has no personality except for his job, I look at my own life and see how little of a life, how little of a self I have except for my means of employment. I mean, I don't have many friends, and I don't really talk to people, and I don't really go out and do anything other than, like, shopping for groceries and shit. I don't really have any hobbies. I don't see my family at all. I go to work, and I come home from work, and I eat, and I sleep. That is just about all there is to me.

                    I feel like I'm only about half a person. I hate that.
