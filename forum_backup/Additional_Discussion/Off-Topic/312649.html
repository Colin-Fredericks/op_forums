                                                          I'm currently studying english here in France. Two years ago, I earned my Bachelor's Degree in translation, and went on to work on my Master. Unfortunately last year, I spent most of year in a hospital, so i took the year off school. When I came back this year in September, I learned that they had changed the entire syllabus this Summer, and made it something that I don't like : I thought that I would be able to choose which classes i wanted to take. But when last year, I had six hours of translation class a week, now it's only a lousy hour and a half. And I have to do an essay, but teachers have already refused 5 differents subjects of mine. I'm starting to get sick of it, and I don't even want to fight anymore. I went into english because that was an easy class for me in highschool. I like translating stuff, but when I think about making it my job, it bores me. I don't even know why I'm in this course anymore.

                                                          I want to study something that I really love, something that I'm passionate about, get a diploma and find some work with it. Problem is, I don't know what. I like. I have two passions, RPG and mythology. The state of the rpg industry in France won't allow me to pay the bills if I get a job in there, and I'm not even a good writer. As for mythology...I really love it. I'm really curious about certain cultures in particular (Norse, Celtic, South American...), but I don't know I'll be able to get any job with that. I've never heard of a job that requires you to have a vast knowledge of myths and legends...

                                                          And I can't stand the idea of getting a job that I don't enjoy. I know it's idealist, but... I know I'll crush me otherwise.

                                                          Thankfully, like Denmark, I have money from the government for my studies. Unlike Denmark, it's only for students that doesn't come from a family with a comfortable situation.
