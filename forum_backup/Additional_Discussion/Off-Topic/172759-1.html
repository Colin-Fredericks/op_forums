                                I think, as far as the New World of Darkness is concerned, there's a few important things to keep in mind.

                                1. That people don't WANT to know the monsters are out there.

                                2. That the monsters enforce Masquerades which don't rely on actually somehow being invisible and unknown. They instead rely on misinformation, stigmatization and a lack of solid proof. Stigmatization dovetails with #1.

                                3. That they're actually not a secret to the groups that would plausibly uncover them. Taskforce Valkyrie knows of and deals with the existence of ENEs. Cheiron harvests their bodyparts. The Union keeps them out of our backyards. VASCU investigates magical serial killers. Network Zero puts videos on websites of monsters. TFV covers them up. Etc. There's all sorts of organizations who, while simultaneously trying to eliminate supernaturals, want to keep them a secret from people.

                                It honestly strikes me that, provided one utilizes those organizations, the secret of the world of darkness isn't that monsters exist, it's that they're not officially acknowledged or accredited. Your average joe doesn't want to know the truth. So the exceptional people that seek it out get swept up in it. All of that comes together pretty nicely, I think.

                                                With a lot of conspiracy theories and suspension of disbelief issues it's a lot easier to drop something in that could be there that you lack proof of than to change around something you do have proof of existing in a certain way.

                                                The way Pentex tends to be presented as destroying the environment to kill off the weres because they're evil, infested with wyrm forces and incidentally are the most profitable and major corporation in the world is an example of this. It's backwards and thus hard to swallow. On the surface all the boxes are checked: Big companies are rich? Check. Big companies pollute? Check. Big companies are often noted for being soulless and having no cares about the individuals or people they impact? Check.

                                                The problem is that as soon as you work with such entities you quickly realize that the pollution is a side effect, treating people poorly is a side effect. It's all focused on making as much money in a given time frame as possible, and the rest of it is a result of that. And that said companies are insanely cutthroat with each other, if one fails to take an opportunity or makes a misstep someone else will profit from it.

                                                Thus that presentation of Pentex is bad, because it builds it in a manner contrary to what I know happens. It's not "money is a side issue", any major corp that isn't scrambling for money is going to get eaten by the ones that are. Oil companies are not Saturday morning cartoon villains poisoning the lake for the lulls, it's done because it's the most profitable way to do what they need to do.


                                                So werewolves, vampires, or mages secretly living among us and influencing decisions? A *lot* easier to swallow since I don't have evidence against it. A Company that's the most profitable while having profit as a secondary goal? That's harder.
