                    I'd say the Mega Threads (tm) are chatrooms pretending to be threads. And I agree that, perhaps, that is not the best practice.

                    I feel like we (I) need a place to dump an idle thought and allow possible banter to evolve from that. It helps, at its best, to create a café-like atmosphere (and at its worst something very terrible) where people can come and go to hang out as they please, and helps build community and forge relationships. Sure, it often also turned pretty sour, but I don't think that's an issue with the format. I also realize that the massive 87-page threads can be absolutely daunting to pore over, especially if you've been away for a week or two, or are completely new. I know I didn't even *look* at the threads in General Discussion when I first started posting on the WW forums, simply because the vastness of the most popular threads was intimidating, and there seemed to be an established crowd of people that did the bantering. I ended up overcoming this intimidation, and I'm glad I did, and I'm glad there was an established medium (in this case in the form of Mega Threads (tm) ) for me to do so.

                    It often isn't evident until after the fact whether a thought, gripe or declaration of love was worthy of its own thread, and therefore I feel like "well, look at the Hate/Love/Let's Talk thread, it contained several individual threads within it!" is a.. technically true but not entirely useful statement in this context. We need a place for idle chatter.

                    ...I also feel like this maybe should be an actual chatroom* rather than a Mega Thread (tm).
