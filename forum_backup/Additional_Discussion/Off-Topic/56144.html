    If Onyx Path had a volunteer promotional group, would you participate?

    01-23-2014, 11:19 AM
    Just last night, I was at my LGS hanging out, and met a person who introduced himself as a fan of Exalted. I was shocked to find that he was completely unaware of the new edition, the launch of Onyx Path, or indeed any information about the changes to the properties and products formerly produced by White Wolf.

    We were both in the store to play a tabletop miniatures game from a certain company who recruits volunteers from their player base to promote these games. These volunteers are vetted, and must prove a certain amount of commitment to the game, as well as signing NDAs to protect any intellectual property to which they may be exposed. In return, they are allowed to participate in playtests of new material, volunteer to man booths and run games at conventions, and get a discount on products for the company.

    This morning, I came across a thread in the Exalted forum, talking about the state of the RPG market, and the need for us, the fans, to better promote the games we love to help expand the market

    The two ideas fused in my brain, and now I am wondering if Onyx Path would benefit from setting up a similar volunteer corp. It could even be run by the volunteers, with oversight from Rich Thomas. I am wondering how many people would be interested in such a program, and would participate if one existed.

    Personally, I wouldn't bring up the idea if I wasn't interested, so coune me in! I would even volunteer to help administer the program.

    This one is for all my Exigents...

          The last time I inquired about such a thing, which granted was back after Ex3 was first announced I got nothing official on the topic, unofficially directed to the Wrecking Crew who are the volunteer demo group.

          However apart from a couple of very brief dips into Scion (iirc, it's been a couple years, it might have been another non-exalted WW game) they only do WoD stuff and are very limited in what/where they cover.

          Determined to drum up interest I took the effort to hack down some of Exalted 2e into something convention playable, at least with the pre-gen characters I had. I asked around official peoples if there was any way I could offer a minor token or hand stuff out at the convention to help with the promotion of the game line. Nothing. Since everything is going through drive-thru I asked over there about the same, since I would basically be promoting the service. Some very minor item (bookmarks IIRC) was offered, never delivered.

          I spent a large part of that convention (outside of running the two games) updating people that no, White Wolf didn't close down back when the oWoD/cWoD ended. No they didn't get shut down. Yes they've actually been making stuff. And highlighting the quality of the books that were produced as Print On Demand. A lot of people were expecting some cheap coil-bound thing.


          Now personal experience aside, the problem with running a volunteer based administration is that by definition volunteers are flakier than a pie crust. When a choice has to be made between the volunteer work and something else, OPP is the loser. This gets into an issue given how much drudge work is involved in such an effort.


          So would OPP benefit? Probably. Would I be interested in such a program? Maybe if it had actual support sure. Do I think it's ever likely to get support? Well, between my own experiences and the fact that Exalted got less official advertising or promotion for it's kickstarter than any other product I've seen come out of White Wolf... I'm inclined to believe it's seen as some generally unpopular appendix to the world of darkness with an unusually fanatical fanbase, and thus attempting to increase or broaden the fanbase would be seen as a waste.


          So... if anything happens, I'd suspect it'll be all-fan driven. Some fans generating interesting scenarios and some characters to make it faster and easier to run something at a convention would be cool. Some people making a video Exalted Quick-start would be awesome. Someone getting Wil Wheaton to play it on one of his shows would be awesome. I'm thinking that anything more than those short-duration efforts are not likely to materialize.
