    Hi my name is Joe. I have 3 dogs and am getting married to my partner of 7 years in the spring (he proposed for my birthday)

    Ive been a fan of World of Darkness since 1997, started with Vampire the Masquerade then jumped onto Werewolf the Apocalypse.

    3 years ago Drumheller (Louis Garcia) tapped me to join Dead Gamers Society, an amazing group of dedicated WOD gamers, and Ive been active in that meet-up as one of its organizers.

    I've gamed with Kismetrose, who manages some amazing WOD themed Facebook groups, and have been happy to be part of this community (in its various iterations) for a long while.

    Im currently storytelling Vampire the Requiem and Mummy the Curse for Dead Gamers Society. We meet at Cal State Fullerton university (OC) and in my neigbhorhood in Los Angeles. Recently, I got to meet some of you in person at Los Angeles By Night, where DGS partnered up with Dave Martin and his awesome Wrecking Crew. All in all I am very happy to be part of such a great group of gamers.

    I hope to continue my love for WOD and to make new friendships here.
