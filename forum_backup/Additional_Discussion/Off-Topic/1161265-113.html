                Sure there is. If you use it with a shield, you’ve got a d12 weapon after your shield breaks instead of a d8. If you want to be able to use items, you don’t have to stow it first. And, yeah, if you take Combat Grab, you can free up that hand more easily.

                Originally posted by Morty View Post

If you’re not the shield-using type, you can stock up on alchemical items, traps, etc. and use those with your free hand. These are all things you can do more easily with a bastard sword than you can with a greatsword, and that give you more options for how to use your action besides attacking.

                                                    Not a "large" number. Any kind of number above "one, maybe two" would do.

                                                    I’m seeing a lot of those options. Power Attack, Sudden Charge, Double Slice, Point Blank Shot... And those are just 1st level Fighter Feats. It seems like the action economy is the primary martial resource, which is exactly what I had been hoping for.
                                                    But you only get one of those, unless you're a human. One 1st level feat, and that's it, unless you sacrifice a higher-level pick for another one. Fighters at least get a decent-sized selection; rogues or rangers get one feat out of four, and not very good ones. If you gave a martial character two or three such abilities on level 1, that might actually make for a decent basis of a varied action selection. Of course, not all of those abilities actually alter your action economy that much.

                                                    Monks are in a similar boat as fighters, in that they get a decent selection of low-level abilities and can only pick one per "tier". This is particularly amusing with stances, since there's three but you can only pick one... so you'll basically use it all the time or close to it. The next chance to pick a stance appears to be on level 8.

                                                    Frankly I'm not sure why they didn't give other fighting styles special actions, like raising your shield and blocking with it for shield-use. Power attack could be the special action for two-handed weapons and combat grab for a single one-handed weapon. Or something else. Either way, more ways to spend your actions without having to spend precious feats.
