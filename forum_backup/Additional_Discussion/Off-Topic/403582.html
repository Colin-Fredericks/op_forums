    Highlander Reboot Dave Bautista is the Kurgan

    02-12-2015, 03:17 PM
    Do I think Highlander should have a reboot? No, I believe the film is a classic for a reason. Then I am reminded that the Highlander: TV Series was great and a classic in its own right was a reboot of the franchise. Changing that the first movie with the Kurgan and Connor Fight was not the Quickening.

    So I am torn. I love Highlander and the TV series. The idea bothers me, but it is going to happen. So I might as well see how it goes. I could be disappointed or like it. Who knows?

    Well the Kurgan is evidently been cast in former WWE star turned actor Dave Bautista of recent Guardians of the Galaxy fame.
    http://www.blastr.com/2015-2-12/guar...hlander-reboot

    I have seen Bautista in 3 films each different and he is cast as the big henchman in the new Bond movie. Not bad for him.

    Since Ryan Reynolds is no longer playing Connor (thank the Gods, he choose Deadpool instead), I am hoping they cast a Scottish actor this time as Connor and not an American (or a Frenchman with a thick French Accent playing a Scotsman no offense Lambert).

    I personally think David Tennant of Doctor Who and Broadchurch fame I think would be perfect as Connor. He would be Scottish and he can pull this off IMO.

    As for Rameriz why not Sir Patrick Stewart...

    Probably my fan in me but I can see it working.

    Since we are stuck with a Reboot why not have fun and let's FAN CAST!!!
