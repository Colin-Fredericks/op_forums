                                                    I honestly hope they do more than just one more... but if that's what Reeves and Pattinson thing is good I'm not going to argue against it.

                                                    I think it's a great chance for Gunn to reassert something that made DC a much stronger franchise than Marvel when it came to their non-comic media up until the MCU, and now after everyone's a bit tired of the MCU: having stuff to watch that doesn't require giving up multiple weekends to binging to catch up on what you need to know to get into it. That's one of the reasons why the DC animated shows were so beloved. The quality is generally high, but also there wasn't a hard joined single universe where everything was getting impacted by everything else. You didn't have to worry about watching Justice League Unlimited to know what was going on in the separate Batman animated show at the same time.

                                                    I really do think fans will reward DC/WBD is Gunn is given the freedom to do "side" continuities that are low bars of entry for casual fans that are still enjoyable for hardcore fans; even hardcore fans want a break from deep diving into the 20th movie in an ongoing franchise.
