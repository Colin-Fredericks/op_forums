                Well, that is indeed a more specific list of complaints, and I won't press for more detail. I never was likely to see the movie, just because I never watched the cartoon and so don't have any attachment to the franchise, so an intricate dissection wouldn't be worthwhile for me.

                The reason I bring this up, though - and I touched on this over in the video game thread recently, concerning The Sims 4 - is that I have an increasingly hard time trusting popular criticism of media. And this goes doubly when the subject is something nerdy, because nerds tend to take the things they like deathly seriously. They're smart enough to remember minutiae and to analyze (and overanalyze) things which might normally be glossed over. And that's honestly not always very helpful - I've seen Star Wars fans write in-depth essays about how Greedo shooting first massively sabotages Han's character, but do most people watching the film enjoy it less or view Han differently? Do they care? Do they remember that detail at all? I certainly couldn't tell you which version I saw.

                So when I hear complaints like this - the CGI is ugly, the plot is convoluted, the turtles are unlikeable - if I want a helpful answer, I have to question what that means exactly and where the reviewer is coming from. What makes the turtles unlikeable? What makes the plot confusing? (Rhetorical questions, just reiterating.) And often, if I want an accurate answer, I just have to view the media in question myself. I've very frequently found that the critics' assessment is way off the mark compared to my own experience.
