                Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                Why do you not like normal clothes on superheroes, Danel?
                I'm not sure that is something I can articulate properly...especial costumes are just something I strongly associate with superheroes. It would be like watching a major Lucha Libre event in Mexico and the participants don't have masks, it just wouldn't be right. Special clothing is a huge aspect of the genre for me and things normal people wear, even when normal people include soldiers, are just not special enough for me.

                There is also a hint of feeling that realistic outfits like Winter Soldier's military fatigues ground the character too much in reality, which doesn't work for me when the guy has a cyborg arm and most of the team around him uses jewelry, spandex, fantasy armor, sci-fi materials and so on.

                Edit: and yet, there are a lot of little particularities in my preferences that make them sometimes contradictory.

                I don't like the ladies to have high heels if they don't have some power that makes it a non-issue, such as flight. I don't like heroes and heroines without especial protective powers, especially street level, to have a lot of exposed skin, but I make exceptions for exposed heads and a few designs I have being fond of from before my preferences started to get more narrow.
                Then I didn't like when Superman got an armored look in his new costume, because he is already invulnerable enough, damn it, and the costume got too detailed and lost a lot of the iconic appeal.
                And so on. A lot of little rules and rules-exception that sometimes I don't even know I have until some costume makes them appear.
