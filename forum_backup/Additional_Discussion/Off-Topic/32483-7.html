                I've been on anti-depressants now for... a little over two months, I think. They started me off with 10mg Lexapro (because that was the drug my sister was taking that was effective for her), and told me to take it right before going to bed. I started doing so, but my sleep was all kinds of fucked up: I was going to bed at about 0400 every night and getting up at 0700 in order to go to school. I felt "rested"ish. A week to a week and a half later, I started taking it in the morning and they upped my dosage to 20mg, which is the normal adult dosage. I started taking it in the morning, and that was better: now I was getting enough sleep... going to bed around 1900 and waking around 0400. Much preferable to me. But, a month after upping my dosage, the doc said he didn't like how it was affecting my sleep, and also asked if I had felt more frustrated on the drug. I said that at the very beginning I had felt much more frustrated, but that that had abated after about a week. He said he wasn't really satisfied and wanted me to switch drugs: he wanted me to switch back to 10mg and do that for a week, then start taking a half dose of Zoloft for another 6 days after that, then start a full dosage of Zoloft (50mg). I started this on January 1, so today is the third day of the full dosage, 9 days total. The Lexapro started helping with the depression right away (though whether this was the effect of the drug, or a placebo effect for starting SOMETHING, I'm not sure). I don't feel like the Zoloft is helping at all. I've had suicidal feelings several times over the last three days.
