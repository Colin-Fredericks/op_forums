        It's complicated, but I think a lot of the problems you're seeing today have to do with rise of social media and the breakdown of the traditional media.

        A few dozen people can post hate-speech attacking whites or cheering for murdered police officers while claiming membership in the Black Lives Matter movement. Then those snippets that get publicized and blown up on various corners of the internet where a lot of people get their news and now, in many people's mind, this hate speech is the summation of the Black Lives Matter movement. And the same can be said for many other movements in which a vocal minority is able to say such loud or abrasive things that they end up speaking for all of the members of that particular movement.

        You have a handful of radical feminists posting messages about how boys need to be circumcised in order to reduce their ability to feel sexual pleasure to ensure they don't grow up to be rapists, those posts get spread around and suddenly for a lot of people, that is the face and the message of the feminist movement - it's not a movement for equality but a movement designed to almost literally emasculate men.

        For all too many Americans, when they think of immigrants from Mexico, due to social media they believe most immigrants desire the "Reconquista" or reconquest of the Southwestern United States. Of course, that should obviously not be true, but for many of them, various forms of social media and quotes from random people have cemented the idea in their minds that the Reconquista is a shared belief among all Mexican immigrants and Mexican-Americans.

        Of course, these are just a handful of radical people who shouldn't be considered talking for the group they claim to belong to, and yet due to the way social media works, this handful of people and their ideas are now considered to be representative of those groups thanks to social media. Twenty years ago, the news media would have laughed and never bothered with those stupid statements - but social media is a different beast.

        So for a lot of people, these movements aren't about "equality" but rather they are specifically about attacking and tearing down white people and the status quo because, well, they've totally read Twitter or Facebook posts from people saying that's the specific end-goal for those movements. They honestly believe they are under siege because they are frequently reading (misleading) articles or posts quoting "the other side" which flat out say their goal is not merely to gain equality but instead their end goal is to literally tear apart American society.

        Sadly a lot of people are dumb - even smart people can be really dumb. I grew up in El Paso, Texas, and several years ago I had to explain to my grandmother that, no, only a tiny handful of Hispanics wanted the whole "Reconquista" thing - it wasn't some unifying vision common to 99% of Mexican-Americans like she thought it was. She's a very smart lady, but due to various Facebook feeds and such she had honestly come away with the opinion that the overwhelming number of Hispanic-Americans wanted to forcibly take the Southwestern United States and rejoin it to Mexico.

        So I feel that sadly, a lot of the opposition to things like immigration, Black Lives Matter, the feminist movement, sexual equality, etc doesn't necessarily come about because people are inherently racist, sexist or misogynistic, but just because people are kind of dumb and social media makes it very, very, very, very easy to mislead the masses into getting the wrong opinion about a certain group of people.

        But I admit, I can be a little naive about people and like to believe that the fundamental nature of most people is actually pretty good and that when people are being assholes it's more likely to be caused by a misunderstanding than simple malice.
