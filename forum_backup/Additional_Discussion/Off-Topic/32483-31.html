            I don't know what's wrong with me anymore. I just know that something is.

            I have been completely unable to even live in and interact with the world around me, because it disgusts me so much. I have been depressed for longer than I can remember, so in those days where it seems to have 'passed over', I don't know what to feel.
            Depressed or not, I am disgusted with the World; This doesn't appear to go away.
            Depressed or not, I am deeply and hopelessly lonely: It doesn't feel saddening in itself, just frustrating and empty. It also feels vaguely-... Petty and mundane, for lack of a better word.

            Being able to admit that loneliness to myself has helped out some. I don't actually think I have friends anywhere close to me anymore, only acquaintances: The only real friends I have are so very far away and can be difficult to get in touch with.
            So, I don't have any friends to interact with on this continent.
            Additionally, I feel like I have convinced my family to hate me, or at least my Grandparents: This-... This actually really hurts, I was trying not to feel the pain, but this really, really hurts. They can't seem to comprehend the viewpoints I approach things from, generally because they are things that would assuredly get me institutionalised if I were only a little bit more vocal with them: So, they might now think that I am either insane or morally disgusting. Both of which are quite likely to be accurate.
            My dog is dying, of course, so that certainly isn't helping my mental state.

            When I started out writing I was only feeling empty, now I feel pathetic and sad again. So pathetic: I feel like the stereotypical young man, all 'No one understands me!'. And I suppose that's because I am that stereotype, though I have not uttered those exact words: Nobody, in fact, does understand me, not even me.
            My mind is like a labyrinth, with myself as the sad and angry Minotaur at the centre and all my family and friends as-... Well sacrifices, I presume, certainly not Theseus, because he actually found his way through; this metaphor has fallen apart very quickly.

            Oh, my psychiatrist recommended I take medication. But honestly, I'm not sure how depressed I actually am. I mean, my mental state is definitely not normal and I am oftentimes morose, angry and melancholy, but I feel like I am right to feel this way. The World is a horrible, horrible place right now and somehow I am convinced that someone needs to fix it. I -should- be angry, I -should- be wrathful, I -should- want to break something: Because there are some eight billion things out there in need of breaking.
            But part of me just wants to be happy, quiet and content and small and stupid and mortal. And I wish that this part of me would shut up and die already, so I can actually strive to be more.

            Sometimes, I just want to cry, but there aren't any tears anymore. I used them all up over eighteen years of misery. And it's not because I'm depressed that I want to cry now, it's because all of that anger and hatred is -justified- and there is nothing I can do about it.

            I probably need to be medicated. I probably need to be institutionalised.
            Maybe I just want someone to talk to who won't judge me.
            Maybe I just want to be left alone.
            Maybe I want to stop disappointing everyone.
            I need to stop feeling the pain, without feeling like I've given up.

            Wow-... That was a wall of text I ended up typing-...

                    It's stupid, I know, and about the same thing I usually recommend.

                    Change one thought from negative to positive. Just one. Start there. When that one works, try another. One at a time.

                    MINOR RANT: Too many people, especially in this country/culture, expect things to get done in big, grand, sweeping gestures. They don't want to take the 1,000 steps in-between to get to the goal. But it's how the world works. Evolution took millions of years. There are no missing links because it was such a slow transition that there isn't a single creature that could be pointed to as the spot between this animal and that one. The continents drifted apart over millions of years in such tiny increments that there were certainly animals fording 50 foot wide streams to travel from the west coast of Africa to the east coast of South America. The first creatures that could be identified as "human" had a good 10,000 year run as nomadic hunters before someone finally complained so much about their feet hurting that someone else invented agriculture just to shut them up. Even after that, many of them kept going in a nomadic style before finally settling down either thru choice or necessity. It's only been in the past 150 years that things have really gone all that fast from a human perspective. Don't try to fight millions of years of inertia with silly human perceptions.

                    Change takes time. Modern culture and technology has fooled you into thinking otherwise. Breathe. One step at a time. You can do it.
