    E3 2015

    06-16-2015, 09:12 AM
    Many of us here like video games. E3 is happening now. Here are some highlights, for me, from E3:

    Bethesda announced Dishonored 2 and a release date for Fallout 4.
    dontnod (the people behind Life is Strange) are making a Vampyr RPG set during WWI.
    The next Mass Effect game was announced. It's called Andromeda and the trailer has a line that says, "Discover a new galaxy," so presumably you'll be exploring the Andromeda galaxy.
    And they're remaking Final Fantasy 7 for the PS4 (and possibly other systems at a later date).

    Anything that has tickled your fancy so far?
