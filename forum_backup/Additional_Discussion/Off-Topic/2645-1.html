                Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                Don’t let anyone fool you: R2 is the main character of the movie series, around which everything else revolves. When he finally kicks the bucket, the series will be over.
                Truer than one might think, at least initially. I was just listening to the New Hope commentary, and Lucas talks about how the entire series was originally to be told thru the eyes of the "insignificant" droid nobodies...But everyone he shared the idea with HATED it, so he toned it down considerably. He does muse about what a risk it was to start the movie with, what, almost a half hour of the droids before getting to the hero of the tale, tho!

                As for the new movies, I'm not crazy about the idea. Honestly, by this point, I just wish they would leave it alone. What makes it worse is that the Disney sale killed off all the interesting things they were already doing, like the Clone Wars show, and will probably make all the post RotJ comics and books completely non canonical (if they continue them at all). Interestingly, Disney's purchase of Marvel could mean that Marvel might start publishing the comics again...

                Aside from Disney making money tentacle over claw with Marvel, Star Wars, AND the Muppets, we also have the original actors signing on, pretty clearly for the money (except maybe Mark Hamill; I could believe that HE is actually still passionate about it). Really, even if I was eager to see these movies, I wouldn't want Han and Leia to be in them; I'm fine with Luke, in the role of aged, wise Jedi Master, who probably sacrifices himself as his mentor did for the new heroes, and the droids, but that's it.

                I'm sure they've already approached John WIlliams, but what will they do if he turns them down?

                Last I heard, JJ Abrams might be close to walking on the project, as Disney wants this in the theaters ASAP, and he can't guarantee a decent movie rushed like that. Who else would make a good director? Personally, I hope they go for a different one each installment. Imagining a SW movie by Brad Bird almost makes me excited to see this happen!

                But then I remember that Disney also wants to make spin-off side movies, like Young Han Solo, Boba Fett and Yoda. I know they want a return on the massive amount of cash they spent, but surely they are aware of the dangers of market oversaturation?? If they did those as made for TV movies, or straight to DVD, preferably animated, I'm in, but actual theatrical releases seems excessive to me.

                Ok, I've ranted long enough. What do you all think about these points?

The EU has pushed the envelope a bit, but there should be *so much more* one can do with this setting!

                                As far as accepting other actors in the classic roles, I'd have agreed before the recent Star Trek reboot movies proved successful. And Carrie Fisher (whose voice on the commentaries is almost unrecognizable) is the same, only more sarcastic. We'd probably get another "Blade Runner" style performance from Ford, at best; only where it arguably worked for the beaten down by life character in that movie, it wouldn't fit for Solo at all...
