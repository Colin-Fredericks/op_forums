                                                        Interesting update on the Arena front today...

                                                        Dominaria has been released for Arena, with each player getting three packs. Not enough to start playing with, but I'm hoping to tinker soon. We also have two new formats: Quick Draft and Quick Constructed. The Draft hasn't opened yet, but rewards come in the form of boosters and gems. Quick Constructed is a tourney with your regular constructed deck, with coins and three cards as the rewards for winning. The buy-in is either 500 coins or 95 gems. For both, the more you win, the better the rewards. If you win at least five games in constructed, you'll have earned back the coins you used to join the tournament.

                                                        The microtransaction economy is now up and running as well. Gems start at 750 gems for $5.99. 600 gems gets you three booster packs, so the prices seem reasonable compared to the physical game. Will have to investigate more. I wonder if this means there will be no more wipes before launch, or if gem purchases/tournament wins will be restored after a wipe. Good question to ask.
