            Life just became darker, so much darker.

            Earlier today, we were having a garage sale. Now, we didn't make any money and it wasn't really busy, but that's beside the point.
            You see my dog seemed to take issue with the activity in the house, or with the heat or something, and was crying/howling all mourning - something had her agitated, more than we had ever seen her, so we struggled to try and keep her calm.
            It didn't work, so we decided to see the vet to figure out if she was in any pain, or was howling for some other reason - and also to figure out what the exact problem was. He checked her over, saying that she was quite healthy, then cleaned her ears and trimmed her fur so it wouldn't obstruct her vision before sending us all home.

            With her senses cleared, my dog seemed to be more or less alright when we got home. But later, when we were clearing up the garage sale, there was this brief period when nobody was watching her. Apparently, she slipped somewhere unnoticed or went outside and got herself lost - she must have moved faster than any dog her age ever did, because it was such a brief window.

            We're still looking for her, but we've mostly given up hope of finding her ourselves, given that we've checked nearly every conceivable location - I still hope that some someonel roll up carrying her, though.
            I think she's gone off to die. I kind of hope she's gone off to die - rather than the alternative of waiting somewhere alone and scared, where we might never find her - so that she might die peacefully on her own terms. But there's no way of knowing if we don't find her.

            It just-... I can't even cry right now. Things just feel horrible and hollow. I just wish we had a way of knowing.
            Forgive me for posting this here, but there's nobody for me to talk to, I need somewhere to vent.
            I should probably go back to the search-...

            Edit: Oh thank God, she's alive and has been found by a friendly neighbour who gave her water. I was so, so worried - I thought I'd lost her forever. But now she's back home, safe.
