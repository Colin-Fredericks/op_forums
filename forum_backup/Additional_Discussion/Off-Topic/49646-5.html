                            Just because I distantly remember being 17 with all that entails (I don't mean to be patronising, but it is true), one thing you might want to bear in mind when making the decision. Sex (defined as penile-vaginal penetration, for sake of ease. No, this is not the only definition) is great, lots of fun, but it goes for maybe 20 minutes (often much less, especially early on), but the fallout can go for ages after. And I don't mean pregnancy or STDs; condoms take care of like 99% of that. No, I'm talking about the emotional and social angle. Sex complicates things, especially the first time.

                            Like I said, sex is great, but try to see past that, and think, "is this under the understanding that this is part of a relationship? If so, is it part of the relationship? Or am I just so excited for sex that I'll do/say anything?" Being dishonest, either with your partner or yourself, in these questions will cause lots of problems for you later. And frankly, for your first time (which will suck), it may not be worth the hassle. Maybe better waiting for a time when those questions can be answered honestly. If nothing else, it'll make the days and weeks after a hell of a lot easier. Plus, greater chance of repeat performances. Which is always great.
