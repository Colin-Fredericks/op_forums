                    I've been watching the animated Clone Wars series recently. I'm not sure how I feel about it. On the one hand, it's delightfully fun. On the other hand, it doesn't really jive with a lot of things that I believe about Star Wars. Case in point: in one episode the AT-TE walkers go straight up the side of a cliff. This is cool and all, but I highly doubt the AT-AT walkers from ESB could go straight up a vertical cliff. Indeed, it seems that the AT-AT walkers being somewhat ungainly is part of the plot in ESB. Yet ESB is in the future from CW point of view, and so the walkers should have improved. Maybe they sacrificed mobility for armor...? I don't know.

                    And another thing I'm not sure about in the series is that they make a point of the fact that the clones are individuals. They have different personalities and they have different thoughts. They seem to have affection for the Jedi that they work with. And yet, in Episode III, they ruthlessly execute the Jedi when Order 66 is given. I don't remember a single one even hesitating. (Granted, I've only seen III once and that was opening day, so it's been awhile and I could very well be wrong.)

                    On the positive side of things, I think the show, taken in and of itself, is great. I think the characters are strong, I think the storylines are meaningful, I think they have variety, while a lot of EU stuff focuses solely on Jedi. If it weren't for the conflicts that I feel exist between the movies and the show, I would have no complaints.
