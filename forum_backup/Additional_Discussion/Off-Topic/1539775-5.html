                    BTW, I know this isn't necessarily the right thread for this, but I watched the Crow remake recently and it was unbelievably bad. I had pretty low expectation going in and it was even worse than I expected.

                    When I first heard about it it was billed as being close to the original comic, but after the first trailer I knew that was an outright lie. But, I liked the other Crow movies for the most part, so I hoped maybe we'd at least get an interning entry in the series. But it's awful. And kind of boring. There's essentially one action scene, and it's not too bad, but aside from that there isn't much happening. I couldn't believe how bad it was..

                    Yep, the Crow remake sucked. Although I toss it into the same bucket as Madame Web and Venom 3 as far as 2024 is concerned. Boring, unsatisfying and disappointing - but above all utterly uninteresting. I doubt I will ever think about these again.

                    The two bad movies from 2024, though, I'm still wrestling with are Joker 2 and Megalopolis. At least, those two are bad in interesting ways! With Joker 2 you have a movie whose whole message is that people who want to see this kind of movie are bad people. A kind of "fuck you" to the audience saying "What do you expect? It's a mentally ill person having a miserable time. There's nothing extraordinary happening here." You have that on one hand, this movie trying to give the audience the middle finger but on the other hand - why did you make the movie then? And this isn't an indie-movie. Todd Philipps had 200 million dollars to play around with. This movie had musical-scenes and he cast Lady Gaga in it! All that to say he feels disappointed in people who want to see this movie...? Still wrestling with that one.

                    The other one is Megalopolis... Haha, now there's a BAD-bad movie if there ever was one. It's more like a fever dream, I guess - but not in any way that's fun.

                    Just look at this for a taste of what it's like to watch Megalopolis:


                    Strangely I think Adam Driver actually gives a good performance in this movie. He's on the same wavelength as this psychotic fever-dream coming to you straight from Francis Ford Coppola's decrepit mind. The other one who really gets it is Aubrey Plaza in her role as (I shit you not) Wow Platinum. Yep, that's her character's name.
