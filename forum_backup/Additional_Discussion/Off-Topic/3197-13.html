                                So I finally got a chance to catch Fall of the Crystal Empire. Here be spoilers: take ten minutes to watch the video in Scutarii's post.

                                First impression: fuck yeah! This is wonderfully animated. A whole ten minute long combat scene with dramaturgy, choreography and voice acting on that level must be acknowledged. The technical fidelity alone makes it entirely worth watching, and well, I won't pretend I don't enjoy ponies kicking the shit out of each other.

                                That said, I know the video has had a mixed reaction, and I must say I can understand it. The video plays fast and loose with one of the few pieces of actual canon we can derive from the show about the war against Sombra, namely that he was banished by Celestia and Luna together. In the video, Luna is clearly doing most of the work, and while I personally think they've made a good product, I do admit, I can understand if people get a little feeling of "with all that freedom, you had to change the one thing we actually knew?" I've also seen some accusations of pandering to the crowd by letting the more popular sister take front stage. I'm not so sure about that one; I think the interaction between Sombra and Luna was the best part of the video, and if that was pandering, then I'd like some more, please.

                                Silly Filly Studios were the group who brought us Snowdrop earlier, and of course, there are more things that differ that story from this one than unites them (but notably, they both relate stories of the distant past and describe some pivotal moment in Equestria's history). The biggest difference, though, is that despite Snowdrop being a bit soppy in a way the show has never been, it could be mistaken for an episode. Fall of the Crystal Empire... can't. At all. I think that's the biggest thing that makes it less well-received than Snowdrop. Snowdrop was a fan-made episode of MLP:FiM. Fall of the Crystal Empire is just a fan-made video. And we have lots of those already.

                                So yeah, I did like it. Really solid video. Unlikely to achieve the acclaim of Snowdrop.
