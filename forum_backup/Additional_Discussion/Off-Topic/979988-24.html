                                        Honestly, i'm not that surprised that conservatives are feeling that way. I'm not saying that it's intentional from the majority of non-conservative, non-religious people. It's certainly not my intention to make anyone feeling as a second-class citizen. That would be grossly hypocrite. However, RPGs, as a conception at least arguably are somewhat left-leaning from the beginning. It's an escapist hobby, where you could be anyone and anything you want. Even D&D, which earlier editions got a lot of heat for not being inclusive was that way and look at the trends from 2000 onward. Look at 5e or Pathfinder, the two biggest games on the market.

                                        Yes, the majority of the games had and has tropes that were "conservative" and positive (mostly), for example, the paladin class as a whole. However, the base assumption of D&D was built on characters who are somewhat antisocial, non-comformist, don't give a shit about laws, or being actively vigilantes and basically outsiders in the society. In other words, adventurers. That was the very beginning of every RPG.

                                        (C)WoD in particular was always quite leftist, just look at the very core of the setting! Gothic-punk. Yes, it has mainly conservative splats too in every game and not just as adversaries, but as player splats. Regardless the games base assumptions are very leftist and always were. Look at the first (or any) edition of Vampire. Look at Mage, Werewolf, Changeling. It's not that surprising that most of the fans there are left-leaning too. I'm not saying that conservatives shouldn't play these games, or that there is no room for them in the games and in the community, because that's not true and it shouldn't be true, ever. But the core of these games has a leftist worldview, so it naturally attracts mostly leftist people.

                                        What I want to say with this, I don't want to make anybody feeling excluded. I have friends and acquaintances in every hobby who are right-leaning, or proudly conservative and I'm playing with them, and mostly we are good with each other, even when we're arguing about things sometimes. It's just more important that the other is a mostly nice person, not a jerk and we have shared interests. It's somewhat the nature of online social media, because the lack of personality, that these things got more heated than they would be IRL.

                                        However, that conservative gamers are feeling uncomfortable sometimes here is just not surprising to me. If I'd happen to go to a church sermon, for whatever reason, or to a gathering, or an event, or a site, where the majority of the people are conservative and right-leaning and/or religious, I'd feel uncomfortable, especially if they're trying to "convert" me and constantly explaining how i'm wrong. I think the best either side of the argument could do on a site like this, where the main theme and goal isn't politics, or worldviews, but a beloved hobby, is to just staying away from topics which are clearly for the other side, like this. Otherwise it will inevitably devolve to shouting and it's toxic to the community.

                                        I myself made the mistake above, so from now on, I'll try not to do it again.

                                        If nothing worked, then let's think!

                                                Somewhat agree, but I won't say WoD is b&w. It's more that the original writers and the games in general pretty much took a stance in the argument. Gothic-punk. The punk angle is a very important part of WoD and one of the reasons why i love it. There are viable player splats, that could be considered conservative. The Ventrue, the Tremere, Technocracy, Order of Hermes, Celestial Chorus, seelie Sidhe, seelie Trolls, Silver fangs, Shadow Lords, etc. Even then, those splats are frequently portrayed in a less-flattering way. Yes, the Ventrue run the Camarilla, but basically everyone hates them. The Technocracy evolved into a playable faction, but they are also the main antagonists of the game and again, not exactly portrayed in a flattering way most of the time. The OoH, pretty much like the Ventrue.

                                                The WoD games' base assumptions are not exactly that the MAN and the status quo are good things. It's not b&w for me, the above splats received interesting books that portrayed them in positive light, or discussed their viewpoints and really the "leftist" splats were got their fair share of heat too. But its unarguable, IMO that the games and writers clearly took a stance and I'm happy they did, because it reverberates with my feelings and worldview and I think rpgs don't have to be absolutely neutral and "politicaly" sanitized every time.

                                                CoD, I think is indeed went to the opposite direction. It distanced itself from "real-world garbage". It didn't want to make political or philosophical statements (ok, the later is not true, but not the kind Wod did) and portrayed the splats along different angles. The punk angle, as a base assumption of the setting is pretty much absent. Requiem doesn't really have a
                                                "punk covenant", the Carthians are communists. The environmentalist angle didn't get a central stage in WWtF, if it got any. And so on.

                                                So, to sum up, I don't think WoD is b&w and CofD is more grays, because WoD is just different shades of grays and blacks too. But the core setting and the games in WoD have a very prominent leftist and punk angle, while CofD is largely neutral on this and focusing more on the character's immediate life instead of these large questions about the world and society. Neither is better than the other and I think there's room and need for both.

                                                To reiterate my earlier post, I don't think conservative gamers should feel excluded in WoD communities. I have friends, who are pretty much pro-Tremere, pro Celestial Chorus or OoH, while I'm more a Brujah/Torreador person and we're good. One of them particularly hates the Hollow Ones, while they are my favorite mage group. It's just doesn't surprise me that the majority of WoD fans are leftists and if a conservative gamer voices her opinion in the various threads, she likely meets a tide of opposing opinions. I just think we should do it politely, because i equally don't like intolerance from either side. Frankly, I'm a leftist person, but I feel sometimes very fed up with the contemporary liberal trends, because I feel more and more that it's just another kind of radicalism. I guess the problem is not with the ideas themselves, but with human nature.

                                                In the end, I absolutely think if conservatives here have the courtesy of not telling us (aka leftist fans) constantly how we're wrong in our worldviews in various threads, then we should have the courtesy of not doing it for them in their "safe space" thread, so I apologize for doing that, however I'm upholding my opinion.

                                                If nothing worked, then let's think!
