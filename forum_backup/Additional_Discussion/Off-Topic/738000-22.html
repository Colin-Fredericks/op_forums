                    Upon hearing the news about Paradox's acquisition of all White Wolf IPs formally held by CCP I was both elated and curious.

                    I was elated that this would be out of CCP's hands. Since CCP seems to be obsessively focused on EVE, much to the detriment of White Wolf, it's for the best that White Wolf IPs were taken off of CCP's hands.

                    Now I am curious as to the future of all of White Wolf's IPs: past & present. I'm also curious as to what the future will hold for Onyx Path, for because of them, they have essentially kept table-top gaming alive for me.

                    While I am cautious, and preparing myself for the worst case scenario, a tiny spark of me still shimmers with hope.
