    Please, anyone who reads this, just know it's never a bad thing to reach out if you need help.
    It's never shameful to speak when you have something you NEED to say.
    Know that your feelings are valid, even if they're factually incorrect or illogical, because they are Your feelings and YOU matter. It's what you do in reaction to those feelings that can sometimes make bad things worse without some support.
    You are valid. You deserve to know comfort and understanding.

    If your thoughts are making you uncomfortable, try to talk them out.
    You're welcome to do that here. If you're in emotional pain or distress, or just need to vent about what's on your mind, you have a place for that. And you have people who care.

        Azrael is spot on, contrary to what some former users of this site have stated this is a community that cares about the well being of its members. I personally have experience dealing with seasonal depression and went through a bout of PTSD after a particularly bad bout of it a few years ago. I may not be able to give direct advice that is useful to someone's particular situation but I find knowing someone gives a damn, even in the abstract, is a lot better than feeling like you are alone and have no one that cares at all.

        I'm currently dealing with some issues myself related to my father's passing at the beginning of the summer. Never got the chance to grieve or attend his funeral he lived on the other side of the country and my sister fucked up the funeral arrangements that were in place, it caused a lot of fighting that I had to smooth over because I'm the oldest son and took on the responsibility of making sure that things my dad didn't want to happen didn't.
        Last week I was having a really shitty day and caught myself trying to call my dad, I finally cried about his passing and it wasn't pretty. My selfish, inconsiderate roommate decided that was a good time to start her passive aggressive bullshit around the house and I'm not one to back down from a fight. At last she made a decision I was struggling with much easier to make, I have an open offer to move to Florida from NY from my mom, now seems like a good time to take her up on the offer and get a fresh start away from the people making my life far more difficult than it already is, moving at the end of October. While I'm really not enthused about living in Florida at least I have some friends in the Tampa area where I'll be moving, hopefully the change of scenery and people will work out. I guess the point I'm trying to make is that toxic people can make a case of depression far worse than it has to be, if there's any advice I can give it's to cut them out as soon as you can, and change your scenery if at all possible. If you keep staring at a pile of crap everything looks like shit, don't let yourself get stuck in that mental trap. I did for a little while and I can attest it is no way to live.
