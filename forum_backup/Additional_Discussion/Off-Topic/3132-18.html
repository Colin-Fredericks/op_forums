                                        I basically feel like finding whoever parked there and saying "Hey, jerk, if I can manage to limp and hobble from a normal parking spot, get all my business taken care of inside, and then limp and hobble back out to my car, I am sure you can manage too - and if you think you have trouble doing it, I'd be glad to smash a knee up real nice like so you can see what "it's such a long walk" really feels like."

                                        ...but at the same time as I am complaining about how someone who doesn't have it as bad as I do is acting like a jerk, I am mindful that others have it a lot worse than I do - which is why I have never applied for any kind of disability assistance even though I am constantly being told (by friends and family, not medical professionals) that I probably qualify.

                                        Also, unrelated mostly, but a good note: Everyone with a phone they keep on them at all times should have the non-emergency number for the local law enforcement programed into the phone - you almost never need it, but the one time that you do (like if you are involved in a vehicular collision that doesn't result in bodily injury - a.k.a. a "fender bender") it is so much less frustrating to be prepared than to have to figure out how you are going to get assistance while in the middle of all the "excitement" of the situation.

                                        Not so noble anymore.

                                                        The first time he approached me about this problem and asked to put it away from the wall connecting our rooms. Quite the opposite, it keeps me sleeping. I told him I'll do what I can, which turned out to be nothing... and a few days later he thanked me for dealing with the issue.

                                                        Some weeks pass and I meet him on the hallway. The conversation goes something like this:
                                                        Neighbour: Your alarm clock woke me up today.
                                                        Me: That's impossible.
                                                        Neighbour: Well, it did!
                                                        Me: But I'm off today, there's no reason for me to switch it on.
                                                        Neighbour: Oh, then it must have been yesterday!
