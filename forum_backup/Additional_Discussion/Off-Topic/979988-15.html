Like, an MRA, a Feminist, a gay black christian, a single-issue(guns) conservative who tries not to come off as sounding racist, and an activist transman. None of them hang out much outside of game, and there are issues that I could not possibly introduce to my games without at least one side of the table rolling their eyes, if by some miracle we're all together. Sometimes I get around this by running different games on different nights of the week, sometimes I do it by banning certain topics ahead of game night. ALWAYS, I remind everyone ahead of time to stay respectful of each other, even if you can't stand their facebook feeds recently. Some people would wonder, why on earth would I do that to myself?

                        Simple! They're all good gamers, and I know how to write a good story for each of them, in whatever setting we play. They treat each other decently, and we've had to democratically boot people before, so everyone is pretty socially aware during game.That's all it really takes to get along, is an active desire to do so, and a bit of self-moderation.

                        Something I've noticed about myself that I find interesting is that when I'm running World of Darkness games, I secretly and spontaneously become some kind of hippie-Christian. It goes away after the session, but I keep it on my mind all throughout before I turn back into a godless nihilist. Nobody ever thinks to look in a spiritually righteous direction, or more likely they are actively running in the opposite direction, but the promise of God's Salvation is (almost)always an option, open to all, not just the one christian sect that "got it right". Eternal bliss awaits christians, jews, muslims, buddhists, wiccans and etcetra. I reward characters with faith, and Heaven is as real as Hell(I refuse to answer this question definitely with them in regards to some kind of Heaven, but to you guys the answer is yes, there exists an Eternal Paradise, and there exists a Creator). Satan(not Lucifer, some noble romantic fallen angel, but The Devil who takes souls, the Father of Lies, Immortal Tempter, Etc.) most certainly walks the earth, in flesh as well as in spirit, wearing many faces and pretending at many roles, seeking always to lead astray the faithful and wicked alike. God, on the other hand, influences the world through the good works of good people, which makes Him much harder to see. Of course, since the WoD is generally a game about bad people, in all the years since I've had this secret headcanon, I've never had anyone die "in grace", even with my standards relaxed and the pearly gates literally WIDE OPEN.
