                    Originally posted by Fat Larry View Post


                    I enjoyed the trailer, but I'm not sure how "heavily" involved he really is. While I know he is executive producer(which can range from literally NOTHING, to anything in terms of impact), there are a number of things in this new Halloween that go against Carpenter's vision for the franchise.

                    I do know he is personally in charge of the soundtrack, so that IS pretty cool.
                    Nah, I did my research and generally follow any word Carpenter says, I'm a huge of fan of him. In this case executive producer means that they asked him opinions and approval across all the parts of the project and while the movie cannot be said to be a Carpenter one, he approached it with enthusiasm and made several suggestions. The man pretty much thought "I had no interest in this for years, but perhaps this time they're onto something and I can help to make it a conclusion to the franchise I can accept and sponsor"

                    As for his vision, this seems to be pretty much the best compromise between what he thinks about Michael and Laurie and what the industry wants to do with the franchise. Unless you're talking about Halloween 3 and Carpenter's idea to make it an anthology series, but that was a long time ago. Michael and Laurie aren't brother and sister, the movie is a direct sequel to the first one and the Carpenter's biggest pet peeve with the other movies and Rob Zombie's remake, the fact that you should not give Michael a backstory or an explanation for his actions because that's the whole point and what really makes him scary, is addressed.

                    But the fact he's in charge of the soundtrack is indeed really cool. Let's hope for the best!
