    Is it a good idea to write a goodbye letter to an Ex ?

    01-13-2016, 04:18 AM
    I've been debating for sometime if I should write a goodbye letter to my ex. I don't want to get into what happened but we were together for almost a full year. Now its been almost a year and half now that we broke up. She started talking to me once again almost 6 months later but then once again things did not go well and we ended contact. I think our feeling were still to raw. Now that is been so long I wonder if maybe I should write her a goodbye. Being I've just finished reading her favorite book

      No.

      Dredging up old stuff is preventing you from moving on, and reading the letter would do the same for her. For both your sakes, keep to low- or no-contact.

      If you're looking for closure, closure comes from within. You don't get it from another person.

                      I know not word one about relationships, and the only one I've had that ended badly I was very, very glad to give her as little thought as possible. But on the other hand don't dismiss the power of discrete endings. Obviously I'm biased given my research topic, but stories are immensely powerful, and sometimes doing something to end the story, as it were, can drastically help us move on. So if that's something you're struggling with, it might be an option.

                      That said, as other people have pointed out, further action can instead stimulate further connection, starting a new story if you will, which is obviously the opposite of what you're going for. So, there's potential benefit, but you need to be very, very clear within yourself as to your purpose of writing that letter. If there's any doubt in your mind, don't do it. From what you've said, that's a real possibility.
