                            I've skimmed the thread (since a lot of it is just a retread of a couple of similar threads on other fora like RPG.net, and I already know about Zak), but I just wanted to chime in that this actually increases my support for OPP.

                            It's important to send a message about what sort of thing you want to see. To steal a metaphor I saw today, Classic White Wolf and the original World of Darkness gave us experiences reminiscent of the Matrix and Underworld; It seems like nuWolf wants to give us A Serbian Film and the Human Centipede. Even if one liked those films, it's a far ways to fall. The Chronicles of Darkness aren't the classic WoD, but they're written by people who understood the approach. The 20th books ARE classic WoD, and of course we have the Onyx Path-direct properties like Aeon Trinity. I understand that it would be unprofessional for Rich or anyone else to say things formally, on the blog, but I appreciated the "hey, we're not with them" statement regardless.

                            I'm mostly an Exalted-head these days, which is another reason I don't poke my head up so much, but if any OPP freelancers are reading - I want Paradox to realize picking the people they did for nuWolf was a terrible mistake, and the best revenge is to live well. (Granted, the RPG business of either OPP or WW is a rounding error to Paradox, but still. Maybe we can get a Trinity-era video game out of all this, one day.)

                                                    Originally posted by RomulusGloriosus View Post

                                                    I STILL think the Sabbat playing american football with a live human is hilarious....

                                                    EDIT: I just realized this is funny to me because of The Room

                                                    To give a bit of context to my despair, whenever I would have a Vampire discussion with my friend and I'd tell them why I prefer to play 2nd Edition Requiem to V20 Masquerade one of the biggest reasons I'd argue is my frustration with the tonal shift between the Sabbat and the rest of the entire game. Masquerade was a revolutionary game because of it's aesthetic and themes, and the bigger pull was the idea of exploring personal horror. The whole, "A beast I am lest a beast I become" thing sums up that aesthetic and feel so wonderfully and is one of the great pulls that the gameline had until contemporaries got in on that kind of storytelling.

                                                    But then, there was the Sabbat. And the example I'd always give to my friends, one that I came up with by thinking about the most player charactery thing a Sabbat Pack could do to pass the time and troll the ST, was this: Imagine a dark congregation dedicated to the power and magic of their accursed state, communing with one another in blasphemous rites that bind each to one another in a war against their fabled creators. A secret conspiracy preparing for the war that will usher in the end of the world...who then go out for a rousing game of single mom & 9 month old baby beach volleyball, where thanks to the power of super David Cronenberg powers they can have an actual volleyball net and ball respectively to pass the time and releive stress after a hard night's work...

                                                    And only a couple of hours ago did I learn that something similar to this is a literal source material for the Sabbat...I am so depressed beyond words.


                                                    PMárk, and here's another funny thing. If it was just vampires expressing their inhumanity then I'd be perfectly fine with that. The Sabbat on paper is a good idea and solid examination of the vampiric condition. The problem, is that they are the very definition of a hot mess in practice.

                                                    They break tone, have no uniformal aesthetic, they don't have personal horror elements because they develope inhuman and unrelatable behaviors and morals that could practically be anything besides human, and have so little social politicking that makes Masquerade so great that it's almost like it's an entirely different game world all together. They want to be the Garu Nation so badly that they include the term Packs. They want to be the Council of Traditions so badly that they have their own unique forms of Blood Magic specific to their Packs, and over all they make the tone of the game go from "Gothic Horror" to "F$&k it, why not?!?"

                                                    An while I sort of went on a tangent there, let me bring this back around full circle by saying that the "F$&k it, why not?!?" attitude of some of the old WW's worst projects are probably the reason so many people are weary of what has been talked about and put forth so far by the new WW. Because it seems more so by the day that they are adopting full heartily this time the "F$&k it, why not?!?" Attitude for their entire products.

                                                            I'm a pretty big fan of the Sabbat, admittedly, but I will say in few words that the Sabbat have had a lot of people writing for them over the past 25 years, and there are a lot of different visions for who and what the Sabbat actually are. My vision of the Sabbat has always been religious extremists who seek to draw themselves closer to their God by embracing their sheer inhumanity. That means using disciplines a lot, reveling in the hunt, showing no mercy in feeding on humanity, and generally treating humans like food and food alone.

                                                            They're the worst parts of the Lance and the Crone combined. Likewise, they're also sort of the BEST parts of the Lance and the Crone combined. They're spooky!

                                                            I think my vision of the Sabbat is generally what most of the writers are going for, but how they interpret that vision varies. Shock humor and cruelty for cruelty's sake might seem Sabbat-ish because the more evil and cruel you are, the more "inhuman" you are, but I couldn't disagree more. The Sabbat aren't supposed to -hate- humanity, they're supposed to -reject- humanity. Playing football with a live human is equivalent to playing football with a guinea pig. Why would you do that, other than to be a really terrible person? That's not really my Sabbat.

                                                            So, once again, I think most of this isn't necessarily inherent flaws in VtM, so much as it is a bleeding thirst for EDGE.
