                Originally posted by cerealkiller View Post
                gotta go to indies man. much better stuff under publishers like Avatar Press. They typically aren't superheroes (unless you are talking about something like No Hero or some of the few other titles on avatar with a superhero facade) but you end up with stuff like Doktor Sleepless, Gravel, Crossed, Caligula, Neonomicon No Hero, Black Summer Freak Angels, Supergod.
                It's not so much about reading about just superheroes. These are the guys who were all over the TV when I was growing up. SuperFriends, Spider-Man & his Amazing Friends, Incredible Hulk with Bill Bixby.....I know it's not the same as when I was under 10 years old, and it's never going to be, but I miss being able to read about these characters as they have (supposedly) matured somewhat with me. And then I read this stuff and I see it's still so much caught in the 90s crap of spectacle over substance that I couldn't get into anymore when I was in my early 20s.

                And I know that a lot of comics, especially superhero ones, are supposed to be that. They're over-the-top spectacles and mythologies and fantasies. But, lately, it seems they're falling rather short of that "modern mythologies" mantle that has been placed upon them. Maybe it's because I'm too world-weary to need the lessons they're hammering home. Maybe it is substandard writing. Maybe it's because the same bunch of goobers that were in the comic book stores with me are the putzes who are in charge now and writing the same crap they grew up with. I don't know.

                What I do know is that I miss the Spider-Man who was married and happy with MJ and didn't hang out with the Avengers and didn't brood over the fake parents who came back and tortured him or the fake Aunt May who died or the real Norman Osborn who didn't. I miss the Batman who responds to "Who are you?" with a simple and scary "I'm Batman." and do not care for the one who says, "I'm the goddamned Batman!". I miss Hulk Speak and HULK SMASH!. I miss Wolverine being a failed samurai who keeps trying and doesn't know jack about his past. I miss Green Arrow being a hot-headed malcontent and his buddy Green Lantern having to come save his ass regularly.


                Sorry, got off on a rant there. tl;dr......it's not the same.
