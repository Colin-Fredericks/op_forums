        I'm playing through Super Mario Sunshine again and am slowly convincing myself it's a bad game. I dislike being hit by an enemy and stun locked so I fall down a bottomless pit with no reprieve or chance of reversal, but it's the several seconds of waiting to die while Mario falls to his end that really get me. If there comes a point where I set down a controller, walk away, get a drink, and hope I have given the game enough time that when I come back it will permit me to participate again, then I feel something in there needs fixing. Maybe being able to get out of stun lock would be too unfair for a Mario game's central conceit, but cut down on the death animation, at least.

        That's not what I hate, though. What I hate is after playing this game for three days now and repeatedly dying because I can never tell where Mario is in relationship to the platforms he needs to land on or the enemies killing him, I'm starting to seriously wonder if my brain has changed in some way that cripples my spatial perception. I literally have no idea where Mario ever in unless I'm staring at his shadow on the ground like I'm checking to see if he's a vampire. Am I getting old? Have I had some kind of mini-stroke? Is this a result of the various times in my life I've gone off psychiatric medication without consulting a doctor?

        This seems like an important skill to have, and I have lost it. It's scary.

                    I always take the view that there is no such thing as a wrong feeling (or lack of feeling). If you don't feel anything, then it means nothing beyond you don't feel anything.
