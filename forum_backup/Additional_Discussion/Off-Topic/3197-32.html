    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    So basically Tri-Stat (Body, Mind, and Charm; and Talents instead of Attrubites) with a Cortex+-style dice mechanic (that is, ratings are how many sides your die has)? To be honest, this strikes me as a bit worrying.

    Given the subject matter, I really hope they get away from the “Combat and everything else” trap that most games fall into. Frankly, I'd rather have something like Hopes and Fears rather than Stats and Talents as the centerpiece, and a focus on cooperative play where there are tangible rewards for working in harmony — a “whole is greater than the sum of its parts” sort of thing.
    I'm not super sure that I think more freeform stats like Hopes and Fears (which I assume are meant to be filled in individually) would work great from a mechanical point of view when it comes to younger kids. After all, with Body, Mind and Charm you at least have some die to roll whatever you're trying to do. I you get to pick fully on your own, there's a risk you'd not have any dice to do something with. The solution would be for the parent to let them do that anyway, and then you remove the point of just not having an all-covering base system.

    I do agree with you that I'd love a Harmony mechanic that incentivises team play, though. That feels like a mechanic you could absolutely build an RPG around.

                                        S6E3 - The Gift of Maud Pie

                                        This episode felt much more accomplished than the opener. Some of the scenes felt very true to character, and you could see how the episode dealt with the themes associated with both Pinkie (laughter/celebration) and Rarity (generosity/care about others), united by a common point that touches them both (gifts), but didn't actually play them "straight". The episode was about it, but didn't typecast the roles. I thought it was very well produced. The script was also significantly better than the premiere. FiM really is at its peak when the characters are the focus, and here, they got to take some space and their personalities could shine through.

                                        Maud is a very effective foil to Pinkie Pie, and yes, she does have some killer lines, but I'm frankly more impressed with Rarity's performance. Tabitha st. Germain routinely kills it, and this is no exception.

                                        There's a lot of continuity happening right now. It's good, but it also kind of annoys me that it doesn't matter more. It's like there are things happening in the background but it won't really affect that show on a character level, which I wish it would since (again) the characters drive the show. Then again, I guess not every series can be Game of Thrones.

                                        Like, this episode was just good. I'm much more hyped for season 6 now than I was last week!
