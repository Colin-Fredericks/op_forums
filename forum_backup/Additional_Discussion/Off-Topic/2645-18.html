                                                Tangent thought: So, the Star Wars RPGs put out by Fantasy Flight are pretty nice. They have their faults, but they're beautifully illustrated and have some interesting ideas.

                                                With this new trailer out and me on a Star Wars binge, I thought about what they could do after they've published the third game in their Star Wars line, Force and Destiny. For those who don't know, FF are publishing three games in the Star Wars universe in a manner similar to the World of Darkness method, being that they are based on a common ruleset and overarching setting (with crossover compatibility built in), with setting details and mechanics unique to each line.

                                                Two lines have come out so far: Edge of the Empire, focusing on Smugglers, Bounty Hunters, and other such vagabonds in the shadows of the galaxy; and Age of Rebellion, featuring those brave souls in the Rebel Alliance battling against tyranny and oppression. The third currently in Beta is Force and Destiny, following the Jedi who survived the purge and struggle to protect a galaxy that is trying to forget or destroy them.

                                                I was considering what game lines they could do after covering the rogues, rebels, and ronin of the galaxy, and I came up with five answers:

                                                1. A villains book: let players see things from the Empire's perspective, perhaps as ace pilots, spies, officers, or perhaps even the oh-so-rare competent Storm Trooper. Show the fans why someone on the inside would choose to fight for a power that seems so monstrously and transparently evil from the outside.

                                                2. The Clone wars: turn the clock back to the prequels, sending the PCs into battle alongside clones fighting against droids. Explore the politics behind the corrupt and crumbling Republic and the self-serving Separatist factions, and show the Jedi when they were still recognized as the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy.

                                                3. Go further back to the Old Republic: the Expanded Universe has been declared non-canon, so look on this as an opportunity to do what you want with the franchise. Case in point: The Old Republic era featured in the excellent Bioware games was formed by the company choosing to look way back into the setting's history so they could have plenty of room to do their thing. So give the setting that's brought about two successful RPGs and a MMORPG another look; it deserves it.

                                                4. Go into the future after the war: the opposite of using the Old Republic, look at the after war period and use the trilogy everybody likes as the foundation for your own contributions. There is a TON of well-received after war fiction, like the Thrawn Trilogy or the Legacy comics that you can use as fodder for the line.

                                                5. Tie-in with the new series: This one's pretty obvious, but if people like the new movies, than follow the Star Wars tradition and cash in on that lovely merchandising.

                                                And come on; you know you want to see the stats for that Sith light-broadsaber.

                                                So yeah, there's my two cents. Heck, it's Star Wars, they'll make money with whatever they do as long as they put effort into it.

                                                I'm feeling bluuuueeeee~
