In fact, here is an official video of my brother's speech that day in DC if any of you guys are curious to see it. It is a very good speech.


                                                        But it did get me thinking about a very saddening statistic about the up-and-coming generation known as Homelanders (also known as Generation Z) and how a lot of sociological polls indicate that they are growing up to be more conservative and right-leaning than previous generations, and I think I know why.

                                                        The PC crowd of my generation has become such stifling moral authoritarians that it's now seen as "cool" and counter-cultural to be right-wing for the first time in American history. Prior to that, youth counter-culture was typically left-leaning.

                                                        I hate how the Millennial PC Crowd has essentially tainted the idea of liberalism and leftism for an entire generation, and I say this as a Millennial myself.

                                                        I'm an old-school Union Democrat who believes in a democratic republic, mixed-market socialism, free speech, free expression, trade unions, and individual liberty.

                                                        I am fiscally liberal and socially libertarian (if you aren't harming anyone, do what you will) and I supported Bernie Sanders in the 2016 Primary and Jill Stein in the 2016 General Election instead of supporting crypto-fascists like Donald Trump or neoconservatives in sheep's clothing like Hillary Clinton. I'd rather "throw away my vote" on a third-party candidate than support either Orange Berlusconi or Grandma Nixon.

                                                        And I hate that people like myself are getting lumped in with whiny upper-middle class dyed-hair hipster college kids who unironically identify as Anarcho-Communist, complain and bitch about anime and video games, demonize geek culture while simultaneously appropriating it in the most shallow manner possible, throw bitch fits about innocent stuff like Halloween costumes and anime, and cry "racism", "sexism", and "transphobia" over the most trivial and innocuous of things, which in all honesty trivializes actual racism, sexism, homophobia, and transphobia.

                                                        Don't get me wrong, racism and sexism are still major issues today and we need to do something about it. But these college hipsters whining about pop culture and other geek stuff are essentially like Don Quixote fighting the windmill.

                                                        Meanwhile, corporations are killing our planet and exploiting innocent workers and treating said workers like human livestock. Women do not get equal pay for equal work, and the prison-industrial complex is way out of control.

                                                        Our civil liberties are being suspended and destroyed by the War on Terror, the War on Drugs, and the surveillance state (the NSA is essentially reading from the playbooks of the KGB and the Spanish Inquisition), police brutality and racial profiling have gotten out of control to an insane degree, race relations are at an all-time low, and income inequality is spiraling out to insane and horrifying levels, while violent hate groups are causing violence in the streets and innocent people are getting killed.

                                                        The country is sinking like the Lusitania, and these spoiled college hipsters are too busy bitching about video games and anime, and calling for a revival of media censorship of anything they deem "problematic" and also advocating for communism, while failing to do anything about the real-life actual issues that affect modern society.

                                                        It's post-modernist slacktivism at its absolute worst.

                                                        I'm all for socialism and sensitivity, but communism and PC censorship can go fuck themselves. There IS a difference!
