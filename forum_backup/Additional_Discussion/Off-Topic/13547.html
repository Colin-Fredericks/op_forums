    In Which I Rant About Sex and Stuff

    11-18-2013, 03:34 PM
    I seem to recall there was a thread on the old forums in which sex was talked about, so I figure it's a safe topic here as well; I shall no doubt be informed if I'm wrong. That said, fair warning: if you don't particularly care for reading about other persons' thoughts on the more intimate sides of life, then you should probably not read further. 'Cause there will be that.

    I have debated with myself for some time whether I should post this or not, and it seems I have come to the conclusion that I shall. I post it here because I feel that this community is among the more open ones that I'm part of, and also one I'm feeling fairly comfortable around, which isn't the case for sites more dedicated to the issue. Before I introduce the main point of the thread, let me give you some background information.

    I am a 26 year old male, I still live at home, and I am a virgin. All of these conditions have their respective causes, some intricate and complex (why I'm still living at home, which I think is much due to passivity brought on by insecurity and bullying in school compounded by being socially awkward and having a weight problem), some rather more straightforward (why I'm a virgin, see "still living at home") and some obvious (why I'm 26 years old, for which I blame the inevitable passing of time and my incessant insistence on not dying). I don't hate my virginity. I don't, as some people I have known, feel it is something that chafes and which it is imperative to be rid of as a matter of course. I'm comfortable with that label.

    However, this isn't to say I don't want to have sex. Believe me: should the opportunity present itself, that virginity will go right out the window. "Woosh", will it say as it plummets towards the street below and is shattered into a billion orgasmic pieces on the pavement of intercourse. I think sex is something beautiful, something natural, something intimate and fun that two people can have together for mutual enjoyment. So why haven't I just sallied forth and had it, then?

    Well, to begin with, there are the things I was talking about earlier: living at home, my general social insecurity and my body issues. Those were my reasons for a good many years. The last few years, though, as I've started to come to terms with at least parts of the social insecurities, a certain other reason has started to make itself known in my head. It's a reason that's kind of getting procedurally generated as time passes, like in an evil spiral that goes downwards towards eternal unsulliedness.

    The new reason I've not had sex yet is that I've not had sex yet.

    At my age, people have had sex. Not everyone, I know (from personal experience), but most will. And I'm kind of... worried I'll disappoint. Sex is a skill, you learn to have it by having it lots. Teens and early twenties are the periods for starting, that's when people don't expect much out of you but enthusiasm and a willingness to explore. That start-up period is over for me. People in my age range are more experienced and expect more of their partners as a rule. This problem will only compound as time moves on.

    Okay, that was some background. Still with me? I'll now come to a rather more practical situation.

    An old, if not so close as he once was, friend of mine, who is not a virgin (oh no, not at all) has shared something with me. He knows a woman who has, to him, expressed a desire to sleep with a virgin. As he told me this, I was overcome by discordant emotions. On the one hand, hell yes! On the other hand... I've never met her, I don't know her at all, and I am certain that at some point, he has slept with her. That's not a problem as such, but I'm not sure I like the idea of having someone else, even a friend like him, so... entangled in my first time. I'm not desperate. Yet.

    On the third hand, I feel like this is an opportunity. A chance to actually learn. Maybe ideas of the "first time" and how it should and shouldn't be are best left unheeded; after all, the first time is almost always among the worst ones, right? Because you're not actually any good at it yet. Maybe this is the opportunity to gain some momentum that can serve to build a base of confidence in later endeavours. That's what I keep thinking.

    I can't ask you what you think I should do, of course. Only I can decide that (but if you like, please tell me what you would have done!). Hell, I don't know if the whole thing will even come to pass; rumours like these have a tendency to get rather overstated. What I would (finally) like to talk about is this: from a purely sexual point, how would you feel about entertaining a "newbie"? Do you feel it would frustrate you? Or do you think you'd find it enjoyable? If someone you could imagine having sex with asked you if you'd help them to learn to give oral sex (which, for the record, I very much would like to learn to do, wink wink, here's my number ladies), how'd you react? Or do you have any other thoughts on the matter?

    In any case, it's been rather cleansing to write it all out like this. If you got through all that text, here's a cookie for your effort: ( : : )
