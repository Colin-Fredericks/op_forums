                                                            It occurs to me that I should probably lay out an ideological stance for me:

                                                            Like, my own personal take on things, which includes beliefs and disbeliefs, tend to play second when I'm any sort of position of power concerning what a person experiences or not. Like, as a minor example, I think Christianity is horseshit and that it's continued presence in society contributes a fair amount to it's ills* and will happily shit talk it on the street after a warning-but if you give me someone who feels their faith is important to them as a part of my gaming group, I am neither going to play a character or run a game that is going to tread on that fact. Out of game, I'm going to find out more of what they're comfortable with, and I will play exactly to that hilt, but while I think there should be more free and open talk out of game than in-game, as a member of the party, I still feel it important to take care of the person in there. If, for any reason, the player's faith becomes a problem for the table at large and it isn't resolvable after a lot of out of game discussion and compromise on my part, I might then ask that person to leave the group,** but I'm not going to tread on that person's faith in anything that resembles a capacity of authority in game, and only push my luck as I feel is appropriate for the individual out of it.

                                                            My problem with this conversation, as it's developed, is that this angle reads, no matter how you phrase it, as "I don't care if someone tells me this hurts them or not, I don't want to have to adjust my behavior to accomodate that" which really just reads as "I will hurt you in this way if I want to." Regarding the specifics about fantasy racism and how it relates to real world problems as such, I'm always fascinated to learn where the ideas in my head came from and learning how to do things another way (not only because I'm keen of social awareness, but also this tends to lead to different and innovative fiction that might not even lose the power of archetypes, which I'm also really keen on), but for me the problems of "Well, does this come from somewhere/is this a real thing?" is less of a logistical reality to catalogue and more a failure to take responsibility for the fact that This Person Is In My Group And Therefore I Am Responsible For Taking Care of Them. Like, let's say I shared Nyru's opinion about how this isn't a problem.*** I would still not make it a thing in my games, because you just don't do that shit. It would still read to me like "I care more about doing what I want then making sure you're all right", which reads "I will hurt you in this way if I want to."

                                                            I have a lot of intellectual argumentation, emotional empathy, and personal damage against pretty much anything that evokes that read. At best, I don't feel like courtesy stays on the table for people who make that statement into a hill. I feel like that forms a basic line for what constitutes an intolerance that must not be tolerated. It goes double for gaming groups, which I just straight regard as My Domain.

                                                            When you're in a gaming group, you take care of each other. If you're not willing to do that, for any reason, you shouldn't be in a gaming group.

                                                            So, yeah, this flat refusal of "It's not a thing!" is unimportant. If someone raises it as a concern, either take it seriously or seriously converse with that person that this table may not be right for them. Anything else reads terribly and shouldn't be done.

                                                            *If anyone wants to discuss this, take it to another thread, please.
                                                            **And, in fact, this has happened-had a friend Tony who started wielding his faith as character motivation that fucked things over in-game and attacked players out of game. It boiled over and I kicked him out of the group. He expected people to follow him! They did not.
                                                            ***But being blunt, it is.
