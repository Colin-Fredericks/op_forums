    If you want the negative side, think of how the brain is a collection of semi-specialized processing networks, all working together and yet all at the same time. And now consider the myriad range of effects and cross effects those sub networks have on each other, and how a simple chemical or associative change can cause all this other work and difficulty.

    Now remember that we're moving towards using this as a model to make self improving and replicating AI's which are expected to become reasonably sentient within our lifetimes.

    Imagine what would happen if the traffic control brain for a city got depressed and decided to take up the electronic version of drinking to feel better.
