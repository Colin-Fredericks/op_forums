                                                I don't think the Equalists are supposed to stand in for Communism, Fascism or Socialism. I think they take cues from some of those positions, but trying to figure out which one they're supposed to stand in for seems like putting square pegs into round holes.

                                                The Equalists are essentially a response to an issue that exists only within Avatar's world. If a large portion of society had superpowers, then simply possessing those powers puts them on an elevated position above those who don't. In a society that treasures the power of Bending to the degree that the Avatar world does, then the ability to Bend lends the individual in question a privileged position in society above that of the average non-Bender.
                                                It's worth noting that Bending doesn't seem to be the be-all-and-end-all of status in Avatar's world. The Earth King in the Last Airbender wasn't himself a Bender and neither was his daughter in the Legend of Korra. Still, a Bender always has something that a non-Bender lacks, something that society sees as extremely valuable.
                                                The Equalists are the response to this issue, not any of the social problems of -our- world.
