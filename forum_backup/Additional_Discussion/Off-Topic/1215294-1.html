        Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post



        So wait, people are upset that Thanos only wiped out half the universe instead of all of it? Because if I'm not mistaken, that's exactly what he did in the comics, too.


        And look on the bright side, this might give Marvel the opportunity to bring new characters into the movies, such as Captain Marvel, or Adam Warlock. Maybe we'll get to see the Kree Empire, or the Skrulls?
        No. Wiping out half the universe is not the problem. From what I hear, the finger snapping thing is actually pretty cool.

        The problem is that his plan(wipe out half the universe's population) to solve the problem(resource depletion leading to overpopulation leading to war and strife leading to suffering, ANAKIN) when the resources he is collecting (the Infinity Stones) allow for an easier, more heroic alternative(infinite cosmic power used to create infinite resources therefore eliminating any conflict due to overpopulation) makes him look like a fucking idiot. It doesn't even look insane, just incredibly stupid.

        They wanted to make Thanos the hero of his own story, with a goal and ambition worthy of someone worth all god damn 19+3 movies. Cool, storytelling 101, solid villain base to work from, modern edgy audiences really love that shit. But by doing so poorly, they have neither delivered that, thereby disappointing the movie goers, nor did they deliver the admittedly goofy but still elementally brutal and powerful comic book version of the character, which pisses off the comic book fans. And that latter one, while simple, probably would have been a thrill for movie audiences as well, just simply because he is so elementally overpowering as a presence and an effect.

        They promised a villain worthy of a franchise and gave us a narratively bankrupt caricature. While I understand the presentation is fucking stellar, the simple fact of the matter is that everything about him falls apart in seconds with an iota of thought, and that sours the experience for people looking back and hardens the hearts of people going in, making future investment into the movie that much harder.

        ​I'm sure it'll be fun. But I don't plan on taking Thanos remotely seriously.

        EDIT: Like, by contrast, comic book Thanos:

        Plan: Wipe out half the Universe Population
        Goal: Get with that sweet, sweet Death.
        Resources: Infinity Stones, aka Infinite Cosmic Power

        At that point, the only question left is "Why only half", and when you are cosmic genocidal asshole with infinite cosmic power killing people to impress that sweet, sweet Death, you pretty much have every right to answer "eh, who gives a shit" and their only recourse is to just go with it because what are they going to do on that point with you.

        ​There's a contrast here.
