            I've been running a Pathfinder game for, shit, I dunno, let's say a year and a half now. When fifth edition came out, I decided I was not going to run Pathfinder any more. I presented the group with the options of converting the existing game to D&D, abandoning the existing game and switching to a superhero game I wanted to run, or me stepping out of the GM's seat altogether. I had to have this discussion twice, once with three of my players and once with the other two because it takes a fuckin' act of Congress to get the five of them together at any given time, and three players said they'd do whatever but were interested in the superhero game. The other two were only interested in continuing the current game, which is understandable given how much time they've put into trying to figure out what's going on (short version, suicidal dragon is gonna take the whole multiverse with him by feeding the world to the Negative Energy Plane and tipping the scales of reality in after the world), so that's what we're doing. Done deal.

            But I just can't stop thinking of stuff for the superhero game.

            Today, while driving back to the office from my final patient of the day, I had an idea for an adventure. It'd last five, maybe six sessions. In it, industrialist Simon Machina, who is secretly the villain Doctor Sinistrad Scientifico (did I mention it's a Silver Age game, the kind where every line of dialogue is punctuated with an exclamation point?) found that his plan of buying cheap residential areas and razing them to build factories to construct his army of killbots got him brought to the attention of some very bad people who had been profiting for a long time, in more ways than one, from the existence of those slums: the Vampire Mafia. They profited from the usual criminal activities sold to the poor to make their lives bearable as well as from the food source of disposable, unloved, second-class citizens, so they decided to solve the problem by killing Machina. They carried out their plan well.

            Unfortunately, they didn't know the killbots would rampage when their creator disappeared. Tracking the murderers, they began killing vampires, and a turf war erupted between robots and vampires. You'd think the killbots would have won in short order, but a fact was uncovered that even the vampires themselves didn't know: vampires represent a spiritual corruption so immense it even affects robots, causing their AI to spontaneously generate viruses that spread from machine to machine. The heroes, who would have spent a session or two trying to foil gang wars, would find they were racing to save the world from complete technological shutdown when the vampirism.malware.exe spread from the robots to Dr. Sinistrad Scientifico's secret array of communication satellites and beamed the virus to every receiving machine in the world.

            Fortunately, when things seemed at their most grim, a secret hope would emerge from the shadows: a single priest, holding a sanctified USB containing blessed anti-malware program created by the Knights Templars' R&D department. If they could upload that program to the satellite array, the virus afflicting the machines could be cured and their insatiable need to cannibalize other machines would be halted. After that, it would really just be a matter of mopping up the aftermath of the killbot/vampire turf war...

            ...Except that Doctor Scientifico survived the attack, after a fashion. The heroes should have known that; the killbots would have found his body otherwise. Newly anti-Christened as Doctor Sinistrad Vampirico, the mad scientist intends to continue his plans for world domination, now with all the power of the grave backing him up as well as his fortune and his industrial complex and his genius, operating even more subtly (and more literally) in the shadows with his cabal of vampbots feeding on new killbots fresh off the assembly line to glut on their power and become stronger as a result.

            I laughed so fucking hard as new details kept throwing themselves into the story, so hard I actually became ashamed of myself for being that amused at my own jokes (it implies an enormous ego). I want to run this so fucking bad, but I won't have the opportunity to do so.

            I can't put this in the Hate thread, because I love the scenario too much. I can't put it in the Love thread, because I hate that I'll never get to run it. I'm putting it here instead. I hope you guys enjoy it.
