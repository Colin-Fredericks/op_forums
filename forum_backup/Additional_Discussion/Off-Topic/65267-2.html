                        I've started reading Hawkeye, since I liked very much that creative team's work on Iron Fist (even if the art is not really my cup of tea, I appreciate some aspects of it). It is very good, but I can't shake the impression that both Hawkeyes (the comic features both Clint Barton and Kate Bishop) are written as being a lot less competent than one would expect. At one point Kate Bishop is overpowered by two mooks. Two aliens soldiers? No. Two ninjas? No. Two common thugs? No. Actually, it is two male nurses that take care of two old formerly famous hippies.

                        Meanwhile Clint gets easily defeated and beat up just about every issue, often by the same gang.

                        I'm reading the new Black Widow book as well, which is also very good and has the advantage of showing her struggling with the challenges before her, but in a way that actually shows why she is an Avenger. That said, Hawkeye has more and better comedy and is doing more creative stuff with page layout and showing the same thing from multiple perspectives over multiple issues and neat gimmicks like that.

                                            I'm pretty much a comic extreme guy. Yet I'm weird in some respects as I tend to enjoy most changes done in the movies (loved IM3's take on the Mandarin for example.) Roleplay on one (about to be two) Super-Hero MU* as five different characters (nine technically.) And I pretty much get as many of the new releases that come out week to week as I can (try nearly every book for atleast five issues)

                                            Right now my favorite books are Amazing Spider-Man (formerly Superior), X-Men (no adjective), Mighty Avengers, She-Hulk, Guardians of the Galaxy, Harley Quinn, Wake and a new book called United States of Murder Inc,

                                            I used to love DC but I have found 90% of the new 52 to be unpalatable to me as most of it just feels like a bad copy rather than a new and fresh version of things. I really did love Animal Man, Aquaman and Justice League Dark for a long time but they slowly soured to me. Still reading Suicide Squad but that's ending. And really Forever Evil was just WAY too much and too long, It felt like the old days of Marvel where the crossovers were just so all encompassing and you needed everything like Infinity War and Infinity Crusade.

                                            Marvel is a really mixed bag for me now a days, it tends to be what I read the most of (see my favorite list) but I'm not entirely happy with Bendis's direction with the X-Men and absolutely hate his characterisation of Emma Frost and the relationship (ended by Bendis) of Emma and Cyclops. Though to be fair Kieron Gillen started the issues I had with it all with his seeming instance on an Emma/Namor OTP. I really hated AvX though I was glad that the event actually mattered it had consequences. So often in the Big Two, events have little repercussions outside of 'a single character died' or 'you don't like this guy anymore.' AvX did change the landscape for Marvel NOW and while I don't like all of that. I give it credit for doing that. The biggest problem I have with Marvel now these days is... and I never thought I'd say this, I miss when Marvel "wrote for the trades" when every story was done in 4-7 issues. Now Uncanny Avengers, New Avengers, Young Avengers(finished book) seem to be writing for longer stories. Yet, the stories instead drag on instead of the glory days of Claremont and Byrne where it was a built epic.

                                            Outside the Big Two I read very few books to be honest. I tried Zenoscope Wonderland books and Liked them until one time I was reading a trade for the second time and went "This is pinup-art with a perverted horror theme. Pass." I'm reading the Star Trek ongoing book and the Doctor Who Comics. I adore the Walking Dead comic even though I'm starting to feel like Kirkman writes too much for the the shock value for the end of the issue, the story is still very good.

                                            So yeah, I'm a minor comic demi-god. And a walking wikipedia on the subject. I'm looking forward to talking more about it.
