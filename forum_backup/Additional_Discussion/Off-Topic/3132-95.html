                                        The forums are geared towards providing a polite environment for the discussion of Onyx Path worlds and games. The rules and moderation policy are designed to support this.

                                        The reason for that is to welcome as large a variety of players as possible, which requires us to make sure people don't rub up against each other the wrong way. All mod decisions are made with this in mind.

                                        Since we want to be welcoming to as large a group of Onyx Path players and potential players as possible, we're obligated (as past history shows) to moderate attacks directed anywhere on the political spectrum. If you feel your particular political preference is being slandered, report it, and we'll probably take action, either privately or publicly.

                                        The Off Topic forum has a somewhat wider remit in terms of what can be discussed here. Being Off Topic, it's harder to go off topic, so you'll see less (but not no) action for derailing. But it's bound by the same rules as any other part of the site, and we're not going to change that.

                                        We do not usually ask users to grow thicker skins. We do not ask them to grow up. We welcome them to come as they are, if they abide by our rules. And they certainly do that. If they clash, we ask them to modify their behavior. This generally works.

                                        Perceptions of "true reality" vary, and so we don't typically make moderation decisions based on how realistic we consider someone's worldview to be, or how realistic their debating partners feel that it is. Again, the rules and moderation are aimed at maintaining peace on a gaming web site focused on the worlds of Onyx Path.

                                        While I'm open to discussing some nuance and taking recommendations via Contact Us, the broad strokes of those policies, most of which were carried over from the White Wolf forums, have been effective in maintaining the peaceful atmosphere they were created to. We're going to keep them.

                                        You've all been invited to the virtual managers' office to talk with Ian and me. If you'd rather discuss these things between yourselves here, go ahead for a little while, but be warned that I haven't declared this thread a rules-free zone. Rather, I'm cutting a bit of slack because I recognize emotions are running high for a couple of posters.

                                        I anticipate this being my last post to this topic.



                                        Addendum: The directive not to post on "policy" in Trouble Tickets didn't mean world politics (an Off Topic subject), but rather forum and moderation policy. Trouble Tickets is a forum exclusively for technical issues. JimB was entirely correct about that.
