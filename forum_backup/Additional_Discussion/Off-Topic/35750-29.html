                                    Hello again everyone! Recently I had a request from David Tveraas to make some character sheets for some games he'll be running at Gen Con based on the video game Vampire the Masquerade: Bloodlines. He needed a 5th Edition sheet in the Bloodlines style, but then he was thinking he would like some sheets based more on the system of the video game, but that could be used for 5th Edition and V20/Revised.
