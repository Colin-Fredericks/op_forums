    Vampire: The Masquerade: Bloodlines Retrospective

    07-04-2016, 07:37 PM
    This is a thread for discussing the Vampire: The Masquerade: Bloodlines game. I know we're all hoping for Onyx Path to license a sequel to Obsidian (I hope it's set in Chicago personally or New Orleans) but I wanted to talk to you guys about it. What was your favorite part of the game? Least favorite part?

    For me, it really was one of the best RPGs ever made.

    I mean, the last third sucked but that was just by comparison to the original. It's a shame they never did a console release.

      Favorite would be the mood and feel. There has been no video game that makes me feel like Bloodlines makes me feel. It's that combination of menacing and sexy. It's moody, it's exciting. It's a night of clubbing, it's standing on the street at 3 am deciding where to go next. I have never played a video game that felt like it, and TT games by other GMs has been rare.

      Honorable mention to NPCs. So.Good. I love the characters in this game. Only a HM because it is far from alone there.

      Least favorite is combat. Terrible. Few games combat I have played in the past 20 years have sucked as hard as Bloodlines.

      Dishonorable mention the last...4th or so? The end of Chinatown and the rest are weak. Well, Giovanni mansion is not bad, but other than that. It becomes a killefest, with combat as the low point.

      Still, it is an amazing game. My #3 of all time and only non Bioware in my top 4.

          Obviously, from my avatar(and name), you can tell I'm a huge fan of the game. Easily my favorite game of all time(and Redemption is not far behind it). Almost everything about the game is amazing(except for the combat, which is merely OKAY).

          The real world setting and story, which has yet to be as well accomplished by any game before or since it's release, is top notch. Downtown L.A., Santa Monica, Hollywood(my favorite), and Chinatown are all a blast to play through. The feel of the characters is awesome; many of which are some of the greatest NPC's in the history of gaming. Smiling Jack, Damsel, Nines, LaCroix, Ming Xiao, etc. hold up extremely well, have voice actors who poured themselves into the roles, and are all different from each other.

          The soundtrack is fantastic. God, I could go on and on about this game. When Bloodlines 2 is announced, I'm not entirely sure if I'll be able to contain myself.

          "Steel isn't strong, boy. Flesh is stronger."

                          I remember the thing which struck me most about Vampire: Bloodlines and that was the fact it was able to combine successfully so many different kinds of creatures/elements from the vampire line's history. They had the Anarch vs. Elder conflict, the Camarilla vs. Sabbat, the effects of Humanity, the Masquerade's importance, the inhuman treatment of ghouls, Gehenna, Thin Bloods, the Madness of the Malkavians, and even the spookiness of the Tremere. About the only things missing was the Followers of Set, Assamites, Inconnu, Golconda, and Ravnos.

                          Hell, if Pisha was a member of the Tal'ma're then they're in the game too.

                          It was a packed-packed game.

                          But none of it felt forced either.

                          A short top list of what I liked:

                          1. The Anarchs have never felt more relevant and likable than they did with Nines' group. I suspect part of that might be Heather's doing.

                          2. The Thin Bloods are awesome and they're the only vampires I've ever wanted to protect. Lily and E are just such nice vampires, much nicer than you are really.

                          3. Everything which needs to be said about the Hotel level has been said but it's still awesome.

                          4. The Kindred of the East I've always hated but they actually work here, especially when contrasted to the less Orientalist Chinatown.

                          5. The Society of Leopold actually didn't look like a complete bunch of losers this game. However, it turns out, they WERE a complete bunch of losers which makes their genuine danger all the better.

                          6. Vivi, you evil tease. I admit, I prefer the mod which allows her to dance for you but there's something to be said the signature Toreador is all flirt, no action.

                          7. Heather Poe's horrible state as a slave but you totally wanted to keep as one too. It was...terrible.

                          8. Prince La Croix is the greatest douche since Prince Lodin.

                          9. Jeanette is vampire Harley Quinn and who doesn't love Harley?

                          10. The werewolf "fight" was awesome. I love how they kept werewolves being impossible to kill for normal vampires.

                          11. Brother Vick's mission is just so off kilter but foreshadows the Sabbat. Are they Sabbat? Baali? Just some insane Caitiff? Path of Evil Revelations worshipers? We'll never know.

                          12. Humans actually are relevant in the game! There's cool human characters like Fat Larry, Venus Dare, and so on.

                          13. Killing Andre is as close to killing Vykos as you're probably going to get.

                          14. Smiling Jack really is a character. The choice of Bender as his VA is perfect. He's also so much more dangerous than he appears and as sneaky an Elder as the rest of them.

                          15. How few people got the swanky penthouse during the first run because La Croix is such an enormous douchebag.

                          16. Caine is almost literally your co-pilot.

                          17. Chunk!

                          18. The Malkavian Run.

                          19. The Nosferatu Run isn't that bad either.

                          20. The entirety of the wereshark hunt and your demon hunter friend Yuki.
