                                        On fanservice in Kill la Kill:

                                        Kill la Kill has plenty of man-service to go with the fanservice.

                                        While there is objectification of women going on in the show, Trigger was perfectly willing to objectify guys too and is rather more equal opportunity than the vast majority of Japanese shows are. By the end of the show everybody had stripped down multiple times and gotten plenty of shots of their bodies. The camera tended to linger a bit more on the girls, but then the guys tended to run around totally butt naked rather often. And both the fanservice and manservice were often directly called out and commented on by characters within the show.

                                        Plus the whole idea of clothing, nudity and being comfortable with who you are, were major themes in the show, so it's not like the fan/man-service was totally gratuitous, existing only to get more bucks. It actually did have a place in the greater context and narrative of the show. The reason why Ryuuko's outfit looks the way it does makes perfect sense once they explain how things are actually working.

                                        So I think it generally evens itself out, and I wish more shows bothered to work their "fanservice" into the story as well as Kill la Kill did.


                                        On Kill la Kill vs Gurren Lagann:

                                        I found Gurren Lagann tended to slow down quite a bit in the middle of the two major arcs. Kill la Kill pretty much had the same level of energy going from episode 1 to episode 24. While it didn't hit the highs of Gurren Lagann, it didn't hit the lows either. I did find Gurren Lagann more unpredictable. In Kill la Kill most of the twists I saw coming, though they tended to do them in ways that I wasn't expecting.

                                        I find the two shows about equal all told, and I think Kill la Kill was a fantastic show for an original series from a brand new animation company. Between Little Witch Academia and Kill la Kill, I can't wait to see what Trigger does in the future.


                                        On Kill la Kill and Exalted:

                                        I think Ragyo fits eerily well as a Reclamation oriented Infernal.

                                        Satsuki is definitely a Zenith.

                                        The Devas (+ tailor guy) fit really nicely as a circle of Dragon Blooded.

                                        Takarada is the hero Creation needs.

                                        From an out-of-game perspective Nui isn't bad as a Raksha - the way she shows up and starts warping the story, and the way she feels like a character who is a little bit outside of the story but trying to get into it (which mirrors her relationship with Satsuki and Ryuuko). She also seems to have the "Craziest" powers. It also fits a bit with what happens in the final episode where:

                                        She uses Assumption of the Person's Heart to fuse with Ragyo.


                                        Ryuuko is the only one I have a tough time deciding on. She fits pretty nicely as any of the Exalted types, though her penchant for bleeding everywhere does make me think about an Abyssal.
