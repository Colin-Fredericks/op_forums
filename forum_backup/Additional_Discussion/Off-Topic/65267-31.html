                        New X-men's Academy springs to mind. I prefer it more than Runaways, though that's a good comic too.

                        Basically it just follows a bunch of students at the X-men academy, most of the focus is on two squads, each having an advisor from the standard X-men, Hellion and the Hellions advised by Emma Frost, and the New Mutants advised by Mirage. There are fights but it's not really focused on that, outside of the little arguments they have with each other.

                        It does get a bit dark at the end, unfortunately.

                        Not a Marvel title, but under the same idea I'd have to really recommend the Intimates, which is just fantastic.
