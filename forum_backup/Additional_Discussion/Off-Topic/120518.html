    Star Wars EU: more Exalted than Star Wars?

    04-27-2014, 05:12 PM
    On another forum, there's an arguement going about the meritw of Timothy Zah's Thrawn Trilogy. In jt someone said this:

    Well... it is only really the gold standard because compared to some of the other books that followed, it was not terrible. They weren't perfect... nothing is. But while it had jedi... it didn't revolve around the jedi. It had political intrigue, underworld adventures, and military operations. Later books often revolved entirely around magical samurai space wizards and their slap fight amongst themselves over which group of a handful of privileged godlings based on a very nebulous binary belief system over who gets to rule over the countless trillions of others who don't have a magic laser sword.
    Almost sounds like he's describing Exalted there. What do you think? Does the EU feel mkre like Exalted than what Star Wars once was?

                                      The history of Exalted is way more complex and interesting than the one in Star Wars, in both 1st edition and 2nd edition.

                                      Really, on a big scale, the only thing that matters and is distinct is the duality between the Light side and the Dark side. If you remove the Force, you get a generic sci-fi setting. If you remove the Force, you make the setting into a generic boring setting without any deepness. If you remove one element from Exalted, you still get Exalted.

                                      Furthermore, the Force is like the generic Good/Evil in DnD. You serve one, or you serve the other. Objective moral forces in the world make for a boring story.
                                      Star Wars actually is very similar to Warhammer 40K. Both are one-trick ponies. Warhammer's main staple is 'Never ending war and misery'

                                      - If you play a Star Wars you are a Jedi/Sith and the story is you being tempted or you redeem yourself.
                                      - If you play Warhammer, you Burn The Heretics.

                                      That's all about it for these 2 settings. They are not made as a coherent working world, they do not deserve to be compared with Exalted.

Not saying that you can't have compelling non-Force using characters, engaged in good drama, just that there's no reason those stories couldn't be told in some other property.

                                              And the overall story of SW is Good vs. Evil, expressing itself through the Light and Dark Sides of the Force, which uses the Jedi and Sith to play out this conflict (who themselves use the Rebellion and Empire as tools). That kind of simplistic, dualistic tone doesn't really work for Exalted, except maybe for some parts of late 2E.

                                              Having said all that, I'm not gonna join the hate train for the EU. I can't claim to have read ALL of them, and I certainly have issues with their obvious flaws, but on the whole, I think it is a solid continuation of the series, and have enjoyed most of the ones I have picked up, esp the ones set during the Old Republic and Clone Wars. I'm glad that Disney just announced that they aren't discontinuing the series, which I was almost certain they were going to do, deciding to respect a continuity that so many fans invested so much into! Reveals good things about Disney's stewardship of SW, hopefully...
