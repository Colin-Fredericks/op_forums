I could be born with chronic illnesses, into an abusive family and many other painful circumstances. The concept of Nirvana or some other transcendental state that doesn't require the approval of a terrifying cosmic judge sounds decent, but I might be too weak and foolish to ever reach it.

        My whole life, everything I am and care about, it all feels like a frail soap bubble in a universe of needles. I try to enjoy activities for their own sake, live in the moment and minimize regrets, but it all amounts to an unfunny joke that no one is listening to.

                                        I'm into my sixth month of psychoanalytic treatment, after a year and a half of being pinned to the bed with crippling depression. Two weeks ago I had my first breakthrough, collapsing and crying during the session. My parents, especially my mother, were abusive, then I was bullied, then I was cheated on by several girlfriends and abandoned by my closest friends when the symptoms began manifesting.

                                        I've done some incredible progress, I've experienced some couples of days being entirely lucid and symptom-free (including my eating disorders which were under control during that time). The last three months were the toughest, I dropped out of my only college course (Calculus) because I couldn't get out of bed in the morning. My friends and family didn't make this any easier, saying I was being a loser that didn't want to face the facts of life and move on. I am not one to dismiss suggestions just because they sound like what an asshole would say, but the days I woke up trying to get the most things done ended up in disaster because failing to do even a couple of things from the total I had planned for that day was devastating.

                                        Now the battle against the symptoms always takes place on my turf and becomes manageable after an hour or two after waking up. Still, some memories and traumatic experiences that have resurfaced from the unconscious, linger around and I have to have a good crying/outburst session before I can get on with my day when I remember them. Mostly stuff about my mother and grandmother, who in their shared hatred of each other used me as cannon fodder, and my current partner's mother who hated me because of her own mental issues (she was the second child, her own family never accepted her or her life choices, so she now does the same to her daughter and her choices which included myself).

                                        Some days are truly great. I came back from a dark place I never thought possible to return without medication and on such a short time frame. Other days, the symptoms return like an itch I can't scratch unless I analyze myself.

                                        I am preparing a Vampire session that will include a Malkavian vampire "treating" a child that experienced trauma similar to mine. I feel excited for the opportunity to confront my past in such a way, although a little bit scared, as the "treatment" involves making the child relive the trauma until she finds the strength to stand up to the parents. If anyone's interested, I will post results (i.e. how I felt) after the session.

                                                        So I've been working on my MPhil in psychology, with the intent to upgrade and complete a PhD. So I need a publication to upgrade. OK, good idea anyway, so I spend a few months gathering and analysing data and writing up an article. Then it got rejected, several times, the last fairly soundly. Fair enough, so I figured I'd finish the MPhil then apply to the PhD - inefficient, but it gets me there in the end. Then it turns out I need a publication to complete the MPhil.

                                                        Then it turns out I can upgrade without a publication, but my supervisors don't want me to for no good reason, and I need their OK to do it. Instead, they want me to get the unpublished article published and write the last third of my 30,000 word thesis in 6 weeks - something they agree is impossible. Fine, I can do the thesis in that time, and I'll just do the best I can with the article. Now it turns out that my thesis is not 30,000 words as expected, but 50,000. (Oh, something to note: I present these as changes, but the administration insists at every point it's always been however it is at that moment.)

                                                        While all this is going on, my social issues have been getting worse, so I finally decided I maybe should get checked for Aspergers (my mother who works with special needs kids, including autistics, insists I am, while I just say I'm an awkward jerk). So I look into that, and to get checked out as an adult will cost me between $600 and $1,500, with no government rebate. Meanwhile, I haven't been able to make much money from my usual source - share trading and investing - because I've been too busy with thesis stuff.

                                                        In more minor news, I've been trying to lose weight, which means my diet has been weight-loss shakes and boiled vegetables. I recently started including more normal-person (yet throughly checked for healthiness and portion-controlled) food, and seems even that is sufficient to stop what small progress I had made so far. So it's back to boiled vegetables.

                                                        Now, I am well aware that even with all this going on, my life is still really, really easy compared to a lot of you here. But seriously, I think I'm starting to approach my limit. What the hell am I supposed to do, when the world seems insistent on preventing any forward movement in any way, shape or form?
