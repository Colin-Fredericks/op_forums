                                                        I suppose it's worth building on this.

                                                        I'll spare you the whole spliel about how our ability to make snap decisions about if something is good or not is deeply ingrained in us because of blah blah blah, but it's important to note that judging if a movie is going to be worthwhile or not is an expansion of the old survival instincts. Mental health is a big thing, more so now than it was ages ago, and the main thing to keep in mind about that is that media is the main thing we ingest, and a lack of catharsis from an experience is diminishing, to say nothing else about what happens from ingesting a movie. On top of that, a brain can't throw up. Much as we would like to remove, say, Revenge of the Fallen from our minds, it's stuck there, complete with anything that disturbs and nauseates us to it. That may be a bit of an extreme comparison to, say Robocop, which I expect to just be mediocre, but the point stands.

                                                        On top of that, making judgments based on the context clues provided before a piece of art's release is a big part of the development and refinement of our personality. I don't have much to say on that right now, oddly.

                                                        Now, it is fair to say that we should be open to alterations of the original opinion. I thought the Lego Movie was going to be a huge freaking pile of crap, but since it's sitting pretty at 97% on RottenTomatoes, I have to concede that there's a real probability my initial impression is wrong. If i get paid this weekend, I intend to see it.

                                                        But it's better to have an initial impression of somethng to challenge than not judging a piece before hand and just going in on it. It is good to make judgments, it is not good to absolutely hold on to them, at least as far as media goes.

                                                        I've avoided a lot of shitty stuff other people have suffered through because I decided it looked like shit before hand.
