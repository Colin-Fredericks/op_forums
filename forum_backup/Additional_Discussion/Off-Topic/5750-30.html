                                            Welp, I finally saw the new Transformers...and was pleasantly surprised!

                                            Pros:
                                            • All the silly, stupid, juvenile "humor" that I so detested throughout the series...pretty much gone. There is humor in it, but it doesn't overshadow a much darker, more serious film. It may be telling that the one obvious comedy-relief character dies a horrible death early on...
                                            • Frank Welker, baby! His "Galvatron" didn't get as much screentime as I'd have liked, but at least he wasn't a criminal waste of a character, like Shockwave was in the last film.
                                            • Much more focus on the Autobots, esp Prime, as opposed to Bumblebee, who's more of a minor figure this time.
                                            • Fewer, less annoying human characters. Mark Wahlberg really carried this part pretty well, and it was refreshing to see an adult interacting with the Autos rather than a kid. Kelsey Grammar pretty much OWNED this movie! And Bingbing Li was pretty sweet and cool, and definitely nice to look at...
                                            • Transformer designs that (gasp) are actually clear and comprehensible, more like the designs from Transformers Prime, tho still with the innards hanging out somewhat. I just wish Prime had kept his G1 cabover look...
                                            • Instead of making each movie more or less separate, they are clearly going for a more connected myth arc this time. Something about the Transformers' creators (who we briefly glimpse) wanting them back...
                                            • Lockdown: One of the cooler original characters from more recent franchises, very interesting to see how they portray him here. Something of the "Boba Fett" of Transformers, he may well be a breakout character!
                                            • The action was pretty over the top and exciting, but that was never a problem with the series...
                                            • Optimus Prime, brandishing his golden sword while riding Grimlock into battle? Hell yeah!!
                                            • Rainbow Dash cameo! Nice...
                                            Cons:
                                            • Cursing Autobots. To be fair, this can make sense...They've been around soldiers most of their time adapting to Earth life, and soldiers cuss. Still rubs me the wrong way, though.
                                            • It's looong! I'm normally for character development, but there's a lot of scenes that could have been trimmed. Esp some of the chase scenes involving the humans.
                                            • Human action scenes. There actually was a good reason for some of them, but...I'm just here for the bots, man.
                                            • Flag City. Here's a drinking game that'll have you wasted before the first hour is done: Take a swig every time an American flag is waving in the shot! Seriously, what was up with that?
                                            • Product placement: Pretty blatant at times. Interestingly, one of them seems to be for a Chinese drink...
                                            • The man-made Transformers' transforming sequences...just, ugh.
                                            • Explosions! Well, I knew what I was getting into...At least one scene is so damn bombastic, it borders on self-parody!
                                            • ...Wasn't this the exact same ending for Prometheus?
                                            So, on the whole, there was more to like in it than to dislike, at least for me. It seems that someone, if not Bay himself, is listening to fan complaints and addressing them. If a creator/studio makes an honest effort to improve their product, I see nothing wrong with supporting it...
