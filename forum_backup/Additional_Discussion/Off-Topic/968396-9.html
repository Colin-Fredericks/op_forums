                                So I recently found out one of my close friends and gaming buddies IRL is a furry.

                                His fursona is a wolf-shark hybrid and is also a pirate, which is actually kind of cool.

                                I briefly looked into the furry fandom and dabbled a little bit into it way back when but honestly, I bowed out of it all and kept my distance until just this week.

                                Without going into too much detail, this avoidance was due to a mix of being legitimately disturbed and creeped out by some of the seedier and more shady aspects of the internet furry fandom (as well as the more overtly politicized parts of the fandom) and even more importantly, fear of social stigma both online and IRL.

                                My friend and I got to talking about furry fandom and why he got into it, and he said he liked the sheer amount of creativity and the wide variety of fursonas and OC's in the fandom, which I must admit is kind of cool. He's not opposed to the sexual side of the furry fandom but he doesn't really involve himself in it either and he keeps his distance from the creeps and drama queens in the fandom

                                I like this guy and he said I might like the furry fandom if I know the right people and know which circles are cool to hang out in. According to him, the IRL furry fandom is actually pretty chill and laid back and most of the stuff I found problematic is largely confined to the internet and the larger conventions. So, now I am reconsidering my past reservations on furries...

                                I do have some ideas for historical OC's that could be classified as "furry" or "funny animals" such as Roman Legionnaires, Celtic barbarians, Ninja, American Civil War soldiers, Vietnam-era G.I.'s, Medieval knights and soldiery, World War I doughboys, Tsarist Cossacks, Cowboys, and even Paleolithic hunter-gatherers.

                                Sort of like "funny animals of military history"

                                Might even write some fiction or start a role-play with a theme around funny animals and furries, although I'd like to keep it fairly clean for now (Nothing wrong with sexuality, but it's not what I am aiming for with my projects)
