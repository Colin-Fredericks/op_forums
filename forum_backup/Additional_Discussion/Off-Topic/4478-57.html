                    How much things can change and how quickly.

                    I don't much want to talk about my depression; it's an old, boring, and not made any more palatable by repetition. Suffice it for the purposes of this story that this has been a rough time for me, between the darkness of the season (people need sunlight) and the fact that I've been doing a lot of driving (it's a Pavlovian thing). Yesterday was pretty bad. I wish I could say it was because I found out the Missouri state Democrats, who had a majority in the state legislation, had spent twelve million dollars campaigning to make jumping jacks be the official state exercise of Missouri while actively refusing to deal with bills supporting gay marriage because this is an election year and they can't risk doing the right thing if that will piss off homophobes, so we are ultimately paying them to do nothing at all in order to perpetuate their ability to stay in office and do nothing at all. Missouri contributes less to the federal government than it takes in assistance programs, human rights are lagging behind the rest of the nation, and our elected leaders refuse to be effectual because that might be hard; and I wish I could say that's why I spent all yesterday dreaming of just going home to bed and finding fourteen hours of oblivion so I wouldn't have to deal with such a hopeless world, but I'm too honest to say that's why. I do understand that it was just an excuse my mind seized on.

                    Well, regardless of the nobility or ignobility of my reasons, that was my plan. At some point on the drive home I realized I probably ought to eat something, though, so I modified my plans to include a casserole of pork chops and wild rice which would spend about an hour in the oven and give me sixty minutes of time to kill before I could drag my sorry carcass to a senseless sleep again. With nothing better to do while the pork baked, I got online and started poking around, just following whatever whims hit me or, more likely whatever links I came across.

                    I have seen the video before, when it originally aired on TV. I forgot it exists, but it's probably three years old (the SuperPAC bit was the year before the last election, right? Something like that, anyway), so I don't feel too bad about it, but the point is, watching this again was like watching it the first time, and something about it had me laughing so fucking hard I immediately forgot everything else. I just sat there, laughing until my face ached, laughing until hot tears rolled out of my eyes, laughing until my stomach hurt. Existential despair and old-fashioned depression just couldn't sustain themselves, and the rest of the night was pretty much okay for me because of one ninety-second video.

                    Go figure.
