                        Yeah, I get a little tired of the whole "Disney is EVIL!!" nonsense thrown around, as if whatever they do must be badwrong just because they're a giant, faceless, soulless corporation...when there really is no such thing (except for legal purposes). All such groups are made of *people*, with all that that implies. Some of them are jerks, but many are decent and cool, and often they're all caught up in cross purposes trying to follow whatever their vision of "good entertainment" is. Sure, the bottom line is $$, as it is for all financial institutions, but that doesn't somehow mean that they can't produce quality products along the way! Avengers was well worth my money, as are almost all of the movies tied into it, and reports about Frozen say the same.

                        As to the comics, I dropped Marvel a bit ago, but from what I hear with my ear to the ground, it sounds like Disney's pretty hands off, there. Some comics will copy things from the movies, like costumes, but comics have always done that, usually willingly. Probably the biggest change I've heard about is that mainstream Nick Fury now looks like the Sam Jackson version of the character (although apparently that's a new character, not actually classic NF)...

                        I'm pretty sure Joss is on lock for Avengers for the forseeable, but I'd love to see him on this, too. Maybe if the movies tank, they'll throw him over there...Who else would be good? What kind of a job do you think Kevin Smith would do?
