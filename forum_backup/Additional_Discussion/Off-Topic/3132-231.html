                                                    Borrow someone's pulse oxometer -- one of these -- and keep track of your blood oxygen. The screen labeled %Sp02 is the amount of oxygen in your blood, PRbpm is your pulse rate. Normal blood oxygen is 95% to 99%. If it drops and stays below that, go back to the emergency room or urgent care, whichever one's closer. If you have a build up of phlegm (mucus) in your lungs and that's not going away, even with your blood oxygen is normal, go to urgent care. Do you have a nebulizer? This?
