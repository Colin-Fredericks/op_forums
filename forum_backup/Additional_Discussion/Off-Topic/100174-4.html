    Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post


    So people who avoid Kill la Kill because of the fanservice are sort of like people who don't want to watch Madoka Magica because they don't like magical girl shows - since magical girl shows are made for little kids and are about cute girls doing cute things.
    I would dispute this, at least when you add that extra note of specificity; Madoka Magica, in its implementation, doesn't actually have cutesy stuff.

    Kill la Kill, while clever, does actually have some of the visuals, so there's still stuff one has to look at that might still feel offputting.

    For myself, it's stretched a little too close during episode 9 when there's stuff like Ryuko hanging by her straps, which are stretched right out, and when she's being pummeled with gratuitous shots (and yes, a person monologuing does draw the attention of the shot somewhat, but it's still present, and you'd still have to see it).

    Kill la Kill frames shots to have these visuals a lot less than plain fanservice shows would, but a bit more then I'd like, and enough that I am fully understanding of it turning off anybody who just plain doesn't like to see those things.

    Really, the better comparison to Madoka Magica is for people who just plain don't like visuals of stylized cute girls in frilly outfits*, no matter what they're doing. That's what I ultimately found to be why my siblings couldn't get into it; they're just not compelled by something that looks like that.

    I can hardly disagree with that, and there can be even more behind being repelled by fanservice visuals than that, so it's something that I just cannot dispute.

    * I'm also driven to recall that somebody on this forum once said that, for all its quality, they ultimately didn't like it because they don't like seeing shows of young girls being put through traumatic experiences or having to go through extreme self-sacrifices, and that's another approach for "I don't care how good it is or clever it's being, I can't actually bear watching it".
