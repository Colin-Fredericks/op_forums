            The problem is the IO balance. In very broad terms, it's possible by the input of energy into a system with the right catalytic substances to convert low energy waste materials back into their prior forms. That would largely eliminate the waste products. The problem is that it would require only enough matter of various sorts going into the system as were leaving the system by other means... meaning you'd largely be reduced to drinking mineral water type drinks. Converting all mass to energy isn't going to be feasible for far longer, that requires far more advanced and compact tech than we're likely to see in the next 500 years, and by that point humanity is largely outmoded.
