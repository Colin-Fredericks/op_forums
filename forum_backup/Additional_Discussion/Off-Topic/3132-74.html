Now, you not only have to pre-buffer against commercials (which, alone, I understand -- like I said, they gotta make money somehow), *but* you also have "kick-out", where if you have more than 2 set points buffered at the same time, one of the videos will kick-out/"error", so prepping a set is just about impossible. (I checked with a more knowledgeable friend and he confirmed that, yeah, it's not my computer -- it's how Youtube works now, specifically often citing music links for the express reason of *not* being "the poor man's mixer" anymore) I know that's a relatively small thing to bitch about, given that people have real problems in life (your's truly, as well), but I *so* miss being able to do that... I remember in the previous decade being able to set up up to an hour on my (then) computer, pre-buffered/cued, and get compliments for my DJing... now, with only my (comparatively) limited computer power (only have my netbook now), I can't do it anymore, and being a starving artist (read: full-time broke person), I can't full-commit to the library of music I'd like + upgrade to a laptop that can do what I want. (And while I am more than willing to use "shared" music on the likes of Youtube -- which stays there, and therefore isn't "illegally copied", at least to my sense of ethics -- I don't illegally download stuff, so the money issue is.. well, an issue... )
