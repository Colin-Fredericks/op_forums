                                                            This is difficult to explain, but...hunger is a part of what I was describing, but it wasn't the only part. Gluttony--the desire to eat for pleasure--was another. Habit was a third. When it comes to my desire for food, physical need is really the least part of it. Mental need often pushes me to eat when I'm not hungry, or even when I'm so full that eating makes me uncomfortable, so plugging myself full of food isn't the way to go. I need to just be good, wait for my stomach to shrink, and work hard at instilling new habits into myself. I will backslide, of course; everyone does, and I'm not so special I will be the one person who gets onto a diet and never ever slips.
