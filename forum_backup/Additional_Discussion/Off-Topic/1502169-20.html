                                                            Eh? Sports are part of humanity. We've been doing sports for all of recorded history, across all cultures, and thus probably for far, far longer. Sports are useful even on a very detached level because they help us relieve a lot of instincts we don't let out much thanks to large scale civilization happening. The vast majority of the real problems in sports is... actually kinda like all the problems in the arts: it's entertainment done by people that happen to love what they do that lots of people love watching. Then a bunch of rich assholes worm into this and want to turn the love of the game, and the love of watching the game, into all the money for them. Once it becomes more about lining rich people's pockets with even more money, instead of the game being played, it always makes things worse.

                                                            Curling is one of my favorite sports to watch. I think the fact that nobody gets full ride scholarships to university for curling, nobody's getting massive paychecks in professional curling, etc. is part of why. There's enough money so that the best of the best can compete at the top levels for the world to enjoy... but not enough money for corporate greed to start fucking it all up. There's a level of good sportsmanship you don't see in sports where so much more is on the line. It's a sport where there isn't a ref on the ice.
