                                        I was thinking about the upcoming Dr. Strange movie the other day. Dr. Strange is a confounding character to me, but one I've always wanted to be interested in, so that's what got me thinking about his movie: I wanted to figure out what it could be that would make me interested in it.

                                        The first thing to pop to mind was magic. It is fairly important to me that magic be a completely irrational process. It bugs me when magic is treated like an observable and repeatable process, because that makes it just another form of science and Dr. Strange becomes just a reskinned version of Iron Man, with pink zaps instead of white repulsors and a cape instead of rocket boots but both ultimately doing the same things the same ways.

                                        No, I thought to myself, if I had my druthers, magic would be based entirely upon the needs of the moment and how they intersect with the magician's ability to believe in the irrational, so that the effectiveness of any spell will be based on the purity of the magician's focus, with stray emotions and distracting thoughts detracting from the magic's effectiveness and/or altering the spell in ways the magician didn't intend.. Each spell--I don't know how you'd do this, I'm not a cinematographer, but in the movie I'd like to see, each spell would be like a snapshot of Dr. Strange's frame of mind at the instant he cast the spell. In fact, the primary conflict would probably not be between him and Dormammu, but rather, that would be more like a metaphor or a backdrop for the real conflict, which is his journey to regain some measure of faith after years of practiced cynicism from his trade and the bitterness of absorbing his injuries.

                                        Then I realized that you just need to cram the Technocracy in there somewhere and I'd have done a pretty good job of describing a Mage: the Ascension movie.

                                        Still not sure how I feel about that.
