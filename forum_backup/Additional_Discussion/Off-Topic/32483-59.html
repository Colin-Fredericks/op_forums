            Hey everyone, I had posted only once here but now I want to share an update on how my analysis is going. It's been 7 months since I started psychoanalysis, despite having psychologists both in my family and in my group of friends who suggested CBT treatment (or even just Cognitive) and tried to prepare me for the possibility of medication (after I spent a year and a half in catatonic depression, plus an eating disorder lingering since I was 17, in which even outbursts like crying were difficult to muster).

            Today I'm on my third day in a row in which I wake up symptom-free. I had some days like these before, but none lasted for more than 24 hours and if you recall my earlier post, the most difficult part was waking up. I even recalled my dreams, which before I did not (perhaps repressing them?). I bought tickets and I'm finally visiting my parents again (after finding out they played a large part in planting the seeds of my symptoms, I avoided them).

            All this progress feels very organic, building upon earlier progress that now becomes more frequent and longer lasting, with no use of meds whatsoever. While I hope the three day streak lasts and starts a new chapter in my life, I no longer fear the return of my symptoms since it is now ridiculously obvious I can defeat them.

            Stay strong lovely people, I will be frequenting this thread more often now that I have my energy back, and offer all the advice I can.
