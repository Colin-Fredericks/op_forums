    Alita: Battle Angel

    Overall I liked it.

    That said it's...nothing mind-blowingly special. To the outsider, it is a pretty typical Pinocchio-esque story: an artificial, sentient and naive construct learning about the world and people she's surrounded with and learning what it means to be human. Still, while it's a familiar kind of story, I do feel like it's well-executed that I don't mind. Rosa Salazar's performance as Alita can't be understated to maintain the innocence but earnest desire to do good and doing whatever it takes to do so.

    Also one of my favorite scenes (that was hinted at in the trailers)

    Was the bit where Alita plucks out her own heart and offers it to Hugo. In context, Hugo is going on about how he doesn't have enough money to get to Zalem, and Alita declaring that she'll do anything for him...which includes selling her own heart. Which the way how it's described, is the equivalent of an Iron Man arc reactor.

    What's great about this scene to me is two-fold. One, it continues to reinforces Alita's own sense of selflessness, that she is more interested in maintaining the well-being and happiness of others except for herself. The second, is that it contrasts that with her own inhumanity. A few scenes before this, Hugo declared that Alita was more human then anyone else he ever knew. And yet here, we see her demonstrating that she...very much is not human. That no, no human can JUST open up their chest and pick out their heart, and then put it back in as if nothing happened. And no human is willing to be as selfless as Alita is here. So while she may look and act human she isn't one.

    A subtle, kinda creepy, but also oddly beautiful scene.


    And as hinted by the reviews...yeah the action is great. All of it is genuinely exciting and thrilling to watch. It also allows me to revel in the sweet death of close-up shaky can, since all of the action is clean and legible, and you can tell what's going on at all times. My personal favorite being the second fight with Grewishka.

    As for Alita's eyes...again, I'm probably in the minority, but I thought in action they were fine. In motion and as you watch the movie you get used to them. I only found a few moments where I felt like the CG was getting stretched at the seams. Other then that? Not a big enough issue to take you out of the movie.

    At the very least, it's leagues ahead of blue-Smith-Genie's look.

    Still haven't made a whole lotta progress into the manga, but what I've read and seen on the screen has been pretty accurate.

    I'd say it's worth a look.

                    Lego Movie 2: The Second Part

                    Hmm.

                    Now don't get me wrong, it's definitely a good movie and its faults really isn't because of poor craftsmanship. And there were enough jokes I got a good laugh out of.

                    It just...doesn't give me the fizz as the last one.

                    The message at the heart, while well-intentioned, I could see coming from a thousand miles away...simply because I saw the ending of the first movie and could get where they were going with in this movie. While this movie does try to portray it as emotional and heart-warming it...the actual content of the message isn't said in any interesting way.

                    Also, between this, Deadpool 2, and to some extent Wreck-it-Ralph 2...I think I really am starting to get tired of 4th wall breaking, self-referential meta humor. It doesn't really feel like anything different or innovative is done with it...there's just more added. And I'm starting to feel like it's only there to cater to adult nerd fans. Which, well, I don't know about you guys but for me I'm not so amused by it anymore.

                    So yeah. Take that for what you will.
