                                            I have mixed feelings about Godzilla. (spoilers follow)

                                            It was flawed. The main character... I couldn't really care for him. But I could have cared, with a few minute changes. There was, unfortunately, no salvaging the faceless military people.

                                            The scenery work had moments of brilliance. That abandoned city, overgrown with plants, straight out of Life After People - except for the secret military base at the center? That was a fantastic setting; I would have liked to see a kaiju battle (or a military vs MUTO battle, more probably) set there, in the ruins of man's civilization.

                                            The transition of the Godzilla character, from a pure force of nature, a moving hurricane that kills countless people as it cuts a path to its prey, to a hero we cheer for as it rips down the beasts that are the actual threat, was a bit... clumsy. I would have preferred if he had remained the first - the monster we need, but who is still incredibly dangerous in that it does not care for us.

                                            The trailers had sold me an anxious, frightening atmosphere, mankind trying desperately to struggle against a single monster. The film proved entirely different from what I had expected, which was jarring; when it came time for that shot of the soldiers jumping from the plane into the red clouds, with that haunting music... it felt forced. It had been a magnificent scene in the teasers, but it was out of place in a movie that had already entirley switched to "giant monsters fight each other! awesome!"

                                            But by god, seeing that flare of blue light and these scales light up one by one, knowing what was about to happen... I could have cheered.

                                            Bits and pieces of Exalted setting.
