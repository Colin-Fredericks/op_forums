                                        Originally posted by BurritoMage View Post
                                        Have we learned nothing from robocop
                                        The big lesson from Robocop is that off-loading social functions best served by an accountable public service to the private sector is inherently corrupt... which no, a lot of societies haven't learned.

                                        The second biggest lesson is rejecting the idea that cybernetics strip you of your humanity, and even though they explicitly tried to do that to Robocop, he does his best to rebel against the programs that limit his human side. Also something that's still an uneasy part of a lot of fiction that deals with cybernetics.

                                        I think the sad thing about Robocop... is that despite being violent, he's still in many ways what we want out of a police officer: applies the law fairly without bias or prejudice (even if his program would rather he didn't sometimes), ignores political pressure to drop cases (and even telling him to drop a case for inappropriate reasons is something he can submit to court), always has a body-cam on that is never shut off, and follows use of force regs even when he doesn't want to (one of the main baddies realizes that once he just surrenders and drops his gun, Robo can't keep attacking him).
