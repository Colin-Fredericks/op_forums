                            Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                            I hate this system isn't there anyway I can become Canadian?
                            Good news bad news. On the one hand, Canada is fairly welcoming to immigration in general. On the other, if you're just up and relocating you generally need a job offer or to meet the skilled worker qualification (this comes down to a degree or significant experience, and it's a bonus if you speak french and a lesser bonus if you speak other languages)

                            As an american, it's actually easier for you to get temporary work visas up there. If you do pretty much anything in the technical or medical fields it's little more than getting a job offer and filling out a visa application at the border and paying a few fees.

                            You can also take the marriage route. That would require you to be in a relationship and marry a man, woman or misc.other from Canada.

                            However, one of the catches with all these routes is that Canada has been clamping down the last few years on people that move in specifically to cash in on social services. It's overblown and more than a bit of a dick move, but from what I've read what will often happen is that immigrants are either denied if they have significant health concerns, or in the cases where denial is difficult (such as marriage) they make the person doing the sponsoring legally responsible for any government assistance that person might claim in the next several years.


                            That having been said, and taking with it the grain of salt that I'm talking about numbers/situations from a decade ago, outside of the invisible amount paid on my taxes, I was paying about $80/month as an independent contractor (business above a certain size are mandated to to cover insurance costs)... IIRC that was providing me with a plan that covered new glasses every couple years, 80% of the prescription costs and the majority of the costs of dental care. So the actual health care costs out of pocket for people up there are reasonable.
