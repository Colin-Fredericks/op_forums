    I'm Done

    04-12-2017, 02:52 PM
    I can no longer comfortably support Onyx Path.

    I've enjoyed these games for years, consider many of them near and dear to my heart, and for a while really hoped to be picked up as a freelancer, but from here on out I'm no longer touching WoD, CofD, or anything else they put out; I'll receive whatever's coming from Kickstarters I've already funded, and that's it. I might come back to playing Mummy or Promethean with my gaming group at some point in the future, but for right now everything is so tainted that I'm pretty damn close to just walking away and playing other things.

    The entire situation with new White Wolf (not only the actions of Martin and Zak, but how threads rightly objecting to those actions on this forum were locked and deleted) pushed me pretty close to the edge, but what's got me finally upset enough to walk away has been everything recently with David Hill. To have the Changeling 2e developer so quickly disavowed by his former peers has been incredibly hard to see, and the prospect of the game he's been previewing for literally years now no longer being the game we'll be getting, in favor of largely new and chopped up content and minimized credit due to him (and according to some, lessened pay), is honestly disgusting. He's done good work, and deserves better than this.

    So I'm done. I'm not going to be buying any further Onyx Path content, and I'm probably not going to be playing much more of it either. It's been nice interacting with some of you, and I've got pleasant memories.

    This thread will probably get locked or deleted.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.
