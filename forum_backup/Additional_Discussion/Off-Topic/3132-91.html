                        1) Just because YOU want something, doesn't mean that we should always have it in stock: Some people look for items that we no longer carry, likely because they didn't sell well enough. But people apparently think that, just because *they* have always used X, that means that it's wildly popular!

                        2) Just because it's in the ad doesn't mean we must have it in the store: There is literally NOTHING in a store that can't sell out within one hour, so don't come in, esp *days after the sale started*, and demand something should be there! Like I said, other than the above, customers are actually pretty decent, respectful and even fun. But if you catch yourself doing the above, please be mindful of the people serving you, and the fact that it's not their fault if what you want isn't in!
