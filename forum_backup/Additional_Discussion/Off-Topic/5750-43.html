                Originally posted by Shadowflame View Post
                Interstellar

                The movie is superficial when it comes to science. Of the big three sciences only physics was represented..kinda sorta well enough. Astronomy not so much. Biology was non-existent (I kept wondering when decontamination was supposed to kick in after visiting a potential planet, but I got nothing.)
                The tech seems represented in the form of two "robots" primarily AI programmed with human emotive responses. (TARS was the main with Honesty factored in at 90%. Diplomacy prevented it from being too honest. Sense of humor factored in at 90% as well, but turned down to 75% because the sarcasm was just a bit much.)
                As it is, humanity is pretty much hopeless and needs a bit of help...from "others" to get out of the galaxy. Those others seem to play out that they may be "us." Not too sure... Surprise cameo by Matt Damon who isn't exactly a good guy.
                This movie isn't for the science guru. It's more a philosophical exercise. There's talk in the movie about fuel and supplies, but it doesn't get specific. Nor does it go into psychological issues that can potentially happen. (I recall reading an indepth plan NASA had for a Mars mission. This movie had none of the things mentioned that should have been.)
                The connection between father and daughter was the main motivation here. And this is the emotional issue that kicks in.
                I could have easily forgiven the slipshod science for the sake of presenting a story, but I wasn't too entertained by the story. (The one guy stuck in the ship in orbit near a black hole. One hour roughly on the planet nearby equaled 7 years on that ship. They got stuck on the planet a little longer and that guy hung in there for 23 years. Really? Yeah, I got that he "hibernated" in and out, but I needed more)
                So, a science movie was a miss. An entertaining story was also a miss.
                I wanted to like this movie but I found little to like. (Hathaway in space and not one person suggested repopulating a planet with her? Well, I was thinking it)
                While it wasn't perfect, I still enjoyed. The science was enough to keep me interested. I loved the Robots! They were great, and pretty original in form.

                I was more just glad this movie wasn't a remake or sequel, and it was nice to see something original, if a little derivative of some similar space movies...
