    Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
    I see it both ways and for me it depends very much on the group I'm running for which route I'd take.
    Sure, it's totally fine as a playstyle. I just prefer characters having what they could and could not do (and know, have, etc.) to be well-defined from the start and through the game. We talked about narrative games and mechanics elsewhere, so you know my stance. It's perfectly fine, it's just not my preferred method.

    It also reminded me how I still didn't worked out my punk elf pistol-adept's second contact in our current Shadowrun game. It bugs me to no end. I confess, I'm not very good at making up NPCs connected with my PCs. I either want them to flesh out fully and thus got disappointed if they don't get a meaningful role, or I just can't put any real effort into them.



    Yeah, a lot of non-4e fans aren't seeing the 4e-isms in the PF2 previews. The thing is, I think people who like 4e and people who don't like it view 4e very differently. What I see as bringing back one of the great ideas 4e had, you might not connect to 4e at all.
    Hmm, that could be a thing, yes.


    In the meantime, I'm reading the fighter preview.

    - AoO: everyone could get it, but fighty has it from the start. Great, makes sense. Everybody should be able to take a suckerpunch, if the enemy is distracted, but capitalizing on small windows of time is something you have to learn. Average people just can't react that fast. Fighty has it as a part of the fighty core training. So far, so good.

    - Special feats for AoO: hmm, okay, I could get behind that, fighty gets some nice ninja moves.

    - Weapon mastery: okay, it's in-line with what I said above how I see the fighter as the, ahem, weapon master, I like that. I'm a bit wary about weapon groups, I hope we'll get some tools to specialize further in specific weapons. I'm just not comfortable with being equally masterful with "swords", but that's my HEMA nerd side talking.

    - Perception boost: nice! Also makes sense. A professional fighter has to have good situational awareness.

    - New Power Attack: thanks Cthulhu, at last a power attack version I like! But what die? Additional die of the wepons's damage die?

    - Quick reversal: I'm seeing some serious dual wielding shenanigans here...

    - Shield warden: good stuff, though I still think bigger shileds should give some level of passive protection.

    - Shooty-hooty: I just hope other classes, like the rogue and ranger could get those cool shooty feats too!


    Overall, I like it very much! Making the staple fighty stuff more mechnaically interesting and giving Fighty a lot of other cool stuff, while focusing the concept on "the best one with weapons, without magic" is great.




    If nothing worked, then let's think!
