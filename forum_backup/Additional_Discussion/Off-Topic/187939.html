    07-04-2014, 09:53 AM
    Hey, we know it's kinda late notice, but we kinda forgot today is America's birthday, and we're throwing a little party. Nothing fancy; just a chill get-together with some friends. We'll barbecue something, have a beer or two, and if you want to stay 'til dark, we'll be shooting off a few fireworks. If you guys wanna come, you're welcome. Don't worry about bringing anything (unless you want a salad or something with dinner, because let's be honest, we in America are not big on salad and you can't really trust us to bring one).

    If you can't make it, no big deal. It's our fault for not inviting you sooner. We just wanted to let you know we'd be glad to have you.


    Love,
