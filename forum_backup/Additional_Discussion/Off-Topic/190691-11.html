                Still working on Arc's second one, here's Jeddy's.

                Like so many of these stories, they begin with you taking a wrong turn. You stepped thru the wrong archway while out for a walk. The ground started to get muddier, the sounds shifted to what seemed to be a river or stream flowing briskly. As you finally found the stream, you noticed it was quite still. The sound was a dual line of swordsman on the other side, clashing their swords together high above in an arch. Behind and above them was an extraordinary large figure, face obscured under a large and wide helmet of sorts. He beckons you forward thru the archway of swords and you move carefully thru, ducking beneath their blows as best you can. The Keeper makes a simple offer: Pledge yourself to me, tend to my men’s wounds, and you will leave here with skills that will dazzle the most skilled healer. Give me 10 years and I will give you a lifetime of adoration. You look it with abandon and saw war and death and wounds and poisons and disease and every method of madness that could harm the body and a few you never imagined could be true. 10 years turned to 20. The work was too good, too tempting. Every time you thought you could leave, the men would beg you to stay and present you a new battalion of wounds to heal. 20 years turned into 30 and they never ran out of wounded, and that’s when you realized that was the game. They never would run out. They would never let you go until you were too bent and broken to serve them anymore. In the dead of night, you finally saw an opportunity. Another wound needing a rare herb. You went out to get it yourself, and just kept walking. Eventually, you found the river, noticing this time, from this side, it was a river of blood. Weary, fingers numb, back aching, knees weak, feet sore, you kept walking. On the other side, you found the way back thru the mud and the trees and the bushes. On the other side was a friend you hadn’t seen in 30 years looking exactly the same. “What took you so long? I’ve been waiting here for HOURS! Let’s go.” Outside, you look the same, if a bit tired looking. Inside and to the other Changelings, they see the Wizened Brewer/Chirurgeon left behind. You’d be a better Chirurgeon if not for the damage done to your body, it’s just too hard for your fingers to work that well or to stay on your feet for that long.
