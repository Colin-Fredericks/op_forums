Five Republican Governors getting replaced by Justice Democrats is worth far more in every way than five centrist Democrat Congressmen being replaced with Justice Democrats.                                     4) Have actual policies ready to go. Not strong policy ideas. The laws need to be written to be submitted for review and consideration in legislatures before the next cycle in them. "When we win, this is the exact law we're going to pass, get on board or get out of the way."

                                    5) Now that the Democrats are a solidly progressive party that's shown it's dedicated to it's populist ideology, grow the party until the Republicans stop having a say for at least a two-term president. Use that power to regulate things properly, replace stop gap efforts like the ACA with a good single payer system (and remind any Republicans that you're consolidating a few dozen programs into one program and making government smaller and more efficient to shut them up about your "big government" programs), and go to work on the big lifts in the platform that are going to run into a need to either go through the Courts or even face a need for Amendments (these are hard, you have to have a ton of political mandate to even hope to make these happen).
