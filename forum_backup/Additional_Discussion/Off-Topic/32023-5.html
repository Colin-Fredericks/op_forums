        Originally posted by Arteliex View Post
        I started my gaming with Vampire the Requiem, and the very next day, Exalted 1st Edition. It wasn't until... about two years ago that I ever picked up or read a DnD book. In fact, because I was so into White-Wolf, I actively avoided it... for some reason, me and my group felt it would corrupt our 'pure' storytelling experience to dirty ourselves with DnD.

        Of course, once we actually sank our teeth in to it, it was actually pretty fun. About a year later, I threw down quite a bit of money on the Pathfinder books, and we've played it often enough. Not as fun as any of White-Wolf's material, of course, but it has its own flavor.

        Still, I started off without DnD, and am oddly proud of the fact.
        Good for you for being open to new experiences.

        Pathfinder is the version of D&D I play most at the moment. It is where I get my fantasy adventure fix.

        I game. This is the way.

                    Actually, I started out playing Palladium's Robotech; then we got into AD&D and Battletech. If you like the Tolkenesque fantasy genre in which you go on adventures, slay monsters and get lots of treasure, fame with out a lot of social RPG situations etc, then D&D is right for you. If you like more RPG while still being somewhat powerful, WW is the pay forward.

                    Since I have read more history books than fantasy, I prefer a bit more RPGing, but I do like fantasy. The one thing I do not like about the Fantasy-genre, is the vast open country-side with no people. Historically, even, "Wilderness", will have people and every mile or so, expect a village with farmers and every one-hundred miles, expect a chief/lord and every 1,000 miles, expect a king.

                            I was roped into a DnD game once, but the game disbanded after a practice session (to understand the mechanics), and a couple proper sessions. I never really read the book as much as I asked my friend running the thing to help me build a character and I learned as I played the game.

                            I am certain the game we played was 4th Edition.

                            I started playing Vampire: the Masquerade (the Dark Ages line to start, actually) after enjoying the computer game Bloodlines. I then played Revised, bought V20 when that came out, ran a few games in turn with friends, and messed around with the New World of Darkness. I don't have much experience with DnD at all - most of my experiences are in homebrew games, Vampire, and Star Wars SAGA. I have been asked to go to a couple LARP games, but the games I have played really don't interest me that much (probably due to being too lazy to swing a foam hammer).
