        So I'm after second week of my COVID19 infection, somehow dangerous conditions now...

        My life partner was little 'covid crazy' WHOLE YEAR - 'Karol, clean with bactericide shoppings!'. 'Did you disinfect the door handles in the car?', 'You are going to leave the house WITHOUT a second face mask?' 'You checked your blood saturation with our pulse oximeter?' We tried to stick to all the restrictions there were mentioned. Do all the smart medicals on radio asked us to.

        I went only to work, our neighborhood supermarket, our apartment and my parents house - WHOLE YEAR, only in those places. I even not partake my friends birthdays cause there were few foreign to me people there. Running RPG online only. We were afraid we can go through COVID badly, despite our relatively young age - I have type 2 diabetes, my partner has poor health and immunity in general.

        And we were somehow right, unfortunately...

        Week before Easter, my new work collogue got me infected. As he is .a Catholic participating in the life of the church - a faith association, etc. - I suspect that he even brought it from there. First week I even was not thinking it was COVID - I was having persistent cough but felt pretty good. After 4 days of coughing I asked my General Practitioner for COVID test, as collogue pointed he has positive - with my result was negative. I was even starting to thinking that maybe I will see family on Easter - but then on Easter Sunday, I got high fever, sore aches and 'superflu' symptoms. From then on, it was only worse and worse...

        Over week later after Easter, and two weeks in to my treatment, I'm just starting to crawl back from place of 'I coughing so much I lose oxygen, my fever is killing me and I'm not sure they will send me to hospital any minute'. Last two days are gradually better and better - I got this 'coughing attacks' 2-3 times a day and quickly stabilize them. Blood Saturation is still on edge on 'you should go to hospital'. And fever I only get once a day now. So it's start to look controlled, finally, at my side.

        And my partner just starting to major symptoms himself - with mythical 'lose of taste and smell'.

        I SSSOOOO MMMUUUCCCHHH want to 'thanks' my work collogue for brought this on us two.
