                                                        Originally posted by AzraelFirestorm View Post
                                                        Correct me if I'm way off base (and many apologies if I offend), but is there some psychological or thematic overlap with the Otherkin community and the Fur community? I'm thinking of a few things that seem like personal gray areas on this subject, when analyzing myself under a furred lens.

                                                        Many thanks for any response!

                                                        To put it in the most simplest of terms for newcomers, a Furry is merely somebody who enjoys the idea of anthropomorphic animals and there are a number of ways in which to explore / express that interest. The most common way is through writing and artwork which, contrary to what you may have been lead to believe, is not always porn related. In fact, I'm given to understand the pornographic side of the furry community is limited to a minority, as opposed to being part of the majority.


                                                        Otherkin, however, are people who self-identify as a completely different species. They don't think of themselves as human, which has resulted in the "I identify as an Apache helicopter" meme that was made to mock and humiliate them. The furry community may indeed house a number of Otherkin who find it an easy way to express themselves in public, but they are not inherently the same thing.
