                                    Alright, well, here's a bit of world building for a Ravenloft themed setting that I've been working on for the last few days.

                                    ---------------------------------------

                                    The Land of Skayuls: One of the many pocket planes created by the Dark Masters to house the forces of evil. The Land of Skayuls is a continent overgrown with hot, tropical jungles and dense rainforests. The ecology of this land appears to be prehistoric in its development, with the most advanced civilizations seeming to be locked within some kind of stone age era of progress. At the center of the continent stands a dormant super volcano, which is responsible for channeling heat throughout the rest of the land using its underground lava flows.

                                    For those who find themselves within The Land of Skayuls, reptilian life rules supreme. The hot, tropical environment is perfectly comfortable for cold blooded species like snakes and lizards. But those of a more warm blooded variety may find it to be stifling and muggy. It is also the reptilians who are responsible for establishing most of civilization across the continent, and have even organized themselves into a mighty empire, ruled over by singular matriarch whom they recognize as Jhaashe, the Lizard Queen. Although she is much loved by her scaly subjects and has ruled the empire for many years, nobody seems to remember who the empire's previous rulers were. Surely, the empire didn't just spring into existence over night, so why is it that Jhaashe is the only documented ruler throughout its history?

                                    Contrary to popular belief regarding the development of the empire's citizens, the people of The Land of Skayuls are not as far behind the rest of the world as one might think. They may not have discovered the secrets of metallurgy yet, but they have encountered rare forms of stone and wood with similar properties to metal. Thus, it is not uncommon to find warriors cladding themselves in armor made from pieces of bark and slabs of rock, while providing equivalent defense to a suit of iron or steel. Even wizards can be found within the empire, fashioning their spell books out of dried bark and ceremonial paint, as a substitute for parchment and magical ink.

                                    As is often the case with many empires throughout history, the practice of slavery is very popular among its citizens. Though in this particular case, most slaves are taken from warm blooded stock, and include the likes of humans, elves, dwarves, halflings and such. On occasion, the Dark Masters have been known to abduct people from across the planes and deposit them within The Land of Skayuls, where they are quickly forced into slavery (if they're warm blooded), or assimilated into imperial culture (if they're reptilian). But for most people living on the continent, they were simply born there as native citizens.

                                    Yet, as great as The Land of Skayuls may seem, anybody who tries to leave the continent quickly realizes that this would be paradise is actually a gilded cage. Beyond the borders of the continent is a great sea that expands in all directions. Anybody who has attempted to sail, swim or fly across the sea has soon encountered a mysterious fog that rolls in from out of nowhere and obscures all attempts to navigate through it. Eventually, the travelers find themselves arriving back on the shores of The Land of Skayuls, either because they got turned around, or the currents of the sea washed them back towards it. Leaving the continent and returning to the outside world, it would seem, is impossible!



                                    ----------------------------------------------


                                    Jhaashe, the Lizard Queen:

                                    Most who know of Jhaashe recognize her as the supreme monarch of The Land of Skayuls, but the true level of her power goes much higher than a simple queendom. In truth, she is a Dark Lord, one of many created by the Dark Masters and imprisoned within her own realm for heinous crimes against the planes. Jhaashe has somehow become convinced that reptiles are the pinnacle of evolution, and wishes to bring all other races under her scale fisted rule. In the material plane, she was was a powerful enchantress, who used her magic to enthrall the minds of others and bring them under her control. But in The Land of Skayuls, she has become something of a demigoddess whom not even the strongest of wills can resist without the aid of magical protection.

                                    When she isn't using magic or her pseudo-deific powers to disguise herself, Jhaashe bares the form of a beautiful (depending on who you ask) female lizardfolk, with features reminiscent to a monitor lizard. The primary scales on her backside are a vibrant orange with dark purple spots. The secondary scales that cover her underside are a pale blue with no other distinguishing marks. Her eyes are almost solid purple, save for her black pupils which are shaped like slits when they aren't dilated.

                                    As is the case with many enchantresses, Jhaashe takes great pride in flaunting her supposed beauty, even to the point of weaponizing it. When presenting herself before the public, she garbs herself in tribal robes that leave little to the imagination, and further accentuates her appearance with jewelry made from precious stones and animal bones. She is often seen carrying a white staff with a headpiece resembling the skull of a tyrannosaurus with a gleaming ruby held between its jaws.

                                    Because of the mind altering effects of her magic, plus some interference by the Dark Masters themselves, nobody knows for sure just how long Jhaashe has been the ruler of The Land of Skayuls. As far as anybody can remember, she has always sat upon the throne and will probably stay there for many decades to come. However, over the course of her reign, she has conceived a multitude of offspring who have risen to prominence within her empire and have become recognized as some of her most powerful servants. The fact that her children appear to come from a variety of different species of lizards also speaks to Jhaashe being a promiscuous queen, who is not content with only having a single mate.

                                    --------------------------

                                    Jhaashe's Children in order of birth:


                                    Prince Vishenk (first son) - A level 18 Fighter (Weapon Master) and commanding general of Jhaashe's armies. He and Botha are both 23 years old, but since he hatched first, he consider himself her elder. His reptilian features are based on a monitor lizard.


                                    Princess Botha (first daughter) - A level 18 Cleric (Nature) and high priestess of Jhaashe's clergy. She and Vishenk are both 23 years old, but since she hatched a few moments after he did, she is constantly reminded of being the younger sibling. Her reptilian features are based on a crocodile.


                                    Princess Tet (second daughter) - A level 15 Bard (College of Swords) and head of distributing information throughout the empire. She is the third born child, ranging in at 21 years of age. Her reptilian features are based on a gecko.


                                    Prince Lirgux (second son) - A level 14 Monk (Way of Shadow) and the head of Jhaashe's secret police force. He is the fourth born child, at 19 years of age. His reptilian features are based on a skink.


                                    Prince Irosk (third son) - A level 12 Ranger (Beastmaster) and grand master of the imperial slave trade. He is Atyss' twin brother, with features resembling those of an iguana and counting in at 16 years of age.


                                    Princess Atyss (third daughter) - A level 12 Wizard (Enchantment) and the apprentice heir to her mother's magical knowledge. She is Irosk' twin sister with features resembling those of an iguana and counting in at 16 years of age.


                                    Prince Iltu (fourth son) - The youngest of Jhaashe's children and one who is training to become a Barbarian some day. Although he is only 10 years old, his reptilian features are derived from those of a thorny devil, which makes him appear no less intimidating than the rest of his siblings.

                                                        Speaking of the Lower Planes, I'd appreciate somebody giving me a bit more clarification on exactly how the fate of the damned works.

                                                        Do the condemned souls actually become Fiends over time, or do they simply use these souls as fuel through which to create more of their kind?

                                                        If it's the former, then killing every villain you come across may not be the wisest course of action. What seems like a lowly pickpocket or bandit one day could come back in the future as a cunning Pit Fiend or rampaging Balor!

                                                        In either case, it seems like killing the bad guys doesn't resolve the issue at hand, but rather serves to make things worse. Fiends are significantly more powerful and evil than anything a mortal brigand might be capable of doing, and every villain you slay is only adding to their ranks.

                                                        Could serve as a reasonable dilemma for those who find themselves in the position of murder hobos who go around killing everything they come across. Maybe at some point in the adventure, they encounter the souls of their past foes, who have all transformed into Fiends of an appropriate level for the setting, and now desire vengeance upon the would be heroes for sending them to Hell!

                                                            Generally speaking dead souls are reborn as the weakest of demons or devils. It will usually take thousands of years for a weak fiend to move up within the ranks of the fiendish hierarchy and begin morphing into more powerful and higher ranking demons and devils. Furthermore, 99% of the time people reborn as fiends lose nearly all of their memories of their mortal lives when they die. And most lower end, weak demons and devils are destroyed by the more powerful ones long before they have the chance to move up the ranks.

                                                            It's entirely possible for a powerful demon to pluck up a weaker demon and charge it up with power in a short period of time, so if the PC's kill an NPC and you want them to come back it's possible but also doesn't really matter. There's effectively an infinite number of fiends in the lower planes, so if the players kill a few hundred or even ten thousand enemies in the course of their adventure that's barely a drop in the bucket.
