                So pretty much the final vote tally in comparison to previous years (it's not quite done yet but the vast majority of votes are counted).

                McCain 59,948,323 million vs Obama 69,498,516 million

                Romney 60,933,504 million vs Obama 65,915,795 million

                Trump 59,535,522 million vs H. Clinton 59,755,284 million

                Interestingly, there wasn't a "Trump Surge." In fact, he actually received fewer votes than either McCain or Romney did in 2008 and 2012 respectively. Hillary however received ten million fewer votes than Obama did back in 2008 and six million fewer votes than he did in 2012. It's honestly a little staggering how poorly she performed. Many Republicans didn't back Trump, but he managed to attract enough Independents and Democrats to more or less make up the difference. In contrast, quite a few Democrats didn't back Hillary and she didn't manage to attract swing voters in any real way with the result of being short millions and millions of votes.

                I think at the end of the day, this election was less an endorsement of Trump and more a rejection of Clinton.
