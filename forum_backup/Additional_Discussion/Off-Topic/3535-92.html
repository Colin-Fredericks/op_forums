                                Despite all the many flaws (and they're many), I loved Pokémon Sword/Shield.

                                Behind the severe consequences of rushing development, the basis and premises are simply fantastic. It is one of the best world building in the series, an excellent and refreshing plot and magnificent character development. Of course, it takes some time to realize that due to the bad and limited level design, rushed and uninvolved storytelling, lack of different skins for NPCs, and so on, but even then it is amazing.

                                Not that it is my favorite one. Ultra Sun/Moon stand at the top for me, with Let's Go Eevee second (best version of Gen One) and the new Legends: Arceus already third and competing for second spot heavily. But among all the bad blood about Sw/Sh in the internet I find it relevant to say, I really liked it despite it all.

                                For context for anyone who cares, I'm a Gen Oner. Didn't played the Japanese versions, but started with Pokémon Blue, played Yellow, and still remember the original Gold/Silver as "that new game".
