    NPC Personality Quirks - help me make a table!

    09-16-2014, 03:22 PM
    Something I find useful when trying to make an NPC distinctive is to give them one obvious character trait that colors everything else they do. I've been thinking it would be handy to make a table of these traits, since I haven't been able to find a good one online - most "trait tables" are for describing physical traits or weird habits which might give a little extra flavor to an NPC, but which probably don't have a meaningful impact on how they interact with other characters. That's not what I want - I want obsessions, passions, and personality disorders that give enough definition to a character that you can build the rest easily after working out a couple background details.

    With that in mind I try to design traits with the following rules:
    a. Each trait must be a mental or social trait, not a physical one.
    b. Each trait must be general enough to apply to a lot of different characters.
    c. Each trait must be expressed in eight words or less.
    d. Each trait must not have a mechanical requirement or otherwise impact on character stats.

    Here's 20 to get us started, and I hope you'll add more!

    Great Trait Table

    1. Enjoys making people uncomfortable.
    2. Very self-conscious about their appearance.
    3. Misogynist (may be self-hating if female)
    4. Misandrist (may be self-hating if male)
    5. Racist against X (may be self-hating if X)
    6. Devoutly religious
    7. Obsessed with sex, dating, or relationships
    8. Seemingly always on drug X.
    9. Total spendthrift.
    10. Total skinflint.
    11. Often tries to be intimidating
    12. Would-be comedian
    13. Married to their job
    14. Very unhappy with their job.
    15. Won't stop talking about one social/political issue
    16. Always ready for a party
    17. Gossips constantly
    18. Really into one or more sports
    19. Very straightforward or blunt
    20. Affects being apathetic about everything.
