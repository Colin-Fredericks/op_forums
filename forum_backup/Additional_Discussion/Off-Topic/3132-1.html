                                                    You know what I hate?

                                                    I've been following the talk of mega threads closely. It's an interest near to my heart. These forums--or, more accurately, the forums most of us come from--are pretty much the only means of expression I have, and I've been accustomed to being able to say whatever's on my mind. Sometime it's a matter of self-defense, treating these forums like a pressure valve to let a little bit of steam off before things build up too much, but most of the time it's just a place where I can put something out there that I think will be interesting or useful to the people in my life.

                                                    There are currently at least indications that I am wrong to do so, that I have always been wrong to do so, that I have been misusing the very concept of a forum for a very long time, that what I want a forum to be for is not what it is actually for. That's not what I hate, though. This site is privately owned and operated, and its owners or at least those to whom the owners have delegated this power have an absolute and unshakable right to declare what is okay to say and do here. I do not begrudge them for setting rules I would not have set myself.

                                                    What I hate is that I am trying very hard to think right now, to figure out if I still have a place in this community or not, to figure out if this is still my home or if I'm homeless, but I can't think about anything because I'm almost consumed by despair at the dreadful certainty that this is it for me, the beginning of the end, that I should just take my burdensome and unwelcome self offline for the good of those who will come after me.

                                                    (I'm also not terribly fond of the self-pitying martyrdom is in that final paragraph, but fuck off if you don't like it, I'm messed up right now and need to get that fear out in the open where I can deal with it later.)
