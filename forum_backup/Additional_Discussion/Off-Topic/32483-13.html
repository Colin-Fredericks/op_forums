                Only things I can suggest are what I've found effective when I have encountered similar feelings/reactions myself.


                Music can help as you've already discovered, something repetitive but upbeat. I tended to go for classical, but whatever works for you.

                Meditation. The simple way is to find a position in which your body has as little physical stress as possible and then focus on not thinking about anything. clear your mind of all thoughts. You don't have to ignore them, there will be time to think on them later, just put them aside as they come up and strive for the mental state of nothing. Picturing a gently moving beach or something similar may help. The idea is something simple and regularly repeating.

                Oddly enough, cold air. I'd crack my window just enough that cold air could spill in and straight down onto my face. Warm body buried under blankets some cool air resulting from the cold air from outside mixing just a bit with the air of the room flowing straight down onto my face... relaxing.

                                    Maybe not, how about the great humanitarians? Gandhi, Teresa, Mandela? Oh wait, they are recorded as a racist, embezzler and terrorist.


                                    What you are calling broken is inherent in every human. EVERY. SINGLE. PERSON. The word ceases to have meaning. Everyone has problems, issues and difficulties, to a greater or lesser extent. Everyone is deserving of positive human contact and is worth keeping alive as long is possible and they want it.

                                    If you grew up with horrible abusive people and live with unstable people that doesn't mean you are bad, wrong or broken, it means you had a shitty childhood and are in a really shitty spot in life. It means you'll have to *work* to develop understanding of how most people interact socially, because your perceptions on things are apt to be screwed up and likely tending towards expecting the worst... because that's what your brain was trained to expect. It means you have to work harder then others and will have issues that others do not. It does NOT mean that those issues can not be overcome, it does not make you inferior, unworthy or not welcome, and it sure as fuck doesn't make you broken.


                                    Calling someone, anyone broken is going seriously piss me off. It's a pointlessly negative one word summary that is simply writing something off as impossible to change or fix. Will you ever be perfect? No. Guess what? There are no perfect people, only people that pretend to be. And simply taking the time to do a mental inventory and assessment, to decide to work towards making yourself better in whatever ways you deem desirable, you will be ahead of a large portion of the world.
