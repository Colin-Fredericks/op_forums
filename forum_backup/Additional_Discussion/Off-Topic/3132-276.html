                    1) The ethics of refusing to perform medical procedures isn't really as simple as saying "no." If a doctor thinks they unqualified to deal with a patients needs, it's their obligation to help the patient through the process of finding a specialist; esp. as referrals matter. If there's no need for a specialist, the refusal isn't ethical.

                    2) Blanket refusal based on non-medical or non-relevant medical factors is unethical. A doctor that refuses to treat the broken leg of a trans person is not acting according to sound medical ethics.
