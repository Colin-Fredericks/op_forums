        A few years ago I complained to you guys about a nursing facility I had to visit, one where the boss of the place kept ordering me to provide cares for patients who were refusing them--ordering me to commit assault on her residents, in other words--and I acquiesced because I thought my job was in danger if I didn't. I was only there temporarily for I forget what reason, I think because the aide assigned to go there was out on maternity leave or something, but whatever it was, I was glad when my tour there was over.

        Yes, I think of it as a tour. You would too.

        Yesterday I was assigned again to that same place, not as the aide but as manual assistance for the aide there because there are patients who cannot be transferred by one person alone and it's the nursing home's policy that its staff will not assist hospice care providers in any way. The place is, if anything, worse than I remember, which shocks me. There are a lot of things I could get into, but let's just leave it that one of our patients died Friday and they did not contact us, then got pissed off when we pointed out that no one contacted us.

        But whatever. I did my job of holding machines steady while they lifted up an obese, demented woman who the entire time sobbed because she thought we were attacking her, got her into bed, dressed her, washed our hands, and were ready to leave, when my co-worker told me, "When you get home, take a hot shower, and then wash your clothes in hot water."

        "Why?" I asked.

        "They're pretending there isn't one, but there's an outbreak of scabies in this facility," she explained. "We know because they ordered medication for it for our dead patient without telling us and tried to bill it to us, but the pharmacy asked if it was something we cover."

        I didn't say anything for a while, then said, "I guess that explains why so many of the staff are walking around with Band-aids on their faces."

        I got home and took her advice. Now I am sitting here feeling my arms itch and wondering if it's innocent or if it's a sign that parasites have burrowed into my epidermis and are laying clutches of eggs in the fatty tissue of my epidermis. I know the itch isn't because of scabies--the infection takes longer than six hours to become symptomatic--but still, it's not too early to wonder if soon I will be seeing a rash of pimple-like pustules where colonies of parasites are tunneling through my flesh.
