                        I've had a number over the years, but I'm not sure if I currently consider any my personal 'fursona'.

                        The one I've liked the most was a fun-loving and optimistic bull, usually going by 'Bright' who stands out as the opposite of my usually glum self. I occasionally role-played him with the few furry friends I have, but I always was a little awkward with it.
