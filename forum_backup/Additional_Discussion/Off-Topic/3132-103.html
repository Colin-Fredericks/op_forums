    I'm looking forward to the combat system. I'm hoping the new system isnt to big of a headache for large groups of 5-6 players to manage. (I sincerly am praying dex still isnt THE god stat for combat, if i see our group pump out one more dex 5 twink i'm gonna punch em..*coughsnotthati'mnottoallyguiltyofdoingthismys elf*cough*) I cant wait to see how my group fucks their characters up hard by trying to build them like they used to only to realize they are totally screwed. Atrribute minmaxing makes me sad, there is sooo much more to a character then attributes..but i'm also a story based play who makes characters around a concept and stats them accordingly, not just based on what I want for the stats.

    Social interactions we had a small taste of the from masters of jade and I adored it, I play social characters, and I found 2.5 to be lacking somehow in how social situations were done...it felt so heavy handed most of the time, and well...there were a few charms that made me cringe cause you could litterally do anything/get people to do anything with the simplest of essence expense, and a roll that most mortals and even some exalts couldnt really resist unless they to were social based or thought to pick up a few social or integrity.

    I LOVE hearing that it rewards players for focusing, i found 2.5 leaned towards forcing one to be to general, at least thats how we saw it. If you focused to much it bit you in the ass. Maybe we played it wrong? *Shrugs*

    Also i wish i could have this discussion in the exalted fourms but that place is soooo full of hate i avoid it...maybe its changed since i last swung by it?
    Unfortunately it hasn't changed significantly (the forum) there's a lot of things that start out positive but everything still tends to either stagnate or turn vitriolic. I expect it'll become a frenzy right after the book drops and then stabilize into a generally more positive form about 6-8 weeks later.

    As for the rest... Dex is still very heavily used in combat BUT and it's a big but, so is strength, and stamina has a significant interaction as well. You really agonize over the attributes and not leaving yourself vulnerable in the different areas. You can lean in different directions but generally I'm a munchkin and even I don't put 5 in dex unless I'm making a straight up fighter of some kind. And if I expected that character to actually take some damage I'd lean heavy on stamina.

    The combat system is different, and flows differently but is *considerably* faster flowing that ex2. The biggest delay source I've found is as people try to work out what charms they want to use on their attack/action, and the big/easy way to speed that up that I've found is to use charm cards with the full text of the charms on them. A small pile for social actions, a small pile for combat actions, and a small pile for everything else.

    Pretty much every ability has a charm or charms that you can get to even at character creation if you really really focus on it and make that ability your "thing". It's an insanely powerful ability, each and every one of them. But the drawback of getting there right out of character creation is that it would mean you're investing almost exclusively in that ability... which means you tend to be lacking in other areas. And need your circle mates to cover you in the areas you lack. Be it the ability to convince a king to trade you his kingdom for a pinecone, the capacity to dodge death itself through your doombot creation skills, the ability call forth giant artifact teaching engines, or the ability to call forth plagues upon the lands of your enemies, there is a lot that can be done there... but doing so right out of the gate does mean near-exclusive focus on that area.


    And all that is aside from the sorcerous project system (who doesn't want to make a sentient potato?) which adds a lot, an actually decent crafting system, and the whole artifacts and evocations overhaul. If you missed the last bit, an artifact in 3e is largely a hunk of effective metal with latent properties and themes. Evocations are the powers you can unlock that come from the unity of the soul of your PC and the artifact coming together to achieve a singular purpose (against statistically long odds in many cases). They're effectively special powers of the weapon that you can unlock and master.
