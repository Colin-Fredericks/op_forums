            So, the new season started a couple weeks ago. Here's a quick list of what I'm watching and enjoying.

            Wizard Barristers seems pretty interesting. It's a "modern world + magic" setting. The main character is working as a lawyer for people caught breaking the law through the use of magic. It seems like mages have their own court system, which tends towards extremely harsh punishments. It's clear that mages, due to the power they have, face fear and discrimination. Fairly interesting first episode. I'm curious to see where it goes.

            Sekai Seifuku - The MC joins a group (led by a little girl) bent on conquering the world using magic and monsters. It seems there's going to be magical girls opposing them. I think I'll stick with it. It seems like it will be amusing, if only because of the "reversed" role, of the MC being part of the world conquering "villains."

            Saki - The Nationals. More mahjong fun. I know nothing about mahjong, but for some reason I enjoyed the heck out of the first two seasons.

            Chuunibyou Season 2 - Continuation of the series that was running last year. More of the same hijinks and such. Fun enough.

            Space Dandy - I like the animation and the characters are all quite entertaining.

            There's two series involving Nobunaga out. I haven't had the chance to watch them yet; might give it a shot next week.

            Anybody else watching anything they like?

                                In the veing that I'm perpetually behind everything, I finished Ga Rei Zero last week and found it immensely satisfying save for the fact it's a prequel,so certain things just don't have closure. Highly recommend it if you're into simple relationships and subtle build up and payoffs int regards to the dyanmics of those relationships peppered with awesome and implausible fighting.

                                On a different tangent though, I'm starting to get to the point where, between this and Fate Zero, where I wish that there was a way these people who do awesome prequels to mediocre stories could just come up with their own ending. It's impossible, but man there are quite a few prequel stories that would have been better if they weren't bound to the future events of the series.

                                                If you haven't already seen the first season of Haruhi, you probably should. While it wasn't a terribly amazing amazing, it is a rather popular and influential series. You see lots of references to it constantly. And if you've seen it and liked it, then you might as well check out the second season (of which Endless 8 is a big part) which some people enjoyed and others hated. It was an interesting experiment, but I totally understand why people who were watching it as it came out, were very upset. If you don't know what you're getting into, then yeah, you pry won't like it. It works a lot better when you can just grab the episodes and watch them all at once, rather than having to watch 1 episode each week expecting something to happen.
