    What media is "comfort food" for you?

    05-27-2016, 08:20 PM
    When you're mentally or emotionally worn, what sorts of media (general, or specific, games count) help you feel a bit better, or at least keep you on an even keel?

    For me, as a general rule, romantic anime/manga. First Person Shooters (because while they can require physical mastery, they don't require or inspire deep emotions, so they let me recharge), ummm oh, and The Crow. I just like it.﻿
