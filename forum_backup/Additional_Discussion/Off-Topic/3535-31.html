                                Over the years I've noticed that most of the dreams that stick to me features somebody getting transformed, usually me. And last night was no different.

                                I was in a pub, reading a book and drinking lots of beer. A nice-looking guy comes to me, we chat, he tells me that the next time we'll see each other, we'll play a game. Then I go back home at night, going through a forest that gets, of course, super dark and I'm starting to get lost in it, even if it's a full moon night. I'm starting to get tired and weary, throns prickle my skin, then I hear blood-chilling howls, then barks, and I know it's coming for me. I start to run, faster and faster. The barks gets closer, until I have a pack of hunting hounds right on my heels, followed by hunters on their horses. As I run, my body starts changing so I can keep the pace, and I progressively transform into a stag, complete with hooves and antlers, sometimes bipedal, sometimes quadrupedal. Eventually, the dogs manage to get me down, and starts chewing on me, but they scatter when the hunters arrive. Some are dressed in modern garbs, the rest is dressed like medieval lords and ladies, complete with armor. One of them get down from his horse and arrive to plunge a spear in my body (in my heart, I guess). Except that I doesn't die, I'm actually aware of my body being carried to a castle, where I'm being cooked and served to those same hunters. And when I'm finally eaten, I'm waking up the next day in the forest, still a stag, with apparently my own small herd. I'm the largest male and thus, the alpha, and I know it's my role to protect them from the hunters, so I lead them to a river so we can drink. Finally, I wake up when the other male adult of the herd, more submissive, nudges me to show me a bear looking at us across the river. And then I woke up for good.

                                This would be such a good changeling story, I've always been fascinated by transformation as a narrative theme.

                                "And for my next trick" I said, "anvils!"
