    Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
    I really hope it launches while Dominaria is still in standard, because I really want to play with that set.
    I'm sure it will be. Constructed is the only format so far. There isn't a way to sort cards by sets yet, although I expect that will become a thing once standard gets introduced.

    According to the State of the Beta, money spent in-game on the Beta will be carried over when the game goes live. The introduction of the game economy is interesting. In a way, I think you could argue that playing tournaments is a better way of getting cards than buying boosters. A regular booster is 1000 coins for 8 cards, 2 of which are uncommon. The quick constructed is 500 coin buy in, but you get back 100 coins even if you get swept out and you get 3 uncommon. 1000 gold for 1 rare (or mythic), 2 uncommons, and 5 commons vs 800 gold for 6 uncommons. It is an interesting tradeoff.
