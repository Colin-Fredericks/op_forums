                    S6E6 - No Second Prances

                    That title is stupid.

                    Originally posted by Hand-of-Omega View Post
                    Oh snap! Can't wait to see how this plays out...
                    But, well, it turns out it played out great. I really liked this episode. The last time Trixie returned, I was far from pleased with the outcome. This time, we actually got some development for her character, and gave us more ground to stand on with Starlight as well. Not only that, but it actually displayed a different image of one of the most explored characters in the show, namely Twilight herself.

                    I've seen some comments complaining about how Twilight (and Celestia, for some reason?) was out of character this episode. I would strongly disagree. It's been well established that Twilight can be rather neurotic when she feels she is being judged and evaluated. This character trait is then explored through the lens of those who are not entirely sympathetic to her, and guess what? It might paint a different picture. And who's to say there's not some truth to that picture?

                    The whole episode is actually rather complex. There's the image of Twilight as we the viewers know it, and as experienced by Trixie, and how Starlight fits in between those poles. There's Trixie's lasting "rivalry" with Twilight (and that can't be a fun comparison for her) contrasting with the budding friendship with her student, which actually came across as one of the most motivated developments I've seen in this show. There's also, of course, Twilight's position as both student and teacher (middle management is rough), and the balance she has to walk between being having an guiding role and letting Starlight go her own way. I super enjoyed the whole mess, because to me, it felt genuine, and certainly not out of character. Just... seen from a new perspective.

                    How anyone could say Celestia is out of character from her one sigh of dialogue is beyond me.

                    The fandom winks fell pretty flat. But hey, I'll take them.

                                        S6E8 - A Hearth's Warming Tail

                                        Man, they've really lowered their title pun game this season. First No Second Prances and now this? It didn't even mention a tail. I mean, I guess they all have tails all the time, but they don't have to name the episode after it. It's the decline of pony, I tell you.

                                        Obviously, I'm milking what little critique I can since the rest of the episode was absolute gold.

                                        The natural focus of this episode is the songs, and I will talk about them. I'd just like to preface with how freaking excited I am that they used the retelling of an old and existing story as a metaphor for mirroring Starlight's history. I'm a big fan of structuralism and post-structuralism as a literary theory, and the idea that stories communicate, quite without the conscious meddling of humans, is a pretty big concept there. I find that this episode utilizes how Starlight's story and A Christmas Carol bleed into each other and help each understand the other, and it's quite moving.

                                        Now, on with the show. I won't talk about all the songs, but in general, I will say one thing for the songs in general this episode: the god damn lyrics! Oh my god, did they consistently nail the lyrical presentation. I can't describe it better than that they felt genuine. There are some times when a song will contain a turn of phrase that'll feel flat, like "oh, they had that line here because they needed a rhyme with a word that was in character" and sometimes it just doesn't work. Except for the Hearth's Warming Eve Is Here Once Again framing song, I don't think I heard a single line that sat badly with me.

                                        Say Goodbye to the Holiday - When I heard this the first time, I was like "oh my freaking god, did they finally beat This Day Aria?" Having listened to it again and compared, I'm forced to conclude that they probably didn't. I still think the dual voices, the "deceptive cadance" trick and the sheer importance of it in the show at that point still manages to cement This Day Aria's place as the best villain song, and from some perspectives the best song, in Gen 4. That said, Say Goodbye to the Holiday more than challenges it when it comes to sheer execution. Kelly Sheridan is on point here, and the fact that this wasn't even my favourite song in the episode when I had to question if it beat my favourite song in the show ever is something else. I can't get over how good of a villain Starlight makes. I'm almost sad that she's becoming a good girl.

                                        Pinkie's Present - And when I heard thisthe first time, I was like "shit, did they beat Smile Smile Smile?" And... I frankly don't know. I could go so far as to say that they might have. This isn't a character statement on Pinkie Pie in the same way Smile is, but it's so in character that it might as well have been. Smile is about Pinkie, but this is about Pinkie and also something more. It's about Pinkie's philosophy, or state of mind if you will. Smile tells you who Pinkie is, but Pinkie's Present tells you why being her is awesome. Combined with the jazzy tune which makes it stand out from the regular feeling of the show's songs, I personally think it's the best song of the episode.

                                        Luna's Future - Right, I'm not a Luna fanboy. Luna singing is neat because she's legitimately underused, but I wasn't squeeing when she sang in Season 4 finale. This time, though. God, this song was just made for her. The lyrics says she has a new voice actor (for singing, I assume) named Aloma Steele, and what a way to introduce her. The depth of her voice really sells Luna, and I can't wait to hear more of her.


                                        This was Michael Vogel's first episode as a main writer. He's been with the show in different capacities before, but still, this is one hell of a strong start.
