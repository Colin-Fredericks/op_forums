            I have to emphatically agree with this. I always hate having to give the cliche "you have to be happy with yourself before you can be happy with someone else" consolation, because I've been in that position before and I remember hearing that same advice. I remember what absolute bullshit that sounded like coming from people who were in healthy, loving relationships, and how sick I would get of being told to love myself before trying to love someone else. It was absolutely infuriating, and so I can't help but cringe every time I have to give someone that same advice that I used to hate to hear. But the truth is, it worked for me. The moment I gave up on trying to find a relationship and decided to actually try to enjoy being single instead of looking for the next relationship, I suddenly found myself in the best relationship of my life. Now, my experience is obviously not going to be the same as anyone else's, but it really is true that when you are actively looking for a relationship, the ones you find tend not to work well, for the reasons Old Canuck explained. If you're not trying to fulfill a desire for a relationship, you have no need to compromise to achieve that goal, so if you do find yourself in one, it will only be because it truly makes you happy.
