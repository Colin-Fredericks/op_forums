    And Oh my FUCKING god, I finally thought of a retcon that actually approaches Cap being a fascist all along!

    Okay, this was mercifully before your time, but there was this supervillain, kind of a harmless idiot called "Dr. Light", his superpower was bending light about. He was a jobber bad guy for the Justice League, Teen Titans and possibly the third Flash, Wally West.

    In the 2004 DC limited series, Identity Crisis, it was revealed that the reason he was so lame was because he was brainwashed. By several members of the Justice League.
    That is, Ray Palmer, Dinah Lance, Carter Hall, Hal Jordan, Barry Allen and Zatanna (who actually performed the brainwashing via magic). These are Heroes that basically defined the Silver and Bronze Ages of comics. And not only did they brainwash Dr. Light, who had, moments before raped Elongated Man's wife*, but Batman, because Batman walked in on the proceedings and raised a number of objections.
    Even the fan reactions to it is reminiscent of some of today's outrage.

    Hell I was about as mad about it then as you seem to be about Cap now. So I guess there might be something to what you're saying.





    *THE ARISTOCRATS! Identity Crisis was horrible**.
    Edit: **However, a number of great stories came along because of Identity Crisis. ReadingRalph's sections of 52 were far more rewarding knowing what he goes through in Identity Crisis. Pretty much the only time I'd suggest reading it is if you're prepping yourself to read 52. Which I cannot gush enough about. It's an inspiring thing about comics, even the bad stories can be re-purposed into telling good ones. Another reason why I don't really get mad if a story turns out to be poor.

                            Back again and unfortunately sober this week. Just going over what I got and read. Or is everyone finally ready to jump on the Hydra bandwagon? Steve Rodgers did!

                            Civil War II #1 I didn’t like the first Civil War, (no, not the one in 1861,) This one starts off ok. This event has Minority Report written all over the premise and I hated that story. At least Thanos wacks a couple of heroes. Yeah I was excited he wacked to characters that I had no emotional attachment too and the story gave me not a lot of reason to mourn their loss. Where’s Captain America to say “Hail Hydra” when you need him to?

                            4001 AD #2 Valiant is so under rated, Might be the best read for the summer for me.

                            Army of Darkness: Furious Road #4 Let’s be honest, its not going to win any Eisner awards. It’s full of clichés from the movies, and not original, but it’s an ok read.

                            Batman Rebirth: I’m not sure what I just read. The whole issue takes place over a year, and Calendar Man crawls out of his own body through his mouth. It was just an odd ‘set up’ for the new on-going series.

                            Green Arrow Rebirth: Pretty decent. The art is hit or miss sometimes. This feels a lot more like the Pre 52 Arrow but with the new 52 history.

                            Green Lanterns Rebirth: Hal leaves Jessica and Baz to watch Earth as he looks for the Corps? (Thought Baz was with the Corps when they vanished?) I swear they have different colorist doing the same book, that’s so annoying! Decent set up book if you’re into “C Lister” Green Lantern Corps

                            Moon Knight #3 I’m not to sure if Moon Knight is actually crazy or really being chased by ancient Egyptian devils. But I feel we need definite answers soon or we are just spinning our wheels. Moon Knight is still fun to read thus far.

                            Predator: Life and Death #4 Last issue but not the last issue..

                            Punisher #2 Still love the Punisher after all these years. Becky Cloonan and Steve Dillion doing a good job writing, but the art can be a turn off for me at times. Tim Bradstreet please come back to do the covers.

                            Superman Rebirth: Another set up issue but was a decent read explaining the difference between the two Supermen and reminds us all stuff in comics are never truly forever..even death

                            Superman: Coming of Supermen #5: When this Miniseries was announced I was real excited. I was a big fan of Neal Adams art in the day, now I can’t wait until this story is over. I don’t know if Neal’s art is being rushed or he lost his touch doing too much acid back in the day. In a few panels it looks like one guy is Marty Feldman looking in two different directions at the same time, like he’s gone full derp.

                            Walking Dead #155 The book has been this set up for ‘All Out War’ part 2 for a while now, will if finally start next issue?

                            What did everyone else get?
