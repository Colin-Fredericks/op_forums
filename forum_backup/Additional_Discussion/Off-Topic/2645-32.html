Neither was her combat skill or her defeating Jace Sol....erm i mean Kylo Ren which was more about Kylo being a shitty sith than her being OP character.

Seriously why they hugging? The never share a single scene, wouldnt it made more sense Leia hug chewie? You know, the BFF of Han and probably the hairy godfather of their son.

Batman doesnt get a free pass from many, neither many other male characters (im looking at you deathstroke) so why does Rey?
