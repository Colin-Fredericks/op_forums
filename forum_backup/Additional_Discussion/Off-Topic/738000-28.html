            I think Paradox Interactive will seek to unify the World of Darkness by having RPG, LARP, computer games and maybe other games (like Eternal Struggle) happening in the same story continuum. Metaplot will be the same across all lines. Sort of like the D&D 5e "events". They tie the RPG, the computer games and others together into a single storyline. I guess we'll start seeing the same from White Wolf.

            That will mean tighter creative control of the LARP and RPG lines. Maybe even tossing out nWoD in the process. Also, if they want to increase brand recognition, the books have to get back into the stores. Only if you can find the books on Amazon, alongside the computer games will get back more of the older fans. White Wolf and Vampire: The Masquerade are the second most-recognizable names in the RPG history outside of the hobby. This recognition can be leveraged if done right.

            OPP is way behind publishing schedule, there books are only found on an obscure website (DriveThru) and quality has grown spotty in places. Also, instead of focusing on the big three (Vampire, Werewolf, Mage) in cWoD and nWoD, they continue to churn out new games about ever more obscure subjects (Beast, Deviant). This fractures the market. I really hope that White Wolf now pushes for consolidation and getting rid of the more obscure stuff.

            I am quite positive that with an official lead storyteller we are going to see exactly that. I am very optimistic about the future.

                                                I still believe that their press release is mostly concerning oWoD, because the "One World of Darkness" makes sense if you look it at from a distance. oWoD is a tangled mess of different editions, revisions, metaplot, splats with different backgrounds that clash with each other so hard at points that you have to start ignoring and changing stuff left, right and center as soon as you bring 2 or even more of them together. It would make sense to make the new oWoD edition a "clean sweep" and begin at square one, while keeping true to the roots of oWoD (Camarilla/Sabbat, Wyrm, Technocracy vs. Traditions and so on) but do away with all the old metaplot and NPCs and give it a fresh start. And since OP has just announced that new edition and going from Rose's statement, they knew this deal was brewing, I can't imagine they'd make that announcement without being confident they'd actually be able to make that new edition.

                                                Nothing of that has to mean anything for nWoD, nothing has been said so far that gives us any real evidence that the nWoD is in trouble (since nothing has been said it all, this also holds true the other way round) that's why I'm still in the "wait and see" camp, the least productive thing people can do now is blindly lashing out at folks at PI when we have no idea what their plans are.
