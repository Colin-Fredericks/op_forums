                            Originally posted by PaladinDemo View Post
                            They only want to focus on Eve and Dust, we're not going to get anything. They even had the trailer and tech demo on the brief VtM documentary on the Kindred the Embraced Book of Nod edition, the exact same trailer and tech demo from way back when, and still trying to convince us that they have made big strides towards completing it. I suspect that CCP is going to keep showing the same trailer and tech demo trying to convince us that their doing something when their not.
                            That is because EVE Online is making them money, and Dust 514 is connected to EVE. They need their cash cows to be healthy so that they can even pay the bills to keep from shuttering WoDMMO. If EVE Online was showing weakness, and Dust 514 could use the help, then they needed to shore up those games.

                            As for their Public Relations campaign for WoDMMO... there really isn't one, and at this early of development there shouldn't be one either. They released a few bits and pieces of very early development to fantatics of the IP to make them feel good, and (as is the way of the Internet) it has caused people to somehow assume that they are entitled to more information, that WoDMMO is their primary concern, and created a massive amount of trolling due to what is unfortunate, but business-related decisions.

                            We are hyper-sensitive to the actions, or perceived inactions of CCP, but until Eddy puts up a post that he's looking for a new job, and the other 40 or so people working on WoDMMO are transferred away, or are fired, we SHOULD assume that the game is still in development.

                            CCP probably should not have announced the game's development, or shown work for the game, or done anything that has lead to anyone knowing/caring about the game, or expecting anything from it until they have officially begun their PR Campaign for the game... But I don't feel let-down because they haven't promised me anything, and the game has continued to be in production, even through the darkest of days of all of this. It may not be in development at a speed that I would like, but if EVE Online suffers and CCP can't afford WoDMMO, then it WILL be canceled, so a strong EVE Online and Dust 514 only benefits WoDMMO.
