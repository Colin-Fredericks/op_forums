        Originally posted by Shock View Post
        The other thing that it maybe hinting at is a one stop website for all World of Darkness information of both lines but that's me being hopeful.
        I think rather "one stop website" implies that computer games, LARP, table-top, possibly even Eternal Struggle (and other WoD games) are accessible at one website. Meaning it's not so much about bridging the gap between cWoD and nWoD, but rather about bridging the gap between table-top and LARP (read: OPP and BNS).

        If I were running a modern WoD publishing company, I'd take my cue from WotC. Establish "events" in the game world that can be experience in table-top, computer game, LARP and maybe even card games. A multi-media event with a common storyline. Especially the LARP angle would be one that WotC hasn't matched yet with D&D 5e events.After a start-up period where I get the game-lines ready and unified, I'd launch the events taking cues from the old "Year of..." thing to build common ground between various game lines.

        But that would mean transfering a lot of creative control back to White Wolf. And that's what I think we will be seeing.
