                                                        Originally posted by Lunatyk View Post
                                                        focusing on good memories can take time...
                                                        Sadly, that's a damn accurate observation.

                                                        When you're dealing with depression, the "downer" memories come easy. Even when more "baseline", the positive thoughts are not as ready as the negative ones.

                                                        Only those really scary people who seem to be positive and happy most of the time have the brains that access the "happy thoughts" easier. They're the ones who need the brain scans to find out what's wrong with them.

                                                        Only thing I can tell you, Jim (or any one else having this problem), is what I try to do when I'm in that same place. Realize that you are there and that whatever your brain it tossing out at you is coming from there because that's how you felt during that memory. Get your head into a better place and the positive memories will be unlocked.

                                                        It's part of that bio-chemical thing where the memories are connected to certain pathways, which are triggered and recorded with certain brain chemicals, and if one is more out of balance than the other, you can only access the ones using that chemical. It's why you can't remember things (sometimes) when you're drunk because your brain is so soaked in alcohol, you'd have to be that drunk AGAIN to remember it.

                                                        K, rant, advice and tangent over. Carry on.
