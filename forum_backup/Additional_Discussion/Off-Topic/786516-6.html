            I don't really... err... Know any trans celebrities. I follow a few trans people on Tumblr who are very popular there, but I don't think that counts. That said, I've got no beef with Caitlyn Jenner. I don't agree with her political views, but I respect her as a trans woman who came out despite having been in the public eye before transitioning. She doesn't speak for me as a trans woman, but good on her for what she did for herself.

            That's just the thing though, and forgive me if this is a slight deviation from your question, but... She's famous for her football career. She became a celebrity before she was out, and then when she came out the media just kind of had to deal with the fact that a person who was already a celebrity was also trans. Openly trans people don't tend to get to her level of fame. Even in media about trans people casts cis actors in trans roles more often than not. That's a big part of why I can't be openly trans in real life. I work in theater and it would pretty much be career suicide. I can't really name trans celebrities I like because there's not many to chose from, and that speaks to the huge issue with trans representation in media.

                                                            Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                                                            I would imagine it beneath ones dignity to deny ones nature for sake of dodging Workplace or professional hardships.
                                                            This is, frankly, a pretty uncool thing to say, considering I just posted on this same page that my profession is the biggest reason I can't be out in my day-to-day life.

                                                            I don't "deny my nature", I know exactly who I am. Part of who I am is an actor, an if I started taking steps to transition, I would no longer be castable in male roles. Breasts are kind of a barrier to that. I would most likely not suddenly become castable in female roles, because I don't fit within the box of classic "feminine beauty". While I may be perfectly confident in my own appearance, that means precisely fuck all when it comes to casting. Between my baritone voice, my prominent Adam's apple, and my masculine facial features, no director is going to want me to play the romantic lead. It's not like I can afford extensive cosmetic surgery on the pittance I make as an actor, so that pretty much means I'd cut myself off from any roles besides trans women. Now, there are not a lot of trans roles out there to begin with, and a good portion of them I would not want to play because they are pretty much a big pile of harmful stereotypes. The few that are actually well written are mostly going to be in the kind of artsy shows that don't draw a big audience, and the kinds of theaters that are going to produce them often can't afford to pay much. And on top of all of that, directors usually cast cis men in trans woman roles. Fuck, I could probably get cast as a trans woman more easily by staying in the closet.

                                                            So no, it's not beneath my dignaty to stay closeted in my professional life. It's a practical concern that if I want to eat, I've got to keep passing as male. That, or find another career, but that's what I like doing, it's what I'm good at doing, and it's just as much a part of my identity as being trans is.

                                                            EDIT: I apologize if I come off a little harsh here. It was not my intention to snap at you, it's just a very personal subject for me and your comment just happened to touch a bit of a nerve. I'm going to leave it here, because I think it's stuff that's worth being said, but please know that I don't mean the rant to be directed at you. More than anything, it's a stream of consciousness about stuff that I constantly have to deal with but very rarely talk about.
