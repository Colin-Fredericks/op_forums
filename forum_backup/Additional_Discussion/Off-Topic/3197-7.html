                        I've not seen the Rainbow Rocks clip you're talking about, but I take great satisfaction in that the movies aren't canon. I can just see them as something entirely disconnected. Looking at it that way, Equestria Girls actually had a few interesting aspects in that the mane six were actively unfriendly from the start. I kind of liked that. This one... I'm not sure if it'll catch my attention, but I'm not opposed to its existence. I'll not comment until I've seen it.

                        Speaking of comments...

                        S4E14 - Filli Vanilli

                        Zecora: I can cure this pony that is hoarse.

                        I died.

                        Right, this one is... split for me. On the one hand, the episode itself touched themes I can totally relate to. Not all people handle attention well, even if that attention is positive. That's something we in the western world, with out celebrity culture, sometimes may forget. I can look at some people I follow on YouTube, for example, for real life examples. In fact, Pinkie kind of took on the role of the "well-meaning critic" in this episode, and I thought it came out wonderfully.

                        On the other hand, fact is, we know that Fluttershy has been on stage before. Hearthswarming Eve, anyone? She didn't sing, but she sure performed. That just gnaws in the back of my head all throughout this episode, and I can't really overlook it.

                        But I still liked the episode, and I'm not going to let a continuity error ruin it, particularly not in a show that's traditionally been rather loose with continuity anyway. The animation, the little jokes, the song - let's not forget about the song, it was great - it was as good as I've come to expect of season 4.

                        Edit: Only thing I didn't get was the title? What?
