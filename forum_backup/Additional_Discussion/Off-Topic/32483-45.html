                                        I hope everything goes well, Thorbes. As you probably know, I'm bipolar with ptsd, general anxiety and high functioning autism, so I've had to deal with both a LOT, separately and together.

                                        A nice simple antidepressant like Prozac and Klonopin, a benzodiazepine, for anxiety worked well enough to keep me alive until I discovered etheogens. Since then, I go back and forth every few years ony the subject. I've taken peyote, mushrooms, Zoloft, and Prozac for depression. All have had some effect, some far better than others.

                                        I've taken marijuana medically for almost ten years for my anxiety and occasional major depressive episodes. It's cut down on my self-harm to the point that adding more scars to my body seems genuinely silly, and I can say that, unlike the ten years before I started using it, I can't say that I've spent some part of every year in a hospital.

                                        I will add, though, that I've had a sporadic history of substance abuse throughout my life since adolescence. It has literally nothing to do with marijuana, though. I didn't even try pot until high school. I simply spent my childhood being fed psychotropic drugs, so at some point I started messing around with my own medication for fun. I'd tweak on my dexedrine (they thought I had ADD), I'd take a twelve hour nap on my anxiety medication.
                                        These are all things I've either eliminated from my life or am taking care of professionals because I have the mental stability that my federally illegal medicine gives me.
