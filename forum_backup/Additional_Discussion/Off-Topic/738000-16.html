                                            I doubt whether we'll get any more big news today. Thanks to the time zone difference, everyone's gone home (or out celebrating) in Europe. So there won't be any new press releases or the like until tomorrow.

                                            And I wouldn't be too worried about the nWoD or Exalted. We're on the brink of a wave of major product launches for both lines - killing them now makes absolutely no sense, either from Paradox or Onyx Path's perspective. Killing them now means years of wasted work, and blowing millions of dollars. Who does that?

                                            Off-hand, I think the biggest immediate impact will be on the in-progress drafts. Now that Paradox owns the White Wolf licenses, Onyx Path will probably have to submit the final drafts of Lore of the Clans and Beast to them, once the backers are done proofing them. And that might mean another round of edits . . . but then, that was probably going to be needed anyway (over in the Beast proofreading thread, the writers commented that it looks like the old Chapter Six was included by accident).

                                            Maybe, maybe we might see a lack of future releases, if Paradox truly isn't interested in nWoD or Exalted . . . but that'd be a year, maybe two down the road. A lot can change in the interim, so I still wouldn't panic.

                                            And even then, there might be an opportunity here. If I was RichT, I'd be making some subtle inquiries at this point. "One World of Darkness" . . . if Paradox truly is only interested in the classic World of Darkness line, Exalted and nWoD might be available - for the right price. Onyx Path already owns Trinity and Scions outright. It makes sense they'd want the rest of the old White Wolf portfolio, if possible.

                                            (Of course, that might necessitate a rebranding of the nWoD line, so that Paradox can have the "one and only" World of Darkness . . . but we could live with a "Seekers of the Onyx Path" setting name, right?)
