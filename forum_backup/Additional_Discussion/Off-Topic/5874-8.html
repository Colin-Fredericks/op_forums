                            I'll admit, I quite liked the Dracula tv show. Yeah, it was kind of cheesy, but it was also fun, and (finally) a fresh take on the characters that made it a bit unpredictable. One friend of mine, who doesn't watch much network tv anymore, was outright shocked by how violent it got! And, wow, that season finale...

                            But it's cancelled now. Too bad...

                            The good news is that it has sort of a "spiritual successor" in Penny Dreadful...and that, quality-wise, this show is SOOO much better! Funnily enough, Dracula was much more like what we think of as a "penny dreadful" in tone than the show actually called such, which is much more a straight up Horror story, full-stop. I'm sure Alan Moore's getting his beard in a twist, as he does over everything, but this is just too much fun and coolness to miss!

                            And any excuse to watch Eva Green do her thing...
