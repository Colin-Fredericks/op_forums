                                                            As many of you now know from discussions in other forums, Paradox Interactive has acquired White Wolf Publishing from CCP. They apparently intend to produce their own products with their own team under the brand, including a single "One World of Darkness." The tone and content of their press release and comments also clearly indicate a bias toward the old World of Darkness. In fact, I don't believe they've even mentioned anything but the classic properties.

                                                            https://www.paradoxplaza.com/news/Paradox-White-Wolf-Publishing/


                                                            http://www.whitewolf-publishing.com/


                                                            What this means for OPP, Mage 2e or any other nWOD property, or DaveB and the other OPP developers and writers, is now anyone's guess.

                                                            While we await updated and credible news, I simply wanted to express my hope that we'll still see Mage 2e as currently envisioned, and that it will continue as a viable game line. I also want to wish Dave and the other OPP staff and freelancers the best of luck during the transition.
