            Originally posted by Knightingale View Post
            Is Silo really that good? The premise seemed a tad dull to me. Weird place, authoritarian rules, weirdness just getting weirder, bad people get overthrown, turns out they had a reason to be bad and now the protagonist has to figure out what the alternative is... It just seemed like a formula, I've seen before... Snow Piercer, Wayward Pines, Lost... been there, done that. What exactly does Silo offer on that terrain?
            I can't speak to those other shows (though I did see the movie Snowpiercer), but Silo really sucked me in. The location is interesting, and there are some mysteries to the weird authoritarian rules. Plus the crux of the show resolves around a bit of a murder mystery that ties into the larger mysteries of the Silo. Maybe a bit of a spoiler, but the bad people don't get overthrown and we still don't totally know why they're bad. I really liked some of the world building too. Anyway, not sure what it was about it, but I really liked it. I got really drawn into it. Maybe it's the acting? It's got some great actors in it, and the lead Rebecca Ferguson is really good in this! I dunno... but I thought it was really well done and I'm definitely looking forward to the next season.
