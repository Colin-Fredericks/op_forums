                                            Originally posted by Zen_Hydra View Post
                                            So...what does this mean for Onyx Path Publishing? Is CCP looking to offload the IP? Are they going to sit on it in perpetuity (essentially killing it)? Is the publishing side just going to continue on as it has been, or should fans brace themselves for some worst case scenario?

                                            I feel for everyone directly impacted (I've been downsized before myself). I hope everyone does their best to roll with the punch, and land on their feet (ready to chew bubblegum and kick ass).
                                            The longer term outlook is unknown. However, CCP has a license to develop the RPG properties for WoD and Exalted. It's profitable, if not hugely so. Rationally it wouldn't make sense to simply shut down an income source for no purpose, and if they were to opt to sell the property on, I doubt any new owners would object to profit... few companies do.

                                            So at the moment, it's a bit up in the air, as with all such cases, follow the money and the money says it's probably going to continue as long as there is demand, even while changes might happen in the ownership. This is not an absolute of course, but it's pretty much the rational assumption to make.
