                I feel as though "this wasn't a thing that was apparent to me as a distinction before" is often what the word privilege refers to.

                ​I don't think it's about making people feel bad about something (by which I mean, that is my personal stance on my priorities in communication; I do not intrinsically seek to make anybody feel about something), I think it's about emphasizing awareness of differences between people, and how certain of those differences have a lot of terrible things directed at them.

                ​I recognize myself as cisgendered to condition myself to normalize awareness of transgendered people, as well as to understand and articulate the fact that being transgendered carries with it a whole host of struggles that I will never personally experience or feel. I utilize the term to rebuild myself as seeing people in terms of an eclectic collection and a spectrum, rather than a default and all of the ways something deviates from it.

                ​I, personally, won't attack people from certain sections of society that have more benefits, but I will at least have a measure of disdain for people who get pissy and defensive about these things being pointed out to them and being emphatic over how the discourse needs to be carefully constructed to not step on any of their toes.

                ​But perhaps I'm biased; my own experience is that when people have been rather stark about things that have hurt them, I have had cause to look upon my own personality and past and find things resembling those complaints, that I judge to be shortcomings. Not even as things people have said directly to me or pointed out about me (although those are things that have happened more recently); it was just enough to hear that this was a thing, and to recognise that thing in myself, and for knowing what it did to people being the motivation to try and make it less of a thing in myself.

                ​I value having my toes stepped on, because I know from experience that I wouldn't know somebody was standing there otherwise.

                I have approximate knowledge of many things.
