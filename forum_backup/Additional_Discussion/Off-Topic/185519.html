                                                      Okay, that? Was awesome. I have nothing more to say.

                                                      Turns out I lied. But it's not that interesting, because it's just me saying how everything was awesome. And really, what a terrific way to start off a season. It was dominated by humour and combat choreography in blessed union, which are two of the strongest points of the series. Add to this the great voice acting, the vastly improved animation (is it just me, or did they add proper shadows?), the glory of a full 15 minutes and the story hooks spread throughout, it's... just great. Just great.

                                                      See? That was boring, generic praise. I'll try some more specific praise.

                                                      Mercury and Emerald make an interesting team and they get some good lines. I like the intimidation scene in the book shop; the way Mercury slams the books are subtly threatening, and I'm giddy at the implication that the Third Crusade book might represent some sort of secret manifesto or code. I hope it becomes relevant again, but then, I just like it when books are made significant.

                                                      Best line #1:
                                                      Ruby: "Sisters! Friends! ...Weiss."
                                                      Weiss: "HEY!"
                                                      Ruby: "Four-score and seven minutes ago, I had a dream."
                                                      Yang: "This ought to be good."

                                                      The guy with the blue hair... I'm not sure about him. My first reaction is that he'll either be a douche or a traitor. Seriously, just look at him. He's way to handsome to not be evil.

                                                      Best line #2:
                                                      Ruby: "Justice will be swift! Justice will be painful! IT WILL BE DELICIOUS!"

                                                      Not really a line, but one of the best moments in the episode is when Weiss does her Myrtenaster dash with a swordfish. I had to pause and laugh for a full minute.

                                                      I actually have one problem with this episode, though, namely the ending of the combat. I'm not entirely sure I understand what Ruby did there. Sure, we know she's fast, but she's never been that much about pure speed. That was some Sonic the Hedgehog shit. I'm not sure I really buy it. It felt like they needed Ruby to end it and couldn't really find a way that didn't involve Crescent Rose.

                                                      So yeah. Awesome episode. I'm already waiting for the next one.

                                                          I know right?

                                                          So, RWBY Volume 2: Best Day Ever.

                                                          The moral of the story is that Nora can kick everyone's ass in a food fight. Also, someone needs to talk to Beacon's food staff, because that's kind of gross.

                                                          It's been a whole year since RWBY came onto the scene, and certainly my opinion of the show has been very mixed. I'm not the only one, lots of reviewers don't know if it's a really well made amateur show or a poor professional one, which I think says more about Rooster Teeth as a company than on RWBY. I think I concluded that while the story and episode focus was lacking, and while a lot of it is not innovative, there is an incredible heart and joy to the show that makes it infectious, and I have no problems saying that I have become warmer to the show over the year. It's just fun. But I had really been wanting to see improvements.

                                                          As openers goes, this shows off the best of both worlds-that incredible heart and fun mixed in with some solid improvements.

                                                          First, the animation. THere's...shading. And texture. And Gradients, and a whole lot of other things I hadn't noticed were missing from the first volume, lots of little things and improvements on stuff that was there. This show already looked fairly nice in a stylized way, but now it feels a little more grounded and fully spruced up. There's a vividness and light to it all in just the right places, and it feels less like a slightly poor turn of the millennium cell shaded art piece now and very definitely more akin to today's level of 3D. Again, it looked good before, but now the entire look and style just is more...more. And the fluidity of motion is also fairly solid, a little more naturalistic in the not-combat scenes. It's hard to articulate al the ways it actually looks better, because it still looks a lot like RWBY, but it looks better, it looks fresher.

                                                          Sound wise, the show it also good. I expected the characters who were here before to really be settled in their roles, and they are, and there were some really fun lines on their part. The thing that impresses me most was the new voice actors for Neptune, Mercury and Emerald though, as they sound like they've been doing this for ages. I'll talk more on the characters later, but suffice it to say these guys are again very naturalistic, and clearly having fun with their roles. The bookshop owner, thouhg, well, he's standard for the "I'm not important" roles of RWBY. He...said lines.

                                                          WHile the story really hasn't started yet, it now feels like something happened last volume, and that's thanks to the show half-focus on the villains. In fact, that's probably my favorite part about this episode. Our villains feel human and relatable, although not sympathetic(which isn't bad, mind), and what's more is that I really believe they are up to something. We really, truly see the culmination of what Roman was up to last semester, and that sense of scale promises the scale for this one. Enough small things have happened to create an explosion, and the villains, while still unclear in their goals, feel potent in a way they never felt before. What's more is their dynamics. Again, I'll talk characters later, but these guys have a great dysfunctional group of friends feel already without losing the sense that Cinder, Mercury, and Emerald would kill-and do worse- for what they want. Roman remains lovably weasely, and the major problem in their little group. But best of all, despite a solid guess with Roman(That honestly had been there since the end of the last volume), I have no idea where the story is going to go with these guys.

                                                          ...Okay, I've refrained from the meat of the episode long enough. Monty starts RWBY off with what Monty does best-explain characters through a badass fight. Thing of the matter is that it's a food fight, and the entire damn thing is ridiculous as hell. They're beating each other up with leeks and french bread and turkey boxing gloves. It's a huge big ball of ROFLOMGWTF. Everything you need to know is just communicated through this fight.

                                                          I don't have anything else to say, and in fact I don't have much to say on the characters, except to say that there's not a one I dislike. I want to see more.

                                                          We're off to a good start folks. Everything you love is here, and everything that was wrong seems to have at least been sanded down.

                                                          Best Line goes to Ruby and Weiss for "Sisters! Friends!...Weiss.""Hey!"
