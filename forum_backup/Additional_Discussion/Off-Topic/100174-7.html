            Originally posted by danelsan View Post
            I'm unfamiliar with this term, which I'm assuming has something to do with the fist of a certain star, but looking for it on google your post is apparently the only mention of it in the entire internet, so can I get some clarification, please?
            No wonder, since the only other person who uses the term was the guy who present when I coined it1.

            Basically, it's when a character (typically a big one) keeps changing size, often between shots, with no regard for consistency. We named it after we noticed it happening to a random thug in the (first) Hokuto no Ken movie, who spent his entire screentime fluctuating wildly between pretty big and gargantuan.

            We needed a term for the phenomenon mostly because both of us keep doing it while roleplaying – if a character is bigger than average, we will eventually come to picture them as arbitrarily enormous.
            Once, he ended up getting the Giant Merit for a character halfway through a game, simply because we'd both started treating him as if he had it.

            1: I may have been present when he coined it. I forget.
