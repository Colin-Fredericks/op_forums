                            This is going to be a kind of long and rambling:
                            I hate that Marvel Comic's shameless pandering to the extreme Left has been killing the comic book store industry. Earlier this week, the oldest comic book store in Canada closed, and the owner said it was because they can't get anyone to buy the crap Marvel's been putting out lately. Since Marvel movies are so popular, many people are interested in the comics, but are disappointed when they pick one up. This is because Marvel has replaced most of their top 20 heroes with box-checking, diversity for diversity's sake, poorly developed stand-ins. I need to be clear; it's not that Marvel's effort to be more diverse that's the problem, it's that they are going about it the wrong way. Hiring more women as writers and artists is good; firing all of your editors and replacing them with barely paid, completely inexperienced interns who don't know anything about comics, and can't be bothered to do some research, is BAD. Introducing more ethnic minority characters, LGBTQ characters, and Muslim characters, and trying to push them into the spotlight is good; having them written by people who don't know anything about the culture of the people they are writing about IS BAD.(A middle-aged white guy trying to write dialogue for a 15 year old black girl...WAY too much "OMG, LOL, SMH, etc.) Trying to appeal to Millennials is... good, but not really much help, since WAY to many Millennials "aren't into things", which is their way of saying they don't have any money. Superheroes are very big right now, but the actual comic books are struggling, and it's at least partially because of these writers who don't know anything about the characters they write, and an "editorial" staff that doesn't care about quality. I know this is kind of a sexist thing to say, but here it is: the average comic book buyer is male, and age 14-45. If we're being honest, he's also most likely straight and cis- gender. So when you try to market something to him...SEX SELLS! So many "artists" at Marvel refuse to draw women with any curves, or in the case of Captain Marvel, no curves, military hair, Man-Hands, and a very man-ish face. This has put her sales in the toilet. Another thing that Marvel is doing is not paying attention to continuity. Having a main character act like they don't know anything about the antagonist they've fought a hundred times before is insulting to the reader. So is lying to the world on social media, claiming that your story won't end in EXACTLY the way someone has predicted. Better to stay silent than to claim something you know to be false.

                            For the record, I would be just as pissed at a company that did the same kind of crap, but with a more conservative/Right-Wing angle. It's not the politics that's the problem, it's letting politics supersede quality.

                            TL;DR: Marvel's been putting out some extremely bad comics, and it's hurting the whole industry.

                                            All valid points. Have I ever mentioned that I hate social media? Because I do. Giving a voice to all the shallow, vapid "activists" who don't actually have any idea what they really want, and the trolls who have nothing better to do than stir the pot. If you want to make a difference in the world, get out and DO SOMETHING, don't just tweet about it. I'm not saying that social media CAN'T be used for something meaningful, I'm just saying that very few of the people who make a big noise actually DO SOMETHING about whatever is bothering them. And I don't mean marches and protests; not right now. Tempers are too high. But you can start planning for the next election, and I don't mean 2020, I mean next year's midterm election; the midterm election decides your State Legislature, and THAT controls the Electoral College.

                                            Marvel's been wasting time trying to appeal to people who think they're too good to buy comics; and alienating the people who DO buy comics. The fact is, they've gone so far as to say that they don't WANT traditional Comic Book fans.

                                            I also hate when someone calls EVERYONE who disagrees with them a Nazi. Unless it's an ACTUAL Nazi, or KKK member, or other overtly racist/sexist/religious hate group, comparing someone to the worst group of people in recorded history because they don't think exactly like you do is NOT the way to get them to see your point of view, no matter what point of view you have. What it does is make them dig in their heels, and become even more opposed to you, and makes you look crazy to people who haven't decided yet.

                                            At either extreme of the political spectrum, totalitarianism is lurking. When you can't simply state your opinion on a topic without someone else shaming you for it, that's how dictatorships get started. The airplane that is democracy needs TWO wings in order to fly. That's why I'm a moderate on most issues.

                                            Regarding Millennials: don't assume you are immune to "Group Think". If you've ever said you don't like "labels", you have labeled yourself. I'm turning 37 later this month, so I'm right on the edge between "Generation X" and Millennial. You have a good point about the older generation being out of touch; they have a good point about most of us being too soft, myself included, if I'm honest. Life is tougher than it should be; so we have to be tougher. It might not be fair, but we're not going to fix it by just complaining about it. And sometimes that means making compromises. I'm not going to tell you WHAT to vote, or WHO to vote for, but not voting because the candidate you liked has been eliminated from the running ensures victory for the other party.
