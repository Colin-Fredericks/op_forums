                                                        I thought I was handling my recent loss of contact with Devilfish and Loxosceles pretty well. I mean, yeah, on any given day it's a roll of the dice whether I'm trying to think of acts that would make me worthy of their regard again, trying to take it all philosophically and hoping to learn something that will help me change into a better person, or am telling myself that my misery is better than I deserve given how many horrible things I did in the course of our relationship, but that's all part of the process and I'm trying to work through it.

                                                        Today started as one of the plotting days. I was composing e-mails in my head when I started sliding into philosophical mode, telling myself that if I'm truly their friend, I'll honor their needs rather than my own selfishness. On the tail of that thought, though, I had another one: Maybe, I thought, this is just a test to see if I can withstand the silence. Maybe if I keep my faith, I'll pass the test and everything can be okay again.

                                                        That is easily the single most pathetic thought I have ever had in my entire life, not because of the pitiful hope it contains, not because of the utter lack of contact with any kind of reality in which I have recast these two people as cruel and weirdly insecure deities who would hurt their followers on a whim, but because of the complete lack of self-esteem. Even if this was such nonsense as a test, for me to endure it and just be grateful to them when it's over rather than outraged at the disrespect such a test requires is all the proof I need that I have not grown or improved at all, that I still possess no sense of self-worth and am just groveling through life, licking up the crumbs of others' esteem as a substitute for my own.

                                                        You could say I hate that.
