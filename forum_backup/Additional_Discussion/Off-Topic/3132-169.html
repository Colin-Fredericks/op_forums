    Originally posted by Drake View Post
    I hate when people insist that something is not worth paying for, but they still want it.

    I just don't get how a person can want something but not see it as having any value, and then start talking about how unfair it is that whoever makes the thing is asking for money in exchange.

    It just seems to me that either you want a thing, and thus it has value to you (even if that isn't a value that matches the price being asked), or a thing has no value to you, and thus you have no desire for it. So every time I hear someone say something along the lines of "I want that, but asking me to pay for it is ridiculous" I can only think that the person is being dishonest (possibly with them self) or is just incredibly out of touch with how the world works for some reason (parents spoiled them to the point they don't understand the core concept of working for something, maybe?)
    I want horse armor in my Elder Scrolls games, but asking me to pay 2.5$ for it is ridiculous. Furthermore, since NexusMods exists and provides a large amount of mods for free, asking me to pay any price for mod-level things for Elder Scrolls is ridiculous. Which is why the new Bethesda system is ridiculous. It's not worth paying for bethesda-hosted mods even though I still want them, because I can get them for free elsewhere.
    Just an example.

    Or, there are things which are nice and I'd like them to exist, but if they didn't exist I wouldn't be too heartbroken. Like, say, fanart. It's nice that I can go to Deviantart and grab a bunch of pictures and use them. But if I had to pay for that, I'd just suck it up and not use pictures.
