                                    It'll be Ravenloft with 3.5, since that was the easiest to get the guys on board, since everyone knows it. I'd have preferred Pathfinder, maybe even trying WHFRP 4e, but I'm not really sad, since it's an one-shot and I'm not sure I could have learn a new system in short time, or convert things to PF from the 3.5 RL books.
