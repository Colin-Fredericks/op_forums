    Originally posted by atamajakki View Post

    I'm not gonna deny that a fairly massive chunk of the art out there is pornographic, but I'm also not sure that has to be a bad thing. The awesome person, Spacetwinks, behind the timeline in my OP had this to say in response to a later question about it, which nicely handles things; in other words, the talk shouldn't be "not all furry stuff is porn!" but rather "why is it bad if so much of it is?
    I think there's elements of transphobia and homophobia which have a lot to do with the negative association of sexuality with furries, and that people attempting to distance their own non-sexual content from the sexual content are often engaged in respectability politics because they feel that their interests are unfairly marginalized by association. Ideally we all understand that this is not a good thing that people feel the need to defend themselves from that kind of stigma, but y'know how people can be.

    I also think there's also a small discomfort with the potential association that furry has with bestiality, and there's the awkward intersection of that stigma with marginalized sexual and gender identities too that turns it into a quagmire that's not easily resolved for people both people on the outside and for those who are within that community.

    By this I mean marginalized sexualities and gender identities get associated with "deviancy" and deviancy is associated with bestiality. It's how you get people like Professors at acclaimed private institutions writing that allowing gay marriage opens the door for necrophilia, bestiality et al. On top of that, you get the more straightforward association of animals as a component of furries and bestiality, and you have an interest group (furries) which gets associated with deviancy both by virtue of base subject matter and by associating with actual marginalized identity. That's very likely where you get continuous discomfort with the notion of furries outside of those groups which are furries or hate them.

        Leetsepeak I think a lot of that is valid, yeah. One of the explanations I've seen for a lot of the anti-furry vitriol out there (beyond what's expected from some of the more unfortunate members of the fandom being altogether too public about some things) is that so much furry content has been shamelessly queer, even well before the internet but certainly in the last decade or so, and that the nascent alt-right latched onto the "yiff in hell" bandwagon at least in part due to the large amount of queer stuff in the mix. There's gay slice-of-life romances from the 90s that saw decent readership, and going after the weird animal people is an easy smokescreen for being upset with LGBT characters. While there's still a ton of gross fetishization of transgender characters within furry art, there's also a massive amount of more inoffensive and tasteful work that's exploded out in the last few years.

                                    My relationship with the furry fandom is a bit complicated. On the one hand I'm adamantly a transhumanist. If it ever becomes possible I intend to upload in a heartbeat, and if/when that happens I see no reason to just be human, though I don't really see myself taking on an anthropomorphic identity or what have you. I grew up on stories like the Firebringer Trilogy, so that's more my speed.

                                    As for the fandom itself? My issue isn't that so much furry art is porn, it's that so much of it is 'bad' porn. I do think there is that distinction. Often the proportions just aren't realistic, the artists' biases show through regarding sex, gender, the roles for them, etc. I'm also transgender, so it doesn't make me uncomfortable at all when I see stuff depicting transformations in a purely fetish light, often done by people who just have a fetish for some kind of helplessness or lack of control. I can filter these, and I do when I bother searching sites like Fur Affinity, but it does make it so I've never fully committed to the community. That said, I think there are some really great artists out there, and if it helps people express themselves then so much the better.
