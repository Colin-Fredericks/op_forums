    Quotes from your games that have stuck.

    01-20-2016, 08:42 AM
    So, here's something that's always fun to talk about. What are the quotes from your games that have stuck with your friend/game group? And what's the story behind them? Not necessarily OP games either, any of your games from the past.

    For instance, in our VtM game we were a few sessions in and my fiance' was sulking a bit because his character continually was seduced by the ladies, landing him in some rough spots. As we were wrapping up the ST turns to him after asking everyone else what their character learned from that sessions events. He asked my fiance what his character had learned and in a very dejected tone he muttered 'Don't trust bitches.'

    Needless to say that is now a common saying among us and one of the players even had a custom shirt made that says Don't Trust Bitches across the front with the Gangrel symbol on it. It's pretty great.

          Here's a few from a variety of games:

          "Shut up and fight the dragon!" -Me, shouting at my buddy who was whining about how I had 5 keys and he didn't have any while we were in the middle of a boss fight player Magic Sword.

          One chronicle involved needing to get past a pair of ghouls with assault rifles guarding the entrance to a building. One player says he's got a plan and everyone should follow his lead, and he sounds confident so everyone agrees. He has his character walk down the street to in front of the guys at the entrance, turns to them, and shouts "Hey you jerks... punch my butt!" (and when that only caused them to share a confused look with each other, he pulled a gun, fired a few shots at them, then ran down the street). For some reason, it became our groups fill-in phrase for hurling the best distracting insult your character can think of at someone.

          "I've got a plan that would work perfectly... if you hadn't just done that thing I told you to." -The "brains" of the group, said to the "muscle" that never hesitated to do as the brains said to do.

          "I did it!" -A phrase said in celebration... of completely failing a roll as terribly as is possible in the given circumstances. A tradition formed on the first night of gaming with a particular new player, because it is what he said and how he said it when I skipped the explanation of how THAC0 works and just said "You need to roll a 12 or higher on the d20 to hit the snake" and he proceeded to roll a 1.

          "Nice trick, [insert NPC's name, followed by awkward silence]" -Me, in character, playing a character that liked to banter during combat, and realizing mid-sentence I had completely nothing in the tank at that particular moment. My group continues to quote it (as "Nice trick, The Arbiter," because it happened to be during a game of Exalted and the NPC had some word-salad name that began that way), mostly because they are used to me being the guy in the group that is lightning fast with witty comebacks and so ready to improvise that I've not been caught with nothing to say any other times in the decade they've all known me.

          Player 1: "We came on a message of peace." Player 2: "Eugh." And because we are full grown children, a simple misspoken "on" where "with" was intended in this situation gives us near-endless laughs.

          Not so noble anymore.

                      D&D game, my character was starting to become crazy and he blamed everything on his friends.

                      "Let me get this straight. You were tortured by strange black, hooded figures, left to rot in a dungeon for days and were saved by the god of Lies and Treacheries, who told you we were to blame for it... AND YOU BELIEVED HIM!?"

                                              A DnD classic from back in the day about 25 years ago -- the only game where the following can be said with a straight face:
                                              "I climb into the dragon's sinus cavity..." We all looked at our game-mate and it took us several seconds to realize just how *utterly absurd* the imagery invoked -- let alone the action the described -- actually was... (Note: he crit'd, doing the damage necessary to finish the beast... leaving us all to carry on the wisdom of the bard who noted "When it doubt -- climb up the snout!" ) Again, only in DnD... (To clarify: the dragon was grounded and cornered in a crevasse at the side of a caldera, and circumstances made the action plausible contextual to everyone's positioning at the time... but still... only in DnD...)

                                              A DA/Ravenloft hybrid (which I've been running on-an-off since the 90s) -- a couple of the PCs had (for real-life years and decades-long game time) essentially been courtesans for a number of powerful/influential people (one of the PCs had started out as a "common", gutter-side whore). One of the public houses they occasionally frequented was "The Brazen Hussy"; this led to the occasional re-cap of past sessions starting as (in the voice of a narrator at the beginning of a TV show): "Previously, on Brazen Hussies..."

                                              "You have no idea how many dots in Faggotry I have..." Meta-in-character-but-as-the-player comment in response to what made *his* character (the character, himself) the more viable candidate for dealing with the pomp and circumstance surrounding a layered-class gathering of "higher" and "lower" born persons (mostly contextual to Vampire, but with some other splats involved), and whether or not he (the character as well as the player) would have the multi-faceted style, panache, wit and humor to handle such a gathering. (The debate ended then-and-there -- everyone was convinced that he, both the player and the character, had what it took to "make that shit *wyrq*, bytch...". A fun time was had by all...)

                                              "Can't we just *ask* the Lich to let us borrow his phylactery so we can research the link it has to the Etruscan necromantic ritual we've been trying to formulate...?" (The question was asked by the player -- who should have known better, being a gamer for many years at that point -- with completely flat intention and sense of legitimate inquiry. The players and the GM just stared at him for literally several seconds -- nobody knew how to "take" the obvious-to-us/anybody-with-any-sense facet of the situation that (apparently) "totes legit" was oblivious in the mind of the player. Needlessly to say, the answer was *no*. It did, though, become the running-joke to go "Can't we just [x] the [y] for [z] reasons?" when said statement was of equal relative absurdity to the first one that started the ball rolling...)

                                              I have been around here for waaaayyyy too fucking long...
