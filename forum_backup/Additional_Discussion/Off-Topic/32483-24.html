        I haven't posted in these forums much, mostly due to time constraints and not knowing anyone, but also a bit of unfriendliness from the old time White Wolf forums. I figured I'd make a post here though, since you guys all seem to be open and understanding. Give it a shot and all that. I feel like I'm hanging on by a fraying rope over a meat grinder that keeps taking off chunks of my wellbeing. Every time I get something figured out, another thing falls to shit. And some things, I never get a handle on. I am not trying to be melodramatic, this is how it's always been, it's just that usually, I don't talk about it because most people don't want to know that your life is a ruin. They want to chat about something asinine and then move on to their concerns, at least in my experience with real life socialization. I alternate between states of being these days, namely feeling hollowed out, or vibrant and full of life, or extremely sad. The main thing keeping me sane at this point is a combination of chain smoking and music. I have finally started to shake off and deal with my past, and now it looks like my future hangs by a mere thread. I have moved on from Montana where I grew up and had so many bad experiences to Washington state, but now am dealing with a string of shitty room mates and the potential of being homeless. I have no one to stay with if I should get evicted, which, we got our "final warning letter" today, because my current batch of room mates won't stop throwing loud parties every friday. I've tried talking to them and it's like the teacher voice in Charlie Brown to them. I don't currently have the money to move and even if I did, am in a year long lease agreement for this place. I've failed out of DigiPen so many times from depression, poor time management skills, and lack of tutoring that I have only one more semester to prove myself, at Bellevue Community College, or I lose my funding from Voc Rehab. I dreamt of being a game designer, but have yet to produce much of substance and seem to be unable to convince the design dept. at DigiPen that I can succeed. I've taken out many loans to get myself set up here, and am barely eking out a living on disability. I tried reaching out to the support groups on facebook for others with my conditions, namely schizoaffective and aspergers, but all I've gotten from that so far, for the most part, is disdain apathy or outright hostility. I have a support network, but only two of them are truly reliable, my best friend and my aunt. And they are both super busy, leaving me to mostly fend for myself these days. On the upside, I've done a lot of growing up in the last couple of years since hitting 30 and have more self awareness than I have in the past, as well as more self sufficiency. I am honestly trying my best, telling my room mates no more parties, getting a tutoring job in english to make ends meet, and "putting myself out there" socially though it is somewhat agonizing most of the time, to try and meet people and maybe even have a relationship. To paraphrase Sir Ken Robinson's Do Schools Kill Creativity?, I lived most of my life in my head and slightly to one side. I am now trying to round out my self and achieve my goals, but it never seems to be enough, like for every thing I do successfully four things go way out of wack. I don't know what to do a lot of the time, with my career goal, how to accomplish it. I am on meds with undesirable side effects, but no doctor trusts me to try tapering off of them. The worst though is the isolation, most people from any walk of life and with any world view that I've previously tried to establish rappoir with do one of three things: blow me off because I don't matter to them, look down on me or vilify me when I'm not around and make me a social pariah. I sometimes have trouble believing my life is anything more than a sick joke, with the punchline being when I die. It's been a rough couple of months, that's for sure, what with my "friend" from the aspie groups spewing hostility at me, my best friend being hostile and pissy, and smaller things have been eating away at what shaky peace of mind I have, like having a dream where I wanted to call my mom only to realize she'd been dead for almost two decades. I feel out of place in the world, almost constantly, and it seems what advice I can glean from other people has backfired in my face most of the time. Perhaps I should try meditation? I just don't know what to do, I think self reflection and discussion with others is a good start though. Sorry if this is TMI, please let me know and I'll delete it.

                                                If only I knew where to start.

                                                It is very complex, but the short version is that I have a chronic pain condition (I don't know for sure which one, but I match the majority of symptoms of fibromyalgia) that has gone undiagnosed for over half my life because when I started to feel pain non-stop my parents told me it was normal, everyone feels pain and they just have to work through it, which at first made sense because I had suffered some injuries and those could have just been residual aches. But when I never stopped feeling pain, their constant "it's normal. You're fine." and insistence that I didn't need to see a doctor left me feeling like they were telling the truth and I was just being a wimp and whining about nothing.

                                                That this is the short version has just become sad to me... anyways, I got heavy into drugs and alcohol so that I didn't have to feel the pain or sort out the feelings in regard to my parents' responses to my complaints. I've been clean and sober (excepting a few drunken nights here or there) for about 8 years now, and the pain has gotten worse and worse over those years, compounded by getting less active jobs because the pain made active jobs (which I had enjoyed the whole time I was strung out) too difficult, and then I gained weight, and the pain worsened, so I did less, gained more weight, and feel stuck because the pain is too much to push through in order to get some exercise and lose some weight to, hopefully, have less pain.

                                                Through all of that, I've also been diagnosed with bipolar disorder, though I tended to be more frequently manic, and I've avoided medicating because I don't like the person that I am on medication because, while it does take away the lows that I hate, it takes away the highs that I've come to enjoy... but even that is potentially just a side effect of the constant pain I am in, with my mania simply accompanying the days on which I am having less pain.

                                                And now, on to why I am currently locked in the lowest low that I've had for years:

                                                A few days ago my mother, who lives out of state but owns the house my girlfriend and I live in and keeps a room here so that she can have somewhere to stay when she comes to town and visits my sister, niece, and nephew, decided to express her concern for my current state - which she does every time she comes to visit, whether I want to talk or not - and the result was the two of us yelling at each other, ending the "conversation" in tears, and not having spoken to each other since.

                                                I expressed for the first time in my entire life the way that my mother makes me feel, which is to say that I feel like she has never believed a single word that I have ever said, including that she doesn't believe that I am even in any pain, and that the way she talks to me makes me feel like she thinks that I am just lazy and using any excuse I can to avoid basic responsibilities.

                                                I told her how her constant insistence of "You are so stubborn, you never even try any suggestions that people give you," reinforces my belief that she doesn't believe anything I ever say, specifically that I have tried all of the suggestions that I am telling her didn't work (note: I've never said "doing that won't help." but only ever "doing that didn't help.") and she just isn't listening to me because she is too busy thinking of what she is going to say next to actually hear anything.

                                                I'm so fucked up about it all that I actually feel guilty for getting upset at her for the way she makes me feel.... meanwhile, she has had a nice chat with my girlfriend to clarify that my mom (and I guess my dad too, since the word used was "we" rather than "I") isn't upset with her at all, just me.

I guess the shorter version is this: I am so fucking wrecked emotionally right now that I am considering rounding up all the booze in the house and having a one-man party morning, even though alcohol makes my pain dramatically worse once it wears off... the 2, maybe 3 hours of not having to fucking deal seems almost worth the 1 or 2 days of barely being able to stand.
