    Really enjoying web serials. Been reading stuff online for decades now but finding Worm opened my eyes to some other genres. I tried the writers other work and really couldn't get into it at the time, but it might just take a break from the epic that was Worm. Then there's things like My Little Ventrue or Morningwood which are probably more like straight fanfic or porn, and not as mature. But I just found the Wandering Inn. My first read through hit a block but I came back to it and finished the first 'book' the other night, it's a pretty solid story. I like the way the writer takes a more generic idea (people from the real world fallen into a fantasy RPG one, complete with levels) and spices it up, also focusing on the character and them not being harem-gatherers or chosen ones.

            Kieron Gillen's DIE is quite good. Might turn out great, but I know for sure I'm loving it so far, both for writing and art.

            The pitch is "Goth Jumanji". It's about a group of roleplayers in the early 90s (think Masquerade times: Gillen is a fan) who get spirited away into their dark fantasy game and reappear into the world months later, scarred from the experience.

            Years after that, they have to confront the past and return in.

            What I'm saying is that is both something people around here might appreciate and a good source of inspiration for Changeling.
