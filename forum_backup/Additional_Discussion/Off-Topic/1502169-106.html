            Yes, that's all that Batman is supposed to be, because Batman is also supposed to be a normal human. While the feats that he is capable of performing may seem extraordinary, it is theoretically possible for other people to achieve the same feats, if they worked for it.

            Unfortunately, the writers sometimes like to wank Batman off, by having him demonstrate pseudo-magical feats that he learned from Tibetan Monks. Or having his reflexes be elevated to such an absurd degree, he's able to catch machine gun fire with his hands.

            For reference, Myth Busters proved that the stories of martial artists catching arrows out of mid flight is just Hollywood showmanship. Even after building a machine that was capable of moving at 10X the world speed record, it was still too slow to catch a flying arrow.


            As a result, Batman's whole character concept has a bad habit of contradicting itself. He's supposed to be a normal human, but the feats he has demonstrated far exceed even the greatest of human capabilities.

                                    DarkKing, the problem with your whole thing on Batman is that you can't analyze Batman's contingency plans "realistically," because Batman isn't realistically possible. While it's true that he's not supposed to have overt superpowers, it's also undeniable that he's supposed to be beyond human in how much he can be good at at the same time. Nobody in reality could do everything Batman does. Even if they had the physical and mental potential combined with the access to obscene wealth... there's literally not enough time in the day. He might not be beyond human in an specific endeavor, but he's far, far beyond human in the number of things he's reached and maintains the pinnacle of human aptitude at.

                                    So how can you fairly state what the appropriate level of planning he's capable of? What's too extreme for a man that can balance being a CEO, a physical workout routine that would require ~15 hrs a day to maintain, a vigilante, a tutor to younger vigilantes, a member of a superhero team, and keep himself up to date on dozens of scientific fields related to forensics and psychology?

                                    He's also canonically stated that the Justice League is his primary contingency plan against both himself and any other hero that goes bad. He doesn't plan on soloing Superman as plans A to T or so (though he has managed to solo Supes at least once...), his initial plans are for the entire rest of the league to help him. Again, official stories have made it explicit that a lot of his plans are meant fail if the Justice League works together because Bruce is just as worried about going bad and wants to lose if that happens as the Justice League is unified against him.

                                    I'm sure there's some fans overstating things... but it's hard to say they're any significant group when Batman having extremely complex planning skills is canonically part of his skill set; and we've seen him pull of some seemingly absolutely insane plans in the official material successfully. If fans are just taking a version of Batman that can pull the kinds of plans they're talking about, at worst their cherry picking which Batman they like... but so is anyone that picks a Batman that's not that good at planning and nobody is actually right or wrong.
