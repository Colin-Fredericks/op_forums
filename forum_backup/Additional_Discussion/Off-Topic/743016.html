    Speculation: The future of White Wolf properties in other media

    10-30-2015, 06:17 PM
    So we all know now that Paradox Interactive bought the White Wolf properties. Presumably that gives them the right to utilize both versions of the World of Darkness and Exalted however they see fit. Let's put aside the impact this is going to have on tabletop games to ask how you'd like to see Paradox incorporate these properties elsewhere. Given that they're a gaming company, the most reasonable relationship is video game computer game adaptations, but don't limit yourself to that.

    I think Vampire: The Requiem is a perfect match for Paradox's complex 4X strategy games. The systems in games like Crusader Kings and Europa Universalis seem like they'd adapt well to a Requiem adaptation about city management, where wonky decision-making like establishing rule of law, zoning territories, and assigning political positions of both ceremonial and practical import sit front and center. Plus, that balance between setting up trusted regents while you can take a torpor nap or trying to reign longer while dealing with the effects of high blood potency create a similar dynamic to the dynasty system in Paradox's existing games. I think Damnation City would be a great blueprint for a game like that.

    Similarly, I think Block by Bloody Block could form a great basis for a Hunter: The Vigil game crafted in the X-Com model. Give us a mix of tactical and strategic gameplay about managing a compact of hunters and expanding outward to claim your home town from the creatures of the night. Combine cautious strategic management where careful expansion, practical research and recruiting, and thorough scouting of areas is necessary to make sure you send the right team with the right equipment into each conflict with brutal, lethal tactical gameplay that that ensures every decision you make in the field could be crippling.

    Both seem tailor made for user-made mods as well. What ST wouldn't want to adapt their game setting into the system for players to explore?

                                          Ok, pipe dream here, but picture an open-world single player game (I imagine Bethesda style, but Ubisoft style would work fine if that's more your cup of tea). You've got your big city sandbox, populated with humans, vampires, werewolves, mages, the works. You start out as a human, and the initial questline has you stumbling into the mysterious and spooky things that walk the city at night. And from there, the sky is the limit. You can play the whole game as a mortal caught up in the machinations of these monsters you just learned existed, take to hunting them, or even become one of them (obviously for splats that are tied to heritage there would have to be a sidequest where you discover your great grandpa was a werewolf, or whatever). Different quests become available or impossible for different splats, so there's a ton of replay value.

                                          EDIT: Sidenote, I think this would work better set in nWoD, just because nWoD is generally more crossover-friendly, and the modular approach lends itself nicely to sandbox play. But I definitely wouldn't say no to such a game set in oWoD either.
