    The problem with VCRs vs. an audio cassette is the output issue. Making a VCR that can output to most modern screens adds a lot of costs to manufacturing that cassettes don't have to deal with since audio jacks basically haven't changed in decades. It's not a technical dilemma in the sense of nobody knows how, it's just an expensive enough fix that you either need people willing to spend a lot of money on it, or a huge boom that can counter the costs with scale.
