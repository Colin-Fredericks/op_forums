I hesitate to say PTSD, but it probably is.

                                            There's one table I like to seat at in my university's food hall and that's because it's the only one where I can have my back to a non-reflective wall. Every other wall in the place is a window or a glass door to another room, and I just can't sit there without having something solid behind me. I've thought about making the complaint to the staff and while I still contemplate doing so, it isn't really something I can reasonably fault them for, because the vast majority of people aren't going to care, the people in charge of the layout and design of these buildings included.
