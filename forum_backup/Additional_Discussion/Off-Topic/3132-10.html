                                                            I have known for a long time that I will always be sick. This is not news to me, and has no ability to surprise.

                                                            I have known for even longer that my depression is both relentless and devious, and will always find new angles of attack. This is not news to me either, and has no ability to surprise.

                                                            I am therefore not exactly surprised that it's found a new approach, which I will not detail here because it's attacking me through my friends and I cannot explain that without insulting people who have done nothing to deserve it. I am not exactly surprised that this tactic is proving effective enough that I'm trying to come up with new defenses (which I can do, because for all that I like to talk about my depression as if it's some separate entity, it is a part of me and that means if it is relentless and devious and adaptable, then so am I). It's not news that always being sick means I will sometimes have to battle my symptoms. It's not surprising.

                                                            What it is is tiring. It's dismaying. It's wearying. It's, well, it's depressing. It's a reminder that the fight never really ends, that I can't ever let my guard down, that my existence will probably be defined by a seemingly endless need to find new reasons to hate myself more than I already do.

                                                            This isn't a cry for help. I'm not giving up; I'm still too humiliated from the last time I gave up to even be tempted by that idea. This is just something I hate. Now you know what I hate. It seemed apropos to the topic.
