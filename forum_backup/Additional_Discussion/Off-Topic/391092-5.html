                        The blue silver is cheaper, generally, but comparable to or better than good Cuervo silver as is their blue Gold in comparison to Cuervo gold. It's definitely sharper, though. It's got an olivey, almost nutty aftertaste, but it's a SHOT tequila. Good for a mixer, too, but it's got that "yowzah" kinda pop when poured as a simple shot.. The Blue Reposada, sometimes labeled as Sauza Blanco (round, shorter bottle) is pure BLUE agave (the others are all pure Agave, but not all the blue breed) and smoother. Good as a tequila alternative to a "sipping" whiskey.

                                                    I'm already drunk and it's not even the afternoon (a cheese seller who grew up in the same city as me back home offered me free drinks at the farmers' market I go to), so I wrote this little piece. It's meant to be given to my family in case something happens to me, but I wanna share it with someone now and you guys are fun and I like most of you, so here it is.

                                                    I grew up alone and grew old alone. I wandered across cities, countries and continents,
                                                    only to make a home out of my loneliness.

                                                    What kept me going is that I finally found a woman that tolerates how much I hate myself,
                                                    and I tolerate how much she hates herself.

                                                    I made friends out of the fools I encountered in life,
                                                    because who better to keep company to the biggest of them all?

                                                    We drink twice a week, roleplay in our imaginary worlds,
                                                    and philosophize about a better world that will never come,
                                                    because people are rotten and we, the dreamers, were the most rotten
                                                    of the lot.

                                                    Spent my early adulthood trying to hold onto a couple of ideals,
                                                    until I grew to resent them and the scars they left on my psyche.

                                                    I wrote stories. Stories where hope emerges victorious, only once
                                                    it has transformed itself into hopelessness.

                                                    I witnessed God, I truly, honestly did, in one of my dreams.
                                                    It was the purest, strongest experience I ever had.

                                                    So naturally I pushed Her away, until we met again,
                                                    once I discovered magick and the occult,
                                                    and gambled my mental health,
                                                    one ritual and evocation at a time.

                                                    Somehow I survived and realized that nothing matters.
                                                    Life, the individual, this is all just a side-effect of consciousness
                                                    as we understand it.

                                                    Our lives are there just because the Universe needed one more
                                                    variable in the algorithm. If we are the Universe experiencing itself,
                                                    or the Universe was created by us, I do not know.
                                                    By the time me, or anyone else finds the answer,
                                                    it will be too late to do anything with it anyway.
                                                    Because behind that door lies either the All, or Nothing and non-existence.
                                                    And it won't make a damn difference either way.

                                                    Greece By Night LARP
