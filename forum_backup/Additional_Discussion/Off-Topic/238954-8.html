                    I'm thinking about the tour. I really didn't put much thought into the name Cute Little Roadkill, but I liked it enough to keep it and get it booked.
                    Iowa is where I'm starting. It was a choice driven, in all honesty, by one enthusiast (I don't have fans) who wanted me to play his home state. That was enough for me. The reception from the close knit Iowa comedy scene is encouraging. I'm hoping we can wind our way through Nebraska, Colorado, Utah, Idaho and finally Oregon. That's the plan.

                    The first show is a month+ off, but that's not a lot, really. I'm getting ready to be crazy and just live my dream. That's something I've surprised myself with. I'm taking a huge huge financial risk, but a big part of me refuses to be scared.

                                I love comedians. Being raised by a tv and a public library instilled in me a respect for the written and spoken word. Granted, my voice is terrible and I write like a drunken macaque, but let's ignore that for now. I want to move forward. Not just conversationally.

                                I want some actual progress and not the crazy bullshit that I see daily on every screen. The world never made total sense to me, but I have to admit, teenage rebellious me couldn't hold a candle to how frustrated I get with life these days. That's like comparing a chihuahua to a werewolf.

                                Comics, to me, are people who should live to speak their minds and lift spirits. You know that scene in the movie Fallen where Denzel Washington's character says "Cops are the chosen people"? It might seem narcissistic of me, but that's how I see comics, writers and artists.
                                ...and then I see the compromises in personal values for shallow reasons, the breakdown of basic logic just for attention, the infighting..
                                It makes my heart break, and it drains a little of my faith in the craft, and humanity. Just a little.

                                We're supposed to make people laugh. Help a crowd have a good time. Hit your smile button.
