              In 2010, Brazilians elected an actual professional clown (a well-known, celebrity comedian) as a congressman. Boosted by his comedic popularity and by a deep anti-political streak that exists in here, he received more votes than any other congressional candidate and was elected to Brazil's equivalent of the House of Representatives. That massive number of protest votes backfired in that it brought along several other members of his party, due to the way votes for a given party are taken into account in our system - which was probably their plan all along.

              Quotes from the campaign's TV spots include:

              "What does a federal deputy do? I don't really know. But vote for me, and I'll tell you!"

              "I promise to help those in need...especially my own family."

              He even went by his stage name during the campaign.

              His election was noted by international media and appeared in a Cracked article. So, here is the question: What is the clown's stage name?
