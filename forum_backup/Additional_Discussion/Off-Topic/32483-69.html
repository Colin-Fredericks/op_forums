                                                Music. LOUD music, dont just listen to it, LISTEN to it focus on the lyrics, try to spin a story in your mind around the song, or find its meaning or what it means to you.

                                                Going for a walk outside. Depression can easily get worse if you lock yourself away, you'd be surprised how refreshing just going outside for a few minuets can be mentally.

                                                ALWAYS get a good night of sleep every night.                                                 When you find a negative thought, Stop, acknoledge it to yourself its a valid thought ignoring your thoughts is just bottling those thoghts up. I'd do the above, then maybe spin that thought into something like: "I'm not useless, I'm a good mother, whose daughter clearly loves and finds her useful."

                                                If its just an emotion not a thought, breath, focus on it, focus on WHY you feel that way. You may not know, it may not even have a reason. If you find a reason for it, what is that reason? Once you find it, think of ways to spin it to something postive. Like: Say i'm feeling angry after a long day. I dont know why i'm mad, so i focus on it, I realize i'm mad because nothings gone right all day." Well shit cant have that. PROVE it wrong. Such as "Actually, I did get to my appointment on time, and am healthy. I didnt burn dinner. " etc in that case find all the reasons as to why that statement was a lie.
