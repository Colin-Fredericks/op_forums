                            There is a sharp difference in the atmosphere and culture between the old forums and here...but when I think about whether I should be nostalgic about it or not, I have to pause.

                            I mean, don't get me wrong-I loved the old forums. I felt constantly engaged on those forums in a way I don't here. Even when people were wrong about things (and they really could be just straight up wrong), there was something about arguments that was vivifying. It was electric, and I felt involved. I had days where I could sit around just waiting for a counter rebuttal because I wanted to say more and just needed a catalyst. I feel like everyone was just more awake.

                            A large part of that was the people there who are no longer with us, who I do really miss-folks like Sikker, Devilfish, Loxo, Demetrius and Omicron being more active, so on and so forth. We had a some really cool, smart, and passionate people. Again, don't get me wrong, I like people in today's forums. They just don't have that edge some of the old crew did.

                            That said, it was a fucking contentious time. I mean, nowhere near as bad as, say, 2004 to 2007, and I can't even begin to imagine what pre-2004 White Wolf looked like on the forums. But in comparison to now, it was still a lot more fighting than goes on. There were rivalries between people, something I haven't quite seen here-remember Sikker and Brainbug? There was a lot more straight up meanness going on. I know one of the major goals of the move was to cut down on that to make it a more open community, and for better or for worse, I think it works.

                            Also, remember Lady Catherine? Silentblood? Blood Lore? The old forums permitted a lot more dickheadery than this one does, and we had some truly noxious people there who could at least get their shit out there for a while before someone did anything, and some really went the mile with it.

                            I suppose what I'm saying is that, while I get times of nostalgia, I don't really miss it. THese forums are, admittedly, more somber and melancholy, but I think that's better than the active contention that went on. I might miss it most when we still have large arguments(Looking at you,Beast the Primordial 0.9 discussion), because while it fits the scale of the old engaging arguments, it just lacks the flavor and zest of the old guard going at it that made it worth it. But on the normal days? Nah.

                            There were good times, but I think we're in a better place now.

                            Kelly R.S. Steele, Freelance Writer(Feel free to call me Kelly, Arcane, or Arc)
                            The world is not beautiful, therefore it is.-Keiichi Sigsawa, Kino's Journey
                            Feminine pronouns, please.

I guess what saddened me about it wasn't the things he posted (they never bothered me, personally, because I never got into an argument with him), but rather watching a person -- who, albeit, I had only discussed things with online, as he and Isobelle were ex-pats living in Japan -- slowly over a period of a couple or so years lose the open-mindedness that he once had in favor of a level of religious fanaticism that just... was just disheartening. (Gone, and Ophid -- I believe -- should remember Mordwyr and Isobelle....)

I knew it even a year ago, but it has slipped my mind now, completely. It's sad because of someone having ended their own life, in the broader aspect, and personally sad because now I realize I've forgotten his name.
