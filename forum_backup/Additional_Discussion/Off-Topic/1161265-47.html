            By the way, I'm curious about something regarding 5th edition. I've been wanting to get into the game, but the nearest book store that carries anything is like 15 miles from my location.

            One thing that’s cool about 5th Edition is that a decent chunk of the rules are are available for free. If you want to try it out before committing a lot of time and money, check out the free Basic Rules first.

            Races available in the Basic Rules are Dwarf (Hill variety only), Elf (High variety only), Halfling (Lightfoot variety only), and Human.

            The PHB races are Dwarf (in Mountain and Hill varieties), Elf (in High, Wood, and Dark varieties), Halfling (in Lightfoot and Stout varieties), Human (including a variant option), Dragonborn, Gnome (in Rock and Forest varieties), Half-Elf, Half-Orc, and Tiefling.

            Also available in a free PDF is the Elemental Evil Player’s Companion, which includes playable Aarakocra, Deep Gnomes, Genasi, and Goliath.

            Other stuff comes from supplements.

It's Jim Gordon, shouting to Batman "Bring him in by the book!" after Joker tortured him and shot his daughter. He'll only abandon the "by the book" mentality when it is absolutely necessary, for maintaining his ethical stance. Conversely, the chaotic good, in my eyes, doesn't want to tear down society and systems just for the sake of it (and I won't go into anarchism here, because that's a HUGE topic). She just doesn't keep them in high regard, more like seeing them as necessary inconveniences at best. She just trusts his own moral compass above external rules. Drizzt, for example is a CG characer, officially and he keeps community in high regard and recognizes that community needs rules. He just also recognizes that he isn't really cut for it and lives the half-hermit life of a ranger instead. Also, when push comes to showe, he doesn't give a half-fuck for the rules and does what he perceives as just and right.
