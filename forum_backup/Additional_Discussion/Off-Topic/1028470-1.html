                    Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                    That is a pretty cool metaphor. The only part where it breaks down for me is that it kind of treats the light side and the dark side as different forces - different waters, as it were. While this may or may not be the case in-setting, I have always preferred the philosophies that say the force is just the force, and light or dark is about how you use it.

                    The Sith Academy on Korriban (no longer canon, but still a good source of insight) taught that the force is powered by emotion. Which if true would explain why the dark side is the easier route to greater force mastery. By letting yourself be ruled by your basest, most powerful emotions as the Sith do, you open the floodgates and channel the force with great ease. But by keeping careful control of all emotions as the Jedi do, you leave the well of force energy to dry, unless you continually dig deeper and deeper for a few more drops of moisture.
                    I haven't read the rest of the thread yet, but I love this and completely agree. I believe it HAS been recanonized that not everyone does ascribe to the philosophy of The Living Force (including Palpatine), which I like very much. I'm not especially interested in those black and white questions, I'm a lot more interested in the actions people take and why we should characterize them as good and evil based on those actions. For example, obviously, Anakin murdering all of those children was disgusting and horrific and terrible. The Jedi Order using slave labor to power the GAR was also disgusting and horrific and terrible, and I typically think of the Jedi Order labeling themselves as "good" and following the "light", to dismiss any sort of criticism about their behavior.

                                Personally, I think the Force isn't alive or sentient in anyway we would understand. There's also probably no Light side or Dark side. To me it's just a Tool that connects and corespondents to random people's Will and Desires allowing said people to achieve what they want through single-minded Willpower*. That's why the Jedi were so obsessed with getting rid of distracting temptations and desires that might lead a Padawan's Focus away from the organization's singular purpose of ensuring peace in the Republic. A fractured Will results in a weakened and divided connection to the Force, just look at Kylo Ren and his temptation to the "Light" and his family as an example of this. The Sith get this as well and just replace the Jedi's singular Focus of "protecting the Republic" with a singular Focus of getting power, and perhaps more importantly freedom**, throwing all other concerns like empathy for others and intergalactic peace to the wayside on account of their distracting nature. That's why dogmatic traditions like the Jedi, the Sith, the Imperial Knights and even the Grey Jedi to some extent exist. Scatter-brained ideologies that don't Focus on a singular pursuit be it Wisdom, Power, Peace, Freedom, etc just don't result in strong Force users. The fractured self is a weakened self in essence.

                                * So, yeah, I think they're basically less powerful Mages Ascension-style.

                                ** Actually the Sith have a real interesting philosophy in that I don't think domination of others is their primary goal. I think building an authoritarian Sith Empire is just a step towards their real goal of achieving completely unfettered freedom from everyone and everything, including death. In the end goal any true Sith to me should be narcissistic apotheosis and perhaps even solipsism.
