                A little of both, in my opinion.
                As human beings, sentient creatures, we are given the ability to choose and the will to extend beyond ourselves. This is what being human means to me, and it is most definitely a good thing, even if it can lead to some darker outcomes.

                Is there a god, or are we just monkeys who've been making it up as we've gone along? If there is a god, why don't I feel their presence? If their isn't, why do I feel like they should be there?
