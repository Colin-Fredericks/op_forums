            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post

            I don't know what specifically you are referring to, but this is definitely untrue. Upgrading government systems costs a lot. It's not as simple as just buying the newest hardware* - you need to update software, migrate old data, check that everything got migrated properly, manually fix errors. You have to update the infrastructure around those systems. You have to have to minimize the downtime where old system is already offline and new system is still offline. There's a reason a lot of financial institutions and universities still use KOBOL. Updating things is expensive and hard. An iphone is pocket change by comparison.

            * And that's before we raise the question of whether the updates are actually needed. If it works - don't fuck with it.
