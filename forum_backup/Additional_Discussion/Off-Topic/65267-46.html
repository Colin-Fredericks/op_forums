        I honestly don’t remember if I went over my pull list from last week, so if I missed it..whatever let’s get drunk.

        Walking Dead #156: Going to be a big issue me thinks. Did Negan do what he did to help Rick or to help himself? People say he was the worst villain in Walking Dead, I disagree. The Governor from the comics was WAY more sadistic, (TV show didn’t do the character any justice IMO,) and did things for his own perverse pleasure. Negan did things for a reason, mostly to set examples, and this issue proves that.

        Tokyo Ghost #8: The buildup is coming, will this end on a happy note?

        Superman: Coming of Supermen #6: Last issue, had a lot of hopes for this mini series. Didn’t really live up to it. I’d avoid this even in Trades unless you are a diehard Superman fan.

        Superman #2: We get the father son team up and it feels pretty good. Someone also makes a cameo at the end, the 90’s are making a comeback.

        Silver Surfer #5: Always liked Surfer, but I just can’t get into what Slott is doing. This issue pretty much is him admitting he didn’t know the characters origin and thumbing his nose at what really made the Surfer the character he was.. Regret and Loneliness. That’s right kids, it wasn’t always about the Power Cosmic.

        The Punisher #3: Still like it, don’t care what other people think.

        Moon Knight #4: I still like the series even though every issue ends in a plot twist. We need some foundation here.

        Justice League Rebirth: The Bad art from JLA meets a story of giant space ticks. Don’t they know a movie is coming out in a few years?

        Green Lanterns #2: It was ok, Red Lanterns are on Earth spreading the Rage.

        4001AD-Shadowman: Decent one shot story that really doesn’t feel like a tie in to the event. If you like Shadowman, (or a concept of an alternate version of the character,) and don’t want to invest on the whole event..this could be worth your 4 bucks.

        Aquaman #2: Good story, but Manta would so kill him, just sayin.

        Batman #2: Cool story, are they implying the new characters are Kryptonians? Anyway Hugo is bringing his Monster Men from Wagners stories.

        Civil War 2-X-Men #2: Not bad really, things continue to shift between the X-Teams. Lot of drama but little action.

        Daredevil/Punisher #3: Was ok, could be a lot worse

        Green Arrow #2: Honestly, this was really good.

        What did everyone else get?

            Picked Ms Marvel #8, its a shame that otherwise solid books have to get dragged into Civil War II.

            However i suspect that either the writters are drunk or they just dont get the concept of "morally ambiguous" between Thanos in Civil Wars and this one is just jarring.

            So between the race and background of Ms Marvel i knew exactly how this comic was gonna go even before i open it. She was gonna side with Carol Danver but in the end she is gonna side with Iron man because profiling is wrong.

            However this comic deals with the first part of the formula. Kamala accepts one of the visions of Ullyses and her with a new team stopped a guy who was driving a stolen tank that was fated to blow up and kill lots of innocent.

            So then kamala goes home and gets a talk by her sister in law about how profiling is bad and if they charge that guy from a crime that he might do then crime would have to be redefined and something about "though-on-crime" that happened in greenville.

            Which is fine except....im pretty sure that stealing a TANK, driving said tank on the street and crushing cars parked are all crimes. Thats not arresting a guy for a crime that he might do, he was already doing lots of crimes.

            Which is even more confusing because next "scene" Ms marvel grabs a guy who, by the predictions, was gonna rob a grocery store. Which is a pretty good example of the previous issue. I mean if the grocery store robber scene was put before the "talk" then it would have drive the point home but as its stand im thinking someone confuse the order of the pages on this one.

                        I thought this was amusing.

                        Though I can't see how they could honestly believe their new books like 'Occupy Avengers,' grim and gritty Avengers, and 'Slapstick' would keep everyone hyped. I admit fifteen years ago a Solo title would have gotten me on board easy.

                        New Super-Man was surprisingly good. I like the character, and the supporting cast so far. DC has had a good record of starting comics in international settings, even Batwing started good. Hoping it stays up.

                        Detective Comics had a nice twist, too. If we're not getting simple detective work this looks like a suitable replacement. I'd still prefer not to have Tim Drake but he's not as omnipotent as he was depicted before.

                        Nightwing: Rebirth was solid but I think sort of jumped the gun in killing a certain character. As good as it seemed, when a comic starts by eradicating aspects of the past often end up replacing it with something not as good.

                        The Paybacks has a new series out, too, which is great. It's a comic about a world of superheroes and villains where, basically, a mysterious entity named Mr. Pierce acts as a moneylender for would-be superpeople, offering loans so they can build their death ray or support their crime-fighting lifestyle. If they default, however, they're legally obligated to join the Paybacks, Mr. Pierce's own team of meta-individuals who act as repo men. It's a fun comic, pretty unique though it obviously takes a lot of jabs at existing super universes. It's descended from another comic, Buzzkill, which was about an ex-superhero trying to go through AA after quitting the life, because his power involves him gaining special abilities when ingesting alcohol or other drugs.
