                    Today, I saw an advanced screening of the film It.

                    ​I've got to say, while I'm somebody with no attachment to the original (although familiar with it through the Nostalgia Critic review) and not much for horror movies in general (no objection, just not much attraction), I found this film to be pretty damn great.
