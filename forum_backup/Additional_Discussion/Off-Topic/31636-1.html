                                                        Not to disparage a game company, but FFG excels at finding ways to relieve you of your funds. Granted, the end result is usually a high quality product and a lot of fun.

                                                        I resisted buying EotE for that very reason, the cost, until last week. I had some store credit at my FLGS, so I used it to defer some of the cost. So far, I am still digging through the crunch, but I am very impressed with the system.

                                                        If you are on the fence, I suggest you check out the Edge of the Empire Beginner Game. It has the basic rules, pregens, adventure material, and a set of dice. Amazon has it listed for $23.95. The set has everything you need to play a few sessions.

                                                        If you can find them, there are also sticker sheets out there to convert normal dice into EotE dice.

                                                        I game. This is the way.
