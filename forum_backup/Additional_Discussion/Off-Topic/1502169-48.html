Or are you saying that the Sisters of Battle faction in WH40k needs to have more male models in its army for the sake of diversity? I don't think Sisters of Battle players would be happy if you told them that there's this unit of prayer-brothers or something that's part of their army now.

    Also, with Custodes you have your diversity right there because in reality they're Custodes + Sisters of Silence. Of course, you don't see a lot of Sisters of Silence because one, their rules aren't very competitive, two, their models are quite old and/or not very impressive and three their model range is quite limited. From a financial standpoint, I get the idea of female Custodes... these Custodes probably sell way more than the Sisters of Silence models. But it's not like GW is really trying to sell people on fielding Sisters of Silence armies.

    And funny thing is... GW already did that. The Stormcast Eternals in Age of Sigmar are fantasy Space Marines and now it doesn't matter who's in the armor-suit, male or female. They are these souls from dead people who are particularly virtuous and Sigmar as a God plucks them out and puts them into armor-suits to fight for him. Those are the Stormcast Eternals. Their tragedy is that while they're definitely on the side of good in their warfare, every time they die a little bit of their humanity dies and yet Sigmar will revive them and send them into battle again untile they are hollowed out husks. And at that point, another soul gets grabbed to fill the armor.

                        Well, I would say that for the sake of being more gender-sensitive, it would be good to get away from using "He/Him" as a default in general. Especially in light of the lore-change, things like alternating pronouns or using they/them would also help in establishing the new idea that Custodians are both men and women now. Additionally, what would have also cemented the idea would have been the release of a model of a named character who is a female Custodian alongside the Codex-release. As shown by this current situation, these lore-changes sometimes can come hard and fast. Having a model that showcases the lore-change would be a great help. Otherwise, there's always the potential of a couple years down the line when the 11th edition Codex comes out that GW is just like "Now that we've thought about it... let's not do the female Custodes thing after all.". At the end of the day, the only real thing GW cares about is selling models, so the one way where you can be sure GW won't change its mind again is if a lore-change is tied to an opportunity to sell more models.

                        Originally posted by TravisLegge View Post
                        If that's the case then they absolutely changed the lore, therefore the old lore is outdated. That's my point.
                        Which brings us right back to the beginning. It's a lore-change. And my argument is that they should've done a better job of worldbuilding. You could've added to the lore that recruiting female Custodes is a new thing. And if it's a retcon, they should've done an article on their website like they usually do. Like, when at the start of 9th they decided to turn back the clock in the setting and now instead of "present-day" being after the Indomitus Crusade, the new "present day" would be at the very beginning of the Indomitus Crusade. And then they made Guy Haley rewrite two novels to get those in line with that change. They have done big retcons in the past and usually, they're not shy about saying out loud that they're doing that. For whatever reason, they didn't do that this time.

                        Additionally the natural follow-up of "There have been female Custodes from the very beginning." is "So why haven't there been female Space Marines from the very beginning?". You kinda have to look then at all the Emperor-stuff and rework it accordingly. You have to bring it all in, female Thunder-Warriors, female Primarchs and female Custodes. Because it doesn't make sense for the Emperor to make no exceptions in who's getting taken to be changed for one of those factions but not the others. And if he's aware of this, why would he insist on making the Primarchs all male?

                        And the all-male-thing is dumb. That's the point of the lore and all the writing in the novels you get on that these days. You're taking children and reforge them on a cellular level to be transhuman warriors. Who gives a shit what gender that child has, right? But they kept the all-male thing because it was the established lore from the 90s and the way it gets addressed these days in the fiction is to call it out as a dumb idea.

                        "Female Space Marines" is of course the true Pandora's Box here. I think if they want female Custodes to be a thing and maintain that, they should do a retcon and introduce female Space Marines next. With that new status-quo in mind, that just makes sense. But of course, the outcry from the usual suspects would be ten times louder if they were to cross that Rubicon.
