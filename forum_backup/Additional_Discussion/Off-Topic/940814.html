                                                  Originally posted by One Vorlon View Post
                                                  Looks cool - although I'm afraid I don't get the reference. What's it from?
                                                  It's not really referencing anything, the website I linked to is where it's from. The SCP foundation is kind of like a themed creepypasta site. All of the "stories" are written wiki-style, as entries in a catologue of unusual and often apparently supernatural objects, creatures, and phenomena under the supervision of a fictional organization called The SCP Foundation. I've heard it compared to the show Warehouse 13.

                                                  The foundation kind of resembles a Hunter: the Vigil Consipiracy, dedicated to Securing, Containing, and Protecting these objects, creatures, and phenomena, which they refer to as SCPs. They have a vague but seemingly nearly limitless amount of resources at their disposal, and maintain numerous facilities in which they keep the SCPs. Some of them are extremely dangerous, others are merely strange but more or less harmless. Some are sentient people with unusual abilities.

                                                  The series is really awesome and I'd definitely recommend giving a few SCPs a read. Some of them are a little hit and miss, but you can easily find best-of lists for them, and there are multiple YouTube channels dedicated to doing dramatic readings of popular entries. There have also been multiple fan-made video games based on it, including one where you try to escape one of the facilities during a containment breach. Markiplier did a huge series on it.
