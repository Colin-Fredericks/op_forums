                        1) Don Rosa doesn't get enough credit
                        2) Scrooge/Duck Tales stuff have a tendency to show up randomly. For instancehis secretary Miss Quackfaster and butler (Battista in italian, Albert in swedish) when the writer feels like it while sometimes the newphews are hardcore scouts and sometimes they're just normal kids. Part of the reason is that the comics are written by entirely different companies. The earliest ones were written by Walt Disney's Comics and Stories however they stopped producing them in 1984. Some of them are written by Scandinavian Egmont, some of them are written by Disney Italia (Who among other things invented Paperinik, Battista and Qu-Qu 7*) while some come from Netherlands/France/Germany.
                        3) Fun fact, he's called Steel-Donald in the Swedish version, yes his secret and real identity have the same name.

                        *Qu-Qu 7 is his secret agent identity where he does missions for Scrooge James Bond style.
