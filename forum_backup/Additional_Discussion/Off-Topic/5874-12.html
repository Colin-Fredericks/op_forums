                    Just binged-watched yesterday the second season of Penny Dreadful -- it had its flaws (the narrative "dips" become perhaps more noticeable when viewing the episodes back-to-back-to-back), and a few specific bits could have been handled better, but overall I was as happy with it as I was with the first season (and its similar flaws). The third episode, though, with Patti Lupone? -- Fucking Amaze-Balls!!! She was just fantastic. She and Eva Green had an instant chemistry that rarely happens in a single episode of an ongoing series (especially when one person is just a guest star who -- for built-in narrative reasons -- will almost certainly never be on the show again).

                    While the flaws of the show can be slightly annoying (Dorian's role this season left his character feeling more two-dimensional than last season; some plot points get dropped for couple episodes or more at a time that diminishes their comparative relevance rather than strengthening them -- "whetting the viewers' appetites", as it were; a couple characters get influenced in ways that are "questionable" relative to how on-guard they all are supposed to be given what is indicted in-story and by said characters, themselves; et al.), the tone, ambiance and the casting (*particularly* Eva Green, Josh Hartnett and Timothy Dalton, with Billie Piper giving good turn this season as her character is significantly different/changed from last season, and others) more than makes up for the short-comings. I'd recommend it for anybody who likes period-setting supernatural/horror, and particularly for anyone WoD-minded (the show is very NWoD-feeling in the context that the supernatural clearly exists, but it is more low-key/powered compared to CWoD).
