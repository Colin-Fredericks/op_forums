    01-15-2014, 05:31 AM
    I was surprised that no one had brought this thread back yet. Well, I'm fixing that.

    ​My sex-life is currently at a standstill, but I did have an interesting experience this afternoon.

    I recently started playing a solo game of Scion with a friend. My character is a lanky, rather nerdy Hispanic college student living in Seattle, WA. His first real mission as a Scion of Quetzalcoatl was to "look into" a less than reputable Auto body shop. The order (suggestion?) came with very little background information, so I just went there and tried to scope the place out.

    Anyway, I ended up making chitchat with a mechanic who worked at said shop and, due to his misunderstanding my meaning (or perhaps reading too far into what I was saying), he assumed I was coming on to him and invited me into the shop for some "alone time". I had casually mentioned that my mouth tends to get me in trouble when I have nothing to do. He took this as an offer for impromptu oral sex.

    ​Ok, so at this point, I had no solid idea of where my character stood, in terms of sexual orientation. What did I do? I rolled a d10 to decide if he was gay, straight or bisexual. Why? I have no clue. Anyway, the dice decided that my Scion was comfortably bisexual, so t figured "why not?" and ended up going down on what turned out to be a Centaur. After that, I was at a loss as to what to do next, but I remembered that the Centaur/mechanic had mentioned he was losing time on his lunch break.

    Long story short: Instead of investigating the place for strange occurrences, I ended up orally pleasuring a Centaur and going on a rather enjoyable lunch-date with him. My Storyteller was amused and completely baffled by my actions. So am I.​

My mother had a few friends from work who were gay, and he made it clear that he disapproved of my mom socializing with them. My issue is, the thought of penetrating someone else, orally or otherwise, is a HUGE turnoff for me. I really dislike the thought of someone coming into contact with my penis. I'm told that I'm a lot like my mom, and the similarities in our mannerisms are actually more pronounced than those of my sister and our mom. The same goes for my aunt and grandmother.
