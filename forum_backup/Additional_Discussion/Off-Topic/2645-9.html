                From a logical and thinking-about-actual-history-and-actual-human-behavior perspective you are likely right, Sorwen. But fiction doesn't always go with that and I'd say the source material disagrees you assertion*.

                Take any Star Wars thing set at any** point in the multiple thousand years of the timeline and, while empires (and republics) may rise and fall, overall the technological level is nearly the same thoroughly.
                Innovations can be found here and there, some technologies are seemingly lost with time (as one could expect in 1000s of years) but to a large extent the most pervasive technologies like droids, blasters, vehicles*** and so on stay just about the same at all points.

                *Disclaimer: my knowledge of the Expanded Universe is limited. There are a lot of novels I have not read, games I have not played and so on. So I may have missed things that make my statement false. Feel free to correct me.

                **Except maybe some things from very early in the timeline, though only the early Lightsabers with their external battery belts come to mind. But from well-established old Republic onward, things get static.

                ***The Star Wars Saga RPG had a clever little way of sneaking-in some advancement in Starships without making play at earlier eras feel much different. Stats for ships from, say, Old Republic and "modern" ones were comparable, but if you flew an ancient ship in "current" times, that ship got a template that made it worse in comparison to the newer model. Still, slight improvements aside, it is still the same technologies: large blasters, tractor beams, the same sort of missiles, no noticeable difference in types of sensors...

                            As far as the tech goes, tracing the development of such things is hard enough just dealing with one species over 6 thousand years; doing it for multiple alien races, over an entire galaxy for tens of thousands of years?!
