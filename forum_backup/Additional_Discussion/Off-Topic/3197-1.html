                                        Spoilers below, as expected.

                                        S4E1-2: Princess Twilight Sparkle

                                        Omigod omigod omigod!!!!!1!

                                        Yeah, had to get that out of the way. I am excited for this season. Not as excited as I may have been other times, but god damn it, it's ponies. I just love the fact that they're back.

                                        And the introductory episode was wonderful. I've said before that FiM isn't too good at doing the adventure type of episodes. This might be attributed to the fact that in the end, it's still a kid's show, and the type of adventure that entails isn't necessarily that impressive for someone who's watched Game of Thrones. But this might very well be the best adventure episode they've done yet. They actually did manage to establish a form of uncertainty about the outcome by kidnapping Celestia and Luna, and I could see half of season 4 being about Twilight learning the pricess trade the hard way. They didn't go that route, and I didn't really think they would either, but it wasn't entirely unrealistic. Well done on that.

                                        This episode also played excellently on tying things back to what we know, and by adding to it. I never even imagined where the Elements of Harmony came from in the first place, but the idea that they're some kind of fruit is not only utterly hilarious to me but also raises questions I'm sure will be developed in depth in fanfics. It also gave a quick refresher on why Discord is infinitely more dangerous than he lets on. I did quite like that. I wonder how many other little time bombs that's been ticking for 1000 years are spread around Equestria. One thing I was not a big fan of is how the episode dealt with Nightmare Moon; turns out Celestia defeated her in about three minutes. Again, we have the problem with rushing things in this show. In a more dedicated adventure show, how epic would the Nightmare Wars have been to delve into? Hella epic, is how. But I'm willing to flat out selectively disregard the flashbacks, to be honest. It's not likely to come up again in the show anyway.

                                        We don't get many friendship moments we haven't honestly already seen, but I like how Applejack takes the fore a lot in this episode. It feels entirely appropriate to her persona, and I'd not object towards making her the voice of reason in an even bigger way than she already is. Otherwise, amusing interactions between the mane six are fairly limited, which is kind of a bummer.

                                        One thing I noted was that Discord prided himself on having given Twilight "a princess lesson" towards the end. That's... wrong. Applejack gave her a princess lesson; the idea that you shouldn't put the only remaining alicorn princess at risk is entirely correct. A leader is needed. Turn out, of course, that sticking to her friends worked out in this case, but as much as they have said that not much will change between the mane six, I'm looking forward to seeing if they'll pull the royal duty card at any other points ahead.

                                        One thing that did change in this episode was, of course, the relinquishing of the Elements of Harmony. This is highly interesting. The status quo is broken, and suddenly there's a whole new threat to keeping Discord around. As has been seen previously in the episode, he is not to be trusted. I still think he's running a long con here. Remains to be seen.

                                        And, of course, that delicious locked box. I hope they'll return to that more throughout the season than they did in the last season with the book with Starswirl's spell in it (ie, one second flash in the first episode and then not a word about it until the last). They made it pretty clear it'd be a returning thing, though, so I have pretty high hopes.

                                        All in all, hell yes. Strong start with only a few annoyances. We're back on track. Here we go!
