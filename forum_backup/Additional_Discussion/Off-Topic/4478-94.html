                                Have you seen Nobilis or Whispering Vault? Both game lines ran with the same basic idea, and took it in radically different directions. Might give you some ideas on how to keep yours distinct.

                                Nobilis is set in the modern day, where the players take on the role of champions/slaves of an angel/demon/god/embodiment-of-human-nature/giant-serpent-monster, seeking to advance their patron's portfolio (say "The Moon", "Capitalism", and "Triangles"). . . in the midst of a behind-the-scenes war against the Creator's enemies. I'm not sure how playable the game actually is, but the metaphysics of the setting are this gloriously convoluted mix of animist, Judeo-Christian, and Norse beliefs.

                                Whispering Vault, on the other hand, is a time travelling game of demigods. Players take on the role of former humans "liberated" from their flesh, and tasked with protecting history from the Unbidden (basically fallen angels who want to re-write reality into their own image). PCs can end up at any point in history, and the game can get very dark, very fast (as the Unbidden aren't bound by scientific principles or human ethics in their . . . "improvements" to the world)

                                    Thanks for the suggestions. I'll look them up. However, I'm really going for the same kind of epic fantasy feel of Exalted, with a Science Fantasy/Cyberpunk twist as well. The setting is a place called the Divine Plane, in which various humans can be turned into heroes and villains called "Ascended". They even manipulate Divinity (the setting's equivalent to Essence) with little magical tricks called "Motifs" (which are pretty much Charms). There's even an equivalent to the goddamn Primordials, called the Aspects (though they are still very different).

                                    Honestly, the thing about this setting is that each Ascended type is inspired by a weird mix of the various Exalted types. The Solar and Abyssal analogues share a lot in common with the Infernals (their Motifs come from the themes of each Aspect like how the Infernal charms come from the various Yozi's, and the Abyssal analogues are straight-up pawns of the Aspects who want them to turn the whole world into hell). The closest guys to Lunars have Sidereal influence as well (they're a bunch of astrology-using rogues who live on the edge of society and are powered by the stars). There's two kinds of Ascended similar to the Dragon-Blooded, one of which mixes them with the Sids (they live in a separate realm, enforce order, and are the best martial artists...but have the weakest Motifs and abilities, use gemstones for armor and weapons, are the most numerous, and are in a giant propaganda-infested state overrun with corruption), and the others are more like the DB's that show up in the Modern shard from SoED.

                                    Then we have the zombie demigods (Abyssals?) who implant themselves with grotesque artificial organs and body parts to use Motifs (Alchemicals?). And the robot-servants who are...yeah, they're pretty much Alchemicals except they're meant to be servants instead of heroes in their own right.

                                    Man, this world is a clusterfuck when you look at it.
