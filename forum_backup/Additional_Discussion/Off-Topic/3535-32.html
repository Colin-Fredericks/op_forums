                                07-09-2015, 10:39 PM
                                It was somewhere between 8 and 9.5 grams.

                                It was the longest and most psychologically jarring trip of my life.

                                I'm alright now, but things were iffy for awhile. Thanks for the concern, Thorbesy. =) it's appreciated. *bro hug*

                                Ok, I'll dispense with the dramatics. I took the things for medicinal purposes, believe it or not. There's a lot of anecdotal evidence that psilocybin can help with depression, sometimes for weeks or months after a trip. I've experienced the phenomenon before, in my late teens. I realize it sounds a bit extreme, but I've tried dozens of mood stabilizers and antidepressants over the years and nothing else has ever had an appreciable and helpful effect. I was desperate.

                                So far, things are feeling better. I'm not experiencing the extreme moodswings that usually pepper my day. I'm not contemplating the sadness of existence. I'm just here. Being a person. It's very unusual for me. I like it.

                                About the actual trip, for the curious:

                                It started with music becoming AMAZING. Music was existence.
                                During a Hard House mix, I might have not had a physical body.

                                Then things got spooky. I suddenly forgot that the rest of my house existed, and for most of the trip labored under the delusion that my bedroom and bathroom were actually a seaside cottage of some sort, Jaws meets Ernest Hemingway.

                                I lost chunks of time, but remember that I failed miserably at eating an apple.
                                Afterwards, that apple was a weird piece of modern art for me.

                                Then I alternately hugged my pillow and sat in front of my fan, depending on how uncomfortable I was.

                                I quoted Lethal Weapon numerous times during my more lucid moments, because I'm apparently too old for this shit.

                                At some point, I remember being convinced that literally EVERYTHING was sharks. If you don't know what that means...me either.

                                After that, things started evening out.

                                ​I won't be doing THAT again for a long time. Possibly never.
