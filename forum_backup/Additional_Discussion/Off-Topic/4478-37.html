                        I am pretty sure I have lost Devilfish and Loxosceles as friends. I could tell the story of the long, slow slide apart we've had, but I don't want to do that because I think it would come off as an airing of grievances obscured by my usual self-hatred as I blame myself for everything (which I legitimately do), and that...that's not why I'm here. I'm not here to be angry or vengeful or anything like that.

                        It comes down to us being too different. I am the kind of person who needs to be needed; just look at the way I've withdrawn from this community now that there are no battles to fight and the help I've tried to offer the comers in the Mental Health Thread is useless to them. I didn't recognize that about myself until just recently, but it makes sense. How would someone like myself with essentially no self-esteem find worth for himself except through being needed by others? Unfortunately, that core of identity is the problem: they are in a place where they need to not be needed, to not have someone attaching parasitically to them in a quest for validation, to not be dragged down into the problems of someone who by now ought to know better. In short, they need to not be around people like me.

                        I could try to change, of course, but it takes no foresight at all to see how that won't work. Never mind the difficulties in changing one's nature; there's just an unsolvable paradox in making a change for their sake, because it means I'm still motivated by a need for them and for their regard for me. It's a cosmetic fix, a Band-aid on a broken leg, aspirin for a brain tumor. It can't work that way. We are at cross purposes here, and that's that.

                        It hurts, of course. It hurts to be rejected, and it hurts to be at fault for the way our relationship has evolved into a dead end like this. The biggest hurt is the dumbest and most childish one, though: I still love them, but that does not matter. How I feel does not stop me from being toxic to them. Having a happy emotion in my heart for them is not some magic spell that fixes the problems and makes us compatible. I know that, of course--I've known for years that how someone feels about you doesn't stop them from being who they are and hurting you with their nature--but I don't know, I guess I just never thought I'd be on this end of it. Surely not me. I am too wise, too empathetic, too kind, too loving. Too special to be that kind of person who poisons the ones he loves.

                        Too arrogant. But never mind. I said earlier I'm not here to be angry or vengeful, and I mean that. I don't want to rally anyone against them, and I don't want to rally anyone against myself either, despite my deep-seated instinct for self-flagellation. Though I do blame myself, and probably always will, I'm not honestly sure there's anyone actually in the wrong here; we're just the people we are, and our edges don't fit together any more. No, I'm here because the best thing I can do is get away from them and out of their life, to accept that by the time I've changed into someone they can be around I will no longer know where they are or how to find them and it is therefore over. I'm here to cope with it, in other words. I'm here to try to content myself with the memories I have because no new memories are forthcoming in this relationship; that program has been canceled. I'm here to figure out what to do, since I can't comfort myself with an irretrievable past or with a future in which I now have nothing to look forward to (I'd pinned all my hopes on them, which I think perfectly underscores their point about how unhealthy my need for them is).

                        Ugh. I am obviously not going to figure anything out tonight. I have lost my capacity to think clearly; all I have left is a driving urge to just dive into my depression so I won't have to deal with these feelings any more, and I can't do that because I'm just not in the mood to find comfort in oblivion no matter what that urge says. I'm probably just going to dick around, say stupid things to people, and maybe go to bed.

                        Sorry this post is such a downer, guys. I probably should have put it in the Hate thread, but I can't bring myself to hate this. No matter how much it hurts to be left behind, there is some comfort in knowing that they are growing in a healthy direction.
