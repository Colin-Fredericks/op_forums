    Hello again everybody! Thought I'd let you know I now have 4 page sheets for V5! Just so you know, instead of a separate expanded backgrounds section(like the old VtM sheets), there is now a backgrounds section set up much like the way that Disciplines are, so you record the background and then have room to add the expanded info for it.
