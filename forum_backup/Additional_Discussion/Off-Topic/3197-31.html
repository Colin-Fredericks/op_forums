    I made a decision, somewhere in the middle of this season, to put pony down. I'm done with the show.

    So I'm pleased to say that with this last finale, I can do so with a smile at least. There's some flaws with the ending, as it happens, but I do think it's an amazing delivery on the series premise, and a solid mark of maturation from the days that Twilight made friends and then blasted the enemy to high hell. It was good. Not enough to change my mind about being done, but good enough to leave me with probably warmer feelings than I otherwise would have.

                Originally posted by Weimann View Post
                S5E25-26 - The Cutie Re-Mark
                Since it seems I've started on the different alternate timelines, I might as well finish them. The Sombra timeline was easily the best one to me. It carried so much potential, and I'm sure it will be explored extensively in fanfics.
                The only thing I disliked there was that we got even *less* dialog from Sombra! This could have been a great opportunity to flesh him out unlike the wasted original appearance, but a missed one...at least the comic did so, which is apparently alluded to in this ep (his lack of a cutie mark) so it may well be canon.

                Also, it would have been nice seeing Celestia fighting him directly...
                Likewise, the Queen Chrysalis (they managed to say her name this time) timeline was similarly evocative, but we weren't told much of the bigger picture. Is this the last remaining resistance to Chrysalis' offensive? Or is it one of many rogue cells that fight a guerilla war against a still stabilizing new queen? This is all open to interpretation, which is a good thing in this case, but it doesn't mean I would have liked to know a bit more about the context.
                Did they actually say where Celestia was, in this one? Or Cadence and Shining Armor? For that matter, those two were missing in all the timelines, weren't they?

                And all of it is fanservice. Awesome, awesome fanservice that I'm still somewhat reeling from (I've seen the episode at least five times, it's that good), but still just something to stroke our sensationalist desires.
                As much as people, for some reason, give the show guff for pandaring to the bronies, IMO doing so gives us some of the Best Episodes! Frankly, at this point, they couldn't go wrong with creating a spin-off show directly targeting the Brony Demographic, most likely featuring the Alternate Mane Six, that would probably do at least as well, if not better!
                What about Starlight Glimmer?

                The situation is resolved into redemption, which given the establishment of Starlight's character is surprisingly credible. Once again, not friendship rainbow laser. I am happy about this.

                Speculations have been going around about Starlight's place in the show next season. Will she be Twilight's "faithful student" and be a new member of the mane cast? Will she be a sometimes character that get an episode a season? I'm open for a closer relationship with her, and I think there are some plot seeds already sown (the aforementioned Sunburst, for example).
                I was actually hoping she wouldn't be reformed, as it would be more interesting to have a recurring pony villain popping up from time-to-time, and hadn't we already done that dance with Sunset Shimmer? Still, it was setup and done pretty well, I must admit, and I also applaud the use of reason instead of Friendship Blaster (which smacks a bit of Mind Control and was the reason I didn't initially buy SS's sudden Face Turn).

                Dunno if I want to see her back next season, as, again, we're getting pretty much the same story as the movie. Well, we might have, if the end of this ep hadn't condensed all that into a montage. Seeing Starlight win back the trust of the Our Towners might have been a great ep, but uh, guess not now. A bit similar to Diamond Tiara suddenly winning friends all over the place, now I think about it. Sunset must be wondering how all these formerly horrible ponies are doing so much better than she has! Maybe ponies are just quicker to forgive than humans?
                In light of all this, I hereby dub Starlight Glimmer Best Villain. Because she managed to make me actually care what became of her without that making her any less threatening.
                Wonder where she'd rank in this list?
                In the end, I think the thing I liked the best about this episode was that it still, after five years, managed to surprise me. Not only be good and meet my expectations, but actually throw a curve all out of left field. That is my beacon of hope for the next season.
                No mention of that final shot? It still gives me shivers (the good kind!), and many people have noted that it seems suited to a *series* finale...Or imagine if the actual upcoming Pony Movie ended that way!
