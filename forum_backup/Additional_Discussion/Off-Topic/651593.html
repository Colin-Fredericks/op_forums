    Why do you like Steven Universe?

    09-04-2015, 03:03 PM
    I just completed the series and I love it to bits. I think it might replace MLP:FiM as the second best animated series for kids and young adults that I've seen (the top spot still securely held by Avatar: the Last Airbender).

    There are many reasons to like it, but I think the biggest one for me is that there's a really strong sense of complexity and layers. The show makes me feel comfortable assuming that Things will Matter in the same way Avatar did and which MLP sadly fails at most of the time. The world feels deep, not just big, and that goes for the characters as well, of course. Aside from in a literal sense, the characters both take up much space in the intrigue and feel like they're grounded, fleshed out and very unique. It's not just a bunch of aliens with different personalities, they're also given genuinely different situations and problems that we actually get to see them grapple with.

    Naturally, there's also the progressive aspects of the series, which are very nice.

    One thing I'm worried about is the schedule, though. From what I understand, it's been pretty sparse and erratic? There's a hiatus right now, and I'm not sure when it'll end. With the episode being ten minutes, it might feel like too little, too thin.

                              This has to do with the way the show is made. They're short episodes, so two can be shown in a single block, and Cartoonnetwork can run them in different combinations. Typically they pair one new(er) episode with one older one. There are long stretches of no new episodes while the creators work, then once they've finished a good chunk, those episodes get added to the list that Cartoonnetwork can run, and those of us who wait for the new episodes and watch them as soon as they're out get a week or so of a new episode every day, rather than several weeks of one new episode a week.

                                              There are so many reasons I like this show, that I can hardly separate them all.

                                              I mean, I like how it all strings together. Despite being episodic, all the stories feel interconnected, unlike some shows where the events of some episodes may as well have never happened. If not the world itself, they all feel like they have an impact on the characters and their personalities.
                                              I like the characters. From the Gems, who can feel mysterious and otherworldly just as often as they feel funny and relatable. To the Humans, who all have their little quirks and depths. I think my favourite has to be Pearl, because all of how her flaws and virtues blend together to make her so complicated and interesting.
                                              I like the world. There is so much mystery and it's all revealed at a great pace, which gives you just enough time to try and figure things out before the curtain is lifted. It fuels speculation and makes me more curious to find out more about the world the show is set in.

                                              It's quickly becoming my favourite show, but it hasn't surpassed Avatar yet.
