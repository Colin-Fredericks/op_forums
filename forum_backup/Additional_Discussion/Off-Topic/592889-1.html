My ring is seen as a non-sexual statement, even though it represents an explicitly sexual relationship, while my friend's collar is seen as a representing a sexual relationship (even though it actually isn't a strictly sexual one).

As you note, it's a borderline strawman, but in my mind not because of its rarity, but because of the inequality in how actions are viewed rather than being judged on more objective (or at least equal) criteria.
