            One World of Darkness sounds better than both cWoD and nWoD fans give the idea credit. First of all, the separation of the community disrupted unity in a very bad way. Have you ever tried LARPing with one of the larger organizations, or worse yet, tried to set up such a wide-scale LARP group (across cities)?

            You have your typical nWoD players who focus on nothing but personal relations (oh, wow, my character was invited to spend the night with this or that elder, the cacophony should mention nothing but my name and my incredible sex life for the next few months at least!), and then the cWoD'ers that try to take things on a whole different scale and constantly ask the ST if any of the Sancitifed characters exudes an "aura of holiness" (aka True Faith). Sooner or later, on of the two groups lose interest and we have participation numbers essentially HALVED by the end of Month 2 of the LARP.

            Seriously, nWoD felt like Valve's beta games. A lot of good ideas thrown in the same universe, beta tested again and again, until they surfaced in 2nd edition nWoD, only to be beta tested all over again. cWoD on the other hand, is so painfully metaplot-heavy, and its fanbase so reluctant to deviate from the lore as printed, that I've already cancelled two V20 campaigns because the players couldn't accept that in my campaign, in my VtM cosmos, the Brujah Prince and the Sabbat Archbishop of Athens, had spent the better of part of the past 40 years in an alliance that benefited Greece so greatly, that the Prince's sudden torpor is also the starting point of the crisis (basically, the ultra-conservative Ventrue decided now was the time to push the country back to the Victorian Age, in terms of inequality and poverty).

            So yeah, if we can get One World of Darkness, that will lack magical Vampire dementia, because Vampires transcending history and having an actual established lore across the centuries (oh the horror) is too Masquerade-y, and also make the metaplot lawyers starve, I'm sold. I seriously cannot be the only one who felt stuck between the two Worlds, never settling for one or the other, while their players had clear preferences and would not reconsider.
