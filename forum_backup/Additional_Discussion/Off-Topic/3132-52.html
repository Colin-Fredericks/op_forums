                            What the hell, I'm here.

                            For the last week and a half, I've had the same headache. It's a stress headache emanating from an ache in the right side of the back of my neck, occasionally manifesting in a spot just above my right eyebrow as well. It's not unbeatable--aspirin, or more likely ibuprofen, or even more likely relaxation techniques can all knock it down--but it always comes back, several times a day. The pain itself isn't bad, at least not by the standards of a guy who occasionally gets the kind of headache that leaves him blind in one eye, but the interminable nature of it is so demoralizing, and the worst part is, I can't sleep for more than three hours at a stretch without waking up to a pounding in front and back of my skull that takes at least half an hour to knock back down.

                            I mentioned off-handedly to a friend that I'd had the same headache for a week, and without any prompting on my part, he asked me if I had the symptoms he described. I allowed as I did, and he said that was how he'd felt before discovering he had narrowing compression in the vertebrae of his neck, which is just fucking fantastic. I don't know what all it entails, but I know that my right shoulder aches like hell today and explodes with a burst of time any time I extend my reach too far. It helps to keep my head and neck straight, or even have my head tilted back some, but boy is it hard to learn a new posture. I guess pain is a good way of recognizing when you're backsliding, though.

                            I assume all of this is a result of how far I've let myself go, which is why I've been trying to be scrupulous about doing my exercises; but I'm the kind of guy who believes pain is your body's way of telling you to stop being an asshole to yourself, and I just can't convince myself it's a good idea to carry on even while in pain.
