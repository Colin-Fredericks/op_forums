                                            I don't really remember my dreams. At most I recall a scene or situation or two, but if you ask me about it half an hour after I'm fully awake, I'll have forgotten it (Though, like everyone else, my memories lie to me and most likely contain fragments of lies I told as a kid and now remember as truth, memories my brain "invented" when someone told me of an event in which they thought I was present but wasn't, different situations conflated into a single jumbled one and so on. So it quite possible that some stuff I "remember" happened in dreams only.).

                                            There have been only two times that I managed to recall relatively clearly (as far as I can tell, anyway) a scene from a dream. One was a pretty typical nightmare, I guess: killer moving at unnatural speed while I moved very slow and can't stop him, I wake up when his blade reaches my mother's neck. That is not the one I want to talk about, though. There was this time that I found a scene amusing enough that I recalled it after waking up. It was a corridor with a safe on the floor at the end of it, and behind it were metal bars like in prison cell. I found it weird/amusing because hey, why are the bars not in front of the safe? (I bet I must have dreamed much stranger stuff over my life, but this was the one that stuck with me).

                                            Months later, I and other people in my College (I graduated in physical education) were working in an sporting event and there on our place of lodging I saw a familiar scene, a corridor, which had half-walls that continued as only iron bars up to the ceiling, with a completely out of place safe at the end, which brought back the memory of that dream.

                                            This was years ago, but to this day I sometimes half-jokingly wonder if seeing that just made me recall a very vaguely similar dream, with my brain filling the blanks and making it seem like the same scene, or if I should be trying to dream about lottery numbers or something.
