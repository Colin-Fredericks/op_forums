        I don't want to be a burden on the people that care about me. I want to be strong for them. I want them to be able to count on me. I'm trying. I promise. I have a damn good reason to try. I refuse to let the fucking demons and goblins in my head win.
        This might be too little too late, but you can always try something (non-pharmaceutical) to calm yourself down. I know it's tough, but it never hurts to try. Despite what Yoda tells us, there is a try. Try or try not, if you don't try, you'll never get to do.

        Calm music, long boring documentaries on The History Channel, a couple of hours watching a bug zapper while contemplating what causes the bugs to be attracted to the light.....stupid shit, sure, but it derails the shit your mind it trying to beat you up with.

        And always come back to the same thing - it's your mind, you are the final judge on all this nonsense and you can tell that inner asshole to shut the fuck up. From experience, I will tell you that you will not always win every second of every day, but the more you work over that inner asshole with a few synaptic kidney punches, the more often he'll back down. I will also warn you now, he'll come back stronger for quite a few rounds. Eventually, you can wear him down enough that you'll win more than lose, but it takes time and a reason to fight.

        For myself, I had a hard time finding a reason to fight for a long time, until I just got pissed off enough to say, "Fuck this! I'm not letting a 5 pound piece of meat that I'M in control of beat my ass at anything!!" Yeah, I've got a serious amount of rage to tap into. If I ever got to close to a gamma bomb, there would be a big problem.

        But I digress.....first technique I tapped into was to derail the thoughts in the first place with other things. Give it a shot.
