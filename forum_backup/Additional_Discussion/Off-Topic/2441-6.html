Either time he tries to take on the world tree he doesn't bother when it was just shown in the prior fight again that his reaction time is so high he becomes a Cuisinart. Endless mobs would have created the same results, but that would have made me felt he really tried. Espcially the second time he tried it. When I get to that point I just kind of want the anime to be over. Though if they did more I would watch them.

I still laugh my ass off at her hopping up and down with glee when Kirito is killing mobs. I really don't find much fault with any of the supporting characters save the fact that every other female has to fall for him. Like it wants to almost be a harem anime, but not quite.

                                    The entire first season is free for streaming on Crunchyroll. I’ll grant that the pace of the series isn’s as fast as in SAO; but paradoxically, that’s because a lot happens in a short period of time in the setting. In SAO, months or years go by between episodes; and the only real changes that occur are either personal (Kirito joins a team that gets slaughtered, etc.) or entirely predictable (the adventurers push on to higher levels). In Log Horizon… well, I won’t spoil it, other to say that the setting is a much more dynamic one than SAO. That, and it plays with aspects of MMOs that SAO conveniently bypassed, such as the disparity between the player’s appearance and his character’s appearance (disposed of in SAO #1 by the Mirror), the ability to respawn after you’ve been killed (likewise disposed of in SAO #1), the in-game economy (briefly hinted at a few times in SAO, though nothing is ever done with it), and the role of NPCs in the game world (IIRC, there are no NPCs in SAO, save for Kirito’s daughter).

                                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
