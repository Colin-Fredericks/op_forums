    I Feel Nothing

    01-31-2014, 01:38 PM
    Hey, folks.

    Wasn't sure where else to discuss this sort of thing, so I figured here was as good a place as anywhere.

    The truth of the matter is that, right now, I don't feel much of anything. This is different from the depression I've grappled with for years, as that manifested as a powerful despair or sadness. This is different.

    First of all, some context.

    2013 was a god awful year. It started out with some hardcore family problems, my sister moving in with me, my starting a new job as my manager that was making it worthwhile was retiring due to serious illness, then my sister blowing up and my having to take her back to my parents. All through it, I was moving through all my problems as best I could and just, well, surviving.

    Most recently, I've undone what could be called the nucleus of all my malaise. You see, I grew up with abusive parents who taught me that people in authority over you had the power to ruin your day on a whim if you did not perfectly meet their expectations. By extension, it taught me that any moment could ruin a day, or a month, or a life. I've learned, on an emotional level, that that is false now. A life is a scale, a balance between what you've accomplished and what you've failed at. As long as the balance is equal or weighed toward accomplishment, then things stay more or less on track.

    And all of that sounds good, right?

    Well ...

    As a corollary to the "anything can be a ruiner" thought, there was "anything can be a succeeder." That is, if any one thing can bring an entire enterprise to ash, then, surely, there must be something that can create a new reality and dramatically improve one's circumstances. However, that's not really true either, as I said in the above section.

    So, now, there's just this lack of drive or meaning to what I want do do. I mean, if there's no the "succeeder" elements, what is there to strive for? If there's no "ruiner" elements, then what is there to overcome?

    Sorry for rambling. Back to your regularly scheduled programming.

      Sounds like you've discovered existentialism! Or is it absurdism? I never studied philosophy.

      It's a place that a lot of people end up, I think. In the absence of prescribed meaning from an outside source, there doesn't seem to be "innate" value to much of anything. So the question of what there is to strive for or to overcome doesn't really have a good answer. The answer is just whatever you feel like. Or you can decide that there isn't an answer, if you prefer.

      It's a bit of a mindscrew, yeah? It's liberating in a way to cast off the meanings that society/religion/family/etc. push on you, but discouraging to think that there isn't a universal answer. The best thing I can say to you, I think, is that it's important to realize that the meaning you ascribe to life isn't wrong or diminished just because you came up with it.

      Congratulations on resolving the damage your parents did to you, though. That's a good first step, and the void you're currently feeling should become less prominent as you fill it in with your own meanings and judgements.
