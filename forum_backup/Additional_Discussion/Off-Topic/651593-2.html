                    Originally posted by etherial View Post
                    The problem is, Steven is an awful little shit in most of the early episodes. Which numerous prior episodes would you recommend?
                    He is not an "awful little shit." He is well-meaning but immature.

                    Steven Universe is a character-based show. The early episodes show Steven growing from 'boy' to 'young man.' Episodes like Cheeseburger Backpack are the foundation for episodes like Serious Steven, Too Many Birthdays, and The Test. Seeing Steven show maturity and empathy in The Test is far more satisfying when you've seen his self-centeredness in Cheeseburger Backpack.

                    It's one of the reasons why I consider it better than a great deal of cartoon shows or adult oriented media. Episodic shows tend to have static characters. Homer Simpson is the same character he was 27 years ago when the Simpson's premiered. It's natural to be put off by a main character with an obnoxious set of behaviors because many times those behaviors will never be addressed or permanently changed.

                        Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
                        It's one of the reasons why I consider it better than a great deal of cartoon shows or adult oriented media. Episodic shows tend to have static characters. Homer Simpson is the same character he was 27 years ago when the Simpson's premiered. It's natural to be put off by a main character with an obnoxious set of behaviors because many times those behaviors will never be addressed or permanently changed.
                        It's also good because it sets up characters in ways that mean looking back on them with the context of later episodes grants greater insight into those prior moments, and an overall sense of character depth and consistency. Look at Amethyst in Tiger Millionaire after On the Run, Pearl in An Indirect Kiss following Rose's Scabbard, and Garnet in Alone Together through what we learn in Jailbreak.

                        They're particularly good because they're not only foreshadowing, they're also things that come off as expressions of traits that we've already seen those characters as having, and even with those revelations they still kind of are; Pearl is fastidious, so whatever else is going on behind her tension when somebody smashes up a shrine, it's still an expression of her aversion to mess and ruin.

                        That's how you make complex characters; multiple qualities behind a single action.

                                        One thing that has me curious is who or what Lapis Lazuli is supposed to be.

                                        Her gem is almost completely shattered and not only does she appear to be fully functional, she takes control of the planet's entire water supply, creates duplicates of all the crystal gems (one of which is a perfect fusion) and duels them all into submission. She is also the only gem we have seen who is capable of forcefully maintaining a fusion even when their partner is fighting against them.


                                        Lapis is the most powerful character we have seen in the series to date, which is why it's frustrating we know almost nothing about her!


                                        By the way, I think I decoded some of the Gem's class systems.

                                        Quartz - Military. Peridot pretty much confirmed this.

                                        Silicate - Work Force. A Peridot is a type of silicate gem.

                                        Pearls - Domestic Servants or possibly slaves. Peridot refers to Pearl as a "made to order" servant and it is blatantly obvious in the series that Pearl has almost fanatical worship towards Rose Quartz.
