        Last night, I dreamed about Star Trek.

        More specifically, I dreamed that I was an extra on the first episode of The Next Generation. But it wasn't the first episode as we know it. It SUPER cheesy. It was filmed in front of a live studio audience, and they were laughing their asses off. I'm not sure whether it was supposed to be a comedy or not. But regardless, the plotline was completely different from the real first episode.

        The plotline of the episode in my dream had something to do with time travel. Captain Picard had Roman soldiers on his ship, and he was trying to tell them that the cold air was making them sick and that they needed to close the window on their front door.

        The costumes were incredibly cheesy. The Roman soldier costumes essentially looked like this, only adult sized.

        Also, for some reason, the show was being filmed in the science building at my school. The bridge set had it's own room and had been built much like the bridge on the actual show, but there was a secondary set, which must have been the battle bridge, in the basement. This is where I was sitting. This wasn't a set at all. They pulled out a plastic chair for me to sit on. My "costume" was wearing a dark blue polo shirt with a small rocket logo on it. I was supposed to be a shuttle pilot. I was an extra, but I was sitting next to Troi at the front of the room. One of the actors, I can't remember who (maybe Frakes) set something on fire right before we started filming.

        At some point, I realized that this couldn't be happening because I was about a year old when the first episode actually came out. (In the dream I decided that I was three when it came out.) However, there was another student at the school here who was also working on the show, but as a creative consultant instead of an "actor". I knew, somehow, that his life had been altered. That he was supposed to be an actor. And so, somehow I'd traveled back in time and altered history. After that, everything was okay.

        Later, in an unconnected dream, I had to fight off man-eating plants, deal with completely frictionless surfaces and a gel that made things super bouncy.

        WTF brain?

                                            Last night, I had another sci-fi dream. We were having some sort of activity, a picnic or something, on another planet. It was to take place in a small shack. And the shack was located in a place that was very cold and snowy. I feel like the entire planet was an ice planet, like Hoth, but perhaps that was not true. I felt like, in the dream, that I had had a dream in this place before, and I knew what was going to happen: there was going to be some sort of yeti/wampa/abominable snowman that was going to attack the gathering. (People were standing in line, like you do at Disneyland or some other place where you have dividers and have to stand in line for extended periods of time.) So I took measures to preempt this happening, but I was younger than I am in real life; I feel like I was 12 or so. We (I'm not sure who "we" consists of, but there were others I convinced to come along) tried to cause the entrance to the lair of the beast to collapse. I think we were successful.

                                            Overnight, an entire city sprung up around this little shack which was in the middle of nowhere. I had some sort of device that allowed me to fly, it was akin to a balloon or a parachute, and I was flying through the streets of this city. There were all sorts of Christmas lights and decorations up; it was kind of like The Grinch movie.

                                            I took this parachute and I flew out over this huge cliff, thousands of feet of drop off beneath me. There was a very fancy hotel embedded into the side of the cliff. I did some flying around and then I woke up to a freezing house.
