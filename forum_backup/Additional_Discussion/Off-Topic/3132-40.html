                                    I have tried a few times to write about my recent hospital experience. I can't do it. It's too...I can't. It's not in me. I can't do it. I thought maybe if some time passed, I'd be able to, but I can't. It took everything I had just to make that stupid joke a few posts up. Oh well. No great loss. The notes I wrote on the experience are beyond pretentious, and no one wants to hear it.

                                    Anyway, I can't talk about it today, and I knew I wasn't going to be able to talk about it that day either. Of course, that didn't stop people from trying to get me to. After some shit with my dad trying to call me repeatedly while I was in the examination room, I eventually sent the following text to my family:

                                    "This has been a horrible, demoralizing, humiliating experience for me and I do not want to say this more than once, so here is the group text: they do not know what is wrong with me, but the ultrasound shows no torsion, so they say it's probably a groin pull. They gave me two tabs of potassium and told me I should just finish the antibiotics and go to work tomorrow. I have no prescriptions, no therapy, just a fond slap on my ass and a finger pointing me to the door. That is all I know."

                                    I was less harsh with that text than I could have been, less than I thought maybe I would have to be. I gave serious consideration to finishing it with, "I do not want to talk about it any more," but I thought they would understand that to be the case from what I had said.

                                    So of course I got home and my dad was waiting for me on the porch to grill me about shit I didn't want to talk about and I had to shut my door in his face before he understood that I did not want to talk about it.

                                    It's my fault for being unclear. Or at least, I hope it is. I hope that I should have just said, "I don't want to talk about it, so please leave me alone," and they would have listened to me. If not, that means I can't trust them any more and I just won't be able to tell them things.

                                    I kind of think it's the second thing. That's what I hate,
