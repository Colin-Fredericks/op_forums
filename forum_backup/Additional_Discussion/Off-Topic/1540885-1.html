                                                XX not withstanding, unless the mods can also see the time reactions are done in and can prove said poster is essentially flooding Satchel's* notifications with negative reactions, you basically just have evidence that people don't like the post, or maybe even the person-and while that can be sour for any conversation where good faith readings, interpretations, and arguments are otherwise being made, there's nothing saying you're not allowed to dislike a person, or even be vocal about it, so long as it's decently respectful in the terms of the rest of the rules. If it doesn't have the time stamp, basically whoever is doing it can basically just say they have a long history of disliking Satchel's takes, and even admitting to some pettiness and negatively reacting to his posts because it's him.

                                                Now of course, XX is there FOR stuff like this, but it's a tricky line to cut where it's harassment (if the mods can legit feel comfortable calling it that, because that can set their precedent for this sort of thing moving forward) and where people are allowed to basically have a bad opinion of a post or a person so long as it meets the criteria for engagement, no matter how otherwise faulty the reasons for that can be.

                                                Arguably the sheer volume of it could considered a form of threadcrapping, particularly if it's adjoined with any thread necromancy, but I think part of the reason the person doing this (if they are deliberately doing it and not just siccing a bot with a loose set of parameters on the forums) is doing this particular thing is because it's kind of small enough that it's hard to justify moderator action against it, particularly since it has a certain level of infrequency.

                                                    Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post

                                                    Rule 1.

                                                    Assuming, of course, it is demonstrably out of proportion and recurrent against a specific poster, this is harassment and goes directly against rule 1. Sure, no one is required to like no one else, but by the very first rule you ARE required to respect even those you dislike.

                                                    You’re right that it also requires attention to timestamps for us to have a strong case, but the forums do have such timestamps. When a reaction is made, the poster is informed of this by a notification with a timestamp.

                                                    Just to be clear, I’m on board with being worried about how this would be applied. I do think we need to be cautious on how to classify a streak of actions as harassment, and even more about the specific actions to be taken in such a case. All that assuming the moderators have access to the salient information to begin with.
