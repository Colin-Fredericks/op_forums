    The Critical Role Thread

    12-22-2016, 04:03 AM
    I was pondering whether one thread would be for nonspoiler discussion and one for Spoilers but well spoilers are intertwined...

    So Critical Role is a broadcast of the weekly D&D game of a bunch of Nerdy Ass Voice Actors with Matt Mercer as the Dungeon Master. It is damn good, Matt Mercer (He was Leon in Resident Evil and a billion other roles), so it also includes Laura Bailey ( the voice of Kid Trunks, Shinchan, Lust from Full Metal Alchemist, and a bunch of Video game roles), Her husband Travis Willingham (He was the voice of Mustang in Full Metal Alchemist, Thor in the Avengers Assemble Cartoon, and a lot of other roles, like a Greaser Robert Duval beat up in 2nd hand Lions).

    Its a great show, you can find it at the Geek and Sundry Website, Twitch TV or Youtube.
    http://geekandsundry.com/shows/critical-role/ https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...rKwXTvJtcqFB8i

    Its a fun show and long running. And while I wish it was a whitewolf game they are playing Matt Mercer is a brilliant and skilled game master and brings a lot of quick thinking and system fidelity to the table. They break the fourth wall a lot, but they also have emotional scenes, and they have fantastic fan art! Go and watch it!! I finally caught up!

    So without Further ado the Spoilery Discussions lie ahead!

    SPOILER DISCUSSIONS!!



    So I caught up! I'm sad Tiberius isn't with the party anymore actually, him and Grog had the most interesting in character moments!

    Big question! Anyone else feel Taliesin Jaffe cheats? Maybe not intentionally but his friggin metal dice are not balanced, all the other guys at the table notice and comment on it, he regularly rolls natural twenties, sometimes like 5+ a night! Others commented he had a high roll when he rolled another dice set, but that does nothing to make me think he doesn't cheat...

    I also think Wil Wheaton cheats, I believe he purposely tries to make it seem like he rolls impossibly poorly as a showman trick....


    Besides that I think it would be fun to have the Vox Machina party in an Exalted game as a bunch of Fairfolk or something.


    I never really read D&D so I only get the jist of the rules and the setting. So like a rating of 10 is average human in a trait? What is the in character explanation for the party after killing some goblins all of the sudden they know more spells and can cook or whatever. It feels to much like a videogame, I don't understand how powers even work in D&D, most things are spells? I don't get how for instance Vex learns new ranger powers? Some people in the world just randomly have magic powers in D&D?

    They also seem to ignore that Tiberius had excellent Charisma, he shouldn't have been treated so much like a goof, and Percy tries to be a world class genius, but he chooses battle stats like Dexterity over Intelligence or Wisdom.

    Thoughts?

      Surprised to see this lacking responses! I'm an avid fan; I watch the show proper and the aftershow every week. I think it's great stuff.

      Its a fun show and long running. And while I wish it was a whitewolf game they are playing Matt Mercer is a brilliant and skilled game master and brings a lot of quick thinking and system fidelity to the table. They break the fourth wall a lot, but they also have emotional scenes, and they have fantastic fan art! Go and watch it!! I finally caught up!
      Worth noting that Taliesin Jaffe is an old-school VtM and WtA player, who has expressed interest several times in running a one-shot for the rest of the group once he familiarises himself with V20 rules: so you might get your wish after all there ;D I personally think the group might enjoy Changeling or something else instead, but we'll see.

      Big question! Anyone else feel Taliesin Jaffe cheats?
      Matt has said before (I think on twitter? Maybe on the subreddit...I could find it if I looked) that the only player who cheats 'is no longer at the table', i.e it was Orion, and he has actually made Tal switch out his dice before. I think it's just luck + the fact that everyone pays more attention to his nat 20s than anyone else's, since they expect him to have them. Confirmation bias is a hell of a thing. I do recall an instance in the Feywild arc where Taliesin rolled with Marisha's dice after she'd rolled several 1s in quick succession, though...and rolled a 20 with them. He's not cheating; I think he's just a witch.

      They also seem to ignore that Tiberius had excellent Charisma, he shouldn't have been treated so much like a goof, and Percy tries to be a world class genius, but he chooses battle stats like Dexterity over Intelligence or Wisdom.
      I think it's a consequence of each class having certain dump stats more than anything. Tibs was a sorcerer, so he needed high Charisma, but he never really felt like he should have it. I still think he would have been better as a Wizard. Still, I have a sorcerer with 18 Cha in my PF game, and he's also an awkward creature. Percy is more interesting: iirc Taliesin has said that Percy is a man who thinks he is a genius, but is very much not, making that stat choice more acceptable imo. [VAGUE SPOILERS] You recall that his hubris here came back to bite him in the end. But a lot of this comes down to the relative lack of customisation one can do in 5e/PF.

      Anyway! Apologies for bumping, but I do love this silly show. Worth a watch.

                                  Re: Taliesin's high rolls - I don't doubt they're unevenly weighted, but I do doubt he's intentionally cheating. Honestly though, most gaming dice are unevenly weighted, and I personally think that's just kinda part of the game.
