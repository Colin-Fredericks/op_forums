        I hate when people bring up how many years they've been playing role-playing games during a discussion about some aspect of gaming - whether it is how a ruling should be made, or how to deal with a problem player - that really doesn't have anything to do with how many years they've been playing.

        First, it is infuriating to me because doing dumb shit for a long period of time doesn't make it not dumb shit - so it doesn't matter if you've been running games for 30 years, you can still be making shitty rulings or mishandling problem players or making other mistakes.

        And secondly, it bothers me because saying "I've been running games for 30 years" doesn't actually tell me whether you've got more experience than me, or less. Because, as an example, there is a difference between 30 years playing RPGs an average of twice a week for 8 hours each time (just under 25,000 hours of experience) and 23 years playing RPGs an average of four times a week for 6 hours each time (almost 4,000 hours more experience).

        Plus there is the whole dick-measuring contest aspect of it that the person is considering not the actual quality or content of an argument itself, but the arbitrary and ill-defined metric that isn't directly related to the argument - all made more ridiculous because of the implication a person tossing their RPG experiencing into the discussion as if it supported their argument in any way has made that, to give it an actual dick-measuring analog, would be "Mine is bigger than yours because I've had it longer."

        Maybe the next time some dipshit thinks telling me how long they've played RPGs proves that I'm wrong about whatever we're discussing, I'll mention that I've been playing guitar for like 25 years now and ask them if that gives them any indication at all how well I play.

        Not so noble anymore.

                                                I strongly disapprove of (I believe the term hate is both over and incorrectly used) those who seek to rob me of my national identity and pride. I resent that instead of being content to have no pride themselves, or if they disapprove of it so strongly to move to a less prideful patch of dirt, such people would attempt to take from me my nation and my pride in it.

                                                I rebuke those who try to tear down, re-write, denigrate, debase, or otherwise destroy the history, culture, and traditions of my nation instead of shuffling off to some population that is more pleasing to them.

                                                I am full of disdain for those who attempt to hold what my nation may (or may not have) done decades, centuries, or millennia ago to today's much softer, kinder, and gentler standards instead of the standards in play among the other nations around them at the time.

                                                I am disheartened by the feckless for whom it is not enough to have neither care nor reverence for their nation and it's treasures, but can't even show a modicum of decency out of respect for those who served, bled, and even died for their nation while following traditions that they valued, under a flag that they honored, and while singing an anthem that graced their heart.

                                                I despair at those who would wish away reality in order to supplant it with some imaginary construct. It is a sad event when someone chooses to ignore that generalizations, in general, have a basis in fact. People in different nations and cultures do in fact share some common characteristics. Whether it is cuisine, art, language, or a shared moral compass, nations are distinct and varied. These are things to be exalted, not diminished. It would be a loss to the world if the French were to be less French, or the Japanese less Japanese. I will resist the thieves who attempt to steal the fabric of what makes my nation great. I would hope the citizens of other nations will do the same for theirs.

                                                And above all else, I revile the ideology of those who would take or destroy what is mine instead of building their own.
