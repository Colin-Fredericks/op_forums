                                S4E5: Flight to the Finish

                                I've said before that I'm typically not well predisposed towards CMC episodes. The dynamic in them hasn't changed in ages. They're usually Those Wacky Kids type of things, Diamond Tiara and Silver Spoon are designated bad fillies and whatever happens at the end will be forgotten by the next episode. I'll admit, Babs Seed did make a return once, but that's about it I think. It seems that they are working on producing more continuity in the show this season, which I cannot praise them enough for, so there's a good chance they'll be showing the CMC as flag-carriers in an upcoming Equestria Games episode. This is, of course, great.

                                With that in mind, this CMC episode isn't that different in structure from others of its ilk. In fact, the episode kind of pokes fun at the sameness at some points, like in the following exchange:

                                Diamond Tiara: "Did you see that?! I can't believe I'm saying this, but they could win!"
                                Silver Spoon: "But how do we stop them? We already called them blankflanks!"

                                The little metaness is actually pretty amusing, and it bodes well for the new writer who did this episode. Appearently, Ed Valentine is a newcomer to FiM, but is well merited with shows like Sesame Street, Fairly Oddparents, and Ultimate Spider-Man under his belt (I got this from the comments at EQD, though, so bit of a source warning there). I am still a little surprised they let a new hand tackle this episode, though, since the fact that you can write for one show doesn't have to imply you can write for another. I think he pulled it off, though, and to great effect, for which I'm grateful.

                                Because yeah, this isn't quite just another CMC episode. This episode addresses one of the oldest issues about Scootaloo; the fact that she can't fly. I'll admit, I actually hoped for a moment that she would actually fly at the end and bring some god damn progression into the CMC, but no such luck. Also, in retrospect, not much is actually said about it. We're not given a reason for why she's landbound, nor any real sense of her being close to flying. Still, the fact that it's getting addressed is actually quite a big deal for me. It's got to be a pretty big burden for a pegasus to not only not have her Cutie Mark but also to not be able to fly. She's an outsider in two ways, and even with friends like the CMC, that hurts. That aspect was definitely the strongest part of the episode for me.

                                Oh, and Rainbow Dash really came into her role as leader. She's so good with that. Earlier, Rainbow Dash was on the bottom of my Favourite Mane Six list, but she has climbed significantly. In fact, recent developments might have to lead me to reconsider the positions on that list fundamentally.

                                The song was meh. Not cringeworthy, but a little... I don't know? Generic? Didn't do much very interesting.
