Add on the fact that they're under greater scrutiny by employers, officers, and random passerby and that they're less likely to get the benefit of the doubt in a criminal case and more likely to get significantly harsher sentences (20% more time according to the more conservative recent studies), and the odds are stacked even more strongly against them. There was a fascinating study to get at the heart of the gender gap in STEM fields. It found that girls perform significantly better on the same tests when they're told that it's a broad curriculum test vs. being told that it will test their aptitude at math and science. This extends to how we perceive women. I have little doubt that similar tests involving black and white children would deliver similar results. Our cultural perceptions of race and gender have a significant and direct effect on both self-value and performance. Another study: officer and random civilians are shown a split second picture of faces and told to say whether they had a negative or positive response to that person. Both black and white participants were more likely to elicit a negative response from black faces than from white faces. I feel like everyone should ask their female and black friends about the precautions they take in their everyday lives or the harassment they deal with on a daily basis. I've known quite a few friends who are shocked when they actually put it in words, because it's simply become part of their daily routine.
