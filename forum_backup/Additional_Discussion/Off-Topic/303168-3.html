    Alright, I've had it with this player before the game even started. His attitude. His actions. His arrogance. Makes me not just kick him out but burn the W20 game I spent 4 months setting up.

    I decided to run Werewolf where I live because everyone is running either D&D or Pathfinder. I decided I would drudge up an old Werewolf idea I have wanted to do for awhile and finally getting the W20 Core, I thought yes! Let's do it! I just got off running a good Mage: The Ascension Revised game before I moved here.

    This is the kind of game, I love, the SL I have is going to be great. I built Colorado as a giant sandbox with lots of dangling threads for the party taking 4 months to design. 2 of the players already have played with me. So I am just recruiting new blood and that is what I did. So let's call him New Cub.

    New Cub had one WoD experience and did not like it. Hey happens. He had no real experience with Werewolf (a plus I thought at the time) but was willing to learn.

    Now I prefer to follow what WoD suggests especially when running Werewolf. Get the group together and everyone work on their characters together. Discuss their characters. Work on the Pack and Pack Totem etc.

    New Cub asks me tons of Q's and I answered them quickly with page numbers and rules clarifications as well as themes of the game and Tribes. I even passed him my book to read. I own a digital copy so he could use my physical to go through. I already had what I needed and worked out. I own a damned library of WoD books of Vampire, Werewolf and Mage and thankfully my wife is happy I have them as she games too.

    I made this clear to New Cub and all my other players that I wanted to do a sit down and design phase. This is different from other games. I want everyone to work together for character creation. Come in with your ideas. I told all of them I added in some CC additional rules (I give a free bonus in Totem and 3 more Background Points) but they did not know that. I would have CC rules and such at the game.

    New Cub decided to build his character all the way through and write a background. All of which I told no one to do. We had Prelude things to do. Go through First Changes. Etc. I told everyone this in email and in person. New Cub builds his PC and not knowing cWoD of course made mistakes and did not understand things as well as he thought. I told him his mistakes and told him we will be building at game after we work out Pack and Characters. I cited the Pages for him to review.

    He sent me a background, which I ignored because we had not had our first game session and told him that. I told him hold off don't build a PC and pigeon hold yourself to what you want. There are other players and you as a group need to work out ideas.

    Well it turns out he got someone else to do the same thing he did...

    So I get to my first game and I start sitting back asking people about their concepts. He is there working on his character sheet... ignoring me. When I asked questions to him and the other player they toss me their character sheets. You know the Questions like who are you,. What are you. Etc. I have my PC building packets with me. We need to have an idea to do all that fun stuff for first game.

    Well New Cub starts talking over everyone and telling me that his Glass Walker would go to none of the Moots. His character is not leaving the house. Ever... The game the Glass Walkers rule Denver and thus the State... so he is talking his character is a Hacker Genius Doogie Hooser Type and everyone needs him etc. He tells me that he will never go out with the Pack to fight the Wyrm because he is too weak (he choose a 1 STR) for this Homid Glass Walker. He is telling me and all the other players he is not working with anyone including me because he has a fully written character sheet and background why should he suffer because no one else has a fully developed character like him.

    Of course over half of what he is saying and such shows his lack of knowledge of Werewolf, his concept and his Tribe. He is telling me and speaking over me, the ST, I mean speaking over me like an arrogant know it all prick who knows nothing really of the game how it is going to be.

    He bullied the other players at the table ordering them around and me like we are to serve him.

    I am a pretty lax ST, I like to have player input. I like to work with the players etc. This was completely out of line and when he left early because he had to be somewhere else. I had to pick up the pieces.

    So because of New Cub I sat back thought about what happened and I sent a general email out saying we are starting over this upcoming week. We are going to do it my way or the highway. That because of the lack of respect that was shown to each other in the group and the ST, I am now forced to make this a dictatorship. I am the ST not the players speaking. This kind of actions will not be tolerated and I am bringing a gavel (no joke, it is a wooden toy hammer to my daughter's toy she no longer plays with).

    Yes, Harsh Email but as an ST, that is my job. I am the one in the end running the game. If I had to drag people down or they leave so be it. I am here to make it fun and a bully and the group's overall lack of maturity and consideration that game was astonishing. In 24 years I have never seen anything like that.

    So I put down the rules. Mentioned we will go over etiquette with one another. Etc. When someone is speaking we listen we do not interrupt each other and the ST especially.

    Well he sent me a response telling me how I should run my game and did not like what I wrote (I mentioned no names. I mentioned all the problems that happened. Only person mentioned was myself, where I felt I failed my players and I am not letting that happen again).

    He sends me a response telling me how to run my game. How he should not be held back by the party because some people had not made or formed ideas yet. Etc. He was the only one with a truly formed out idea and they should be working with him not the other way around. And he can't handle a ST that is bringing in a Dictatorship that this is the Storyteller system etc.

    Tonight when I get home. I am sending him an email kicking his ass out. I don't give a damn that he runs one of the big local RPG Meet Up Groups in town. I don't give a damn he will bad mouth me because of what I am going to do. If no one wants to play with me because he is that kind of arrogant asshole so be it.

    I am not compromising my players, my obligation to them as a ST to bring a fun and entertaining game each session, because someone is trying to bully me. If it does not stop here. It will just get worse.

        Originally posted by Nene Richards View Post
        Yes, Harsh Email but as an ST, that is my job. I am the one in the end running the game. If I had to drag people down or they leave so be it.         I'm actually in a vaguely similar situation myself, albeit from the other side (no, I'm not getting into it). Now, I don't presume to know the whole situation, for all I know it's exactly as you describe.

        But the way you write about it concerns me. Yeah, seems like this player is a problem, but on the other hand it does seem like you've taken a very "my way or the highway" approach right from the get-go, and that pretty much never works out long- or even medium-term. It discourages the players from working with you, and RPing, at least WW games, is all about co-operation. Now, from what you say New Cub is out of line, no disputing that. But on the other hand, if the other players are doing as you describe, and these guys are more experienced players, then it seems unlikely for him to be the only problem. I mean, one player can disrupt things, but a new player has very little in the way of leverage to push others into doing what they want, so unless he's either really eloquent, or threatening them with violence, or they're just incredible doormats (being RPers, not out of the question), I very much doubt it's all New Cub's doing.

        As I said, I don't know your situation beyond what you've said, so I'm speaking generally here. But the fact that you have, from what you described, constantly tried to deal with the situation through exerting control rather than compromise, doesn't sound like a workable solution. Often I find that the most successful STs throw the player a bone and ask the player to do the same. As an example (not saying this would have worked specifically): "I see that you have a strong character concept worked out ahead of time. I'm glad you're excited about the game, but Werewolf is primarily a game about werewolf packs, which means working together, which is why I wanted to do character creation together, so peoples characters can complement each other a bit better. Now, the hacker concept is good, and there's a lot of story stuff I can work with with that, but it seems a bit restrictive; never leaving the house is going to seriously limit how much you can actually participate. How about you throw in some social skills, and he can also help support the pack through shmoozing and asking people questions, gathering information that way as well?"

        Or, you know, something.

        Obviously, New Cub is out of line. But I don't think that the trouble is entirely down to him; from what you describe your management of the situation seems a bit sub-optimal. I'd tentatively suggest mentioning at the start of the next session that you want the game to be fun for everyone, which means co-operation, and you'd like to pursue that pathway rather than hammering people going outside your expectations.

        I mean, after all. You can tell a story by yourself, but that's hardly as fun as telling it with people.

        My Commandments for GMs My Commandments for Players
