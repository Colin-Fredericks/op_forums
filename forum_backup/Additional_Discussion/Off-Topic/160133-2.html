                                I'm starting to believe that a massive multiplayer wod game wouldn't work. I think the WoD setting implies a relative small monsters population. So, unless it has a lot of mortal NPCs in a huge map or unless players rather go mortal instead of supernatural, it would break the mood of the setting I guess.

                                Probably a 'just multiplayer' game would work better. I'd like to see a 'friendly-fire allowed' co-operative multiplayer online rpg (without the first M in MMORPG): The concept for me is cooperation and fun but pvp should be allowed too, of course. I like 1st/3st person rpgs because they get me better immersed - I want to play a character not to be a pupeteer of a group of chars (at least for this game). And multiplayer because single-player games lack the warmth of companionship. But, as it was similarly stated above, the important thing is to have an engaging product which puts into practice the wod concepts, setting and operating rules no matter the media. It just needs to be simple but expandable, I think. If only CCP would grant rights to a small group of skillful artists.
