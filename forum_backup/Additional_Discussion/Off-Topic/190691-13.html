    EXPANSION/EXPLANATION:
    I feel like tossing this out, not only to show how I’m coming up with these things, but just generally to discuss it and because I want to share.
    To me, Changeling: the Lost is the most terrifying game White Wolf/Onyx Path has ever produced. It hits me on a primal, visceral level that the others can’t match. I’ve never even played it, just read the books. There’s several reasons for that, and I’ll go into a few here.
    1. More so than the other games, as a Changeling, your life is taken from you. It’s not as simple as a transformation into a vampire or a werewolf where you have to give up that life or adjust it. A mage can continue in that former life for some time. A Promethean, unless they manage to keep their former memories, is essentially dead and has to start over. But a Changeling…..they get taken someplace else, removed entirely from the situation and left to deal with that for the entire Durance. Then, upon their return, they could find no one noticed (which hurts a lot), they’ve had a Fetch destroying their lives (which hurts a lot), a Fetch has been doing a better job of being them than they ever did (which hurts a lot), having missed out on years of their life (which hurts a lot), or (if it’s been a really long Durance) no life at all to return to (which hurts a lot). And then there’s always a chance you’ll be dragged RIGHT BACK INTO IT.
    2. While they are all fantasy, it’s more “realistic” in the sense that one can be abducted, abused and returned to their life very changed. It’s a common occurrence with rape victims, kidnapping victims, soldiers, police, etc. There’s a whole disorder for it, PTSD.
    3. What you can become is what you fear. While each of the splats can be scary and represent something, both actually and metaphorically, that is feared, to me the Seemings take this to another level by manifesting as common human fears. The Beast is the fear of struggling for life, the Darkling is the fear of the unknown, the Elemental is the fear of losing control and being overwhelmed, the Fairest is the fear of being held to such expectations that you can never attain them, the Ogre is the fear of becoming the monster in the attempt to beat it, the Wizened is the fear of being left behind and/or forgotten. The Gentry works with what is within the character, so those fears are what is most likely to come out.
    4. We have different names for dreams and nightmares because we want them to be different things, but in reality a dream is a dream is a dream and they all come from the same place. That part of our heads that can give us wonderful dreams can also give us horrible nightmares. They have to go together, so it’s always going to be terrific and horrific and beautiful at the same time. Again, the other may have the potential for these things, too, but it’s so built into Changeling that there’s no getting around it.
    5. I think those of us with mental problems can see the dilemma in Clarity a bit better than others. It’s real easy to get lost inside your own perceptions to the point of losing touch with reality.
