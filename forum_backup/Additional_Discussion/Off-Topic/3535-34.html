                I was driving down I-70 today, and just outside the Centralia exit I saw two signs in the land between the shoulder of the interstate and the access road running parallel two it. They're vinyl banners, big enough to need two metal rods each to support them; both white backgrounds with three words in Capital red letters on them. The first one reads,
                HILARY
                TREASON
                PRISON
                It's a weird-ass sign, but basically comprehensible, I think. I mean, I'm pretty sure I get it. It's the second sign that really confounded me:

                OBAMA
                HALF
                WHITE
                I have no idea what to make of this. I mean, let's all agree right now that we can ignore as irrelevant that the statement is verifiably incorrect; the President has one white grandparent, not a white parent, making him a quarter white, not half. No, the thing that hung me up was trying to figure out why the sign's creator would even say that. What is the purpose of spreading that (mis)information? In the context of its physical proximity to the first sign, I have to assume it's somehow a condemnation, but of what?

                I thought about that a bit while driving, and it occurred to me that maybe the complaint here is one I haven't heard in ages, and then only in fiction: miscegenation. Maybe the person who created that sign is one of those guys who thinks Obama is an abomination because he has all the animal amorality and untameable lusts of the inferior Negro race, but also has inherited the intellect and cunning of the superior white man, so his evil soul has the tools to achieve long-lasting harm. Maybe the guy who made that sign basically thinks President Obama is an Uruk-Hai.

                Then I exploded laughing and haven't really stopped, because it just makes me very, very happy to think the sign's creator put that up as a warning that President Obama bears the white hand of Saruman.

                                                        I like this idea I am having for a Classic WoD fanfiction. It's based on old nostalgic memories of the chronicles I'd play and run with my younger brothers and occasionally one or two friends from school when I was 12-13 years old. We mostly played D&D 3.5 (a heavily house ruled and homebrewed variant of it, anyway) but we also played Vampire The Masquerade using only the 2e corebook and little else. Then I moved to Roanoke and we discovered Big Eyes Small Mouth and New World of Darkness, as well as the rest of the Classic WoD as well.

                                                        Well, the idea is that it's a story within a story, using fictionalized versions of my younger self, my brothers, and my friends (names will be changed of course). Things won't be exactly the same as the old games as it's been literally a decade since those days, and I'll be focusing on the actual campaign's story more than the framing story. I kind of like this idea I am having and I look forward to writing this fanfic and posting it over on the Masquerade forum if the mods are okay with fanfiction being posted here. I hope you guys will like the story once it starts.
