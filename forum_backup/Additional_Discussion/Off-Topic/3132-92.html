                                                        Perhaps a controversial subject for this particular site, but I am rapidly growing to have an utter contempt for role players. Not the role players who simply make a character with a background and stick to it for the duration of the game, but role players who do any of the following:

                                                        1: Deliberately build a weak character because "They're about characters, not numbers", and thereby become a burden on the party's overall performance and resources.

                                                        2: Insert themselves in any discussion where someone is looking for pointers on what stats or powers might work for their build and say something like "Just develop your character naturally!"

                                                        3: Accuse character concepts they don't like (usually ones that involve an objective skill set, like a police officer, a secret agent, or magic user) of being "shallow" or "too munchkin-y" and that the player should "try to make something with more depth."

                                                        4: Knowingly make a really stupid decision that hurts the rest of the party and/or the GM's plot because it's "In character" for them, and then take any suggestions that they should reconsider as a personal attack.

                                                        5: Refuse to learn the rules, and in some cases the basic concepts of dice rolling systems, because they're there to "play a character, not a game."

                                                        6: Accuse players who have an understanding of the system who try to help them with the above of "letting the rules get in the way of character development."

                                                        7: Enter any discussion about the merits and flaws of certain gameplay mechanic(s) and accuse everyone of "roll playing' instead of "role playing"

                                                        8: While doing any of the above, continuously implying that their way of playing the game is somehow magically superior to the people who enjoy crunching numbers and making optimal builds.

                                                        9: Implying (or outright stating) that people who don't do any of the above are playing the game wrong, and that they would't be welcome at their game table.

                                                        Seriously, fuck these people and their elitism. They're worse than the munchkins because at least they can be mitigated by throwing the occasional system goodie in their direction, but the role player will insist that you do things their way or you're having the wrong kind of fun.

                                                        EDIT: Oh, and a personal favorite is #10, where they insist that their way of playing is how RPGs have always been played, implying that all RPG games are not descended from miniature war simulation games.
