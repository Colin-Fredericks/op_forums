            People can hold any hateful opinion they want as long as they do not post it here. One can hate whites, blacks, jews, athiests, trans persons, gays, whatever, but when they post opinions on this forum attacking those groups, we act on it. It's an attack. Misgendering trans persons is an attack on trans persons. There are trans persons here. So we act on it.

            I'd love to live in a world where people are not judged by the color of their skin, their gender, who they love, or anything like that, but for their character alone. We are not there yet. But on the forum, we can at least try to stop this kind of thing. And it's not a witch hunt; either one posts that sort of thing or they don't. If someone is transphobic, for example, but never misgenders people or makes similar comments and posts helpful and insightful stuff, we won't go digging to see if they are a transphobe. We just act when the line is crossed.
