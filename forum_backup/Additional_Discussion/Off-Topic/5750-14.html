                                            I would like to discuss the Lego Movie, but I can't think of any meaningful way to do so without spoiling the plot. I know I was very underwhelmed by the commercials and previews, but for all that the commercials make the jokes seem like random wackiness hurled with desperate panic in a hope to make someone, anyone think the shtick they're seeing is funny, the movie itself in genuinely hilarious. I laughed at damned near everything I saw, whether out of appreciation for a good joke or simple delight at the imagination behind the set pieces and the skill of the animation, and I have not left a movie feeling so emotionally satisfied as I did yesterday in a very long time. The humor and plot are a bit meta, which may or may not be against its favor in your eyes, but I loved it. I also feel like the charges of the movie being unsubtle have to be answered: there's some truth to them, but at the same time, I have to give the movie mad props for making a villain who isn't actually a bad person, but one who has a caring and nuanced relationship with the hero and whose conflict with him is ultimately a differing ideology.

                                            I have made a solemn vow not to go to any toy store or toy aisle for the next six months, though, because I want to own everything I saw on that screen, and I'm sure Lego is happily selling it all. If you saw Metalbeard, then you know what I mean.
