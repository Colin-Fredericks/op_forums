                        Wow, I really need to get down to watching that last episode. On the other hand, since the next one is two weeks away still, maybe it's good that I (involuntarily) paced myself this time.

                        As for discussing the sexuality of the mane six, seems harmless enough. It's part of what the fanbase is supposed to do, I think; add and expand on the material provided in the show. Sexulaity is a facet of a character that the show will likely never address in earnest, so it falls on the fandom to do it.

                        Rarity is in fact one of the few we've seen semi-romantically interested in someone else, namely Trenderhoof in Simple Ways, and he was male. It's mixed up with the hope that he'll write an article about her as well, of course, but I think it's pretty evident that she wouldn't disapprove of a more intimate relationship either.

                        Some would probably argue that there's something going between Rarity and Applejack too, and they do have a very special relationship, but I think that's more about how their friendship shapes itself around their very distinct personalities (but they're also very similar; they are easily the two most hard-working ponies of the mane six and they both have little sister, for example). Do I ship it? Maybe a little. :3

                        I've not read many shipfics, but most people seem to agree that Rainbow Dash is bi ("Rainbow Dash ships with everyone" was a meme for a bit) or lesbian, probably due to stereotypes relating to her tomboy personality.

                                                Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                                                "We're going to celebrate the 100th episode of My Little Pony by showing all your favorite characters who have been sitting un-importantly in the background all of a sudden doing things.."
                                                In any other fandom, I'd be questioning this so much. It still sounds really backwards.
                                                When you put it like that, yeah, it does seem strange. :P

                                                On the other hand, the fandom has been integral in developing those background characters. Remember that at the start, they were literally just filler to make Ponyville seem populated. Names like Lyra, Bonbon, Octavia, Vinyl Scratch, Carrot Top and (naturally) Derpy all came from the fandom. Only recently have the writers started giving new background ponies their own names. Since the fandom were the ones to develop the background ponies into any kind of charcters, it kind of makes sense that an homage might involve them.

                                                The question is if the show intends to focus on canonizing fan-made aspects of the characters, or if it'll focus on providing new aspects of the characters for the fandom to relate to. I'm kind of hoping the latter, though surely some of the former will happen as well.
