    My layman's understanding is that True Lies was shot on a specific type of 35mm camera set up that was designed to make it easier to adapt the footage for television and VHS release at the time but ends up having some issues when doing a transfer to more modern DVD/BR and wide screen TV formats. Bigelow's Point Break was shot using the same type of camera, and apparently the recent Shout Factory release shows those issues when you try to do it unaltered. But the True Lies 4K ends up overcorrecting to the point where it looks like a movie made in the past five or ten years instead of a film made in the 1990s.

                                        Another guilty pleasure film is the Guy Pearce, Jeremy Irons and Orlando Jones film The Time Machine that was based somewhat on H.G. Wells book.

                                        Certainly it wasn't as good as the film a few decades earlier, but I found it enjoyable and giving a dead girlfriend as the motivation for the Doctor to create a time machine to save her and find that his efforts were fruitless and going further into the future to figure out why and seeing a Victorian Era person reaction to a society that would be unthinkable to him.

                                        Then we have Orlando Jones as a artificial intelligence that survives the end of the old world and us still around nearly a million years later with no real clue as to why. Perhaps the Morlocks machines were unknowingly keeping him around and the fact that he didn't go insane during this time is surprising.

                                        Then you have Jeremy Irons playing a Uber Morlock who explained why the Doctor can never save his girlfriend as it would create a paradox as it was her death that gave him the idea for the time machine and had she lived,. He would never come up with the idea and how could he go back in time to save her.
