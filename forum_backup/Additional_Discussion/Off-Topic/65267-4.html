                    #65
                    05-29-2014, 05:38 PM
                    Got through my New Release pile for this week and here's my in brief on them:

                    Justice League Dark #31- Better than last months but the book is still trying to find itself in the wake of Forever Evil and it just doesn't seem to know what to do with itself. Introduction of Black Alice/Alice Walker felt a little weak as I expected it to. But the twist at the end gives me hope for next month

                    Inhuman #2 - This was just great! Great follow up from the first issue and the things that are happening in the book seem like a good continuation of the after effects of the release of the Terrigan Gas. It's just too bad that its taken this long to get the new status quo of the Inhumans going. Also like the Nuhuman name for those who have just been changed. The conversations between Medusa and Captain America especially show how this can be an interesting game changer to the 616 universe

                    Suicide Squad #30 - More Forever Evil follow up but hey Deadshot actually looks like Deadshot at last so that makes me happy. It was also cool to see a super-villain break out attempt get foiled by actual law enforcement, that's not something you see often in comics. I especially liked Black Manta in this book and the subplot of "What's a supervillain supposed to actual DO when they get released from jail"

                    Wolverine #7 - I'm still bored with this whole "going under cover as a bad guy to get to Sabertooth thing." Some cool moments but they have been doing this for 7 issues now with no real payoff.

                    Avengers #30 - Okay so looks like the Avengers are going to be jumping ahead in time multiple times due to the shattering of the Time Gem, which does lead me to wonder why nothing similar to this happened when the other gems shattered. It just seems like an excuse to jump around in time. Which is fine, and the book itself is handling it well. I specifically liked Old Clint and Young Clint interacting.

                    Thunderbolts #29 - This one was a mixed bag for me, after they killed off some major characters last book I knew there was going to be some great deus ex machina at the end to restore everything and here it was. It was just a really BAD one. However some really good moments with Deadpool and the Leader made this a good and entertaining read, despite the flaws.

                    Guardians of the Galaxy #15 - Misleading cover is misleading. Don't advertise Captain Marvel on the cover if she's not anywhere in the book. And Bendis continues to show me that he is a good writer as long as it isn't Uncanny X-Men. I'm starting to think he just has too many books and too much clout at Marvel. The story itself was great, the humor was good and while it did feel a bit like filler, I was still entertained.

                    Uncanny Avengers #20 - FINALLY THIS PLOT IS GOING SOME WHERE! The whole Death Twins story arch has been taking WAY too long to actually accomplish anything. It's been like fifteen issues since this plot started and just now they are getting somewhere, where it feels like it might actually end. More time travel though (Marvel seems to adore the concept right now, wonder if it has anything to do with a certain movie in theatres?) but I did like the part were Logan said that had to be sure to return before Rogue was killed or he was scuttle the entire mission. Shows his guilt for what happened to her like... 10 issues ago.

                    All-New Invaders #5 - Great book is Great. Don't really have any more to say about this. The Invaders book has been very good, has had good dialogue between the characters and the writer seems to understand the characters motives and personalities. Read it.

                    Fantastic Four #5 - Another great book. The Fantastic Four having to defend against charges of Negligence, Child Endangerment, etc is a good twist for the book and She-Hulk being their lawyer is a nice touch to it. The book references stories that have been told throughout the entire history of the F4 and even uses classic art where it can. A good read in general and a good jumping on point for the F4 specifically.

                    Mighty Avengers #10 - Original Sin tie-in, but a good one. Deals with Blue Marvel's relationship with the Watcher and remembers that the FF book (previous to the current F4 book) gave the Watcher a wife who was pregnant. Also did a good job of making an alien pregnancy seem alien.

                    Ms. Marvel #4 - Concept is still solid, book is still moving a bit on the slow side. Four issues in before we get into her origin far enough for her to make her own costume. Just feels like a book that is being held back for some reason. But Kamala and her supporting cast continue to be good and the interactions between them feel very real.

                    Deadpool #29 - I still can't decide... 29 issues later if this book is awesome or bad. It just sort of jumps around between the two so much that it's hard to even view this as part of the 616 universe, even though it is supposed to be. But Deadpool being married to the Queen of the Monsters is pretty cool and having to now deal with Dracula might be fun.

                    Iron Man #26 - Though the story with Maleketh wrapped itself up a bit too easily the journey to the end of it was fun and this kept that going. The Rings of the Mandarin story has been pretty awesome and I'm interested to see where its all going to lead to in the end.



                    And that's it from me. That's all I read this week.
