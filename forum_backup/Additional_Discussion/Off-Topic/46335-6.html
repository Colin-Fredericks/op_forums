                                                Originally posted by Scelesta View Post
                                                but u've never made a cake on my own that wasnt out of a box) but dear god it sounds like so much work
                                                Magic Ratio: 1 part flour, 1 part sugar, 1 part cocoa. Add in a bit of baking powder. Add oil, milk/water, butter and egg to make it into a batter. The more oil and less water the more moist the cake.

                                                This is a slightly more advanced version, but you'll find the core is still there.

                                                The pre-mixed packages are pretty much just powdered versions of stuff such as egg and milk mixed in with the basic ingredients, this goes for stuff like hamburger helper as well.

                                                One of the more economical things you can do is grab some of the components from the bulk goods isle and use 'em instead of the pre-packaged stuff. Also makes it easier to tweak things a bit, mix a little of this into a little of that and the meals don't taste quite the same every time. Also can help cut back on salt.
