                                                            Visited the Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum (Weston, WV) the other day . . .and wow. Talk about iconic - the whole thing was like walking through a horror movie. There's a (commercial) haunted house on the site . . . but that's off to the side (in the former TB ward). All of the old facilities are still there, too . . . and large swaths of it date back to the old "snake pit"/Nellie Bly-era.

                                                            Rusty iron bars cover the windows. Paint hangs off the walls in great sheets. The old isolation cells, where inmates were chained to the wall, and periodically hosed off . . . Touring the main building (the Kirkbride) after dark is amazingly, gorgeously, creepy. I'm hard pressed to think of a better place to spend an October evening
