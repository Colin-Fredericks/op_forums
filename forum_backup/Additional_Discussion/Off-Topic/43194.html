                                          Few points of advice if you're not used to dealing with freezing cold weather.

                                          If it's getting particularly cold that night, you might want to eat the slightly higher water bill and leave some taps running a little bit. It's much harder for the water to freeze solid and burst pipes expensively if the water is flowing.

                                          If your tiny little heaters are not cutting it, always remember that you've got a giant heater in the kitchen. The stove. Turning it on high and cracking the door open, while not the best thing from a safety point of view (hot oven door being open) it does a wonderful job of heating the room up.

                                          Those ceiling fans you have for keeping yourself cool in the summer? They work the other way too you know, getting the hot air back down from above your head can help.

                                          Encourage people to stay in one room together, preferably in the center of the house. You can make it into a camp-out inside thing if you want, but focusing heating in the center of the house and turning the outside rooms into additional buffer space also helps to keep things warm. It also helps trap body heat, which is a good thing.
