    Does OPP allow the singular "They" in published work?

    09-01-2016, 03:55 AM
    I feel like I read somewhere that the somewhat awkward "s/he" pronoun usage for one character in Mage 2e was due to the Onyx Path style guide not allowing the singular They as a pronoun. Does anyone know if this is true, or if it was and has since changed?

    I'm curious about it as a wannabe freelancer.

      I don't recall that.

      I know that from a very old OPP style guide that they put out to the public the singular they is usually discouraged, but that's more of the old WW philosophy of using specific pronouns and just using different characters/example players so that a mix of "he" and "she" are represented (though other alternates have shown up in recent years as well).

      I thought the s/he usage in Mage 2e (esp. since it is only for that one character) was to denote a lack of certainty for a character that thinks s/he used to identify as one of the gender binaries but can't remember which anymore, as opposed to actively using "they" as an indicator of a gender non-binary identity.

      Even if the old guides didn't like the singular they, I'm pretty sure these days it would fly to expand the old idea of using multiple common pronouns to demonstrate an inclusive atmosphere in the text.

                      Fun fact:

                      Including a larger number of non-binary pronouns, without resorting to generic pronouns, makes the games better for gender minorities that rarely see representation because they see a game that says, "people like you exist in this game," and doesn't hurt anyone else in anyway.

                      Just like how way back in '91 WW opted to use "he" and "she" in equal numbers helped make the games extremely popular with women RPGers by showing a dedication to inclusive language, without any men having their game's ruined by seeing the word "she" to describe someone playing the game.

                                                          Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                          I know that from a very old OPP style guide that they put out to the public the singular they is usually discouraged
                                                          To be fair, it's in the current style guide linked in the Submission Guidelines.
                                                          Pronouns (singular vs. plural): A singular antecedent takes a singular pronoun. Pick a gender (or it), but don't use they, their, etc.
                                                          If your opponent is damaged by this attack, he (not they) may play combat cards.
                                                          If affected by this rite, the victim takes a one-die penalty to all of her (not their) actions.
                                                          Stop and think about this rule and your writing style; most people break it and don't even realize it.
