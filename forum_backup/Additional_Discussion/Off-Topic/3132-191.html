                    Wish I could hold on positivity longer. Or just believe more in myself in general.

                    I swear, Stephen King could proclaim me his literary heir and kiss me on the forehead to mark the event and the following day I'd still doubt if I was any good.

                    This does not apply only to writing, it's pretty much an issue everytime I confront with myself, but what frustrates me is the lack of control and logic I have when it comes to fighting it back. I know that I'm working hard, I know I'm getting results that one year ago I would have never dreamed of and I know that even by making simple comparisons with the past things are improving as a matter of fact, but that awareness does nothing to help.

                    One busy week, a couple of arguments and the barriers to keep this self-doubt at bay are gone, leaving me burnt out.

                    I don't even know what kind of achievements I demand to happen before I can outgrow this issue. Part of me wants to know, the other does not because I'm not sure I'd be ever able to get them. Another is afraid the issue can't be outgrown in the first place.

                    But no matter what happens, I just don't seem able to start a reaction that would make my self-esteem indipendent from what's happening around me. Even timid embers of confidence would be better than the current situation, where I honestly have never an idea if I'm a decent person or any good of a writer unless somebody else tells me so. Which silences my doubts only for a day or two.

                    And that's a thing I hate.


                    Anyways, sorry for the vent people. Things are not that bad, especially when compared to a few months ago. I'm just tired, disappointed and needed to let some steam off.
