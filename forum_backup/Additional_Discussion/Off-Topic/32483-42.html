                                I just re-joined this board, and I was a member for some time at the old White Wolf Forums... anyhow, I was reading through these posts. Really just kind of taking in what people were saying and the myriad of issues that all of you are dealing with. I just felt like jumping in and responding in a general sense, because I don't think that I actually know any of you... perhaps we chatted at some point in times past? Anyhow...

                                1) Suicide: Those of you who are thinking about ending it all, Think about the people that do actually love and care for you. To some, there may not be anyone and to you I then ask, have yu actually looked or paid attention? Or have you been so overcome by your issues that you failed to recognize a hand when it was offered? Suicide... do not take your life! Call on someone, anyone, somebody and just talk. Talk about anything and everything. Talk about the nothingness, but just talk and do not give up. I can tell you, life is a bitch. But life is also the most singularly wondrous thing that you will ever experience. If you end it now, you will never know what comes next if you don't. So, don't. For now, wait. Every day brings new opportunity.

                                2) Transgenders: Not my topic and I have oft been criticized because of my views on Transgender, homosexual, etc. The only point I want to make in this is that YOU are who YOU are. Do not let others (Myself included) stand against you. Be WHO you are because THAT is the REAL YOU! When you find who you are, you will be a bit happier. Look at Bruce Jenner (Or don't, I turn the tube off every time I hear his/her name(s). Anyhow, just be you and you will discover more about yourself that you ever thought possible.

                                3) Depression: Peeps, we all go through it. Some of us are clinically diagnosed with it (I am not but fight it every single day). Depression is what it is, a phase. Sometimes it takes a really long ass time to kick it.. sometimes its a mood swing, but it is just as real as you are sitting there reading this. If you have clinical depression, again I refer you to my notes under Suicide. Talk to someone, anyone, anybody. But just talk. It does in fact help, even if you don't realize it at the moment.

                                4) People, one of the most important things that everyone needs to remember is that you are loved for WHO and for WHAT you are. Sometimes, you can't see it. Sometimes you can. A Hug can help tremendously but it can also bring about strange unfamiliar feelings within you. Just take it at face value and try not to overthink it.
