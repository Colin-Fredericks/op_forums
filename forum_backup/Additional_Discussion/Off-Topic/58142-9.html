        @wulf : I hope this guy can cheer you up.

        So, I've read through the whole thread, and I figured that I should share my own tale.

        I am a bisexual man. I know that since I'm 16. I'm more attracted to men than women, but I don't really know if I should consider myself gay. In fact, it's kinda weird. When I think about a man, I think about having sex with him. When I'm attracted to a woman, I don't really fantasize about sleeping with her, but I have an easier time envisioning a lasting relationship. But I don't really have any ground to compare. I've only had one girlfriend two years ago, and what we had was hardly a relationship. Our first kiss was two months after our first date, and we didn't really see each other. In fact, I'm still a virgin at 23. Never had any boyfriend. Had and have some crushes on friends, but I know they're straight or not interested in men anymore. I only know three gay guys, and the only one living near my city is 15 years older than me.
        I'd like to be free, and I envy those who can have one-night-stand, or even short relationships. But I can't. First of all, I'm desperately romantic, and I have trouble flirting with people. It's awkward, and friends keep telling me I'm too subtle in my clues. And I've only been hit on once, and by a druken guy : I'm suffering from the Nice Guy Syndrome. People find me funny, and nice, but apparently I'm no Boyfriend Material. Also, when it comes to men, I have trouble identifying myself with the LGBT community. I don't like how we're represented, with the flamboyant gay stereotype. I'd like to meet someone... "normal". You know, someone that isn't defined by his sexuality, that doesn't dress in a way that screams "I LIKE GUYS". It's not a matter of shame, it's more like... I don't know, I don't see why we should be seeing this as something to be proud of. It's just a part of who we are, nothing more.
        That's also why I'm having trouble finding someone. I don't go to gay bars : they're really not my kind of place. If I wanna go for a drink, I'd rather go to a pub. I thought about exploring the bear community, but I'm hardly a bear myself, and I'm kinda afraid of being rejected. Also, you know, I don't have any close gay friend, so if I go to those places, I'm gonna be on my own, and I'll feel kinda awkward.

        So yeah. I feel lonely at times. I'm not feeling depressed or anything, I have friends, hobbies,.. But I'd just like to finally have someone. My friends keep saying that I'll certainly meet someone that i deserve soon, that I shouldn't rush it... But I'm so tired of waiting. I'm a student, I should be experimenting stuff. I'm at the prime of my life, dammit. So why can't I have someone?


        There. Sorry to unwind all of this on you guys, but this rant really helped me.
