                                    While I wouldn't say that all so called American centrists are lying. I WOULD say that a great many such people are lying to themselves or otherwise displaying a great deal of naivety about political realities. Extremism by nature invites confrontation, but it is not the only form of political action that invites confrontation. Protest by nature invites confrontation, rightfully so, but protest and extremism in the political sense are hardly synonyms. Yet, particularly recently in American politics I see that brush being used deliberately and cynically as a weapon against protest and activism. The American centrist would be wise to take a moment to examine the motivations of those that are trying to convince them of that. Before they find themselves labeling people who are fighting against extremism rife in America, the economic, the environmental, in the arena of civil liberty, as extremists themselves.
