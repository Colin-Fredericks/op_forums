                                        On a whim, I pulled out an old AD&D supplement a couple of days ago while I was boxing up my Exalted library. The book is called Powers & Skills, or something. It's an optional rule set designed to add more variety and player option to the character creation process by adding point-build systems for races and classes, as well as a bunch of other stuff I honestly don't remember AD&D well enough to say how it differed from the default. Whatever. Not the point.

                                        The point is, there's an alternate ability score system, where each of the six abilities is divided into two sub-abilities (so Charisma is linked to Leadership and Appearance, for instance). This obviously necessitated new ways to determine ability scores, so one the book presents is: roll 3d6 to determine the core ability score; apply it to both sub-abilities; flip a coin to determine whether the first sub-ability will be higher or lower than the second one; roll 1d4 and either add or subtract that from the first sub-ability depending on the result of the coin flip.

                                        I am pretty sure you have to be out of your mind to come up with a system like that.
