I've tried in person, online dating, even an app where it helps people get together for hugs (just hugs. Explicitly non-sexual; even has a function where you can report someone if they try to do anything other than what has been agreed on) - nothing. Not a single, tiny, drop of a hint that someone might potentially look at me as a remote romantic possibility (and one quite clear explicit statement that nobody ever will, but I try not to give that undue weight). Now, I can't really blame anyone for this, I'm not really a catch. I'm not physically attractive, I'm kind-of clever but nothing out of the box, and my personality isn't anything to write home about. And it's not that I need to be in a relationship to be happy, but there's a big difference between not being in a relationship because you choose not to be, and not being even eligible because nobody would ever want you.

                                        I've tried raising this with my friends (god knows why, they're immensely self-interested and shy away from talking about anything of weight that does not immediately concern them), to no avail. Well, unless you count laughs like I'm joking 'avail'. Which I do not. So I try turning to the Internet... I'll leave that to your imagination how well that particular adventure went.

                                        Now, before anyone accuses me of being 'entitled to a relationship' or any of that bullshit (not that I think any of you guys would, but, well, it happens), I don't feel 'entitled'. I'm just really depressed that apparently I'm so low on the totem pole that people find the idea of me being in a relationship as being funny. And either ignore me, let me down easy (which I guess is a nice way to say no, but it's still no) or outright abuse me for daring to approach them.

                                        All I want is some tiny crumb of a hint that there might be some kind of value to my existence. Because, to be honest, I'm having trouble seeing any. My work is laughably irrelevant, both in topic and scope, I've made roughly 10% of the progress I'm supposed to have made, largely due to my own incompetence, and I'm fairly certain I'm a lousy tutor on top of it all (when my students are less engaged than those of my immensely self-absorbed and disorganised colleague, I'm in serious trouble).

                                        Just really, really frustrated with the world. And myself, because that is where the problem is. I've even considered hiring a prostitute to pretend to be interested in me for a night, just so I can delude myself for a second there's something about me someone might find desirable, even if it's just my money, except I'm sure I'd just feel worse after.
