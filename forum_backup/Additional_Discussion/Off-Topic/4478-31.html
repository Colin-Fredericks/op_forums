My go-to response to these protestations is that I don't care what invisible psychic state he claims he may or may not be in if he puts me in a position of having to choose between believing his words or his actions. I'll go with the actions every time.

                            As a result of this, I've stopped defining emotions as feelings. I mean, they are feelings too, but I don't care about the feelings any more. I prefer these days to define emotions as behaviors. No matter what emotions a person feels or claims to feel or thinks he feels, I don't see how any reasonable person can look at the behavior of someone fighting to oppress a minority and from that infer anything other than hate.

                            Lately, I have turned that lens on myself. I have looked at the way I behave toward people I believe I love, at the way I have put them in a position of having to choose between their own stability and mine (and no, I will not be going into details on that; don't worry about it) and at the neglect, and I do not see how any reasonable person can look at my behavior and from that infer love.

                            What I feel is irrelevant next to what I show. There are a lot of things I can do to try to process and incorporate this information so I can be a better person; but, because I'm me, of course my very first instinct is to write a lot of apology e-mails, drop off the grid, and go kill myself so I won't have to live with the hot, sticky feeling of shame burning in my throat and choking me right now...which is the kind of action a person would look at and infer love for the people left behind, right? Dammit.

                            I am at a point where I have to grow up, admit that my pain does not excuse anything, accept responsibility for the the people I've used and hurt, and do what I can to make amends not because I want absolution but because the people I've hurt deserve that. I have no idea what I'll do, but I'll come up with something...just, you know, later. First I need to go to bed and have a pity-party for a while.

                            No one said growing up would be a quick process.
